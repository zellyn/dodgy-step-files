# TKShHealing PARTIAL verdict re-sweep (the 12 not sampled by VERDICT_AUDIT.md)

Scope: `VERDICT_AUDIT.md` sampled 8 of the 20 PARTIAL problems in `problems.json`
(`tkshh-closed-edge-full-period-unsplit`, `tkshh-edge-crossing-surface-singularity`,
`tkshh-edge-curve-inconsistent-with-vertex-removed`, `tkshh-edge-missing-3d-curve`,
`tkshh-edge-missing-pcurve` [overturned → COVERED via Gp012],
`tkshh-face-closed-surface-unsplit-at-seam`, `tkshh-face-intersecting-wires`,
`tkshh-face-natural-bound-missing`). This re-sweep covers the remaining 12.

Method: for each problem, the allegedly-missing subvariants in the `notes` field were
re-searched against `STEP_PROBLEM_CATALOG.md` (45,469 lines) with synonym, STEP-entity,
sibling-section, tier-3-vocabulary and Notes-text greps, plus structural byte-level sweeps
over `step-examples/**/*.stp` where the missing variant has a mechanical signature (e.g.
"EDGE_LOOP with exactly two ORIENTED_EDGEs referencing one EDGE_CURVE on a multi-bound
face"). Every candidate that survived the catalog search was read entity-by-entity from
its `.stp` bytes and checked for reachability from the shape representation (orphaned
`GEOMETRIC_CURVE_SET` carriers and `shape_null == True` fixtures do not count toward a
repair-on-real-topology variant). Every UPGRADE below was independently re-verified
byte-for-byte by the report author, not accepted on a sweep agent's verdict alone.

## Summary

Of the 12 PARTIAL verdicts the audit did not sample, 9 hold and **3 are overturned to
COVERED**: `tkshh-face-wires-bound-multiple-disjoint-regions` (uncited Tsh013 + Tfa011
provide exactly the "canonical two-disjoint-outer-wires" input the notes call missing),
`tkshh-shell-free-boundary-gap` (uncited Tfa020 provides the nonzero-gap free-boundary
crack for the closing-tolerance branch), and `tkshh-wire-3d-curve-gap` (whose own cited
fixtures Twi180/Twi189/Twi199/Twi266 are face-less free wires with genuine gaps — the
notes mischaracterized them, so the "free wire" branch was covered all along). All three
overturns are the same failure mode the audit predicted from the Gp012 find — incomplete
catalog/bytes re-search, twice via uncited fixtures, once via mischaracterized cited ones —
and its "a full re-sweep would likely find 1-3 more" estimate lands exactly at 3. The nine
upheld PARTIALs were each confirmed with mechanism-targeted grep sweeps plus structural
byte-level searches (two-coincident-edge slit and cross-face cascade sweeps ran over the
whole corpus and returned no qualifying topology), and two candidate-killing reachability
findings were confirmed en route (Gs002's empty `SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL`, Gn034/N142
`shape_null==True`). Corrected TKShHealing tallies (after the audit's Gp012 upgrade, 36/19/5):
**39 COVERED / 16 PARTIAL / 5 GAP** of 60.
