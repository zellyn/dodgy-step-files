# Catalog↔Fixture entity-citation match report

Generated by validation/src/step_corpus/tier_entity_match.py.

## What it does

For every canonical catalog entry, extract uppercase STEP entity-type
tokens from the **Reproducer recipe** field and confirm they appear in
the matching fixture. Drift detector: catches catalog edits that did not
propagate into the fixture, or fixtures rebuilt with a different scaffold
whose recipe was not refreshed.

Tokens are filtered against a denylist of standards/vendor/keyword names
(see _DENYLIST in tier_entity_match.py); short non-underscore tokens
(< 5 chars) are skipped because they are almost always abbreviations.

Entries in §12.10 (perf) and §12.11 (adversarial) are flagged abstract:
their recipes describe scaling patterns or attack vectors, not specific
entity sets.
