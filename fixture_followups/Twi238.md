## OCCT source pointer + prose

Branch (prose-laundered): FixConnected detects pairs of adjacent
ORIENTED_EDGEs where the previous edge's end vertex doesn't match
the next edge's start vertex; it either inserts a bridging
degenerate edge, snaps the endpoints, or rejects the gap.

## Attempts

### Attempt 1 (wave 53, 2026-06-17) — INVALID

- **Approach**: 4-vertex (P0..P3) wire with 3 edges in EDGE_LOOP,
  with a 0.05-unit gap between P0 and P1.
- **Refutation**: EDGE_LOOP per ISO 10303-21 schema MUST be a *closed*
  loop of edges. Putting a 3-edge sequence in EDGE_LOOP that doesn't
  close to its starting vertex is structurally invalid Part-21 —
  parser rejects before FixConnected sees the wire.

## Why it's hard

The defect class is about gap-bridging in an *unclosed* wire, but
Part-21's EDGE_LOOP entity demands closure. To demonstrate gap-bridging
the fixture needs to:
- Use a Wire wrapper that allows openness (e.g. inside MANIFOLD_SOLID
  context where wires are referenced loosely), or
- Embed the disconnected ORIENTED_EDGEs in a structure that the healer
  receives directly without parser-level EDGE_LOOP validation, or
- Use a closed EDGE_LOOP but introduce the disconnect via 3D-vs-pcurve
  mismatch (so the topology claims closure but the geometry doesn't).

## Next directions

  ID (topology says closed) but the underlying 3D curves have endpoints

  wire.
- Consider promoting to `kernel-test-pair` — the disconnect may only
