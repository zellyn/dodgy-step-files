## Why it's hard

The defect class is about how the healer *constructs* the apex pole edge after seeing a wire that needs healing. A test fixture has to provide input geometry that triggers the branch without already containing the (possibly buggy) output the branch produces. That's intrinsically a "input that forces the healer to act" rather than "input that contains the buggy state", which is the harder of the two patterns.
