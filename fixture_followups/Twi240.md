## OCCT source pointer + prose

Branch (prose-laundered): FixShifted detects pcurves on periodic
surfaces (cylinder, cone, torus) whose parametric U-values are
shifted by full periods relative to neighboring edges. The fix
re-parametrizes the shifted pcurve back into the canonical [0, 2π]
range while preserving the underlying 3D geometry.

## Why it's hard

The defect requires:
- A periodic surface (CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE, CONICAL_SURFACE, or
  TOROIDAL_SURFACE)
- A wire wrapping the surface in a way that crosses the seam
- Pcurves on the wire edges declared with U-values outside [0, 2π]
  (e.g. one edge at U=2.0..4.0 instead of U=0.0..2.0)

The fixture needs all three layers correct, and the geometry has to
be a wire on a surface — not a wire in free space.
