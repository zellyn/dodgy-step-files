## OCCT source pointer + prose

Branch (prose-laundered): FixAddCurve3d reconstructs an edge's 3D
curve from its pcurve when the 3D curve is missing. The reconstruction
samples the pcurve over its parametric range and lifts samples to
3D via the underlying surface. When the pcurve is a closed circle
but the EDGE_CURVE itself is declared with distinct start/end vertices
(open edge), the lift produces a 3D curve whose endpoints don't match
either declared vertex — the resulting EDGE_CURVE is geometrically
inconsistent with its topology.
