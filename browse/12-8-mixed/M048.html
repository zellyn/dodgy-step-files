M048 — Material / appearance / physical attributes silently dropped on STEP export

Sender

Autodesk Fusion (V025); SolidWorks/Inventor → Inventor (V048)

Description

Visual appearances and physical (density, material name) attributes are absent from Fusion's STEP output (V025); Inventor strips appearance from STEP imports (V048); HOOPS Exchange merging materials on export (W024); Material with missing name crashes reader (G057); Material with 0-density emits invalid STEP density entities (G059).

Reproducer recipe

XCAF document with material density=0 or material with empty name field; export to STEP.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: emit `STYLED_ITEM`, `COLOUR_RGB`, `MECHANICAL_DESIGN_GEOMETRIC_PRESENTATION_REPRESENTATION` at minimum; skip density emission for zero-density materials; default empty datum/material description. Must not silently drop attributes.

Notes

Synonyms: "material attributes silently dropped", "STEP export loses appearance", "physical attributes missing on export", "material/density lost on STEP write", "appearance stripped on STEP output". **See also**: Pmi059.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'MATERIAL_DESIGNATION')
  • contains(b'MASS_DENSITY_MEASURE(0.0)')
