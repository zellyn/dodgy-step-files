M067 — Transformation relation creation failed (AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D with zero-vector DIRECTION ratios in ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION); placement falls back to identity

Description

A child component's transformation reference (e.g., `ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION`) cannot be resolved to a valid 4×4 matrix; the reader falls back to identity placement. The child appears at the wrong location. Bug-reporter language: "child placement wrong", "STEP transformation creation failed", "components stack at origin".

Reproducer recipe

An NAUO whose transformation references an `ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION` whose `transform_item_2` axis directions are zero vectors.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject the assembly placement as malformed; do not silently use identity.

Notes

Synonyms: "child at origin bug", "STEP transformation creation failed", "child placement wrong", "ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION fell back to identity", "zero-vector DIRECTION in placement". **OCC behavior**: silently substitutes the identity placement when the transformation reference cannot be resolved, leaving children at the origin without a diagnostic; kernel mishandling; the catalog above forbids silent identity-substitution.
