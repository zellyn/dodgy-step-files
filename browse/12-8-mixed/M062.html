Description

A `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP` declares parent→child while a corresponding `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` declares child→parent. The reader silently picks one. Bug-reporter language: "assembly relation reversed", "parent/child conflict in STEP", "SRR vs NAUO direction mismatch".

Reproducer recipe

An NAUO declaring A is parent of B, plus an SRR with the opposite direction.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject the conflict with diagnostic; trust NAUO by convention but make the choice explicit. Must not silently resolve.

Notes

Synonyms: "assembly direction conflict", "SRR vs NAUO direction mismatch", "parent/child conflict in STEP", "assembly relation reversed".
