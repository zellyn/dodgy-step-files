M110 — AP209 ply orientation vector not normalized

Sender

deduced — composite-modeling tool storing ply angle as raw vector without normalization

Description

A composite ply's fiber orientation `DIRECTION` ratios sum to a magnitude of e.g. `2.0` (vector `(2.0, 0.0, 0.0)`) instead of unit length. STEP `DIRECTION` is supposed to be unit-normalized — consumers that use the ratios directly (e.g. for stiffness-matrix transformation) compute doubled effective fiber-angle weight. Bug-reporter language: "AP209 ply direction not unit", "fiber angle vector wrong magnitude", "composite stiffness 2x off".

Reproducer recipe

`DIRECTION('ply_0deg', (2.0, 0.0, 0.0))` referenced as a ply orientation in a `LAMINATE_TABLE`.

Expected kernel behavior

normalize direction silently and warn; or reject the ply specification as malformed.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP209 unnormalized direction", "ply orientation magnitude wrong", "fiber DIRECTION not unit length", "STEP DIRECTION ratios sum >1". Synonyms: "AP209 unnormalized direction", "ply orientation magnitude wrong".
