M168 — AP238 turning_finishing_face with feed direction inverted

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M168.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 turning_finishing_face feed-direction semantics
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP-NC face-turn feeds outward", "AP238 finishing pass leaves stock", "lathe retracts during finish cut".

Description

A `TURNING_FINISHING_FACE` declares a feed_direction vector pointing OUTWARD (away from the spindle axis) where face-turning requires inward feed (toward the axis). The tool retracts during what should be the finishing pass, leaving uncut stock and a witness mark from the entry point. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 face-turning direction reversed", "STEP-NC feed_direction wrong sign", "turning finish pass goes outward".

Reproducer recipe

`TURNING_FINISHING_FACE` with `feed_direction = DIRECTION('feed_outward_WRONG',(1.0,0.0,0.0))` (outward +X) instead of inward (-X) toward the spindle axis.

Expected kernel behavior

validate feed_direction against operation semantics for face-turning (must be inward); reject as malformed or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 face-turn feed reversed", "STEP-NC turning finishing direction wrong", "feed_direction outward on face turn", "lathe finish pass retracts not cuts".
