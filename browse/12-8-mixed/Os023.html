Description

A thin shell is "thickened" inward by a distance that causes the inner offset surface to self-intersect; the inner surfaces from neighbouring faces collide. Bug-reporter language: "thicken self-intersects", "thickening too much", "shell thickness exceeds local diameter".

Reproducer recipe

A small cylindrical hole of radius 5 mm in a plate; request inward thickening of 10 mm (more than the hole diameter).

Expected kernel behavior

Detect via self-intersection check; reject; or clamp to the maximum feasible thickness.

Notes

Synonyms: "thicken collision", "thicken self-intersects", "thickening too much", "shell thickness exceeds local diameter", "thicken inner surface collides".
