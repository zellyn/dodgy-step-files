M088 — AP210 etherometric vs metric measurement context confusion

Sender

deduced — ECAD exporter writing schematic-sheet coordinates into the layout context

Description

A PCB stackup has ply outline coordinates that look like etherometric (sheet-normalized 0.1) values, but the file's `representation_context` is the global Cartesian one with mm `LENGTH_UNIT`. Consumed as mm, the ply outline collapses from a 100mm board to a 1mm speck. Bug-reporter language: "PCB outline tiny on import", "AP210 board shrunk to 1mm", "etherometric vs metric confusion".

Reproducer recipe

A `POLYLINE` with corner coordinates (0,0)(1,0) (1,1)(0,1) used as a `PLY_BOUNDARY_REPRESENTATION` in a context whose `LENGTH_UNIT` is mm.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: validate that the coordinate magnitudes are plausible for the declared unit; warn on suspiciously small footprints, or reject (require an explicit conversion) when crossing context kinds.

Notes

Synonyms: "PCB units wrong", "outline scale 100x off", "AP210 etherometric vs metric mismatch", "stackup coordinates 0.1 but unit is mm", "PCB outline collapses to speck".
