M173 — AP238 milling_toolpath consecutive points spaced beyond tool diameter

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M173.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §6.2 milling_toolpath sample-density invariant
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 toolpath samples too sparse", "STEP-NC milling skips between points", "uncut islands between toolpath samples".

Description

A `MILLING_TOOLPATH` lists consecutive points spaced 12 mm apart while the tool diameter is 6 mm. Linear interpolation between samples leaves stock between passes; the toolpath has no implicit guarantee of contiguous engagement. Controllers either over-feed (gouging) or leave uncut islands. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 milling sample distance > tool dia", "STEP-NC toolpath skips", "uncut stock between samples".

Reproducer recipe

a `MILLING_CUTTING_TOOL` with diameter 6 mm; a `MILLING_TOOLPATH` whose underlying `POLYLINE` has points 12 mm apart.

Expected kernel behavior

validate sample-density against tool diameter; warn or refine the path before emission.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 toolpath sample gap > tool diameter", "STEP-NC sparse milling samples", "milling path discontinuity", "toolpath leaves uncut between points".
