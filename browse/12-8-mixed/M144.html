M144 — AP210 NETWORK_NODE chain forms a cycle (signal feedback path)

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP210 net connectivity)

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-210 §7 (network connectivity acyclicity for non-feedback nets)
  • bug-reporter language: "AP210 net loop", "PCB signal connectivity cycle", "ECAD net never terminates".

Sender

deduced — ECAD exporter that wrote net node order from a graph traversal without cycle detection

Description

`NETWORK_NODE` entries link node A -> B -> C -> A. A connectivity walk of the net never terminates. The net is electrically nonsensical for a non-feedback signal: a clock distribution net is short-circuited to itself. Bug-reporter language: "AP210 net cycle", "PCB net loop", "ECAD signal feedback path".

Reproducer recipe

three `NETWORK_NODE`s and a `NETWORK` listing them in cyclic order without a DAG terminating step.

Expected kernel behavior

detect cycles in the net connectivity graph; reject or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "PCB net loop", "AP210 connectivity cycle", "signal feedback path".
