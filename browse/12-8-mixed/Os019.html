Description

A medial-axis-style "middle path" between two boundary wires of a thin region cannot be computed because the wires diverge or the region is non-simply-connected. Bug-reporter language: "middle path fails", "centerline of strip can't be computed".

Reproducer recipe

Two diverging wires (a fan shape) whose envelope is not strip-like.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with "non-strip region" diagnostic.

Notes

Synonyms: "centerline failure", "middle path fails", "centerline of strip can't be computed", "medial axis between wires fails".
