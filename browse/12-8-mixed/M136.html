M136 — AP203 APPROVED_ITEM points to a product not in the design pool

§12.8 (sub-class: AP203 approval cycle integrity)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M136.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-203 §4.4 (approval / approved_item)
  • bug-reporter language: "AP203 approval phantom product", "CC_DESIGN_APPROVAL dangling reference", "approved entity not declared".

Sender

deduced — PDM exporter that emitted approvals from a stale cache while the design pool was filtered

Description

An `APPROVAL` entity is bound via `CC_DESIGN_APPROVAL` to a `PRODUCT_DEFINITION` whose entity number is not declared in the file. The approval references a phantom product. The approval cycle is broken: what was approved cannot be located. Bug-reporter language: "AP203 approval points to nothing", "PDM approval orphan".

Reproducer recipe

`CC_DESIGN_APPROVAL(approval_ref,(#999))` where `#999` is undeclared.

Expected kernel behavior

detect dangling approval references; reject or drop the approval with diagnostic.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP203 approval orphan", "phantom approved item", "PDM approval points to phantom".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'CONFIG_CONTROL_DESIGN')
  • contains(b'CC_DESIGN_APPROVAL')
  • contains(b'#999')
