M105 — AP238 ISO 8601 timestamps on operations going backwards

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 timestamp consistency)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M105.stp

Sources

  • deduced from ISO 10303-238 §6.4 operation chronology rules
  • ISO 8601 ordering

Sender

deduced — STEP-NC publisher copying timestamps from a different job or with a clock-skew bug

Description

Successive `MACHINING_OPERATION`s carry `time_stamp` attributes that go backwards: operation A's timestamp `2026-04-30T10:00:00`, operation B (next in workplan) `2026-04-30T09:00:00`. Either the publisher has a clock-skew issue, the ops were authored out of order and stamped at authoring time, or the file was assembled from multiple jobs. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 timestamps inverted", "STEP-NC operation B before A", "timestamps backwards".

Reproducer recipe

Two `MACHINING_OPERATION`s in workplan order with monotonically *decreasing* ISO 8601 timestamps in their description / approval attributes.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: validate timestamp monotonicity along workplan order; emit a warning diagnostic on inversions and continue.

Notes

Synonyms: "timestamp regression", "AP238 chronology wrong", "operation timestamps decreasing", "ISO 8601 backwards across ops", "machining operation time_stamp out of order".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'AP238_STEP_NC_MIM')
  • contains(b'2026-04-30T10:00:00')
  • contains(b'2026-04-30T09:00:00')
