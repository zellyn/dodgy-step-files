Os002 — Inward offset by a distance equal to a cylinder's radius (face collapses to axis)

§12.8 (sub-class: offset degenerate)

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/Os002.stp

Sources

  • OCCT BRepOffset_Status.Degenerated (uncovered class evidence)

Sender

deduced — OCCT uncovered-class sweep

Description

A cylindrical face of radius R is offset inward by exactly R. The offset surface collapses to the cylinder axis (a line); no surface remains.

Reproducer recipe

A cylinder of radius 5 mm. Request inward offset of 5 mm.

Expected kernel behavior

Detect the degenerate case; emit a "result is the axis" diagnostic; refuse to produce a face.

Notes

Synonyms: "axis collapse offset", "offset collapses to axis", "offset surface degenerate", "offset distance equals radius", "cylinder offset to centerline". **See also**: Os003.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE')
  • contains(b'OFFSET_SURFACE(#14,-5.0')

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == True
