Description

A pipe / sweep operation places the section profile on a plane that, evaluated along the guide curve, never intersects the guide. Bug-reporter language: "pipe section plane misses guide", "sweep section misplaced".

Reproducer recipe

Guide curve is a `LINE` along the X axis at z=0. Section plane at z=10, normal (0,0,1). The plane is parallel to the guide at non-zero offset.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with "section plane parallel to guide" diagnostic.

Notes

Synonyms: "sweep plane miss", "pipe section plane misses guide", "sweep section plane doesn't intersect spine", "section plane parallel to guide".
