Os020 — MakeEvolved: profile sweep along a non-planar spine self-intersects

Description

A profile is "evolved" (swept with rotation) along a 3D spine; the resulting swept surface self-intersects where the profile rotates past itself. Bug-reporter language: "evolved surface self-intersects", "swept profile crosses itself".

Reproducer recipe

A small circular profile evolved along a tight 3D helix where the profile diameter exceeds the helix pitch.

Expected kernel behavior

Detect self-intersection; trim or reject.

Notes

Synonyms: "swept-surface fold", "evolved surface self-intersects", "swept profile crosses itself", "MakeEvolved self-intersection along spine".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
