M152 — AP210 microvia depth exceeds substrate thickness

  • bug-reporter language: "AP210 via deeper than board", "microvia drill exits back side", "ECAD via depth out of range".

Sender

deduced — ECAD exporter that copied a via depth value from a thicker reference design

Description

A `VIA_DEFINITION` has stackup_depth = 0.5 mm but the laminate's total stackup thickness is 0.4 mm. The drilled microvia is deeper than the board itself; the drill exits through the back of the board. Bug-reporter language: "AP210 microvia exceeds stackup", "PCB drill exits back".

Reproducer recipe

`LAMINATE_TABLE` summing to 0.4 mm; a `VIA_DEFINITION(..,LENGTH_MEASURE(0.5),..)`.

Expected kernel behavior

validate via depth <= stackup thickness; reject or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP210 via depth violation", "PCB drill through", "via depth exceeds substrate", "microvia exits back of board", "drill_depth > stackup thickness".
