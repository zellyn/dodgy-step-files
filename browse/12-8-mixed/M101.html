M101 — AP238 two5d_milling_operation applied to a 3D-only feature

  • deduced from ISO 10303-238 §5.8 two5d_milling_operation applicability constraints

Sender

deduced — CAM publisher mis-classifying feature dimensionality

Description

A `TWO5D_MILLING_OPERATION` (Z-constant, X-Y contour pass) references a feature whose toolpath topology is intrinsically 3D; e.g. a `FREEFORM_FEATURE` on a sculpted surface. The 2.5D Z-step semantics cannot represent the surface; consumers either silently approximate (gouging the surface or leaving stock) or fail to plan motion. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 2.5D op on 3D feature", "STEP-NC contour can't follow surface", "wrong operation type for feature".

Reproducer recipe

A `TWO5D_MILLING_OPERATION` whose `its_feature` attribute references a freeform-surface feature.

Expected kernel behavior

validate operation-feature dimensionality match; reject or warn that operation is geometrically inadequate.

Notes

Synonyms: "2.5D vs 3D mismatch", "AP238 operation feature dimensionality", "Z-step on freeform".
