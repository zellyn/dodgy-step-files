Description

A multi-edge fill is requested with constraint edges whose tangent directions at shared corners contradict each other (e.g., two edges meet at a corner with conflicting tangent demands). The fill surface cannot satisfy both. Bug-reporter language: "fill constraint incompatible", "fill corner tangent conflict".

Reproducer recipe

Three edges defining a triangular hole; specify G1 tangent constraints at all three corners with mutually inconsistent tangent vectors.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: relax constraints in priority order; report which constraint was relaxed.

Notes

Synonyms: "incompatible fill constraints", "fill constraint incompatible", "fill corner tangent contradicts", "n-sided fill conflicting tangents".
