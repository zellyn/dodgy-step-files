M151 — AP210 declared trace impedance contradicts stackup geometry

  • bug-reporter language: "AP210 50 ohm trace not 50 ohm", "PCB impedance contradicts stackup", "ECAD declared Z0 wrong".

Sender

deduced — ECAD exporter that copied 50 ohm impedance metadata from a template without re-deriving from the new stackup

Description

An electrical `PROPERTY_DEFINITION` on a `CONDUCTOR_TRACK_PROFILE` declares `characteristic_impedance = 50` ohm, but the actual stackup geometry (15 um copper, 0.2 mm dielectric, 4 mm trace width with FR4 Er=4.4) yields ~ 22 ohm by standard microstrip calculation. The declared impedance and the geometry-derived impedance disagree by > 50 %.

Reproducer recipe

`CONDUCTOR_TRACK_PROFILE` with width 4.0 over a 0.2 mm `LAMINATE_OR_PLY_DEFINITION`; `PROPERTY_DEFINITION` declares characteristic_impedance = 50 ohm.

Expected kernel behavior

cross-check declared characteristic impedance against the stackup-derived value; warn or reject on significant disagreement.
