M036 — Datum target axes nearly identical

Description

Two direction vectors used to define a datum-target or supplemental-geometry frame have a cross-product norm ≤ 0.05 — orthonormal frame cannot be reliably constructed.

Reproducer recipe

Datum target axis with two near-parallel `direction` ratios.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept, or heal-and-accept by substituting an orthonormalized frame.

Notes

Synonyms: "datum target axes nearly identical", "near-collinear DIRECTION pair", "frame cross-product near zero", "datum frame ill-conditioned".
