M164 — Linear pattern of arc-shaped feature emits twisted/corrupted faces in STEP

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: tessellation / pattern)

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M164.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #12176
  • bug-reporter language: "Arc shape in linear pattern corrupted / twisted", "appears when number of slots = 4 but not other numbers", "tessellation issue, has workaround".

Sender

FreeCAD PartDesign LinearPattern feature instantiated 4 times over an arc-shaped Pad.

Description

A LinearPattern of count=4 over an arc-shaped Pad yields a solid where the 4th instance's face normals are inverted relative to the first 3. The on-disk symptom is 4 `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`s where instances 1–3 have consistent `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` direction vectors but the 4th has its `axis` direction `(0.0, 0.0, -1.0)` instead of `(0.0, 0.0, 1.0)`. Pattern counts other than 4 reproduce the issue at counts 8, 12 (multiples of 4); suggesting a sign-bit overflow in the pattern's index-to-transform table.

Reproducer recipe

STEP file with 4 `MAPPED_ITEM`s referencing the same `REPRESENTATION_MAP`, where the 4th's `transformation_operator.axis` is `(0.0, 0.0, -1.0)` while instances 1–3 have `(0.0, 0.0, 1.0)`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: writer builds the pattern transform table without sign-bit corruption; receivers should validate consistency of repeated-instance transforms within a pattern and emit a warning when an instance's axis flips relative to its siblings. Reject with E_PATTERN_INSTANCE_INCONSISTENT or warn-and-proceed by repairing the orientation; never silently accept inverted instances.

Notes

**See also**: P018, A006. Synonyms: "LinearPattern of arc twisted on STEP export", "pattern instance count 4 corrupts orientation", "tessellation issue with arc pattern", "fourth instance is inverted".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(8) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'MAPPED_ITEM') >= 4
  • matches(rb'DIRECTION\([^)]*,\(0\.0,0\.0,-1\.0\)')
