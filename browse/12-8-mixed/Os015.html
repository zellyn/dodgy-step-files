Description

A draft (taper) angle applied to a face produces a deformed surface that cannot be re-fitted to the kernel's surface representation: the deformed B-spline degenerates or the analytic surface family doesn't extend to the requested angle. Bug-reporter language: "draft face recomputation fails", "draft angle breaks face".

Reproducer recipe

A `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` face; request a 45° draft. Cylinder cannot become a cone-like surface in the analytic family — recompute fails.

Expected kernel behavior

Convert to B-spline before drafting; or reject.

Notes

Synonyms: "draft taper failure", "draft face recomputation fails", "drafted surface won't refit", "draft angle exceeds analytic range".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(3) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].surface_type == "cylinder"
  • n_edges_total >= 1
