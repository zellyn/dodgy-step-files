M184 — AP238 executable_relationship forms a cycle (A precedes B, B precedes A)

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §6.4 executable_relationship acyclicity invariant
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 dependency cycle", "STEP-NC workplan won't schedule", "executable graph has loop".

Description

Two `EXECUTABLE_RELATIONSHIP` entities form a cycle where operation A precedes operation B and operation B precedes operation A. The workplan has no valid topological order; consumers either deadlock during scheduling or pick an arbitrary order that breaks the dependency. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 executable_relationship cycle", "STEP-NC dependency graph cyclic", "workplan deadlock from circular precedes".

Reproducer recipe

two `MACHINING_OPERATION`s `#100` and `#101`; two `EXECUTABLE_RELATIONSHIP`s `(#100→#101)` and `(#101→#100)` declared in the same file.

Expected kernel behavior

detect cycles in the executable-relationship graph; reject as unschedulable.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 dependency cycle", "STEP-NC executable graph loop", "workplan A precedes B precedes A", "circular precedes".
