M179 — AP238 touch_probing tolerance smaller than probe stylus radius

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M179.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 touch_probing measurement-resolution invariant
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 probe tolerance below stylus", "STEP-NC inspection unattainable resolution", "touch probe can't resolve sub-radius features".

Description

A `TOUCH_PROBING` op declares a measurement tolerance of 0.005 mm while the touch probe's stylus tip ball diameter is 4.0 mm (radius 2.0 mm). The stylus radius compensation cannot resolve features below the tip radius - the declared tolerance is fundamentally unattainable with this probe. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 probe tolerance < stylus radius", "STEP-NC inspection resolution impossible", "touch probe tip too big".

Reproducer recipe

a `TOUCH_PROBE` with stylus diameter 4.0 mm; a `TOUCH_PROBING` op with tolerance 0.005 mm referencing that probe.

Expected kernel behavior

validate measurement tolerance against probe stylus radius; reject as unattainable or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 probe tolerance below stylus", "STEP-NC touch probe over-spec'd", "inspection resolution exceeds probe capability", "stylus diameter exceeds tolerance".
