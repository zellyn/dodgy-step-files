M182 — AP238 setup_instruction sequence with non-monotonic time stamps

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M182.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.4 setup_instruction chronology rule
  • ISO 8601 ordering
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 setup steps out of order", "STEP-NC setup timestamps wrong", "operator instructions go backward in time".

Description

A sequence of `SETUP_INSTRUCTION` entities (mount fixture, locate workpiece, tighten clamps) carries time_stamps in non-monotonic order: instruction A at 09:00, B at 08:30, C at 09:15. Either the publisher copied stamps from a different job or the operator instructions were re-ordered without updating times. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 setup-instruction order wrong", "STEP-NC setup timestamps backwards", "operator step time goes back".

Reproducer recipe

three `SETUP_INSTRUCTION` entities with timestamps `2026-05-02T09:00:00`, `2026-05-02T08:30:00`, `2026-05-02T09:15:00` (non-monotonic).

Expected kernel behavior

warn-and-accept; validate timestamp monotonicity along setup-instruction sequence and emit a diagnostic on inversions.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 setup-instruction time inversion", "STEP-NC operator steps non-monotonic", "setup chronology wrong", "instruction timestamps decreasing".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'AP238_STEP_NC_MIM')
  • contains(b'SETUP_INSTRUCTION')
  • contains(b'2026-05-02T09:00:00')
  • contains(b'2026-05-02T08:30:00')

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == True
