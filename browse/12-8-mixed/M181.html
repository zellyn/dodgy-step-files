M181 — AP238 workpiece_complete_for_nc missing required clamping references

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M181.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.4 workpiece_complete_for_nc clamping-mandatory invariant
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 workpiece has no clamps", "STEP-NC fixturing missing", "no keep-out zones for collision check".

Description

A `WORKPIECE_COMPLETE_FOR_NC` entity is declared with an empty clamping_position list. AP238 requires that workpieces be tied to their fixturing for collision-checking; an unfixed workpiece cannot be safely machined. The controller has nothing to enforce keep-out zones against. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 workpiece-complete missing clamps", "STEP-NC no fixturing references", "workpiece floats with no clamping".

Reproducer recipe

`WORKPIECE_COMPLETE_FOR_NC('finished_part',$,$,$,$,$,$,(),$)` - empty parenthesised clamping list.

Expected kernel behavior

validate that workpiece-complete entries declare at least one clamping reference; reject as malformed.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 fixturing absent", "STEP-NC clamps missing on workpiece", "workpiece-complete with empty clamping list", "no keep-out zones declared".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'AP238_STEP_NC_MIM')
  • contains(b'WORKPIECE_COMPLETE_FOR_NC')
