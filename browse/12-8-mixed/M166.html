M166 — Heatsink-style fin array crashes export pipeline at faceted sub-shape boundary

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: tessellation / fin array)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • FreeCAD #18524
  • bug-reporter language: "When I export below simple model of a heatsink to VRML FreeCAD crashes", "Export to STEP or WEBGL goes without issues", "heatsink fins make exporter unhappy".

Sender

FreeCAD heatsink-style PartDesign body with a tight linear array of thin fins.

Description

A body comprising a base + 30 thin parallel fins (each fin a 1 mm × 30 mm × 30 mm rectangular Pad) crashes the VRML writer in the tessellator and emits a misshapen STEP where the fins' inter-fin gap faces are paired with normals oriented inwards toward the gap (negative-volume slivers). Although the STEP writer doesn't crash, the fin-pair faces in the on-disk file have `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` orientation flags that don't agree with the surface normal direction.

Reproducer recipe

STEP body containing 30 parallel thin `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`s arranged 1 mm apart along Y; some fin-pair faces have `FACE_OUTER_BOUND.orientation` `.F.` while the surface normal is also flipped — double-flip cancels but still produces the wrong sign on volume integral.

Expected kernel behavior

writer's tessellator handles fin arrays without crashing; receivers detect double-flipped orientation pairs and reject or heal.

Notes

**See also**: P023. Synonyms: "fin array crashes export tessellator", "heatsink causes vrml crash and step misorientation", "tightly spaced parallel solids cause writer issues".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(6) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP') >= 3
  • matches(rb'FACE_OUTER_BOUND[^;]*\.F\.')
