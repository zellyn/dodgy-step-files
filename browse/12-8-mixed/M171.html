M171 — AP238 thread_turning pitch differs from thread-spec pitch

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 thread_turning pitch-vs-thread-spec consistency
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 thread pitch wrong", "STEP-NC cuts wrong thread", "thread won't engage mating part".

Description

A `THREAD_TURNING` op declares a lead/pitch of 1.5 mm while the referenced thread feature's spec is M10x1.0 (pitch 1.0 mm). The controller cuts a thread whose pitch doesn't match the design intent; the mating fastener won't thread in. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 thread pitch mismatch", "STEP-NC thread-turn wrong lead", "M10x1 cut as M10x1.5".

Reproducer recipe

a `THREAD` feature with spec pitch 1.0 mm and a `THREAD_TURNING` op whose declared pitch is 1.5 mm; the two attributes refer to the same feature.

Expected kernel behavior

validate that thread-turning pitch matches the referenced thread feature's spec; reject as malformed or warn loudly.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 thread pitch disagrees with spec", "STEP-NC thread-turn lead wrong", "thread feature vs op pitch mismatch", "lathe cuts wrong thread pitch".
