M170 — AP238 turning feed-direction ambiguous (radial vs axial conflict)

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M170.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 turning_feed_direction single-direction invariant
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 turn op has two feed directions", "STEP-NC radial AND axial feed", "turning operation contradicts itself".

Description

A `TURNING_OPERATION` carries two contradictory feed-direction attributes: one says radial (X-axis), the other says axial (Z-axis). The controller has no way to decide whether to perform a face-turning or longitudinal-turning move; some implementations pick the first, some the second, some refuse. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 turning feed direction ambiguous", "STEP-NC radial-vs-axial feed conflict", "turning op declares two feed axes".

Reproducer recipe

a `TURNING_OPERATION` populated with both `radial_feed = DIRECTION((1.0,0.0,0.0))` and `axial_feed = DIRECTION((0.0,0.0,1.0))` as separate non-null feed_direction-class attributes.

Expected kernel behavior

detect contradictory feed-direction attributes; reject as malformed or pick a documented default and warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 feed direction conflict", "STEP-NC turning has two feeds", "radial vs axial ambiguity", "turning operation feed axis undecidable".
