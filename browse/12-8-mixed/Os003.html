Os003 — Inward offset by a distance greater than a cylinder's radius (face inverts)

§12.8 (sub-class: offset reversed)

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/Os003.stp

Sources

  • OCCT BRepOffset_Status.Reversed (uncovered class evidence)

Sender

deduced — OCCT uncovered-class sweep

Description

A cylindrical face of radius R is offset inward by R + δ (δ > 0). The offset surface is on the opposite side of the axis from the original; the face has been turned inside out.

Reproducer recipe

A cylinder of radius 5 mm. Request inward offset of 6 mm.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject as physically impossible, or accept and tag as reversed for the consumer to handle.

Notes

Synonyms: "negative offset", "flipped offset face", "offset reversed", "offset inside-out", "offset distance greater than radius". **See also**: Os002.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
