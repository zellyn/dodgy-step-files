M172 — AP238 turning_operation on workpiece without rotation axis

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M172.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 turning_operation rotational-workpiece prerequisite
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 turning a block", "STEP-NC lathe op on non-rotating part", "turning workpiece has no spindle axis".

Description

A `TURNING_OPERATION` targets a `WORKPIECE` that has no rotational-axis declaration (no spindle_axis, no axis_of_revolution placement). The controller has no axis to spin the stock around; turning is meaningless on a non-rotating workpiece. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 turn op on prismatic block", "STEP-NC missing rotation axis", "lathe op without spindle axis".

Reproducer recipe

a `WORKPIECE` declared with no axis_of_revolution; a `TURNING_OPERATION` whose workingstep mounts that workpiece.

Expected kernel behavior

validate that the workpiece declares a rotational axis when targeted by a turning operation; reject or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 turning prismatic part", "STEP-NC missing rotation axis on lathe op", "block targeted by turning operation", "turn op without spindle axis".
