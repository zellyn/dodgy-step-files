M167 — AP238 turning operation cut depth exceeds workpiece radius

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M167.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 turning_operation depth-of-cut bounds
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP-NC turning cuts through part", "AP238 depth too deep on bar stock", "turn op parts the workpiece".

Description

A `TURNING_OPERATION` declares a radial depth-of-cut (e.g. 30 mm) that exceeds the stock cylinder's radius (12.5 mm on a 25 mm bar). The single-pass move passes through the spindle axis and out the far side, parting the workpiece with no support. The controller either crashes the tool against the chuck or severs the bar mid-cut. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 turning cut-through", "STEP-NC depth bigger than radius", "lathe op slices bar in half".

Reproducer recipe

`TURNING_OPERATION` with a depth-of-cut `MEASURE_WITH_UNIT(POSITIVE_LENGTH_MEASURE(30.0), mm_unit)` on a workpiece whose stock-cylinder radius is 12.5 mm.

Expected kernel behavior

validate depth-of-cut against stock geometry; reject as malformed or warn that the operation severs the workpiece.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 turning cuts through stock", "lathe depth exceeds radius", "STEP-NC parting cut unintended", "turning depth-of-cut > workpiece radius".
