M077 — AP238 fixture references different workpiece than the setup is machining

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 fixture / workpiece linking)

P1 — silent data loss

topology

Sources

  • deduced from AP238 §3.4 setup / workpiece_setup invariants

Sender

deduced — STEP-NC publisher with fixture-stack copy/paste error

Description

A `SETUP`'s `its_workpiece_setup` chain wires a fixture (vise jaw, clamp, tombstone) to one `WORKPIECE`, but the `WORKINGSTEP` beneath that setup names a *different* `WORKPIECE` as the part being machined. The fixture is geometrically constraining a part that isn't in the cut. Bug-reporter language: "STEP-NC fixture in wrong place", "vise attached to wrong part", "AP238 workpiece_setup orphaned".

Reproducer recipe

`WORKPIECE_SETUP(#100, .., (#110), ..)` (fixture to plate_a) but `WORKINGSTEP(.., #101, #200)` (machining plate_b).

Expected kernel behavior

cross-validate workpiece references between setup and workingstep; reject inconsistency or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "fixture orphaned", "wrong workpiece in setup", "AP238 fixture-workpiece mismatch", "setup fixture wires to different part".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
