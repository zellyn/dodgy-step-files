M085 — AP210 ply with mismatched top/bottom material designations

deduced — ECAD exporter conflating ply with prepreg+core stack

Description

A `LAMINATE_OR_PLY_DEFINITION` (one homogeneous layer in a PCB stackup) is expected to have a single material across its thickness, but its top and bottom material references point to different `MATERIAL_DESIGNATION`s (e.g. FR4 top, polyimide bottom). The "ply" is actually a transition film that should be modeled as two adjacent plies. Bug-reporter language: "PCB ply has two materials", "AP210 stackup inconsistent", "laminate top/bottom mismatch".

Reproducer recipe

`LAMINATE_OR_PLY_DEFINITION('core_ply', $, FR4_ref, polyimide_ref, LENGTH_MEASURE(0.2))`.

Expected kernel behavior

detect the mismatch; either split the ply into two plies, reject, or honor only one of the two materials and warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "PCB layer with split material", "stackup ply inconsistent", "AP210 ply has mismatched top/bottom material", "transition film mislabeled as ply", "ply has two different MATERIAL_DESIGNATIONs". Synonyms: "PCB layer with split material", "stackup ply inconsistent".
