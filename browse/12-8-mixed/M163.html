M163 — Imported STEP per-face colours scramble after any unrelated edit operation (style binding instability)

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: appearance / styled-item binding)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M163.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #13363
  • bug-reporter language: "Material/colors look right after import", "after any operation (even adding an unrelated Part Cube) colors get corrupted", "each face seems to get a random color and is non-reversible".

Sender

FreeCAD STEP importer's `STYLED_ITEM` binding pass that uses per-session sub-shape pointers as the binding key.

Description

An imported STEP body whose `STYLED_ITEM` records bind colours to specific `ADVANCED_FACE` entity numbers loads with correct per-face colours, but adding an unrelated Part Cube to the document re-walks the OCAF tree and the per-session sub-shape pointer indices shift by one, leaving every coloured face referencing the wrong sub-shape. The on-disk symptom is a STEP whose `STYLED_ITEM` `item` attribute is `#NN` where `#NN` is the *N*-th `ADVANCED_FACE` in document-order, and the receiver's binding logic relies on order rather than on the entity number.

Reproducer recipe

STEP solid with 6 faces, each carrying a distinct `STYLED_ITEM` referencing a `COLOUR_RGB` — entity numbering deliberately interleaved so the document-order doesn't match entity-id order.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: receivers must bind styled-items by stable attributes (entity number or `name`), not by document-walk order. Index-based bindings that drift on document edits are a coverage gap; emit W_UNSTABLE_STYLE_BINDING and recover by re-binding to entity identity.

Notes

**See also**: A018. Synonyms: "imported step colors scramble after edit", "STYLED_ITEM binding by index breaks on document change", "face colors get random after unrelated Part add", "color corruption non-reversible".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(6) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'STYLED_ITEM') >= 4
  • contains(b'COLOUR_RGB')
