M087 — AP210 signal net node references unresolved terminal

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP210 connectivity)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M087.stp

Sources

  • deduced from AP210 §7 network connectivity invariants

Sender

deduced — ECAD exporter dropping terminal definitions

Description

A `NETWORK` (signal net) lists `NETWORK_NODE`s, one of which references a `TERMINAL` by id that isn't declared in the file. The net is dangling at one end. A connectivity-check downstream reports the net as having one endpoint instead of two. Bug-reporter language: "AP210 net has missing terminal", "PCB net dangling reference", "signal connects to nothing".

Reproducer recipe

`NETWORK_NODE('CLK_net_node_b', #999)` where `#999` is undeclared.

Expected kernel behavior

detect unresolved references; warn and drop the node, or reject the net.

Notes

Synonyms: "PCB net dangling", "unresolved terminal", "AP210 net references missing TERMINAL", "signal net node has no endpoint", "network has dangling node".
