Os009 — ThruSections loft profile is closed wire mixed with open wire

Description

A loft (ThruSections) is requested between two profiles: profile 1 is a closed wire (a circle), profile 2 is an open wire (an arc). The loft algorithm needs both endpoints free or both joined; mixing them is unsupported. Bug-reporter language: "loft profiles topology mismatch", "open and closed loft profiles".

Reproducer recipe

Two `WIRE` entities — one a `CIRCLE`-edge wire (closed), one a single-`LINE`-edge wire (open) — submitted as ThruSections profiles.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with topology-mismatch diagnostic, or close the open profile by adding endpoints.

Notes

Synonyms: "loft topology mismatch", "open and closed loft profile", "ThruSections mixed open/closed wires", "loft profile boundary inconsistent". **See also**: Os010.
