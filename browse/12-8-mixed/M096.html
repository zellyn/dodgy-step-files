M096 — AP238 trajectory missing connecting curves between adjacent segments

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 trajectory continuity)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M096.stp

Sources

  • deduced from ISO 10303-238 §6.2 trajectory G0/G1 continuity rules

Sender

deduced — CAM post-processor emitting tool-up moves implicitly

Description

A `TRAJECTORY` consists of two `COMPOSITE_CURVE_SEGMENT`s whose endpoints don't coincide; segment A ends at `(10,0,0)` and segment B starts at `(20,0,0)`, with no connecting `CURVE` between them. The CAM tool presumably intended a rapid-traverse retract / reposition, but emitted no explicit connector. The controller either fails to plan motion or executes a feed-rate move through the workpiece. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 toolpath has gap", "STEP-NC missing rapid move", "trajectory segments don't connect".

Reproducer recipe

`COMPOSITE_CURVE` with two segments `(0,0,0)→(10,0,0)` and `(20,0,0)→(30,0,0)`, with no third connecting segment, used as a `TRAJECTORY` basis curve.

Expected kernel behavior

validate adjacent-segment continuity; insert a rapid-traverse connector and warn, or reject as discontinuous.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 trajectory gap", "missing rapid retract", "trajectory segments don't connect", "STEP-NC composite curve not chained", "toolpath has unconnected segments".
