Os011 — ThruSections loft used outside its supported envelope

Description

ThruSections is invoked on input combinations the algorithm does not handle (e.g., one section is a single `VERTEX`, another is a wire; or sections are added after `Build()` was called). Bug-reporter language: "loft wrong usage", "loft section is a point".

Reproducer recipe

A loft pipeline that begins with a `VERTEX_POINT` profile and then a closed wire profile.

Expected kernel behavior

Detect at section-add time; reject or auto-promote vertex to a degenerate wire.

Notes

Synonyms: "point-to-wire loft", "loft wrong usage", "loft section is a point", "ThruSections used outside envelope", "loft from vertex to wire".
