M061 — Scaling-factor encoded as non-unit DIRECTION ratios in AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D / ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION skipped on write

Description

A child component's placement transformation includes a non-unit scale factor — typically encoded as DIRECTION ratios with magnitude != 1.0 (e.g., `(2.0, 0.0, 0.0)`) inside an AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D or ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION. The writer drops the scale silently because STEP placements canonically have no scale and DIRECTION should be normalized. The exported file represents the child at the wrong size. Bug-reporter language: "scale factor lost on write", "STEP doesn't preserve scale", "exported assembly wrong size", "non-unit DIRECTION encodes hidden scale".

Reproducer recipe

An assembly where a child's AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D references DIRECTION entities with non-unit ratios (e.g., (2.0, 0.0, 0.0) and (0.0, 2.0, 0.0)) implicitly encoding scale 2.0; on write, the scale silently becomes 1.0.

Expected kernel behavior

Bake the scale into the child's geometry before write; or reject the assembly with a "scale not representable" diagnostic.

Notes

Synonyms: "scale dropped", "AXIS2_PLACEMENT scale lost", "non-unit DIRECTION scale stripped", "ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION ignored scale".
