M103 — AP238 tool trajectory passes through workpiece body before designated cut

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 toolpath collision)

P1 — silent data loss

crash spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M103.stp

Sources

  • deduced from ISO 10303-238 §6.2 toolpath safety / clearance invariants

Sender

deduced — CAM publisher emitting trajectory with omitted retract height

Description

A `TRAJECTORY`'s approach segment passes from the tool-change position straight through the workpiece volume to reach the feature start — i.e., the line segment from `(0,0,200)` to `(50,30,-5)` intersects the workpiece body at `(50,30,0)`. The implicit retract is missing. The controller either crashes the tool against the workpiece or performs an unintended plunge. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 tool collision", "STEP-NC plunge through part", "approach trajectory hits workpiece".

Reproducer recipe

`TRAJECTORY` whose first segment is a straight `LINE` from `(0,0,200)` to `(50,30,-5)`, with workpiece geometry occupying the box `(0.100, 0.100, 0.50)`.

Expected kernel behavior

collision-check toolpath against workpiece envelope; reject the trajectory or warn loudly.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 tool crash", "trajectory through part", "missing retract".

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
