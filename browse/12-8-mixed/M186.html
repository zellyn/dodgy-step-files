M186 — AP238 coolant type incompatible with workpiece material

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M186.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 coolant_operation material-compatibility safety rule
  • OSHA/NFPA shop-safety guidance
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 water on magnesium", "STEP-NC unsafe coolant", "coolant type wrong for material".

Description

A `COOLANT_OPERATION` declares coolant type WATER on a workpiece whose material is declared as MAGNESIUM. Water on burning magnesium accelerates the fire (hydrogen liberation); shop safety codes prohibit this combination. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 coolant unsafe for material", "STEP-NC water on Mg", "coolant material incompatibility".

Reproducer recipe

a `WORKPIECE` with `MATERIAL('AZ31_magnesium', ..)`; a `COOLANT_OPERATION` with mode `.WATER.` targeting that workpiece.

Expected kernel behavior

cross-check coolant type against workpiece material; reject incompatible safety-critical combinations.
