M074 — AP238 left-handed setup coordinate frame on multi-axis machine

Description

A `SETUP`'s csys is built from an `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` whose `axis` (Z) and `ref_direction` (X) yield `Y = Z × X` pointing in the opposite direction from the intended right-handed Y. On a 3-axis machine the consequence is a mirrored part; on a 5-axis machine, all rotary motions invert and the resulting geometry is the geometric mirror of the design. Bug-reporter language: "machined part is mirrored", "5-axis output is the reflection of the design", "STEP-NC axis frame wrong-handed".

Reproducer recipe

`AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` with `axis = DIRECTION(0,0,-1)` and `ref_direction = DIRECTION(1,0,0)` used as a setup origin; combined with a multi-axis `MACHINING_WORKINGSTEP`.

Expected kernel behavior

validate handedness via cross-product sign; reject as malformed or honor the explicit frame and warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "mirrored toolpath", "left-handed coordinate system", "axis2_placement Z flipped".
