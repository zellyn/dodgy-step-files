M050 — Composite material LLAI (Limited Length / Area Indicator) gaps

Description

Composite-ply geometry that requires "limited length or area indicators" to mark plies extending only over part of a base surface is not yet uniformly implemented; older AP242 implementations either drop the LLAI metadata or encode it inconsistently across vendors. LLAI extension targeted at AP242 Ed.4.

Reproducer recipe

A composite layup with a partial-area ply round-tripped through AP242 Ed.2 — partial-area boundary lost.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept (archive-stable) only when AP242 Ed.4 LLAI extension is available; otherwise warn-and-accept (document loss).

Notes

Synonyms: "composite LLAI gaps", "limited length area indicator missing", "ply extends only over part of base", "LLAI metadata inconsistent across vendors".
