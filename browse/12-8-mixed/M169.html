M169 — AP238 turning_roughing stepover exceeds tool nose radius

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M169.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 turning_roughing stepover-vs-nose-radius rule
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 lathe stepover too big", "STEP-NC scallops between turn passes", "turning leaves stair-step".

Description

A `TURNING_ROUGHING` op declares a radial stepover (e.g. 4.0 mm) larger than the cutting tool's nose radius (0.8 mm). Each successive pass leaves an unsupported scallop the nose can't reach, producing a stair-stepped surface that the next pass either gouges or leaves visible. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 turning stepover > nose r", "STEP-NC roughing scallops", "lathe rough pass leaves steps".

Reproducer recipe

a `TURNING_MACHINING_TOOL` with `nose_radius = 0.8` mm; a `TURNING_ROUGHING` op with `stepover = 4.0` mm.

Expected kernel behavior

validate stepover against tool nose radius; warn or reject when the ratio leaves un-machinable scallops.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 stepover gouge", "STEP-NC turning rough pass scallops", "stepover larger than nose radius", "lathe stair-step finish".
