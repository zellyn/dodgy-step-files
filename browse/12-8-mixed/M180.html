M180 — AP238 inspection_result measurement uncertainty exceeds feature tolerance

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M180.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 inspection_result uncertainty-vs-tolerance gauge-capability rule
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 measurement uncertainty exceeds tol", "STEP-NC inspection meaningless", "gauge can't resolve tolerance".

Description

An `INSPECTION_RESULT` records a `measurement_uncertainty` of 0.05 mm against a feature whose design tolerance is +/-0.02 mm. The measurement is statistically incapable of confirming or denying conformance - it tells you nothing. Reporting it as "in spec" or "out of spec" is misleading. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 inspection uncertainty > tolerance", "STEP-NC gauge R&R insufficient", "measurement uncertainty exceeds feature tol".

Reproducer recipe

a `PLUS_MINUS_TOLERANCE` of +/-0.02 mm on a feature; an `INSPECTION_RESULT` with `measurement_uncertainty = 0.05` mm against the same feature.

Expected kernel behavior

validate that measurement uncertainty is below feature tolerance; reject as gauge-incapable or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 gauge R&R insufficient", "STEP-NC inspection uncertainty exceeds tol", "measurement uncertainty too large", "gauge can't resolve feature tolerance".
