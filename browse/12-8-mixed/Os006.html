Os006 — Offset vertex-fuse step fails (corner vertices don't merge)

deduced — OCCT uncovered-class sweep

Description

Per-edge offset produces a small triangle of "extra" surface near each corner; the algorithm tries to fuse the three (or more) corner vertices into one but fails when the offset distances or local angles disagree.

Reproducer recipe

A polyhedral solid with one corner vertex of valence 4; request a uniform outward offset.

Expected kernel behavior

Synthesize spherical patches at corners to bridge the gap, or accept the unfused result with diagnostic.

Notes

Synonyms: "offset corner", "corner cap missing", "offset corner gap", "offset can't merge corners", "offset vertex fuse failure".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=reject ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • n_edges_total >= 8
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[3].surface_type == "plane"
