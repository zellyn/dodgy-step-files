M138 — AP203 APPROVAL_PERSON_ORGANIZATION with empty PERSON field

§12.8 (sub-class: AP203 approval personal record missing)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M138.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-41 (approval_person_organization required attributes)
  • bug-reporter language: "AP203 approver missing", "approval has no person", "PDM audit trail incomplete".

Sender

deduced — PDM exporter that omitted the personal name when the approver was identified only by organizational role

Description

An `APPROVAL_PERSON_ORGANIZATION` binds an `APPROVAL` to a `PERSON_AND_ORGANIZATION` whose `PERSON` field is empty - no identifier, no last name, no first name. The approval has an organizational stamp but no individual approver. Audit trail is incomplete; the approval cannot be attributed to a person.

Reproducer recipe

`PERSON('','',$,$,$,(),(),())` referenced inside `PERSON_AND_ORGANIZATION` referenced inside `APPROVAL_PERSON_ORGANIZATION`.

Expected kernel behavior

detect empty PERSON identity; reject the approval or warn that the audit trail is incomplete.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP203 anonymous approver", "PDM approval no signer", "missing approver name".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'CONFIG_CONTROL_DESIGN')
  • contains(b'APPROVAL_PERSON_ORGANIZATION')
  • contains(b"PERSON('','',")
