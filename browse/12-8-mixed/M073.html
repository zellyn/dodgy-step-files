M073 — AP238 part-coordinate vs machine-coordinate unit mismatch

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 unit context)

Sources

  • deduced from AP238 §5.1 representation_context partitioning

Sender

deduced — STEP-NC system mixing imperial machine and metric design

Description

The workpiece geometry is declared in a representation_context with mm units; the toolpath is declared in a separate context with inch units. No conversion / context-relating entity links them. A consumer that overlays toolpath on workpiece without consulting unit metadata produces a 25.4x scale error: tiny toolpaths on a large part, or vice versa. Bug-reporter language: "STEP-NC toolpath is 25× too small", "G-code in inches but model in mm", "imported AP238 has wrong scale".

Reproducer recipe

Two `GEOMETRIC_REPRESENTATION_CONTEXT`s — part with mm `LENGTH_UNIT`, machine with `CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT('INCH', 25.4 mm)` — hosting separate `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION`s that share entity references but no `REPRESENTATION_CONTEXT_RELATIONSHIP`.

Expected kernel behavior

require / synthesize a context-relating entity; warn loudly when toolpath and workpiece live in different unit contexts; reject if the relationship cannot be resolved.

Notes

Synonyms: "STEP-NC unit confusion", "AP238 inch/mm mismatch", "model and toolpath in different units". **See also**: U-prefix unit entries.

Expected validation

occt=signal(11)/signal(11) gmsh=signal(11) ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'AP238_STEP_NC_MIM')
  • contains(b'TRAJECTORY')
  • count_entity_def(b'GEOMETRIC_REPRESENTATION_ITEM') >= 1
