M185 — AP238 machining_tool_feedspeed product exceeds tool envelope

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M185.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 machining_tool_feedspeed manufacturer-envelope cross-check
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 chipload too aggressive", "STEP-NC feed-per-tooth above max", "tool will fracture from rpm × ipt".

Description

A `MACHINING_TOOL_FEEDSPEED` declares spindle 12000 rpm and feed-per-tooth 0.10 mm with a 4-flute end-mill, giving an effective feed of `12000 × 4 × 0.10 = 4800` mm/min. Tool manufacturer's rated chip-load for a 6 mm carbide cutter at this RPM is closer to 0.04 mm per tooth; the tool will fracture. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 feedrate × rpm too high", "STEP-NC chipload exceeds tool max", "feed-per-tooth × spindle exceeds envelope".

Reproducer recipe

`MACHINING_TOOL_FEEDSPEED` with spindle_speed 12000 rpm, feed_per_tooth 0.10 mm, on a 4-flute carbide end-mill rated for 0.04 mm/tooth at that RPM.

Expected kernel behavior

validate feedspeed product against tool-family envelope; warn loudly or reject.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 aggressive chipload", "STEP-NC feed-per-tooth × rpm too high", "feedspeed exceeds tool envelope", "milling tool overspeed".
