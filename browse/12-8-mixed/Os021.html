Description

A 3D curve is requested to be projected normally onto a target surface, but the curve is far from the surface and no normal foot exists. Bug-reporter language: "projection failed", "no normal projection".

Reproducer recipe

A `LINE` at z=10. Target surface is a small sphere at the origin radius 1. No normal projection from the line to the sphere.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject; or compute orthogonal-projection-with-extension.

Notes

Synonyms: "curve too far from surface", "projection failed", "no normal projection", "normal projection has no foot", "curve has no projection on target".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(1) ifc=schema_n/a
