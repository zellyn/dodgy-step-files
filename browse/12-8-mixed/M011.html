M011 — Supplemental geometry inadvertently included in part-level GVP

§12.8 (sub-class: validation-property + supplemental)

pmi

Description

Supplemental elements (datum planes, centerlines, axis placements) may be reported by a writer as if they were part of the manufactured shape, inflating volume/area/centroid. Some unbounded supplemental elements cannot even produce a finite volume/area.

Reproducer recipe

Build a part with a `constructive_geometry_representation` containing an unbounded plane and a centerline. Compute and emit the volume/area/centroid GVP at part level by accidentally summing the supplemental elements.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: kernel must exclude any element referenced solely by `constructive_geometry_representation` from part-level GVP.

Notes

Synonyms: "supplemental geometry inflates GVP", "datum plane in part-level volume", "validation property fails check", "centerline counted in shape area", "unbounded supplemental geometry yields infinite volume". **See also**: A038, M033.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(27) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[5].surface_type == "plane"
