M082 — AP209 surface element node order is clockwise (negative Jacobian)

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP209 element winding)

deduced — FEA exporter with mesh-orientation export bug

Description

A `SURFACE_3D_ELEMENT_DESCRIPTOR` for a `linear_quadrilateral` lists its corner nodes in clockwise order when viewed from the surface normal (CCW is the convention). The element's Jacobian determinant is negative everywhere; integral computations come out with reversed sign. Bug-reporter language: "FEM element has negative Jacobian", "quad winding wrong", "AP209 reversed element".

Reproducer recipe

A planar-square quad with corners (0,0)(1,0)(1,1)(0,1) listed in order n1,n4,n3,n2.

Expected kernel behavior

detect negative Jacobian; reject, reverse the node order, or warn the consumer that downstream signs may be inverted.

Notes

Synonyms: "negative Jacobian element", "wound backwards", "element flipped".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'STRUCTURAL_ANALYSIS_DESIGN')
  • contains(b'SURFACE_3D_ELEMENT_DESCRIPTOR')
