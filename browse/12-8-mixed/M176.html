M176 — AP238 boring tool diameter exceeds start-hole diameter

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M176.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 boring_operation insertion-feasibility rule
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 boring bar bigger than hole", "STEP-NC bore tool can't enter hole", "boring impossible".

Description

A `BORING_OPERATION` declares a boring-bar tool diameter (15 mm) larger than the pre-drilled start-hole diameter (10 mm). The boring bar physically cannot enter the hole - the operation is impossible. The controller either crashes the tool against the workpiece face or refuses to plunge. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 bore tool too big for hole", "STEP-NC boring insertion impossible", "boring bar exceeds start-hole dia".

Reproducer recipe

a `ROUND_HOLE` of diameter 10 mm; a `BORING_TOOL` of diameter 15 mm referenced by a `BORING_OPERATION` targeting that hole.

Expected kernel behavior

validate that boring tool diameter is less than start-hole diameter; reject as infeasible.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 boring tool larger than hole", "STEP-NC bore-bar won't fit", "boring insertion impossible", "tool diameter exceeds start-hole".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
