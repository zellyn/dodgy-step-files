Sender

deduced — PDM exporter that batched a single ACTION across related items spanning incompatible classes

Description

An `APPLIED_ACTION_ASSIGNMENT` distributes a single `ACTION` (e.g. an engineering review) across a list that mixes incompatible classes - both a `PRODUCT_DEFINITION` and a `DOCUMENT_FILE`. The action's domain semantics are ambiguous: was the review of the design or of the document? Bug-reporter language: "AP203 action ambiguous arity", "PDM action targets multiple kinds".

Reproducer recipe

`APPLIED_ACTION_ASSIGNMENT(action_ref, (product_def, document_file))`.

Expected kernel behavior

detect mixed-class items list; reject or warn and split into homogeneous assignments.
