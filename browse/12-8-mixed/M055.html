Description

`FACETED_BREP` requires planar faces; `POLY_LOOP` placed on a non-planar surface violates the schema.

Reproducer recipe

`FACETED_BREP` referencing a face on a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` bounded by a `POLY_LOOP`.

Expected kernel behavior

reject the face; warn and skip.

Notes

Synonyms: "POLY_LOOP on non-planar surface", "FACETED_BREP non-planar face", "schema-illegal polyloop placement", "faceted brep requires planar faces".

Expected validation

  • n_edges_total >= 4
  • n_vertices_total >= 8
  • brepcheck.valid == True
