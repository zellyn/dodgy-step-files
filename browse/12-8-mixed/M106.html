M106 — AP238 workplan total execution time exceeds shift length

Sender

deduced — STEP-NC publisher with feed-rate mis-applied (slow rate on long pass)

Description

The summed `EXECUTION_TIME` of the operations in a `WORKPLAN` exceeds 28800 seconds (8-hour shift); e.g., a single workplan declaring 50 hours of continuous machining. While not formally a STEP error, it indicates a unit-confusion or feedrate-stuck-at-zero condition upstream worth flagging. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 cycle time absurdly long", "STEP-NC workplan won't finish in a shift", "feedrate units wrong (cycle time 50h)".

Reproducer recipe

A `MACHINING_OPERATION` with `EXECUTION_TIME` of 180000.0 seconds (50 hours) attached.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: emit a warning diagnostic when any individual operation exceeds shift length, treating this as a signal of upstream feedrate or unit error, and continue.

Notes

Synonyms: "STEP-NC cycle time too long", "AP238 unrealistic execution time", "workplan exceeds shift length", "machining time impossibly long". Synonyms: "STEP-NC cycle time too long", "AP238 unrealistic execution time".
