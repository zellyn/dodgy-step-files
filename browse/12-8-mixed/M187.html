M187 — AP238 knurling_operation pitch finer than tool resolution

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 deep machining)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M187.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-238 §5.8 knurling_operation tool-resolution invariant
  • bug-reporter language: "AP238 knurl pitch too fine", "STEP-NC knurling under tool resolution", "knurl spec finer than tooth spacing".

Description

A `KNURLING_OPERATION` declares a knurl pitch of 0.05 mm while the knurling tool's tooth spacing (resolution) is 0.5 mm. The tool physically cannot impress a finer pattern than its own teeth - the declared pitch is unattainable. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 knurl finer than tool", "STEP-NC knurling pitch impossible", "knurl spec below tool resolution".

Reproducer recipe

a `KNURLING_TOOL` with tooth spacing 0.5 mm; a `KNURLING_OPERATION` with knurl_pitch 0.05 mm referencing that tool.

Expected kernel behavior

validate knurl pitch against tool tooth spacing; reject as unattainable.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP238 knurl pitch < tool teeth", "STEP-NC knurling resolution exceeds tool", "knurl spec finer than tool", "knurling tool can't make finer pitch".
