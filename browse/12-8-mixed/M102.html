M102 — AP238 drilling sequence with coincident hole positions

Sources

Sender

deduced — CAM publisher producing drill-table from CAD with duplicate features

Description

A workplan emits two `DRILLING_OPERATION`s targeting features whose locator placements are coincident (within tolerance). The second drill attempts to enter an already-fully-drilled hole and breaks the tool from a zero-load entry, or wastes cycle time. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 two drills at same point", "STEP-NC duplicate hole position", "drilling same hole twice".

Reproducer recipe

Two `DRILLING_OPERATION`s whose feature locator `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D`s have identical origins `(50,30,0)`.

Expected kernel behavior

detect coincident hole positions; warn or collapse duplicate operations.

Notes

Synonyms: "duplicate hole drill", "coincident drilling targets", "AP238 two drill ops at same point", "STEP-NC redundant drill", "drill-on-drill collision".

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
