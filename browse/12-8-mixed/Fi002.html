Fi002 — Fillet contour walks into a high-valence vertex (> 3 incident edges)

§12.7 feature defects (sub-class: fillet walk failure)

P1 — silent data loss

Description

A fillet contour reaches a vertex with four or more incident edges. The kernel cannot decide which adjacent edge to walk onto next. Bug-reporter language: "fillet walking failure", "fillet hits a vertex it can't continue past", "high-valence vertex breaks fillet".

Reproducer recipe

A solid where the user-selected fillet edge ends at a vertex incident to four edges (e.g., a corner of a stair-step where three edges and the original spine meet).

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with diagnostic: stop the fillet at that vertex with diagnostic; or accept user input to disambiguate the next edge.

Notes

Synonyms: "fillet stops short", "ambiguous fillet next-edge", "fillet walking failure", "fillet hits a vertex it can't continue past", "high-valence vertex breaks fillet".
