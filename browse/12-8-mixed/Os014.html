Description

A pipe-shell sweep with `KeepContact` mode: the section is required to maintain contact with an auxiliary spine, but the auxiliary spine is too short or diverges from the main spine such that contact cannot be maintained throughout. Bug-reporter language: "pipe contact impossible", "auxiliary spine too short".

Reproducer recipe

Main spine length 10. Auxiliary spine length 5, parallel-offset by 2. KeepContact mode requested.

Expected kernel behavior

Truncate sweep to the contact-feasible domain; or reject.

Notes

Synonyms: "sweep auxiliary failure", "pipe contact impossible", "auxiliary spine too short", "KeepContact fails along sweep".
