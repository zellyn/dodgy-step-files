M140 — AP203 make-or-buy declaration contradicts external supplier record

§12.8 (sub-class: AP203 make-or-buy / supplier consistency)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-203 §5.4 (specified_source / supplier)
  • bug-reporter language: "AP203 made part has supplier", "make-or-buy contradiction", "PDM source attribute wrong".

Sender

deduced — PDM exporter that emitted both an in-house source flag and a third-party supplier record without reconciling

Description

A `PRODUCT_DEFINITION_FORMATION_WITH_SPECIFIED_SOURCE` declares `source = .MADE.` (made in-house) but an `APPLIED_ORGANIZATION_ASSIGNMENT` of role 'supplier' designates an external organization for the same part. The make-or-buy declaration contradicts the supplier record. Bug-reporter language: "AP203 make-or- buy contradiction", "in-house part has supplier", "PDM source vs supplier mismatch".

Reproducer recipe

`PRODUCT_DEFINITION_FORMATION_WITH_SPECIFIED_SOURCE ('rev','..',product,.MADE.)` and `APPLIED_ORGANIZATION_ASSIGNMENT(org,'supplier',(pd))`.

Expected kernel behavior

detect the contradiction; reject or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP203 source/supplier mismatch", "PDM make-or-buy ambiguity", "MADE part has external supplier", "make-or-buy contradicts supplier record". Synonyms: "AP203 source/supplier mismatch", "PDM make-or-buy ambiguity".

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
