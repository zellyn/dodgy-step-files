M100 — AP238 spindle-on issued after first tool-workpiece contact

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP238 process ordering)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M100.stp

Sources

  • deduced from ISO 10303-238 §6.4 spindle / coolant / approach ordering

Sender

deduced — STEP-NC publisher concatenating canned cycles in wrong order

Description

A workplan's `MACHINING_OPERATION` chain begins with a `CUTTING_OPERATION` (tool descends into workpiece) and only afterwards issues `SPINDLE_SPEED` / `START_SPINDLE`. The first contact occurs with the spindle stopped, breaking the tool or marring the workpiece surface. Bug-reporter language: "AP238 spindle starts after cut begins", "STEP-NC contact without spindle", "machining without spindle on".

Reproducer recipe

A `WORKPLAN` listing a `DRILLING_OPERATION` *before* any explicit spindle-control operation.

Expected kernel behavior

validate that spindle is commanded on before first cutting contact; warn or reject.

Notes

Synonyms: "spindle off during cut", "AP238 ordering hazard", "missing spindle-on prefix".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'AP238_STEP_NC_MIM')
  • contains(b'DRILLING_OPERATION')
  • contains(b'WORKPLAN')
