M155 — AP210 net connectivity ends at a non-pin element

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP210 net endpoint type)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-8-mixed/M155.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-210 §7 (network_node referenced endpoint type)
  • bug-reporter language: "AP210 net ends at point", "PCB trace ends in nothing", "ECAD net dangling at non-pin".

Sender

deduced — ECAD exporter that wrote net endpoints from a routing-graph node (point), missing the conversion to a TERMINAL

Description

A `NETWORK_NODE` references an element that is not a `TERMINAL` - it points to a `CARTESIAN_POINT` instead. The trace ends in the middle of nothing - there is no electrical pin at the endpoint. Bug-reporter language: "AP210 net to non-pin", "PCB trace ends at point", "ECAD connectivity to non-terminal".

Reproducer recipe

`NETWORK_NODE('node_floating', cartesian_point)` in a `NETWORK` list.

Expected kernel behavior

validate `NETWORK_NODE` references point at `TERMINAL` (or compatible electrical endpoint); reject or warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP210 endpoint not a pin", "PCB trace dangling", "ECAD net to wrong type".
