M120 — AP209 symmetry plane not perpendicular to a coordinate axis

§12.8 mixed (sub-class: AP209 symmetry boundary condition)

Sender

deduced — FEA preprocessor permitting arbitrary symmetry plane orientations without rotating the constraint frame

Description

A symmetry boundary condition restricts displacement perpendicular to a plane and rotation about its in-plane axes. Most solvers assume the plane is perpendicular to one of the X/Y/Z axes (so the constraint reduces to "U_x = 0, R_y = R_z = 0", etc.). A user-defined symmetry plane with normal `(0.5, 0.5, 0.707)` is non-axis-aligned; if the preprocessor encodes the constraint as "U_x = 0" without rotating to the plane frame, the actual physics is wrong. Bug-reporter language: "AP209 symmetry plane not aligned", "FEM symmetry BC at angle", "skew symmetry applied as axis-aligned".

Reproducer recipe

A `SINGLE_POINT_CONSTRAINT` interpreted as symmetry with a normal direction `(0.5, 0.5, 0.707)`, paired with a constraint pattern restricting only `U_x` (X-DOF), without rotating the constraint basis to the symmetry frame.

Expected kernel behavior

reject if the symmetry plane normal is non-axis-aligned; or transform the constraint into the plane frame.

Notes

Synonyms: "AP209 skew symmetry", "non-axis symmetry plane", "symmetry plane not aligned with coordinate axis", "FEM symmetry boundary off-axis", "symmetry constraint can't reduce to scalar BCs".
