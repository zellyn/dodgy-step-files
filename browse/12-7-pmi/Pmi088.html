Pmi088 — `GEOMETRIC_VALIDATION_PROPERTY` claimed surface area disagrees with geometry

§12.7 (sub-class: validation property)

P1 — silent data loss

pmi

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-7-pmi/Pmi088.stp

Sources

  • 02-caxif (Validation Properties Recommended Practice §6.3 — area validation)
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP validation area wrong"

Sender

deduced — exporters where the surface-area cache becomes stale after Boolean trimming or face merging

Description

A `GEOMETRIC_VALIDATION_PROPERTY` of type surface-area is claimed for a body; the actually-computed surface area of the body disagrees. Common cause: the producer's exporter computes area before a final face-merge / sliver-removal pass. A receiver that recomputes detects the drift; one that trusts the producer-emitted value carries forward a value the geometry no longer supports. Bug-reporter language: "STEP surface area wrong", "validation area disagrees".

Reproducer recipe

a `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` representing a 10x10x10 mm cube (surface area 600 mm²); a `GEOMETRIC_VALIDATION_PROPERTY` with `AREA_MEASURE(540.0)` claiming an area lower by 10%.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: compute the surface area at load time, compare against the validation-property value, and emit a warning diagnostic for the disagreement.

Notes

**See also**: Pmi087, Pmi089. Synonyms: "claimed surface area disagrees with geometry", "validation surface area mismatch", "GVP surface area incorrect".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(27) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • n_faces_total == 6
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[0].edge_count == 4
  • face[0].area > 99
  • face[0].area < 101
  • face[5].edge_count == 4
  • face[5].area > 99
  • face[5].area < 101
  • n_edges_total >= 24
