Pmi074 — Hole feature whose `depth` exceeds host-part bounding-box thickness

§12.7 (sub-class: hole feature)

P1 — silent data loss

pmi

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-7-pmi/Pmi074.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec AP242 §ROUND_HOLE

Description

AP242 ROUND_HOLE carries a `depth` or `through_depth` attribute. A producer that copies a hole feature from a thicker design without updating the depth emits a hole deeper than the material thickness; geometrically the hole exits the back face but is encoded as a blind hole. Receivers that simulate hole removal compute either a non-watertight result or an error.

Reproducer recipe

SHAPE_ASPECT `'round_hole'` with DIMENSIONAL_SIZE(`'distance'`) of 50.0mm in a host part 5mm thick.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: validate hole depth against host-part topology when the host is loaded; warn if the depth exceeds the local thickness; in tolerant mode, heal and accept by coercing the feature to a through-hole.

Notes

**See also**: Pmi055. Synonyms: "hole depth exceeds part thickness", "blind hole drilled deeper than part", "hole feature depth bigger than bounding box".
