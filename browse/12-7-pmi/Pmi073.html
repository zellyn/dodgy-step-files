Pmi073 — Compound feature with self-referential pattern membership

§12.7 (sub-class: compound feature)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation pmi

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-7-pmi/Pmi073.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec AP242 ED4 §COMPOUND_FEATURE

Description

AP242 Edition 4 introduces COMPOUND_FEATURE as a grouping of feature definitions (e.g. a "hole pattern" of N counterbore holes treated as one PMI target). The spec does not explicitly forbid a compound feature including itself as one of its components; a programmatic exporter that builds compound features by recursive descent and forgets the recursion guard emits a cycle.

Reproducer recipe

SHAPE_ASPECT `'compound_pattern'` linked via SHAPE_ASPECT_RELATIONSHIP `'part_of'` to itself, alongside SHAPE_ASPECT_RELATIONSHIP linking two member features `'hole1'` and `'hole2'` to the same pattern.

Expected kernel behavior

detect cycles in the part-of graph and reject; or treat the self-loop as a no-op (the pattern is "part of" itself trivially) with a warning.

Notes

**See also**: Pmi070. Synonyms: "compound feature with self-referential pattern membership", "hole pattern includes itself", "self-loop in pattern membership".
