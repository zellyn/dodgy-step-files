Pmi059 — Surface status (cosmetic / construction / blanked) preservation lost

§12.7 (sub-class: semantic-vs-graphic)

round-trip pmi

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-7-pmi/Pmi059.stp

Sources

  • 11-translator-vendors (W029 SDHE-21748)

Description

Faces tagged in source CAD with construction-status (cosmetic, construction, blanked) lose their status flags after STEP round-trip.

Reproducer recipe

Face with a `property_definition` of "status=construction" round-tripped.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: preserve as `property_definition`; ignore semantically if unknown but never drop.

Notes

**See also**: M048. Synonyms: "surface status (cosmetic / construction / blanked) lost on round-trip", "construction surface flag stripped", "cosmetic-vs-real surface distinction lost".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(1) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • brepcheck.valid == True
