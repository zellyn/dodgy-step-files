Pmi071 — `PROJECTED_ZONE_DEFINITION` with zero offset

§12.7 (sub-class: projected zone)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation pmi

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-7-pmi/Pmi071.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec AP242 §PROJECTED_ZONE_DEFINITION

Description

PROJECTED_ZONE_DEFINITION on a tolerance frame carries projection_length (positive offset above the surface). Edition-2 / AP242 ED2 examples use values like 12mm. A zero offset is geometrically degenerate (the projected zone collapses onto the original feature, identical to having no projected zone at all).

Reproducer recipe

PROJECTED_ZONE_DEFINITION with `LENGTH_MEASURE(0.0)`.

Expected kernel behavior

reject zero-length projected zones; treat as "no projection" with a warning is acceptable as a tolerant policy.

Notes

**See also**: Pmi030, Pmi031. Synonyms: "PROJECTED_ZONE_DEFINITION with zero offset", "projected zone offset is zero", "zero-length projected zone".
