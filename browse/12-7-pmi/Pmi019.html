Pmi019 — camera_model_d3 viewpoint not modeled per RP §9

§12.7 (sub-class: saved-view)

P0 — receiver crash / hang / OOM

crash pmi

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-7-pmi/Pmi019.stp

Sources

  • 06-nist-sfa (S064)

Description

`camera_model_d3` lacks the orthonormal/orientation conventions expected by RP §9 (e.g., view direction not orthogonal to up direction).

Reproducer recipe

`camera_model_d3` with view-up parallel to view-direction.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: emit a warning diagnostic; provide an option to repair viewpoints via Gram-Schmidt orthonormalization.

Notes

Synonyms: "camera_model_d3 viewpoint not modeled per RP §9", "saved-view camera missing per recommended practice", "camera viewpoint not encoded as required".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=signal(11) ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'VIEW_VOLUME')
