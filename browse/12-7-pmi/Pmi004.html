Pmi004 — Non-coplanar leader collapsed onto annotation plane loses attachment

§12.7 (sub-class: semantic-vs-graphic / annotation-plane)

P1 — silent data loss

pmi

Sources

  • 02-caxif (PMI v4.1 §8 intro, §8.3.1
  • CAx-IF Round 56J JPMI scope)

Sender

NX, JAMA/JAPIA-style models

Description

NX permits an annotation whose leader is not in the same plane as the text. Polyline and Tessellated graphic presentation in STEP both require all geometry of an `annotation_occurrence` to be coplanar. Writers project the leader onto the annotation plane, but the projection no longer touches the geometry it was attached to. Required handling is to split into multiple `annotation_occurrence`s (one per coplanar subset) under one `draughting_callout`, with the first subset as principal.

Reproducer recipe

NX-style annotation: text in plane Z=10; leader endpoints (0,0,5) and (0,0,0). Export as either Polyline or Tessellated graphic presentation.

Expected kernel behavior

Split such annotations into coplanar subsets gathered under one `draughting_callout`; preserve attachment by referencing original geometry from the leader subset.

Notes

Synonyms: "non-coplanar leader collapsed onto annotation plane", "leader attachment point lost when projected to plane", "PMI leader pulled out of 3D into 2D", "leader dropped when not in annotation plane".

Expected validation
