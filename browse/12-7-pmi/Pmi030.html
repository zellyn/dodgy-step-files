Pmi030 — Negative projected tolerance zone length

§12.7 (sub-class: tolerance-zone)

silent-loss pmi

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-7-pmi/Pmi030.stp

Sources

  • 06-nist-sfa (S046)

Description

`projected_zone_definition.magnitude` is negative.

Reproducer recipe

`projected_zone_definition` with magnitude `-5.0`.

Expected kernel behavior

Take absolute value and warn, or reject; but never silently use the negative value.

Notes

**OCC behavior**: silently accepts the negative-magnitude `projected_zone_definition` with no diagnostic; kernel mishandling; the catalog above forbids silent use of the negative value. Synonyms: "negative projected tolerance zone length", "projected zone length below zero", "tolerance projection length encoded negative".
