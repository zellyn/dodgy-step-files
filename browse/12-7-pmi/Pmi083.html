Pmi083 — Annotation plane orientation flipped on GD&T import

§12.7 PMI/GD&T

pmi

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-7-pmi/Pmi083.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0028449 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A GD&T annotation plane on a face has its normal vector pointing into the part rather than out. Reader copies the plane normal from the underlying surface but does not consult the face orientation, so flipped faces produce flipped annotation planes; text appears facing away from viewer.

Reproducer recipe

Face with reversed orientation (`.F.`); GD&T annotation plane sharing the face's surface. Reader produces annotation with normal opposite the face's outward direction.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: coerce the annotation plane normal to follow face orientation, not just the underlying surface; consult `ADVANCED_FACE` same-sense flag.

Notes

**See also**: Tsh001. Synonyms: "annotation plane orientation flipped on GD&T import", "GD&T text faces wrong way on read", "annotation reads backward", "PMI plane normal points into part".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(1) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
