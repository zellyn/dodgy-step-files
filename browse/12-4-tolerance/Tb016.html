Tb016 — Tolerance budget exhausted by accumulation across long edge chain

§12.4 (sub-class: cumulative tolerance)

P1 — silent data loss

Description

A wire has 1000 consecutive edges; each pair of adjacent vertices is 1e-9 mm apart in 3D against a declared tolerance of 1e-6. Each individual gap is sub-tolerance and absorbed. But the cumulative drift end-to-end is `1000 * 1e-9 = 1e-6`, exactly equal to the declared tolerance. A kernel comparing endpoint-to-endpoint declares the wire closed (or just barely not). Whether the wire is closed depends on whether the kernel sums residuals or compares only adjacent pairs.

Reproducer recipe

Long polyline with 1000 short edges of length ~1e-3 mm each; consecutive vertices have a constant 1e-9 mm gap that always points in the same direction; closing edge declared but its 3D endpoints are at the cumulative-drifted positions.

Expected kernel behavior

tolerance budget across N-edge chains must be documented; either reject as un-closeable, or apply a global re-stitch that distributes residual back across the chain.

Notes
