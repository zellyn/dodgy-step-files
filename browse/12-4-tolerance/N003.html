N003 — Same-domain face merge inflates shared-vertex tolerance to worst input

§12.4 (sub-class: tolerance ballooning by healing)

P1 — silent data loss

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-4-tolerance/N003.stp

Sources

  • 03-occt-tests R030 (OCC26642, OCC26656)

Description

When two adjacent faces with the same supporting surface are merged into one (their shared edge is dissolved), the resulting shared vertex inherits a tolerance equal to the worst-case vertex among the merged inputs; e.g. `1e-1` even though one input vertex was `2e-7`. Downstream algorithms then treat points as fuzzy-equal where they were previously precise.

Reproducer recipe

Two coplanar faces with vertex tolerance 2e-7 and one vertex with tolerance 1e-1 (from prior healing). Apply same-domain face merging.

Expected kernel behavior

recompute tolerance from actual post-merge geometric deviation rather than aggregating the worst input. A merge that takes a previously-precise vertex and balloons its tolerance is a fidelity loss.

Notes

**See also**: A019, N040. Synonyms: "merge adopts worst input vertex tolerance", "shared vertex tolerance jumps after merge", "merged vertex inherits 1e-1 from worst neighbor", "face merge bloats fuzzy compare on shared vertex".
