Tb017 — Two coincident faces at distance equal to declared tolerance

§12.4 (sub-class: face-coincidence threshold)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-4-tolerance/Tb017.stp

Sources

  • synthesized time-bomb.

Description

Two `ADVANCED_FACE`s (planar, parallel, same outline) are separated by exactly 1.0e-6 mm; equal to the declared tolerance. A kernel comparing strict `<` keeps them as separate faces (a thin slab). A kernel comparing `<=` merges them and the slab disappears, leaving a single face. Both interpretations are mathematically reasonable but produce different geometry.

Reproducer recipe

Two coplanar `ADVANCED_FACE`s on parallel `PLANE`s offset by 1.0e-6 mm; declared uncertainty 1.0e-6 mm. Wrap in PRODUCT chain.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: normalize the equality-vs-strict-inequality choice at the tolerance boundary; tie-breaking is documented.

Notes

Searchable as "duplicate coincident faces", "thin slab. Synonyms: "faces at exact tolerance distance", "is this coincident or not at exactly tol", "boundary case for face dedup", "parallel faces separated by exactly the tolerance". disappeared after import".
