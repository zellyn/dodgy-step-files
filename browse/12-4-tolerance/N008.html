N008 — Vertex tolerance inflation by repeated self-intersection healing cascades

§12.4 (sub-class: vertex tolerance bumps / healing cascade)

Description

A self-intersection-resolution pass modifies a pcurve and bumps the affected vertex's tolerance to encompass both the original and the new position. Successive rounds of this pass grow the vertex tolerance ball geometrically (e.g. 1e-7 → 1e-3 in a few iterations), eventually masking real gaps in modelled threads or fillets. The user-visible symptom is a "healed" model whose vertex tolerance balls have swallowed adjacent feature geometry.

Reproducer recipe

Face with pcurve self-intersecting within the kernel's confusion tolerance. Apply self-intersection repair repeatedly. Track vertex tolerance per pass.

Expected kernel behavior

cap tolerance inflation, warn when a single healing pass inflates a vertex tolerance beyond a stated multiple of the file-level uncertainty measure, or refuse to heal further once tolerance balls overlap adjacent features. Carrying a "healed" provenance flag on inflated tolerances helps downstream consumers reject silent loss of fidelity.

Notes

Synonyms: "vertex tolerance grows with each healing pass", "tolerance balloons over time", "tolerance ball swallows neighbor feature after multiple heals", "self-intersection healing keeps inflating tolerance", "vertex range increases monotonically across healers".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(32) ifc=schema_n/a

  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[5].surface_type == "plane"
