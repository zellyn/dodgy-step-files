N008 — Inner-hole wire has two near-parallel B-spline edges sharing only their common endpoint (no crossing); five inert GLOBAL uncertainty entries labelled as a healing cascade

Description

A closed-shell cube whose top face has an inner-loop hole bounded by two B-spline edges (`curveA`, `curveB`) that both end at a shared vertex and stay within ~2.5e-4mm of each other along their length, but do not cross — a genuine near-tangent, non-intersecting pair. The file separately carries five `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` entities (values `1.0E-7` through `1.0E-3`) inside one `GLOBAL_UNCERTAINTY_ASSIGNED_CONTEXT`, with labels suggesting successive healing passes; these are static file-level metadata, not evidence of any actual tolerance-inflation process (no self-intersection exists for a healer to act on, and OCCT reads all 54 vertices at the default `1e-7` tolerance, aside from one unrelated `10.0001` outlier).

Reproducer recipe

Face with pcurve self-intersecting within the kernel's confusion tolerance. Apply self-intersection repair repeatedly. Track vertex tolerance per pass.

Expected kernel behavior

cap tolerance inflation, warn when a single healing pass inflates a vertex tolerance beyond a stated multiple of the file-level uncertainty measure, or refuse to heal further once tolerance balls overlap adjacent features. Carrying a "healed" provenance flag on inflated tolerances helps downstream consumers reject silent loss of fidelity.

Notes

Corrected 2026-08-07 (title-vs-bytes audit): retitled/redescribed — original claimed a self-intersection-triggered, multi-pass vertex-tolerance-inflation cascade; direct curve evaluation shows the two inner-loop edges never cross (they share only their common endpoint), and the five `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` entries are inert file-level metadata with suggestive labels, not an executed healing sequence — no such per-vertex cascade exists in STEP/AP214, which has no per-vertex tolerance entity. Synonyms: "near-tangent non-crossing B-spline pair sharing an endpoint", "five global uncertainty entries with pass-labelled names but no functional cascade", "tolerance-cascade labels are decorative, not computed".
