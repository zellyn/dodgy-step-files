N007 — Vertex tolerance bump factor hard-coded `1.000001 *` on disagreement

§12.4 (sub-class: vertex tolerance bumps)

P1 — silent data loss

Sender

Pro/E PRO8845

Description

When the imported edge's 3D curve and its endpoint vertices disagree beyond a precision threshold, the translator bumps the vertex tolerance by a constant multiplier (slightly above 1.0×) of the deviation, just enough to make the edge pass invariants. Repeated reads through the same path accumulate the multiplier and bloat tolerance without anchoring it against the file-level uncertainty.

Reproducer recipe

AP203 file where a `VERTEX_POINT.cartesian_point` lies 1e-3 from the curve evaluation at the edge's parametric endpoint, on a model with 1e-6 declared uncertainty.

Expected kernel behavior

cap vertex-tolerance bumps against a multiple of the model's `uncertainty_measure_with_unit`, warn when the bump exceeds that threshold, or reject as malformed.

Notes

**See also**: N025. Synonyms: "vertex tolerance bumped by tiny constant multiplier", "translator multiplies tolerance by 1.000001", "tolerance creeps up across repeated reads", "constant multiplier applied to vertex tolerance".
