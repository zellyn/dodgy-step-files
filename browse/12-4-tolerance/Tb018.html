Tb018 — UNCERTAINTY value of exactly zero — no fuzzy compare

Description

`UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT(LENGTH_MEASURE(0.0), ..)` declares zero tolerance. A receiver that uses the declared value verbatim performs exact-equality comparisons; any sub-bit drift is a "real" defect. A receiver that floors the working tolerance at `Precision::Confusion()` silently substitutes 1e-7 and behaves identically to a normal file. The zero declaration is a producer's request for exact arithmetic that may or may not be honoured.

Reproducer recipe

A simple shape (planar quad or single edge) in a PRODUCT-wrapped representation with `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT(LENGTH_MEASURE(0.0), ..)`.

Expected kernel behavior

zero uncertainty must be either honoured (exact arithmetic enabled if available) or rejected; silently substituting a non-zero working tolerance must be flagged in diagnostics.

Notes
