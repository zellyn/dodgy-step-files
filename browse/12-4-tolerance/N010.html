N010 — `EDGE_CURVE` shorter than vertex tolerance (tiny edge covered by vertices, `distance_accuracy` exceeds edge length)

Sender

IGES (also affects STEP)

Description

An `EDGE_CURVE` whose 3D length is less than the bounding vertex tolerances. Typical signature: vertices at (0,0,0) and (5e-5,0,0) — edge length 5e-5 mm — with the global `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` / `distance_accuracy` declared as 1e-4 mm, so vertex tolerance is roughly twice the entire edge length. The two vertex tolerance balls already touch each other; the edge geometry adds nothing and trips Boolean / mesh algorithms.

Reproducer recipe

Edge with vertices nearly coincident (gap < tol) and short non-degenerate 3D curve. `IsUClosed`/`IsVClosed` previously returned wrong without a midpoint distance check.

Expected kernel behavior

heal — detect by `len(curve) < tol(V1) + tol(V2)`; remove edge or merge endpoints; preserve adjacent edge wires.

Notes

**See also**: N011. Synonyms: "edge shorter than its endpoint tolerance", "edge length below vertex range", "vertex sphere covers entire edge", "tiny edge under tolerance disc", "edge geometry adds nothing because vertices already merge".
