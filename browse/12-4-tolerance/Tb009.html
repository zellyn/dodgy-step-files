Tb009 — Self-intersection visible at full precision but rounded out at receiver tolerance

§12.4 (sub-class: rounding-induced topology change)

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-4-tolerance/Tb009.stp

Sources

  • synthesized time-bomb.

Description

A wire's two consecutive edges cross at a separation of 3e-7 mm (well-defined and detectable by an exact-arithmetic kernel). A receiver that rounds coordinates to its working precision (1e-6) before intersection-testing sees the two edges as touching at a single point and reports the wire as simple. An exact kernel, or one with `tol < 3e-7`, reports a self-intersection. Two kernels disagree on whether the input is malformed.

Reproducer recipe

Two short B-spline edges crossing in 3D at separation 3e-7 mm; declared `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` is 1e-6 (looser than the crossing). Wrap in a face with the crossing wire as `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` and a PRODUCT chain.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: self-intersection detection normalizes to a documented threshold, not the working precision; if the threshold is the working precision, the kernel emits a warning diagnostic that "self-intersection within working precision" is the same input class as "no self-intersection".
