N031 — Surface discontinuities introduced by translation (G0/G1/G2 break at shared edges)

Description

G0/G1/G2 discontinuities introduced when neighbouring faces are re-parametrized into different surface types during translation. Tangent-plane jumps appear at shared edges that were smooth in the source. Translation re-fits surfaces independently; tangent agreement is not a constraint of the per-surface fit.

Reproducer recipe

Convert a Catia procedural blend to STEP — the blend often becomes several `RATIONAL_B_SPLINE_SURFACE` patches whose tangent planes at shared edges differ by more than the resolution tolerance.

Expected kernel behavior

accept; report as quality warning; archive-stable only if validation properties (volume, area) still match within tolerance.

Notes

**See also**: M026, M027. Synonyms: "tangent jump at shared edge after translation", "G1 broken across faces after import", "smooth surfaces become creased on round-trip", "translator re-fits surfaces and loses tangent continuity".
