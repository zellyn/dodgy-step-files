Tb004 — Vertex-edge-face hierarchy holds in mm but inverts when scaled to inches

Description

Variant of Tb003 with a non-decimal scale factor. Same hierarchy invariant in mm; the file is converted on import to inches (factor 25.4). Floating-point round-off during the multiplication leaves tolerances at unequal numbers of significant digits, and the strict hierarchy ordering may be violated by ULP-scale drift. A vertex tolerance was 1.000e-6 mm, an edge tolerance was 1.001e-6 mm; after `*25.4` they may end up `2.54e-5` and `2.54254e-5`, but with a kernel that round-trips through `float32` for some fields the equality flips.

Reproducer recipe

Three uncertainties at 1.000e-6, 1.001e-6, 1.002e-6 in mm-declared file with hierarchy invariant just barely holding; wrap in PRODUCT chain.

Expected kernel behavior

hierarchy invariant must be preserved across unit conversion; if precision is insufficient to preserve it, reject the input or widen the inflated tolerance to absorb the conversion drift, and emit a warning.

Notes

Synonyms: "tolerance hierarchy inverts under inch conversion", "non-decimal scale flips uncertainty ordering", "inch unit breaks vertex/edge tolerance ratio", "tolerance hierarchy fails after mm-to-inch conversion".
