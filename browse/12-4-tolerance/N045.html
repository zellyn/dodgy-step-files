N045 — Vertex tolerance under-checked: edge-internal intersection ignored

§12.4 tolerance

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-4-tolerance/N045.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0024922 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

Two edges intersect inside their parameter range but the intersection point is within vertex tolerance of one edge's endpoint. A wire-intersection check filters out the intersection because the vertex "owns" that point; leaving an undetected interior intersection that invalidates the wire downstream.

Reproducer recipe

Two edges meeting at a `T`-junction: one edge ends at vertex V; the other passes through V's tolerance ball but does not end at V. Edge-intersection check returns "no intersection" because the point is inside V's tolerance.

Expected kernel behavior

edge-intersection report "intersection inside vertex tolerance" as a distinct status; do not silently filter.

Notes

**See also**: Twi049, N039. **OCC behavior**: silently filters out the edge-internal intersection with no diagnostic; kernel mishandling; the catalog above forbids silent filtering. Synonyms: "edge-internal intersection ignored", "two edges cross inside parameter range but missed", "intersection check only at endpoints", "vertex tolerance check skips edge interior".
