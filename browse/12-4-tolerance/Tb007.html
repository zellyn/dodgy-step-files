Tb007 — NURBS knot-difference quantum below tolerance: knot collapses or doesn't

Description

A `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` has knot vector `(0.0, 1e-8, 1.0)` with multiplicities `(degree+1, 1, degree+1)`. A receiver that collapses near-equal knots within `Precision::PConfusion()` (~1e-9) preserves the inner knot. A receiver collapsing within `Precision::Confusion()` (~1e-7) merges it into the leading knot, effectively raising leading multiplicity by 1 and degenerating the curve start. The B-spline curve has different topology (knot count, control point relevance) under the two regimes.

Reproducer recipe

Cubic `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` with knots `(0.0, 1e-8, 1.0)`, multiplicities `(4, 1, 4)`, five control points; wrap in a `TRIMMED_CURVE` and a minimal PRODUCT-bound shape representation.

Expected kernel behavior

knot-equivalence threshold must be derived from a documented invariant; the curve evaluator must produce identical geometry under both interpretations or reject the input as ambiguous.
