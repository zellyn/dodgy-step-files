N006 — Pcurve drifts out of sync with 3D-curve parameterisation (geometric flavour)

§12.4 (sub-class: sameparameter)

P1 — silent data loss

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-4-tolerance/N006.stp

Sources

  • 13-quaoar B017, B018

Description

A pcurve was reprojected at non-uniform sampling without rebasing its parameter range, so parameter-by-parameter evaluation against the 3D curve drifts. Some receivers compensate by silently inflating edge tolerance to cover the residual deviation, which hides a real geometric gap; the affected edges show up as "hot" (unusually large tolerance) on a tolerance-summary check.

Reproducer recipe

project a 3D curve onto a host surface with non-uniform spacing; do not rebase parameter; run a topology checker.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: re-parameterise the pcurve so the same-parameter invariant holds, or reject loudly when the deviation exceeds budget. Silent edge-tolerance inflation discards information.

Notes

**See also**: N004, N005. Synonyms: "pcurve and 3D curve out of sync", "non-uniform pcurve sampling drift", "tolerance silently inflated to cover pcurve drift", "edge tolerance unusually hot after reprojection".

Expected validation
