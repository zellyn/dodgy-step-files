N048 — Walk-line initial step unrelated to tolerance during surface intersection

§12.4 tolerance

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-4-tolerance/N048.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0026554 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A surface-surface intersection algorithm walks along the intersection curve in fixed-size parameter steps, ignoring the surfaces' tolerances. On a coarse-tolerance shape (1e-3) the steps overshoot the tolerance, producing a coarse polyline that is then mistakenly accepted as the intersection curve.

Reproducer recipe

Two cylinders intersecting at a curve; cylinders have tolerance 1e-3. Algorithm walks at step 1e-7 (way over-sampled, slow) or step 1e-1 (under-sampled, wrong curve).

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: walking step adapts to surface curvature and tolerance; emit fewer / more samples to maintain tolerance-bounded deviation.

Notes

**See also**: N046. Synonyms: "surface intersection walks with fixed step", "walk step ignores tolerance", "intersection algorithm uses constant initial step", "walking step unrelated to working precision".
