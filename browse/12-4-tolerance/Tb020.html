Tb020 — UNCERTAINTY value of `1.0E+30` — astronomically loose

§12.4 (sub-class: out-of-range tolerance declaration)

Sources

  • synthesized time-bomb.

Description

Inverse of Tb019. `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT(LENGTH_MEASURE(1.0E+30), ..)` declares a tolerance larger than the model. A kernel respecting the declaration treats every pair of vertices as coincident, every face as identical, and collapses the model to nothing. A kernel ceiling-clamping to a sane maximum (e.g. min-feature-size / 100) silently behaves normally. Receiver behaviour diverges wildly.

Reproducer recipe

PRODUCT-wrapped simple shape with `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT(LENGTH_MEASURE(1.0E+30), ..)`.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept, or reject with E_TOLERANCE_OUT_OF_RANGE diagnostic: same as Tb019; out-of-range tolerance is flagged via warning, not silently clamped.

Notes

**See also**: Tb019. Synonyms: "uncertainty 1e30 absurdly loose", "tolerance bigger than the model", "everything fuzzy-equal under huge tolerance", "tolerance value impossibly large".
