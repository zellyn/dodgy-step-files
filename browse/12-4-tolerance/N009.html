N009 — Vertex 3D point lies far from incident-edge curve endpoints (dispersion exceeds tolerance)

§12.4 (sub-class: vertex tolerance bumps)

Description

A vertex's stored 3D point is far enough from the evaluated parametric endpoints of its incident edges that the would-be vertex-tolerance disc spans the entire local feature. The vertex says "I am at point P", but every edge ending at it claims the edge actually ends somewhere else.

Reproducer recipe

`EDGE_CURVE` with line `(0,0,0)→(1,0,0)`, vertex at `(0, 0.001, 0)`, declared tolerance `0.0001`.

Expected kernel behavior

snap the vertex onto the curve endpoints if within a snap tolerance, inflate vertex tolerance to encompass the dispersion (and warn), or reject as malformed.

Notes

Synonyms: "vertex sits far from edge endpoints", "edges end far from their stored vertex", "vertex tolerance disc spans whole feature", "incident edges disagree with vertex position".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'1.0E-4')
  • count_entity_def(b'VERTEX_POINT') == 2 and count_entity_def(b'EDGE_CURVE') == 1
