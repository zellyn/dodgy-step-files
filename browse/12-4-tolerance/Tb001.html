Tb001 — Sub-tolerance vertex gap that closes at 1e-3 but yawns open at 1e-7

§12.4 (sub-class: tolerance-boundary, vertex-merge regime)

silent-loss round-trip

Sources

  • synthesized time-bomb.

Description

Two `VERTEX_POINT`s referenced by an end-to-end edge pair are positioned 5e-5 mm apart in 3D. The file declares `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT(LENGTH_MEASURE(1.0E-3))`. A receiver honouring the declared tolerance treats the vertices as coincident and the wire as closed. A receiver hardcoding `Precision::Confusion()` (~1e-7) treats the vertices as distinct and the wire as a free-edge defect (also called "naked edge" or "open boundary"). The file is simultaneously valid and invalid depending on tolerance interpretation; a classic round-trip time bomb where re-saving under stricter precision turns a clean import into a leaky shell.

Reproducer recipe

Two `EDGE_CURVE`s sharing an endpoint logically but with separate `VERTEX_POINT`s 5e-5 mm apart; declared `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` is 1e-3. Wrap in a minimal PRODUCT chain so the receiver sees a topology to walk.

Expected kernel behavior

produce a consistent result regardless of tolerance setting within the documented operating range; either honour the declared uncertainty and treat the vertices as coincident, or reject the file with a diagnostic naming the gap distance vs. working precision. The result must not silently flip between "closed shell" and "open shell" on tolerance reconfiguration.
