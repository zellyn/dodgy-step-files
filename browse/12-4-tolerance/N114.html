Expected kernel behavior

reject-with-diagnostic: the edge's B-spline curve genuinely has one control point at (1.0E+308, 0.0, 0.0) -- effectively double-precision's overflow boundary -- while its two endpoint vertices sit at (0,0,0) and (1,0,0), 1mm apart, against a declared 1.0E-7mm tolerance.
