Tb002 — Sub-tolerance vertex gap that closes at 1e-7 but inflates at 1e-3

Description

Inverse of Tb001. Vertices are 5e-5 mm apart but the file declares `1e-7` uncertainty. A 1e-7 receiver treats them as separate (which the input is honestly representing). A receiver that loosens tolerance to 1e-3 — for example a downstream Boolean engine that uses `tol*1000` for fuzzy operations — folds them together and silently changes the topology (shared edge becomes single edge, two faces become one). The "fix" applied by the lax tolerance receiver actively corrupts the input.

Reproducer recipe

Two `VERTEX_POINT`s at (0,0,0) and (5e-5,0,0) on separate `EDGE_CURVE`s; `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` is 1e-7. Wrap in PRODUCT chain.

Expected kernel behavior

produce a consistent result regardless of tolerance setting; if the kernel relaxes tolerance for any operation, it must flag the topology change and not silently merge entities the input declared distinct.

Synonyms: "gap closed at fine tolerance grows at coarse tolerance", "coarse tolerance creates gap that fine doesn't see", "1e-3 reader sees gap that 1e-7 doesn't", "tolerance setting changes connectivity". separate features", "two edges became one after import".
