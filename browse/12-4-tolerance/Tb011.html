Tb011 — Periodic-surface seam matching requires period-relative tolerance

§12.4 (sub-class: periodic-surface × tolerance)

Description

A `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` of radius 1e6 mm has period 2π·R ≈ 6.28e6. A wire's pcurve U-coordinates differ by `2π + 1e-3` between consecutive edges (one period plus a small drift). A kernel using *fixed* parametric tolerance (e.g. 1e-7 rad) rejects the seam as misaligned. A kernel using `fmod(diff, 2π)` semantics absorbs the period and reports alignment within 1e-3. The same wire is closed or not depending on whose arithmetic policy.

Reproducer recipe

`CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` with R = 1e6 mm; a `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` whose two consecutive `EDGE_CURVE`s have pcurve endpoints differing by exactly `2π + 1e-3` rad in U. Wrap in PRODUCT chain.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: seam-matching policy is documented and consistent; on a periodic surface, normalize "same modulo period" as the canonical equality.
