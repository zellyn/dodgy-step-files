Tb012 — Sphere seam matching across pole singularity

Description

A `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` has a parametric singularity at the poles (V = ±π/2). A wire passing 1e-9 below V=π/2 is at the edge of the pole singularity. A kernel that snaps "near-pole" pcurves to the pole using a fixed angular tolerance (1e-7 rad) does not snap; one using a larger working tolerance (1e-3 rad) snaps the wire onto the pole and collapses the upper face. Different kernels produce a hemisphere vs. a spherical zone.

Reproducer recipe

`SPHERICAL_SURFACE` with R=1; face with outer-bound wire at constant V = π/2 - 1e-9 (a near-pole circle).

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: pole-snapping is a documented invariant; the snap threshold normalizes into diagnostics, not hidden behind working precision.

Notes

Synonyms: "sphere pole singularity breaks seam match", "v-parameter singular at pole", "pole singularity fails parametric tolerance", "sphere seam at pole has multi-valued UV". Provenance tier: bytes-only (Q5 full-corpus 2026-07-01).
