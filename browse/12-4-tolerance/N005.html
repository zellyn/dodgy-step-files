N005 — Splitting a closed periodic face leaves new edges with reversed pcurve vs 3D curve parameterisation

§12.4 (sub-class: sameparameter)

P1 — silent data loss

topology

Sources

  • 13-quaoar B008

Description

Splitting a closed periodic shape (full `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE`, `TOROIDAL_SURFACE`) along its seam produces new edges whose 3D-curve and pcurve parameterisations no longer match — they were inherited from the unsplit edge but split independently. Common signature: a seam `EDGE_CURVE` on a cylinder with a 3D `LINE` from z=0 to z=10 (sense `.T.`) but its pcurve running from v=10 down to v=0 along −V (the `EDGE_CURVE` sense or pcurve direction inverted, e.g. `.F.`) — pcurve and 3D curve have opposite parameterisations across the seam. A topology checker reports invalid edges until a follow-up same-parameter recomputation runs.

Reproducer recipe

Full cylinder solid → split along seam → run topology check; observe invalid edges. Recompute same-parameter; check passes.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: a splitter that produces new edges must restore the same-parameter invariant on those edges before returning, or reject the result. Returning a topologically-invalid intermediate result puts the burden on every caller.

Notes

**See also**: N006. Synonyms: "pcurve direction opposite 3D curve after seam split", "split periodic face has reversed pcurve", "edge sense flipped vs pcurve sense across seam", "topology checker reports inconsistent parameterisation after split".
