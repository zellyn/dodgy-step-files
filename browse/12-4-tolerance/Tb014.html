Tb014 — Sliver triangle whose smallest internal angle is at the hardcoded trigger

§12.4 (sub-class: sliver-detection threshold)

spec-violation

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-4-tolerance/Tb014.stp

Sources

  • synthesized time-bomb.

Description

A planar triangular face has internal angles of 89.9999°, 89.9999°, and 0.0002°. The 0.0002° angle equals 3.49e-6 rad. Many sliver detectors trigger at "internal angle < 1e-5 rad" (Pro/E vintage policy); some at "1e-7 rad" (precise sliver only); some at "1e-3 rad" (aggressive sliver removal). The triangle is alive in one regime and dead in another.

Reproducer recipe

Triangle with vertices at `(0,0,0)`, `(1,0,0)`, `(1, 3.49e-6, 0)` — angle at vertex 1 is 3.49e-6 rad. Wrap in PRODUCT chain.

Expected kernel behavior

sliver threshold must be a documented invariant; consistency across kernel versions and configuration; never silently drop a face.

Notes

Searchable as "thin sliver triangle", "face removed by ShapeFix",. Synonyms: "sliver triangle exactly at angle threshold", "internal angle 0.0001 degrees at trigger", "sliver detector boundary case", "near-degenerate triangle at hardcoded angle limit". "long skinny face missing".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(7) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].area < 1e-3
  • n_edges_total >= 3
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • n_vertices_total >= 6
