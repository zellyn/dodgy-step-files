Ls024 — Tail garbage after end marker

§12.1c structural / framing

P1 — silent data loss

silent-loss syntax

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-1c-syntax/Ls024.stp

Sources

  • A025, N002 (notes)

Description

Material appearing after `END-ISO-10303-21;` — appended log lines, multiple concatenated files, or stray `DATA;ENDSEC;` blocks. Greedy regex parsers may match across boundaries.

Reproducer recipe

`END-ISO-10303-21;\n<garbage bytes>` or `..ENDSEC;DATA;ENDSEC;DATA;..END-ISO-10303-21;`

Expected kernel behavior

Stop reading at the first end marker; treat trailing bytes as warning and discard. Reject second `DATA` at top level.

Notes

Cross-oracle: pure-Python Part-21 validator rejects (reject(E_NO_CLOSE)); OCCT silently accepts (load is `empty`). OCC auto-heals a spec-level violation. Synonyms: "garbage after STEP end marker", "log lines after END-ISO-10303-21", "concatenated STEP files", "data after STEP terminator", "stray DATA after end marker".

Expected validation
