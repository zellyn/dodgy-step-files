Ls003 — Lower-case `e` exponent or whitespace around exponent

§12.1c numeric-literal grammar

P1 — silent data loss

syntax

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-1c-syntax/Ls003.stp

Sources

  • T022, N018, stepcode #244 #291

Description

ISO 10303-21 specifies upper-case `E` for the REAL exponent; lower-case `e`, embedded whitespace (`1.5 E+3`), or `+` sign in the exponent (`1e+18`) are non-conforming. Locale-aware printers also produce these.

Reproducer recipe

`(1.5e+3,0.,0.)`, `(1.5 E+3,0.,0.)`, `1.E+18`.

Expected kernel behavior

Lex `[+-]?\d+(\.\d*)?([eE][+-]?\d+)?` permissively; emit warning for non-canonical subsets.

Notes

**See also**: Ls001. Synonyms: "lowercase e in REAL exponent", "1e+18 instead of 1E+18", "whitespace in REAL exponent", "STEP exponent has plus sign", "non-standard exponent in STEP".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • matches(rb'\d[Ee][+-]?\d')
