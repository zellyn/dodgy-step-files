Sender

Description

BRL-CAD step-g virtual-dispatches every subtype through `LoadONBrep`; the base implementation prints to stderr and returns false. Any subtype the importer does not specifically override (e.g. `SeamCurve`, `CompositeCurve`, `BoundedPCurve`, `CompositeCurveOnSurface`, `CurveBoundedSurface`, `RectangularCompositeSurface`, `SurfacePatch`, `CartesianTransformationOperator`, `Polyline`) falls through and gets dropped without caller-side knowledge that geometry was lost. The diagnostic prints to stderr only and never propagates as an error code; callers cannot distinguish full success from partial drop. Fixture demonstrates the pattern with `SEAM_CURVE` since cylinders almost always carry one.

Reproducer recipe

a `SEAM_CURVE` referenced from a cylinder host; step-g's `SeamCurve::LoadONBrep` stub returns false and the seam geometry is lost.

Expected kernel behavior

reject; every stubbed dispatch must escalate to a `STEP_LOAD_ERROR` that propagates to the top-level converter; the converter must exit non-zero when any subgraph reachable from a referenced `ADVANCED_BREP_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` returned false. Distinct from In013 (the kernel-actor "no actor" case): here the actor exists but is a stub.

**See also**: Gn038, Twi100, Twi101, Gs057, Gs058, U045, In013, Pf037. Synonyms: "step-g LoadONBrep not implemented umbrella", "virtual-dispatch fall-through silently drops geometry", "stderr-only diagnostic for missing subtype", "BRL-CAD step-g cannot escalate stub-misses to error".
