Ls020 — Trailing semicolon after `ENDSEC` missing

§12.1c structural / framing

syntax

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-1c-syntax/Ls020.stp

Sources

  • N049

Description

Each `ENDSEC` must be followed by a semicolon. Files sometimes carry `ENDSEC` followed directly by a newline and the next section keyword.

Reproducer recipe

`HEADER;\n..\nENDSEC\nDATA;`

Expected kernel behavior

Reject in strict mode; lenient may accept and warn.

Notes

Synonyms: "ENDSEC without trailing semicolon", "STEP ENDSEC missing semicolon", "section terminator no semicolon", "ENDSEC followed by newline only", "STEP ENDSEC newline before next keyword".

Expected validation

occt=reject/reject gmsh=reject ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • matches(rb'ENDSEC\s*\n\s*DATA;')
  • count(b'ENDSEC') >= 1

Tier-3 assertions

  • load != "ok"
