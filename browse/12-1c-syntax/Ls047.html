Ls047 — Complex / multiple-inheritance entity record: leaves separated by commas

§12.1c complex entity instances

syntax

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-1c-syntax/Ls047.stp

Sources

  • T025, Q046, G088, N032

Description

The Part-21 grammar requires the leaves of a complex entity record (`#1=( A() B() C() )`) to be separated by whitespace, not commas. A writer that emits commas (carrying habit from argument lists) produces a token that a strict reader must reject.

Reproducer recipe

`#10=( CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT('DEG',#9), NAMED_UNIT(*), PLANE_ANGLE_UNIT() );` — commas between leaves.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject comma-separated leaves in strict mode; lenient mode may accept-with-warning.

Notes

**See also**: Ls010, Ls048. Synonyms: "complex entity leaves separated by commas", "multiple-inheritance leaves with comma separators", "STEP complex record uses commas not whitespace", "comma in complex entity rejected", "complex entity grammar violation".
