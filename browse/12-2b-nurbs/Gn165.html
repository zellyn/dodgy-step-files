Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

heal: the surface's U-knots carry an extreme spacing ratio (about 44:1 between the widest and narrowest interior spans: 0.956 versus 0.022). A kernel performing same-parameter reconciliation (aligning a 3D curve's parameterization with its 2D pcurve) on a surface with knot spacing this uneven should switch to an arc-length-based reparameterization rather than a purely knot-proportional one, since a purely proportional approach amplifies the spacing imbalance into the reconciled result.
