Gn007 — Under-sampled `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` for long helical thread (Creo relative-accuracy tessellation)

Sender

PTC Creo with default relative accuracy

Description

Long thin parts (e.g. 2.5 m extrusion with sub-mm helical features) export with visibly warped helical `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` because Creo's relative accuracy tessellates surfaces too coarsely on export. Typical signature: a B-spline approximating a tall helix carries only ~16 control points across z=0.2500, the poles zig-zag in x sign and barely move radially; severe under-sampling, far fewer poles per turn than a faithful helix needs. Recommended workaround is absolute accuracy 1e-4 + disable surface simplification.

Reproducer recipe

Author a 2500 mm shaft with a 0.5 mm-pitch helical thread feature in Creo at default (relative) accuracy; export to STEP.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: writers should preserve authored B-spline degree/knot structure exactly; never resample/refit during export; consumers should warn-and-accept (report tessellation-induced distortion via `geometric_validation_property` mismatch).

Notes

Synonyms: "Creo helical thread under-sampled in STEP export", "B-spline approximation of helix has too few control points", "helix BSpline zigzags from coarse tessellation", "Creo relative-accuracy export warps long helix", "tall helical thread visibly distorted in STEP".
