Description

A single `ADVANCED_FACE` on an analytic `CONICAL_SURFACE` (apex-reference radius 2.0, semi-angle π/6, quarter-wedge boundary, slant length 4.0) is an elementary/canonical surface type. A consumer that only accepts the generic `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` (swept) representation — not analytic-elementary quadrics — cannot use this face without conversion. This is the direction Gn013 (mixed `PLANE`+B-spline mismatch) does not cover: Gn013 never puts an elementary quadric (cylinder/cone/sphere/torus) in the file at all.

Reproducer recipe

`CONICAL_SURFACE('name',#axis2placement3d,2.0,0.5235987755982988)` (radius 2.0, semi-angle π/6) as `ADVANCED_FACE.face_geometry`; boundary a 4-edge quarter-wedge loop (two `TRIMMED_CURVE`-over-`CIRCLE` isoparametric arcs at the two axial extremes — radius 2.0 at the base, radius 4.0 at the top — and two straight `LINE` isoparametric edges running along the cone's slant generatrix at u=0 and u=π/2).

Expected kernel behavior

A receiver restricted to swept (`SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION`) representation must recognize the elementary quadric, build the correct generatrix (a `Geom_Line` along the slant for a cone; a trimmed `Geom_Circle` for sphere/torus), and rewrap it as a `Geom_SurfaceOfRevolution` about the surface's own axis — reproducing the exact same point set (no approximation error, unlike the rational-to-polynomial case in Gn177) rather than leaving the elementary surface in place or rejecting the file.

Notes

Live-verified directly against the fixture's own bytes (the reader loads the face, then the elementary-to-revolution conversion healer is invoked directly on it — matching the established convention of driving the healer directly since it is not part of the default read): raw read is 1 face, a conical surface, face area `18.8495559215388` (exactly the wedge lateral-area formula `θ·(R1+R2)/2·L = (π/2)·(2+4)/2·4 = 6π`, confirming the exact quarter-wedge-frustum geometry); after `ConvertToRevolution`, the result is 1 face, `Geom_SurfaceOfRevolution` with `BasisCurve()` a `Geom_Line` along the cone's slant direction, axis `(0,0,1)` (matching the cone's own axis) and IDENTICAL face area `18.8495559215388` (bit-for-bit, since this is an exact analytic re-expression, not an approximation) — confirming genuine, lossless elementary-to-revolution conversion. Fills `seq-elementary-to-revolution`'s previously-"weak" coverage (only Gn013's unrelated PLANE/B-spline mismatch existed). Distinct from Gn179 (the inverse direction: a swept `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` recognized as elementary). Synonyms: "analytic cone requires surface of revolution form", "ShapeCustom_ConvertToRevolution elementary-to-swept conversion", "CONICAL_SURFACE to Geom_SurfaceOfRevolution", "consumer rejects elementary quadric wants generic sweep", "cone generatrix Geom_Line revolution rebuild".
