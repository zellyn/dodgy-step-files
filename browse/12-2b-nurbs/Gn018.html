Gn018 — Bezier surface and rational Bezier surface unimplemented (BRL-CAD step-g)

§12.2b NURBS/knots

silent-loss geometry

Sender

rare CATIA exports

Description

`BEZIER_SURFACE` and `RATIONAL_BEZIER_SURFACE` cause step-g to print `LoadONBrep() not implemented for <entity>` and produce no geometry, although they are algebraically degree-d NURBS patches with knot multiplicity = degree+1.

Reproducer recipe

STEP entity `#N=BEZIER_SURFACE(..)` or `#N=RATIONAL_BEZIER_SURFACE(..)` referenced from an `ADVANCED_FACE`.

Expected kernel behavior

convert Bezier→NURBS internally (algebraic identity); never silently drop face geometry due to unsupported but trivially-convertible surface form.

Notes

**OCC behavior**: silently drops the Bezier surface and produces empty geometry without a diagnostic; kernel mishandling; the catalog above forbids silent face-geometry loss. Synonyms: "BEZIER_SURFACE algebraically degree-d NURBS dropped", "rational Bezier patch with knot multiplicity equal to degree+1 unhandled", "Bezier basis surface needs internal NURBS conversion", "trivially-convertible Bezier surface form unhandled".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'B_SPLINE_SURFACE')
  • contains(b'BEZIER')

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == True
