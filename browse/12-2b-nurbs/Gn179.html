Description

A single `ADVANCED_FACE` on a `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` (quarter-wedge boundary) sweeps a straight `LINE` (offset radius 3.0, direction parallel to the revolution axis) about that same axis — a genuinely SWEPT authoring of a cylinder, not a B-spline that merely happens to resemble one. This is the missing subvariant the packet spec calls out: N030 (quarter cylinder stored as a B-spline) and Gn015 (rational B-spline that is exactly a surface of revolution) both demonstrate canonical-form recovery from B-SPLINE sources; neither encodes the surface via the actual generic-sweep `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION`+`LINE` entity pair that this class's mechanism (classifying the swept adaptor curve by its underlying analytic type) is specifically built to recognize.

Reproducer recipe

`AXIS1_PLACEMENT` at the origin along Z; `LINE` at `(3.0,0.0,0.0)` with direction `(0.0,0.0,1.0)` (parallel to the revolution axis, offset by the target cylinder radius); `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION('name', line, axis1_placement)` as `ADVANCED_FACE.face_geometry`; boundary a 4-edge quarter-wedge loop (two `TRIMMED_CURVE`-over-`CIRCLE` isoparametric arcs at the two axial extremes, both radius 3.0, and two straight `LINE` isoparametric edges at u=0 and u=π/2 parallel to the axis).

Expected kernel behavior

A receiver targeting canonical elementary-surface representation must recognize that the swept generatrix (a straight line parallel to the revolution axis) produces exactly a cylinder, and rebuild the face on a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` rather than leaving the generic `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` wrapper in place — an exact, lossless re-expression (same point set), not an approximation.

Notes

Live-verified directly against the fixture's own bytes (the reader loads the face, then the swept-to-elementary recognition healer is invoked directly on it — matching the established convention of driving the healer directly since it is not part of the default read): raw read is 1 face, a surface of revolution, face area `28.274333882308202` (exactly the quarter-cylinder lateral-area formula `θ·R·H = (π/2)·3·6 = 9π`, confirming the exact geometry); after `SweptToElementary`, the result is 1 face, `Geom_CylindricalSurface` with `Radius()==3.0` and IDENTICAL face area `28.274333882308202` (bit-for-bit — an exact analytic re-expression) — confirming genuine, lossless swept-to-elementary recognition from a truly swept (not B-spline) source. Fills `seq-swept-to-elementary`'s previously-missing "swept-source direction" subvariant; N030/Gn015 cover the B-spline-source direction of the same class. Inverse direction of Gn178 (elementary-to-revolution). Synonyms: "swept cylinder recognized as elementary surface", "swept-adaptor-curve type classification recovers elementary form", "SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION LINE generatrix parallel to axis", "generic sweep authored cylinder canonical-form recovery", "genuinely swept source not B-spline disguise".
