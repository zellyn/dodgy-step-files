Gn010 — Out-of-range / NaN NURBS control points

Description

NURBS control points have coordinates far outside the model's valid extent (huge values, NaN, geometrically impossible relative to the surface intent). Often caused by exporters botching the rational arithmetic or by integer-overflow on weight normalization.

Reproducer recipe

`B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` with one interior control point at `(1e100, 1e100, 1e100)` but otherwise sensible knots and degree-3 net.

Expected kernel behavior

reject the surface; refuse to materialize. At minimum, refuse to use it for projection or intersection. Validate magnitudes against bounding-box envelope.

Notes

**See also**: Gs036. **OCC behavior**: silently accepts (no diagnostic, empty result); outside catalog's allowed set ({reject}). Kernel-bug witnessed: receivers must reject this fixture per the catalog's stated invariant.
