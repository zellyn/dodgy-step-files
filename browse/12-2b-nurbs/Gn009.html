Gn009 — High-degree NURBS surface bloat (degree >5, redundant knots)

§12.2b NURBS/knots

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2b-nurbs/Gn009.stp

Sources

  • 11-translator-vendors.md (W016 — CADfix tutorial "Reducing High Degree NURBS Surfaces")

Description

A `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` of degree >5 in either direction with hundreds of control points where degree-3 would suffice. Often produced by aggressive degree-elevation in source kernels. Causes meshing slowdowns; main impact on FEA prep.

Reproducer recipe

Simple flat-ish patch exported as `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` of degree (9,9) with redundant interior knots and ~1000 control points.

Expected kernel behavior

Accept on import; offer heal-and-accept (re-fit / degree-reduction within tolerance) via `ShapeCustom_BSplineRestriction`.

Notes

Synonyms: "high-degree NURBS surface bloat", "B-spline degree above 5 with hundreds of poles", "redundant knots inflate NURBS surface", "degree-9 NURBS where degree-3 would suffice", "BSpline surface degree-elevation bloat slows mesher".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'B_SPLINE_SURFACE')

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == True
