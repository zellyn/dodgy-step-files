Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

heal: the surface is rational (declared weights alternate 1.0/0.5 row by row), which a shortcut that infers rationality purely from control-point layout (rather than reading the declared weight array) can miss, since the pole positions alone do not encode weight information. A kernel checking whether a surface is rational must read the actual weight array, not infer it from the grid layout or sampling pattern, or it can wrongly treat a genuinely rational surface as non-rational and skip weight-aware evaluation and closure logic that the surface actually needs.
