Description

ShapeUpgrade_ConvertSurfaceToBezierBasis u-and-v-knot-asymmetry

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

reject-with-diagnostic or heal: a surface with U-degree 3 and V-degree 5 is a legal asymmetric-degree surface. Bezier-basis conversion must compute each direction's patch count independently from that direction's own degree and knot vector; assuming both directions share one degree (a symmetric-degree shortcut) produces the wrong number of Bezier patches in whichever direction has the higher degree.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • load == "ok"
  • face[0].bspline.u_degree == 3
  • face[0].bspline.v_degree == 5
