Gn025 — Folded/non-injective BSpline surface (Jacobian flip)

§12.2b NURBS/knots

silent-loss geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2b-nurbs/Gn025.stp

Sources

  • 11-translator-vendors.md (W007 — CADfix "Fix folded surfaces")

Description

A `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` whose control net has interior rows that swap order, producing a fold where the Jacobian flips sign within the parameter range; surface is non-injective.

Reproducer recipe

NURBS surface whose two interior rows (e.g. row 2 and row 3 of a 5-row degree-3 net) swap relative position; reference from an `ADVANCED_FACE`.

Expected kernel behavior

heal by surface re-fit; reject with "non-injective surface parametrization" diagnostic; never silently use such a surface for projection.

Notes

**See also**: Gs010, Gs034. **OCC behavior**: silently accepts the folded surface and uses it for projection without a diagnostic; kernel mishandling; the catalog above forbids silent acceptance. Synonyms: "folded BSpline surface Jacobian flips sign", "non-injective NURBS surface parametrisation", "B-spline surface has interior rows transposed", "Jacobian sign change inside surface domain", "surface folds back on itself in parameter space".
