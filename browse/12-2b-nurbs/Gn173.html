Description

Degree-1 rational B-spline surface (2×2 poles) with poles clustered in [0, 0.001] region in Y and weights ranging 1:1e6. The interior poles have weight 1e6, creating a numerical singularity. ComputeTol and knot-ratio checks may fail to normalize, causing parametric solver convergence loss in reparametrization and C0-upgrade branches.

Expected kernel behavior

Notes
