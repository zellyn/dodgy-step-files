Gn036 — `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` V knot vector strictly descending

§12.2b NURBS/knots

Description

A `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` V knot vector violates the non-decreasing invariant by containing a strictly descending pair (e.g. `(0.0, 0.6, 0.4, 1.0)`; 0.6 then 0.4). A compliant reader must reject the surface with a diagnostic naming the offending index.

Reproducer recipe

`B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` whose `v_knots` array contains a value strictly less than the prior value.

Expected kernel behavior

reject the surface with a diagnostic naming the offending index; never silently accept.

Notes

**See also**: Gn001, Gn003. **OCC behavior**: silently accepts (no diagnostic, empty result); catalog disallows silent-accept. Kernel-bug witnessed: receivers enforcing the spec must reject (or surface a diagnostic); silent acceptance defeats the catalog's stated invariant. Synonyms: "B-spline knots non-monotonic", "V knot vector strictly descending", "BSpline surface knot vector not non-decreasing", "knots violate non-decreasing invariant", "knot value smaller than prior in BSpline surface".
