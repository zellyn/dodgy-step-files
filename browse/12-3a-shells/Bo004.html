Bo004 — Closed shell encloses an unrepresented cavity (genus mismatch)

§12.3a (sub-class: enclosed region)

Sender

deduced — OCCT uncovered-class sweep

Description

The outer `CLOSED_SHELL` of a solid has topological genus ≥ 1 — it bounds one or more interior cavities — yet the `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` carries no `BREP_WITH_VOIDS` wrapper to describe those cavities. The kernel observes that point-in-solid classification disagrees with the user's intent: regions inside the cavity look "solid" rather than "void". Bug-reporter language: "interior cavity not described", "hidden hole inside solid".

Reproducer recipe

A `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` whose outer shell is the boundary of a torus-like region (or a cube with a cube cut out where the cut is fully interior). No `BREP_WITH_VOIDS` wrapper.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal by promoting the solid to `BREP_WITH_VOIDS`, synthesizing inner shells from the disconnected components of the outer shell's complement; or reject with a diagnostic that the solid encloses an undescribed region.

Notes

This is the "EnclosedRegion" branch; distinct from a leaky shell (Tsh007). Synonyms: "interior cavity not described", "hidden hole inside solid", "torus-like solid missing void wrapper", "solid genus mismatch", "internal hollow not flagged as void".

Expected validation

  • face[5].surface_type == "plane"
