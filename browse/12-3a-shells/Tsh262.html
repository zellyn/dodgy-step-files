Tsh262 — Hanging vertex lands on the interior of a cylinder's seam edge: the split sub-edges must keep both of the seam's 2D curve representations

  • the bound-registration precondition is `BRepBuilderAPI_Sewing.cxx:2565`). Companions: Tsh260 (base split), Tsh261 (snap-vs-new-node threshold) — both cut ordinary single-pcurve edges.

Description

An `OPEN_SHELL` with two `ADVANCED_FACE`s. Face CYL is a full 360° `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` (r=1, axis +Z, z:[0,2]) whose `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` is the standard four-element seam loop — bottom `CIRCLE` forward, one vertical `EDGE_CURVE` (1,0,0)→(1,0,2) FORWARD, top `CIRCLE` reversed, and THE SAME vertical `EDGE_CURVE` again REVERSED. Using one entity twice in one loop is what makes it a true seam: the reader gives that edge two distinct 2D curves on the one face, at u=0 and u=2π. Face TAB is a planar triangle (1.3,0,1)-(1.3,0,2)-(4,1.2,1.5); its first vertex hangs 0.3 off the seam and projects onto the seam's INTERIOR at z=1 while sharing no vertex with it, and its first edge runs parallel to the seam's upper half so the upper cut section has a merge partner.

Reproducer recipe

full-period `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` `ADVANCED_FACE` whose outer bound lists one vertical `EDGE_CURVE` twice with opposite `ORIENTED_EDGE` senses (bottom circle .T., seam .T., top circle .F., seam .F.); plus a second, planar `ADVANCED_FACE` with one vertex offset 0.3 from the seam line at the seam's mid-height and an edge running parallel to the seam's upper half. Sew at tolerance 0.5 with non-manifold mode on.

Expected kernel behavior

the hanging vertex is detected projecting onto the seam edge's interior and the seam is split there; because the edge being cut is a seam on a closed surface, BOTH its forward and reversed 2D curve representations are re-attached to each resulting sub-edge, so every sub-edge is still a seam (two pcurves, u=0 and u=2π) and the face's periodicity information survives the split.

Notes

Why we believe this test is valid — RUNTIME-SCAFFOLD verified against this fixture's own bytes (this worktree's `validation/.venv`, OCP/OCCT 7.8.1). Default STEP read → shape_null=False, 2 faces, 6 unique edges (`TopTools_IndexedMapOfShape`; the tier-3 `n_edges_total`=7 counts the seam edge once per loop occurrence); the seam edge `[(1,0,0),(1,0,2)]` is genuine — `BRep_Tool::IsClosed(edge, face_cyl)` is True and its forward/reversed pcurves evaluate to u=6.28319 / u=0.0 at the first parameter. Feeding the read shape to `BRepBuilderAPI_Sewing(0.5, sew=True, analysis=True, cutting=True, nonmanifold=True)` and calling `Perform()`: the seam edge is GONE, replaced by two collinear sub-edges cut exactly at the hanging vertex's projection z=1 — `[(1,0,0),(1.15,0,1)]` and `[(1.15,0,1),(1.15,0,2)]` (the upper section merged with Face TAB's parallel edge, hence the averaged x=1.15; `NbContigousEdges()==1`, `NbMultipleEdges()==0`). BOTH sub-edges still satisfy `BRep_Tool::IsClosed(sub, face_cyl)` and carry two distinct pcurves on the cylinder, evaluating forward/reversed to (6.28319, 0.0) on the lower section and (0.0, 6.28319) on the upper one — the orientation-dependent argument order of the re-attach. Two perturbations flip the outcome, so the split is genuinely driven by the hanging vertex: (a) same bytes with nonmanifold=False → no cut at all (a seam edge is listed against its single face twice, so it is only registered as a cuttable bound in non-manifold mode); (b) the tab offset widened 0.3 → 0.8, i.e. past the 0.5 tolerance → no cut, seam intact. Sewing is NOT on the default STEP read path, so this fixture is oracle-invisible to the shape-count oracle and requires the sewing scaffold to observe. Synonyms: "seam edge split dual pcurve sewing STEP", "cut seam edge keeps forward and reversed pcurve", "cylinder seam T-junction hanging vertex", "CreateSections seam pcurve propagation", "periodicity lost when seam edge split".
