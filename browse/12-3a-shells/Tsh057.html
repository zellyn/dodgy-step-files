Tsh057 — Merging near-tangent adjacent ADVANCED_FACEs (PLANE normals differ ~1e-4 rad, shared EDGE_CURVE with mismatched vertex z) returns self-overlapping face

Description

Two adjacent ADVANCED_FACEs meet at an EDGE_CURVE with PLANE normals differing by a small angle (e.g., 1e-4 rad, just below the merge threshold). The shared edge's VERTEX_POINTs may even have slightly different coordinates on each side (~1e-4 mm offset in z). Single-edge same-surface check passes; merging produces a face whose interior is on the wrong side of the original edges (a self-overlapping face with negative area in some region).

Reproducer recipe

Two near-coplanar ADVANCED_FACEs with PLANE normals differing by ~0.0001 radians (e.g., (0,0,1) vs (0.0001,0,~1)) sharing an EDGE_CURVE. Optionally the shared edge has different VERTEX_POINT z coords on each side (~9.999e-5 vs 0). After "merge", the resulting face's parameter-space outline overlaps itself.

Expected kernel behavior

post-merge validity check on the resulting face's wire (no self-intersections, no zero-area regions); revert merge if invalidity is detected.

Notes

**See also**: Tsh055, Tfa018. Synonyms: "near-tangent face merge produces self-overlapping face", "small angle deviation passes merge but creates invalid result", "merged face wire crosses itself", "merge across near-coplanar faces breaks geometry", "tiny normal mismatch corrupts face union".
