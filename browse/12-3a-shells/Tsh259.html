Tsh259 — Small-area internal hole wire removal cascades into removing a whole neighboring face built on the same edges

Description

`big_face` (10×10 `PLANE`, Tfa208's proven-tripping scale) carries a `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` (10×10) plus a `FACE_BOUND` internal hole wire (side `1e-4`, area `~1e-8`, built from 4 named `EDGE_CURVE`s `hole_e1..hole_e4`). A SEPARATE face, `small_neighbor_face`, has its ENTIRE `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` built from a second `EDGE_LOOP` referencing those SAME 4 `hole_e1..hole_e4` entities (genuine shared topology — not merely coincident geometry at the same coordinates). Both faces sit in one `OPEN_SHELL`, sharing one translation scope, so the shared `EDGE_CURVE` entities resolve to the identical edge objects in both faces.

Reproducer recipe

`big_face`: `ADVANCED_FACE('big_face',(#35,#65),#5,.T.)` where `#35` is the 10×10 `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` and `#65=FACE_BOUND('',#64,.F.)` wraps the tiny hole's `EDGE_LOOP` `#64=(hole_e1,hole_e2,hole_e3,hole_e4)`. `small_neighbor_face`: `ADVANCED_FACE('small_neighbor_face',(#76),#75,.T.)` where `#76=FACE_OUTER_BOUND('',#71,.T.)` wraps a SECOND `EDGE_LOOP` `#71` referencing the IDENTICAL `#47(hole_e1),#51(hole_e2),#55(hole_e3),#59(hole_e4)` entities. Both faces in `OPEN_SHELL('tsh259_shell',(#66,#77))`.

Expected kernel behavior

`removeSmallWire` finds `big_face`'s hole area below the minimum threshold and removes it, recording its 4 edges as removed. `removeSmallFaces` then examines every OTHER face touching those 4 edges (via the shape-wide edge→face map computed before any removal) — finds `small_neighbor_face`, whose entire outer wire is composed exclusively of those same 4 (now-removed) edges — and removes `small_neighbor_face` too, cascading the small-wire cleanup into a whole extra face removal, not just the hole itself.

Notes

**Empirical finding (live-verified, decisive, this worktree's OCP/OCCT 7.8.1)**: the reader's DEFAULT read (no supplementary invocation) does NOT auto-run the internal-wire-removal healer — the translated shape retains both faces intact (`n_faces_total=2`, `big_face` with 2 wires including the untouched hole, `small_neighbor_face` with its own 1 wire) — this is the class's cited mechanism, but it is not part of the ordinary STEP-read healing sequence (distinct from the small-area-wire face fixer, which the merged class record documents IS live on ordinary read for a per-face-only hole removal, e.g. Tfa208). A SUPPLEMENTARY, explicitly-invoked internal-wire-removal pass (same corpus convention as this packet's A3 sibling's supplementary sewing checks) with a minimum area of 1e-6 was run directly on the DEFAULT-translated shape: it reports success, with both the small-wire-removed status AND the small-faces-removed status flagged true; the resulting shape drops from 2 faces to exactly 1 — `big_face` survives with its hole gone (1 remaining wire, area recomputes to `100.0`) and `small_neighbor_face` is entirely gone, decisively confirming the cascade fires on this exact shared-edge input. Synonyms: "small-area wire removal cascades to neighbor face", "RemoveInternalWires cross-face cascade", "hole removal deletes adjacent face", "whole face boundary made of removed-hole edges", "cascading small-face cleanup".
