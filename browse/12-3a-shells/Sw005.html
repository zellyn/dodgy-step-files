Description

A face's underlying surface is an unbounded analytic (`PLANE`, `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` with no trim, etc.) and the fast-sewing path requires bounded UV space. Bug-reporter language: "infinite surface in sewing", "unbounded face fails fast sew".

Reproducer recipe

An `ADVANCED_FACE` with `face_geometry` an untrimmed `PLANE`, no `RECTANGULAR_TRIMMED_SURFACE` wrapper.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: compute the UV bounding box from the wire, coerce the trim implicitly, and fall through.

Notes

Synonyms: "untrimmed face", "infinite extent face", "infinite surface in sewing", "unbounded plane face fails fast sew", "face missing rectangular trim".
