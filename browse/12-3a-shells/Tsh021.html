Tsh021 — Non-manifold vertex (bowtie / hourglass / fan-of-fans)

§12.3a shell-orientation

P1 — silent data loss

topology

  • 15-academic.md L012, L047 (Attene 2013)

Description

A vertex whose 1-ring is not a single fan; touching cones, bowtie meshes (two fans glued at a single point). Common when two solids touch at exactly one vertex (hourglass).

Reproducer recipe

Two solids that share only one common `VERTEX_POINT`; or hand-construct a face fan-of-fans.

Expected kernel behavior

heal; duplicate the vertex into one copy per fan; topology becomes 2-manifold at cost of vertex count growth.

Notes

**See also**: Tsh019. Synonyms: "two solids touch at a single point", "bowtie geometry pinches at vertex", "hourglass topology", "fan of fans at one vertex", "non-manifold pinch point".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(13) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

  • contains(b'SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL')
  • count_entity_def(b'ADVANCED_FACE') == 2
