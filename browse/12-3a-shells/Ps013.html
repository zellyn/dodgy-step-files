Ps013 — Chiral part mirrored by silent X-coordinate negation

§12.3a shells / orientation (sub-class: pathological-success / chirality)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • deduced from CARTESIAN_POINT semantics combined with CARTESIAN_TRANSFORMATION_OPERATOR_3D scale
  • bug-report patterns "left-hand part imported as right-hand", "thread direction reversed", "bracket mirrored after import"

Description

An L-shaped bracket whose intended right-hand configuration was mirrored across the X axis during export; every X coordinate was negated. Topology, face count, edge count, and unit context are all unchanged; the part is a valid L-bracket, just the wrong-handed one. For chiral mechanical parts (threads, hand-side brackets, steering linkages) this is a critical defect that the kernel cannot detect from the geometry alone.

Reproducer recipe

An extruded L-shape with vertices at (0,0), (-1,0), (-1,2), (1,2), (1,1), (0,1) (in z=0 plane), extruded to z=1 — i.e., the X-mirror of the producer's intended footprint (0,0), (1,0), (1,2), (-1,2), (-1,1), (0,1).

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: a kernel cannot self-detect chirality errors on a single file, but it normalizes and exposes the AABB and the principal-axes / inertia-tensor of every solid so downstream tools comparing against a known-good reference can detect mirroring (negative determinant of the rotation that aligns inertial axes).

Notes

Tier-3 metric: determinant of the rigid alignment between this part's principal axes and a reference part's; -1 indicates a mirror. Synonyms: "left-hand part imported as right-hand", "thread direction reversed", "bracket mirrored after import", "chiral part comes in wrong-handed", "x-coordinate negated on export".
