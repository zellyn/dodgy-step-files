Description

A `FACE_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` wraps one `CONNECTED_FACE_SET` containing two `ADVANCED_FACE`s: `good_face` (a normal 4-edge planar face, `(0,0,0)-(5,0,0)-(5,5,0)-(0,5,0)`) and `bad_face_null_surface` (a second, geometrically well-formed 4-edge loop, `x:[10,15]`) whose `ADVANCED_FACE.face_geometry` attribute is left unset (`$`) — mirroring Xp008's proven null-surface pattern, now inside a `FACE_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` container instead of a `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`.

Reproducer recipe

`CONNECTED_FACE_SET('cfs',(#35,#66))` where `#35=ADVANCED_FACE('good_face',(#34),#33,.T.)` (real `PLANE`) and `#66=ADVANCED_FACE('bad_face_null_surface',(#65),$,.T.)` (surface `$`); `FACE_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL('fbsm',(#67))` wraps the one connected-face-set.

Expected kernel behavior

the face-translation stage cannot build a face without a surface and reports not-done for `bad_face_null_surface`; the `FaceBasedSurfaceModel` builder's per-face loop skips it with a warning and still returns a shell built from the surviving `good_face`, rather than aborting the whole connected-face-set or the whole model.

Notes

Live-verified (this worktree's OCP/OCCT 7.8.1): the reader's root-transfer step on this fixture returns 1 transferred root and 1 output shape, and the resulting shape has exactly 1 face / 4 edges / 8 (raw) vertices — `good_face` alone, confirming `bad_face_null_surface` was silently dropped rather than nulling the whole model or raising a hard failure. `brepcheck.valid == True` on the surviving shape. Synonyms: "FaceBasedSurfaceModel drops failed face", "connected_face_set null surface skipped", "partial container continuation surface model", "one bad face doesn't sink the whole model", "face geometry unset item skipped". Provenance tier: bytes-sufficient.
