ShapeAnalysis_Shell.CheckOrientedShells multi-component

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

Three faces, each in its own separate OPEN_SHELL (x=[0,1], x=[2,3], x=[4,5]), share no edges between any pair — three genuinely disjoint single-face shells wrapped in one SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL. An orientation check run per-shell must produce and report a verdict for every shell present, including the last one in the list; an accumulator or merge step that only covers shells up to but excluding the final index silently drops that shell's result from the reported verdict.
