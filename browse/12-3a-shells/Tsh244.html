Tsh244 — Within-tolerance gap but sampled-point coverage below ~50%: insufficient-overlap rejection

Sources

  • occt-coverage PARTIAL audit, `exchange/problems.json` `sew-free-edge-gap-merge` (`BRepBuilderAPI_Sewing::EvaluateDistances`/`FindCandidates`, `BRepBuilderAPI_Sewing.cxx:1053-1247,1502`). Second of the class's two missing subvariants (distinct from Tsh243's min-length-floor rejection).

Description

An `OPEN_SHELL` with TWO `ADVANCED_FACE`s. Face A hosts a 10mm reference free edge E=(0,0,0)-(10,0,0). Face B hosts a 10mm candidate edge C=(9.5,1e-4,0)-(19.5,1e-4,0) — a genuine within-tolerance perpendicular gap (1e-4mm) along its whole length, but only x:[9.5,10] (5% of C's own extent) actually overlaps E's parametric span [0,10]. `EvaluateDistances` discretizes both curves at 8 points; when the cheap "betweenness" test finds fewer than half of the candidate's sample points fall between the reference's own endpoints, it falls back to an endpoint-clamped projection whose resulting max-distance metric is driven up by the largely non-overlapping candidate, and `FindCandidates` rejects the pairing (`aMaxDist <= myTolerance` fails).

Reproducer recipe

Two face-hosted free edges with a genuine within-tolerance perpendicular gap along their full parallel run, but whose parametric ranges overlap by only a small fraction (~5%) of the candidate's own length — not merely a short candidate (see Tsh243), a long candidate that's mostly *elsewhere*.

Expected kernel behavior

heal-or-reject-explicitly; a candidate edge with insufficient parametric overlap with the reference (even though a genuinely-overlapping sub-region is well within tolerance) should not be merged wholesale — either reject the pairing (current OCCT behavior) or merge only the genuinely-overlapping sub-segment.

Notes

Live-verified (this worktree's OCP/OCCT 7.8.1, runtime the sewing stage(tolerance=1.0)` scaffold — reader-default the reader does not itself invoke Sewing): `NbContigousEdges()==0` (no merge) for this fixture's 5%-overlap candidate. A control build with the SAME tolerance and gap, but the candidate repositioned to 90% overlap (x:[1,11] instead of x:[9.5,19.5]), shows `NbContigousEdges()==1` (merge succeeds) — isolating overlap fraction, not distance or length, as the deciding factor at this configuration. The overlap-vs-rejection boundary was found empirically to be tolerance-dependent and non-monotonic in a few tested configurations (a smaller operator tolerance paradoxically merged a low-overlap pair that a larger tolerance rejected, in one probe) — the specific 5%-overlap/tolerance=1.0 combination shipped here was verified to reject reliably across repeated runs, but the general "coverage threshold" boundary should be treated as empirically-mapped for this exact geometry, not as a universal linear rule.
