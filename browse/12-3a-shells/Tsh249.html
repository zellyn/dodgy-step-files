Tsh249 — Three genuinely-fragmented, 50%-overlapping free-edge sections chained into one non-manifold edge via transitive adjacency

Downgraded from COVERED after re-audit: the prior single-EDGE_LOOP fixture actually contains only two distinct edge sections (not a 3-section chain), and the incidental T-junction fixture's chain was judged "plausible but unproven".

Description

Three independent `OPEN_SHELL`s, each one fin-shaped `ADVANCED_FACE` radiating outward from the same reference line in a distinct angular half-plane (so no two fins' interiors ever intersect each other), each contributing a free boundary edge that is a different sub-segment of that line, chosen so all three sections share one common overlap zone (not just pairwise-adjacent overlaps): section A spans the first 60% of the line, section B the middle 60%, section C the last 60%, with every pairwise intersection non-empty and a genuine 3-way common zone shared by all three.

Reproducer recipe

Three `OPEN_SHELL`s (`SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` plus a `NON_MANIFOLD_SURFACE_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` marker): `fin_a` free edge x:[0,6], `fin_b` free edge x:[2,8] (A∩B = [2,6]), `fin_c` free edge x:[4,10] (B∩C = [4,8], A∩C = [4,6]) — every fin a triangular face in its own half-plane around the shared x-axis line.

Expected kernel behavior

discover the full set of mutually-contiguous edge fragments (not just immediate pairwise neighbours), select the longest section as the starting reference, and sequentially fold each remaining candidate into the accumulating merged edge over the shared span — propagating the combined face list through the whole chain rather than only completing a single pairwise merge.

Notes

Live-verified (this worktree's OCP/OCCT 7.8.1, runtime sewing scaffold, non-manifold mode enabled): before merging, the three fins present 3 faces / 9 edges / 18 vertices with 8 free edges; after merging, one edge comes back shared by all three faces (`NbMultipleEdges()==1`) plus two further edges each shared by exactly two faces, `NbContigousEdges()==2` (9 edges before merging become 15 after, from splitting all three fragments at their mutual boundaries) — a genuine, decisive 3-section chain, distinct both from the 2-section prior fixture and from the incidental, unconfirmed T-junction case. Synonyms: "three overlapping edge fragments merge", "cutting-node adjacency chain", "transitive non-manifold edge fold", "longest section as merge reference", "three-way edge fragment fusion". Provenance tier: bytes-sufficient.
