Tsh055 — Merging adjacent same-surface faces with opposite normals returns inverted face

Description

Two faces that should be merged have surface normals pointing in opposite directions (one face is reversed relative to its partner). Same-surface detection compares unsigned surface descriptors and decides to merge; but the merge produces an inverted-orientation face that breaks shell normals.

Reproducer recipe

Two coplanar quads on the same plane; one with outward normal `+Z`, the other reversed (`-Z`). Both faces share an edge along their common boundary. Unify expected to either skip (different orientations) or merge with a single normal.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: normalize the same-surface check to compare oriented surfaces (normal direction included); opposite-orientation faces are not merged unless the kernel deliberately re-orients first.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa016, Tsh001. Synonyms: "merge of opposite-normal faces returns inverted face", "merging same-plane faces with opposite normals breaks shell", "unifier ignores face direction", "merged face has wrong outward direction", "opposite-orientation faces merged silently".
