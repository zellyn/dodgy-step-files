Sw006 — Fast-sewing face whose parametric bounds are tighter than surface natural bounds

Description

A face's wire pcurve excursion in UV is strictly smaller than the surface's natural parameter range, but the fast-sewing path expects natural-bounds equality. Bug-reporter language: "trimmed face fails fast sewing", "non-natural bounds".

Reproducer recipe

A `RECTANGULAR_TRIMMED_SURFACE` of a `PLANE` with `u1=0.2, u2=0.8` while the face wire visits only `u∈[0.3,0.7]`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: refit (repair) the surface to the wire's actual extent, or fall back to the slow-sewing path.

Notes

Bug-reporter synonyms: "wire doesn't fill face", "loose pcurve bounds", "trimmed face fails fast sewing", "non-natural bounds reject fast sew", "wire excursion smaller than surface trim range".
