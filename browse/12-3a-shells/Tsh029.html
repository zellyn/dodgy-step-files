Tsh029 — Naked / dangling edge in shell (free edges, LOTAR integrity defect)

§12.3a shell-orientation

Description

An edge that belongs to fewer than two faces inside a shell intended to be closed (a "naked" edge), or to zero faces (a "dangling" edge). Acceptable for an open shell, fatal for a solid. LOTAR explicitly classifies this as an integrity defect preventing reliable archive retrieval.

Reproducer recipe

`CLOSED_SHELL` containing an `ORIENTED_EDGE` whose underlying `EDGE_CURVE` is referenced by exactly one `FACE_BOUND` instead of two; or remove one face from a closed shell.

Expected kernel behavior

stitch the free edges back into the shell when the gap is small enough to close, or reject as a non-watertight / non-solid input. Archive contexts may require strict rejection regardless of gap size.

Notes

**See also**: Twi037. Synonyms: "shell has free naked edges", "edge belongs to fewer than two faces", "dangling edge on solid body", "missing face leaves edge exposed", "non-watertight body fails archive integrity".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(25) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • n_edges_total >= 20
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[4].surface_type == "plane"
