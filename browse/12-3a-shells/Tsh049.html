Tsh049 — Internal edges lost during shell-level face merge

§12.3a shells/orientation

P1 — silent data loss

crash topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-3a-shells/Tsh049.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0026635
  • OCCT MANTIS#0027315 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A face-unification step encounters two same-domain faces with a shared boundary plus one or more internal wires (holes). The algorithm merges the outer boundaries correctly but discards the inner wires entirely, producing a face without its expected holes. Visible as solid mass appearing where a hole should be.

Reproducer recipe

Two coplanar rectangular faces, each carrying one inner circular wire (`FACE_INNER_BOUND`). Merge expected to produce one rectangular face with two circular holes; defective merge produces one rectangle with no holes.

Expected kernel behavior

face-merge must preserve all `FACE_INNER_BOUND` wires from input faces; only the outer boundary is unified.

Notes

**See also**: Tsh046. Synonyms: "holes lost during face merge", "inner wires dropped on unification", "face inner bounds disappear after merge", "circular hole disappears after healing", "FACE_INNER_BOUND not preserved through unification".
