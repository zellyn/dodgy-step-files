Description

ShapeFix_Solid.SolidFromShell hollow-solid cavity loss

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

BREP_WITH_VOIDS references an outer CLOSED_SHELL (2 representative faces of a 2x2x2 cube, z=0 and z=2) and one void, an ORIENTED_CLOSED_SHELL wrapping a second CLOSED_SHELL (2 representative faces of a 1x1x1 cube offset to (0.5,0.5,0.5)..(1.5,1.5,1.5)) with orientation .T.. When promoting this to a solid representation, carry the void shell(s) through into the result — dropping BREP_WITH_VOIDS's void list during solid construction silently turns a hollow part into a solid block, discarding the cavity the input explicitly declared.
