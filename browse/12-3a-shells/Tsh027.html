Tsh027 — Coincident-but-not-shared faces between adjacent solids

§12.3a shell-orientation

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • 22-niche-forums.md Y022
  • 17-standards-bodies.md M022 (tangential contact)

Sender

assembly tools exporting per-part bodies (CATProduct, SLDASM-as-STEP, Inventor IAM) without "merge coincident faces"

Description

A built-up assembly imports as N disconnected `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`s; faces shared between adjacent parts are coincident-but-not-shared (each part has its own copy). FEA mesh has no node continuity across the interface; CFD mesh has interior walls. Tangential / zero-thickness contact between solids creates a non-manifold body that meshers refuse.

Reproducer recipe

Two `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`s whose oriented bounding boxes touch along a single shared planar face that resolves to identical control points.

Expected kernel behavior

heal — sew across coincident faces with ε-tolerance; or warn and let downstream offset by ±ε.

Notes

**See also**: Tsh026. Synonyms: "duplicate face between adjacent parts", "two solids touch but don't share geometry", "non-shared coincident face", "FEA mesh has no node continuity at part interface", "zero-thickness contact between parts".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • count_entity_def(b'CLOSED_SHELL') == 2
