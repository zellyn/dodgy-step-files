Tsh052 — Inversed normals on revolved-shape import

Description

A revolved solid (`SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` over a profile that closes through the axis) is imported with face normals pointing inward. Visible as the part appearing inside-out in viewers, or as inverted normals when exported to VRML/STL. Fixture exhibits the kernel's choice of revolved-face orientation when the profile direction and axis convention disagree.

Reproducer recipe

Profile is a half-circle in XZ plane, revolved 360° about the Z axis to form a sphere. Profile is parameterised in the +X-from-axis direction; revolution direction is `+Z`. The resulting face normal can point inward or outward depending on which orientation convention the kernel applies — both are spec-legal.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: normalize / coerce the sphere face to be oriented outward (away from the enclosed volume) regardless of profile direction; for non-closed revolutions the convention is "outward of the swept body".

Notes

**See also**: Tsh001, Gs034. Synonyms: "revolved part imports inside out", "sphere normals point inward after import", "VRML export shows reversed normals", "revolution direction convention mismatch", "revolved face orientation backwards".
