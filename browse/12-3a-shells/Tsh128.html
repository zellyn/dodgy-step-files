Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

Two of the five faces are individually broken: the x=0 face's three-edge loop contains an edge whose `LINE` lies along x=0, y=10 while its declared end vertex is (0,0,10), and the x=10 face uses two edges whose `LINE`s carry a zero-magnitude `VECTOR`. Validate each edge against its own curve and vertices before the shell-level pass, and keep any such failure local to its face: record the diagnostic, set that face aside, and continue so the remaining faces still receive a consistent orientation. A per-face failure must never short-circuit the shell pass and leave later faces unprocessed.
