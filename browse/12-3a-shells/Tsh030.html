Tsh030 — Non-finite (infinite) solid built from open shell

Description

A solid whose oriented shells do not bound a finite region; typically a half-space accidentally produced by a Boolean op. The volume integral over the shell returns infinite or NaN; the "solid" describes an unbounded volume rather than a bounded body.

Reproducer recipe

Build a single planar face wrapped as a solid; or shells whose orientations enclose unbounded volume.

Expected kernel behavior

reject — refuse to construct, or emit a diagnostic and downgrade to shell.

Notes

Synonyms: "solid encloses unbounded volume", "infinite half-space treated as solid", "solid has NaN volume", "Boolean op produced unbounded body", "solid bounds infinite region".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • n_edges_total >= 4
  • n_vertices_total >= 8
  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
