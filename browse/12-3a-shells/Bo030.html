Bo030 — EDGE_CURVE start VERTEX_POINT lies off the underlying LINE: inner-vertex tolerance smaller than the deviation it bounds

§12.4 (sub-class: tolerance underflow)

P1 — silent data loss

Sender

deduced — OCCT uncovered-class sweep, Pass C

Description

A vertex deep inside a face/shell carries a tolerance value that's smaller than the actual geometric deviation between its 3D point and the curves/surfaces it sits on. The vertex's "tolerance ball" doesn't actually contain the geometry it indexes. Bug-reporter language: "vertex tolerance too small", "tolerance underestimates gap", "inner tolerance lies".

Reproducer recipe

An `EDGE_CURVE` whose endpoints are vertices declared with tolerance `1e-7` but whose 3D coordinates are 1e-3 distant from the curve evaluation at the boundary parameter.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: recompute inner tolerances from actual deviations; bump tolerance up, or rebuild geometry to match.

Notes

Distinct from N-tolerance entries (which are about declared vs. file-scope tolerance); this is per-vertex. Synonyms: "vertex tolerance too small", "tolerance underestimates gap", "inner tolerance lies", "vertex tolerance ball doesn't cover deviation", "per-vertex tolerance smaller than actual error".
