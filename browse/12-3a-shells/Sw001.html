Sw001 — Three or more faces share a boundary edge after sewing (non-manifold result)

Description

Three or more faces are positioned with a common boundary curve. When the sewer joins them, the result is a "multiple edge" — a non-manifold join — but no diagnostic warns the consumer that the result is non-manifold. Typical fixture: three ADVANCED_FACEs on three different PLANEs (e.g., horizontal, vertical_xz, vertical_yz) sharing a common origin or edge, often with empty bound lists, none yet wrapped in a SHELL. Bug-reporter language: "sewing produces non-manifold", "multiple edge", "T-junction after sew", "three faces meet at one edge".

Reproducer recipe

Three rectangular ADVANCED_FACEs on distinct PLANEs arranged so each shares one straight edge with the same line in 3D (T-junction). Empty bound lists are common in sewing-input fixtures.

Expected kernel behavior

Detect multiple-edge condition; emit a non-manifold diagnostic; offer a manifold-only mode that rejects.

Notes

Bug-reporter synonyms: "T-edge after sewing", "non-manifold sewing", "three faces meet at one edge", "sewing produces multiple edge", "non-manifold join after stitch".
