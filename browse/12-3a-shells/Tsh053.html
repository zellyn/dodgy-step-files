Tsh053 — Merging coplanar adjacent faces fails with construction error on shape with empty edge loop

Description

A shape on which adjacent same-surface faces are being merged has a face whose wire references zero edges (empty edge list inside `EDGE_LOOP`). The merge algorithm hits an internal precondition that requires at least one edge per loop and raises a construction error, aborting the entire merge operation.

Reproducer recipe

A face with a `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` whose `EDGE_LOOP` is empty (`EDGE_LOOP('',())`); fed to face unification.

Expected kernel behavior

same-surface face-merge must validate input topology before running; faces with empty edge loops are skipped or the entire shape is rejected with a clear diagnostic, not a low-level construction error.

Notes

**See also**: Tsh023. Synonyms: "face merge crashes on empty edge loop", "construction error during unification", "merge aborts on face with no edges", "low-level error from face unifier on empty wire", "empty wire fails precondition during merge".
