Tsh046 — Adjacent same-domain faces fail to merge across linear-edge chain

Description

A flat face has been split along a chain of small, near-collinear edges (a "stitched" boundary from sliver-face removal). After a face-unification step, the chain is still present: the merge skipped these edges because the per-edge angle test was satisfied but the chain's *cumulative* deviation was not considered. The shell still has dozens of redundant tiny faces.

Reproducer recipe

Single rectangular face whose right-hand boundary is composed of 8 short LINE edges, each turning by 0.5 degrees from the previous, so that the cumulative deviation across the chain is 4 degrees. A neighbour face shares the same chain of edges. Expected to unify into one face after cleanup, but the per-edge angle threshold of 1 degree marks each pair as non-collinear.

Expected kernel behavior

when merging same-domain faces, accumulate angular deviation across consecutive collinear-tested edges and report a single merge decision for the entire chain, not per-edge.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa016, Tsh047. Synonyms: "tiny stitched edges not merged", "near-collinear edge chain not unified", "cumulative angle deviation across edges", "redundant slivers left after healing", "per-edge angle threshold misses chain".
