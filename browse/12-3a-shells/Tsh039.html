Description

A face whose boundary loop folds back on itself after triangulation, or "4-manifold edge" (one shared edge has two pairs of triangles).

Reproducer recipe

Mesh derived from STEP face whose outer wire pinches; or two solids merged by index union without proper Boolean.

Expected kernel behavior
