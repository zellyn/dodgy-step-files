Description

A vector entity that names itself as its own direction makes the reader abort

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

The two faces abut along x=1 but each owns its boundary: face 2's vertices sit at x=1.0000001, exactly the file's declared 1.0E-7 distance accuracy away from face 1's, and the two boundary edges are separate entities. Never let a caller-supplied sewing tolerance fall below the model's declared accuracy — clamp it to that floor — so this pair is recognised as one boundary and merged into a single shared edge with merged vertices instead of requiring bit-exact equality. Separately, one `VECTOR` entity names itself as its own direction attribute: detect the reference cycle while resolving the instance graph and reject that entity with a diagnostic rather than recursing into it.
