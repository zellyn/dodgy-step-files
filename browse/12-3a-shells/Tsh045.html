Tsh045 — `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` whose outer shell loses the closed flag after face unification

P1 — silent data loss

Description

A solid is built from a closed shell (six cube faces sharing every edge twice). After a face-unification pass, the kernel rebuilds the shell with merged faces but neglects to reset the "closed" / "watertight" flag on the wires/shell. Downstream consumers that rely on the flag — STL export, meshing, Boolean operations — treat the result as an open shell even though topologically it still closes.

Reproducer recipe

`CLOSED_SHELL` of six rectangular faces sharing all 12 edges; one pair of co-planar coplanar faces left as separate faces with an artificial internal edge; expected to be unified by a downstream pass. Producer-side defect: the input file's wires have a `closed` flag computed before merging that becomes stale after merging.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: normalize / recompute the closed/watertight flag from current topology after any healing or simplification pass; do not propagate stale flags from the input file.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa016, Tsh001. Synonyms: "shell loses watertight flag after merge", "stale closed flag after face unification", "STL export thinks closed shell is open", "shell flag not recomputed after healing", "downstream sees open shell after Boolean".
