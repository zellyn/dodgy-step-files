Pf028 — Rhino joins > 10k face polysurfaces in O(n²)

  • 14-brlcad-rhino.md K028 (Rhino 8 STEP help)

Description

Very large solids (> 10 000 faces) take O(n²) time to join; nested blocks with deep hierarchies trigger constant warnings. Rhino exposes both as configurable cutoffs; an admission of scale-related defect classes.

Reproducer recipe

AP242 file with assembly depth > 10 or single body with > 10 000 faces.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: configurable join cutoff with a quiet mode for non-interactive callers; coerce the join policy.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(900) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'CARTESIAN_POINT') >= 1

Tier-3 assertions

  • load == "ok"
