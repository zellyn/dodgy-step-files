Sender

large single-shell STEP

Description

STEP importer hangs and consumes huge memory during the import-time healing pass on one big shell; each pass exposes another defect, requiring further iteration; the iteration loop is unbounded and does not converge.

Reproducer recipe

Any large single-`CLOSED_SHELL` STEP that triggers a full healing pass during import.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: size-aware healing logic that bypasses or chunks the multi-pass step on huge single-shell input. Must not hang.

Notes

Cross-oracle: pure-Python Part-21 validator rejects (reject(E_REAL_NO_DOT)); OCCT silently accepts (load is `empty`). OCC auto-heals a spec-level violation.
