Description

Recursive-descent parser for Part-21 LIST/SET/BAG aggregates without depth ceiling crashes when an attacker supplies thousands of nested parentheses inside a single attribute.

Reproducer recipe

`#1=FOO(((((…10 000 levels…)))));`

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with E_DEPTH_EXCEEDED diagnostic: enforce a depth ceiling (e.g. 64) and emit a structured error. Must not crash via stack overflow.
