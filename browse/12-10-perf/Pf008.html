Pf008 — Stack overflow on huge faces-per-shell counts

Description

Recursive traversal of a single `CLOSED_SHELL` with ~10⁵ faces overflows the default Windows C-stack. Parse succeeds; topology assembly crashes.

Reproducer recipe

One `CLOSED_SHELL` with > 80k `ADVANCED_FACE` children, all referencing the same surface; or `StepData_EntityCluster` chain hand-built to ~10⁴ depth.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: iterative traversal with bounded recursion. Must not crash. Must not hang.

Notes

**See also**: M035, Pf009, Pf012, Ad055.
