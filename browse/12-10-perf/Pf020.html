Pf020 — OCAF modification-delta leak on long-running healing test suites

§12.10 (sub-class: leak / multi-pass-healing)

performance

Description

OCAF's modification-delta mechanism for array-style attributes (Active Data Mesh Attribute) skips a destructor on the previous state; long-running suites that perform thousands of modify/commit cycles accumulate memory. Hides as "test-suite OOM".

Reproducer recipe

regression suite performing thousands of modify/commit cycles on a mesh attribute; observe RSS growth proportional to iteration count.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: `Backup`/`Delta` lifetime in OCAF must be bounded; coerce to release after commit.

Notes

Provenance tier: cross-file-state; bytes alone cannot demonstrate this defect; the symptom is monotonic memory growth across thousands of modify/commit cycles in long-running kernel state. A single fixture cannot embody the leak; the kernel's cross-transaction state cache must retain (and fail to release) snapshots over many iterations. An external memory measurement against a long-running test loop is the appropriate venue.
