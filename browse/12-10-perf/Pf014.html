Pf014 — Long helix exported as huge B-spline (millions of poles)

Description

When a long 3D helix is projected onto its hosting cylinder, OCCT emitted a B-spline approximation rather than the analytic 2D line segment; analogous issue: `sweep(circle, helix(h≥30))` produces missing-faces output sensitive to sub-percent offset (build123d #1064). Parameter-space curve becomes huge B-spline with thousands of knots.

Reproducer recipe

`EDGE_CURVE` whose 3D curve is a helix on a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` with no pcurve; or sweep a circle along a tall (h ≥ 30) helix and export to STEP.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: detect the line-on-elementary-surface helix case and coerce export to a compact analytical form rather than a million-pole B-spline.

Notes

**See also**: P008, P009.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(1) ifc=schema_n/a
