Pf021 — Floating / non-deterministic crash in shape healing on near-apex `CONICAL_SURFACE` edge

Description

Healing pipeline crashes intermittently on the same input; symptoms are uninitialized memory, threading races, or floating-point exceptions in projection routines. Whole class, not a single defect — every vendor fixes it repeatedly. Common trigger: a `CONICAL_SURFACE` whose `EDGE_CURVE` passes through the cone apex, with a near-zero-length edge between the apex and a 1e-12-offset `VERTEX_POINT` — singular projection in healing produces an FP exception or uninitialised-memory read.

Reproducer recipe

run healing on the same large STEP 1000 times; detect non-deterministic crashes / divergent outputs.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: deterministic same-input-same-output writer; normalize the output across runs.
