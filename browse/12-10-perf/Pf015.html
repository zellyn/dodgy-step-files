Pf015 — Pathological B-rep from mesh conversion: `OPEN_SHELL` from OpenSCAD/STL→STEP

Sender

OpenSCAD → STL → FreeCAD "Convert to Shape/Solid" → STEP

Description

Mesh-derived B-reps emerge as huge `OPEN_SHELL`s with one tiny `ADVANCED_FACE` per triangle. Stitching pairs fail, Boolean cuts fail with builder-arg check errors, joining more than ~10k faces takes quadratic time. Rhino exposes "do not join polysurfaces with more than ___ faces" as an explicit scale escape hatch.

Reproducer recipe

OpenSCAD design with `hull()` or `minkowski()`; export STL; FreeCAD: Mesh → Shape → Solid → STEP. Try a Boolean cut on the result.

Expected kernel behavior

reject with explicit "non-watertight,

Notes

**See also**: M023, Pf027. Provenance tier: runtime-only; bytes alone cannot demonstrate this defect; the catalogued symptoms (quadratic-time stitching, Boolean failures, resource exhaustion at ~10k+ faces) are runtime resource consumption that scales with mesh size. A fixture-scale `OPEN_SHELL` does not embody the production-scale exhaustion; a behavioral test on the stitching/Boolean pipeline at scale is the appropriate venue.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(700) ifc=schema_n/a

Provenance tier

runtime-only — defect not fully embodied in fixture bytes.

Closure intent

solid (defect kind: gap) — authored as a closed solid; openness is the defect — heal it shut.
