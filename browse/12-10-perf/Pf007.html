Pf007 — `ADVANCED_FACE` with many circular inner `FACE_BOUND` holes triggers eager UV-bounds wire-walk on every type-only surface query

§12.10 (sub-class: dependency-depth)

P2 — spec deviation (no data loss)

performance

Sources

  • 13-quaoar.md B003

Description

Querying the supporting surface of a face eagerly walks every edge of every bounding wire to compute UV bounds, even when the caller only needs the surface type. Common shape: an `ADVANCED_FACE` with one outer bound plus many circular inner `FACE_BOUND` holes on a single plane (perforated sheet); the cost multiplies by hole count, producing quadratic-in-feature-count writer time.

Reproducer recipe

time the STEP writer's transfer pass on a sheet with thousands of holes.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept (performance contract): lazy UV-bound computation; cheap type-only surface queries that do not trigger eager wire-walks. Must not hang on faces with many holes.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(1) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'CARTESIAN_POINT') >= 8

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == False
  • n_vertices_total == 1
