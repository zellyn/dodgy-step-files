Pf034 — Shape-divide pass raises end-of-iteration on large shape

§12.10 performance

P2 — spec deviation (no data loss)

performance

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0030593 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A shape-divide pass on a large shape iterates faces building intermediate shape lists; on a shape with >10⁵ faces the internal `NoMoreObject` exception is thrown when the iterator wraps — apparently a hard-coded limit.

Reproducer recipe

Compound of ~200 cubes (1,200 faces total — close to internal limit); apply shape-divide.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: no fixed-size collections in the divide pipeline; coerce capacity to grow with input. Must not crash on overflow.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa051, Hea010.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'CARTESIAN_POINT') >= 1
  • contains(b'MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP')

Tier-3 assertions

  • load == "ok"
