Wr050 — Geometry coordinate values doubled on export (writer applies scale and unit-conversion both)

§12.13 writer-pathology (sub-class: units / coordinate scale)

P1 — silent data loss

round-trip writer

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-13-writer-pathology/Wr050.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #17729
  • bug-reporter language: "outer diameter 20mm exports as 40mm", "model is 2x bigger after export", "stl and step both wrong size".

Sender

FreeCAD writer when both a Draft Scale operation and a unit conversion are in the pipeline; the writer composes them in series instead of choosing one path.

Description

A part the user dimensioned as 20 mm outer / 13 mm inner round-trips as 40 mm / 26 mm — every coordinate doubled. The writer applies both the source-document scale (which was already 1.0) and the export-unit conversion factor — but treats the document scale as if it were a coordinate-space transform on top. The output STEP carries doubled coordinates in a context that still claims millimetres. Distinct from Wr035 (×1000) and Wr044 (inch label only) by the specific 2× factor.

Reproducer recipe

STEP cube authored as 20 mm side; emit `CARTESIAN_POINT` coordinates of `(40.0, 40.0, 40.0)` with `LENGTH_UNIT` declaring SI MILLI METRE.

Expected kernel behavior

writer composes the Placement-and-unit pipeline once; refuses to chain document-scale into unit-conversion; emits a self-test that round-trips a known-volume primitive before writing user data.

Notes

**See also**: Wr035, U015, Wr044. Synonyms: "STEP export doubles all coordinates", "20mm becomes 40mm on import", "two-times scale factor on STEP export", "writer applies scale twice".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'40.0') and contains(b'MILLI')
  • contains(b'CARTESIAN_POINT')
