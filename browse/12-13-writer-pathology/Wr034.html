Wr034 — Coordinate-system axis swap without notification (Y-up vs Z-up)

Sources

  • OCCT forum "STEP from web-CAD has wrong axis"
  • bug-reporter language: "model is on its side after import", "Y up vs Z up confusion"

Sender

Web-CAD and game-engine-derived writers whose internal convention is Y-up, exporting to STEP without applying the Z-up transform

Description

The geometry coordinates were authored in a Y-up coordinate system (the engine convention), but the writer emitted them as-is into STEP without applying the rotation that makes Z the up-axis (STEP / CAD convention). The receiver loads the geometry rotated 90° around X. Bug-reporter language: "model lying on its side", "Y-up vs Z-up", "STEP imported sideways", "coordinate axes swapped".

Reproducer recipe

a `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` whose vertices imply a vertical extent along Y rather than Z, with a comment noting the input was authored Y-up and emitted without rotation.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept: normalize by trusting the file's coordinate system (DATA-section AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D); if the application expects Z-up, emit a "model appears Y-up" diagnostic when the geometry's bounding box has inverted aspect.
