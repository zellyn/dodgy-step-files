Wr037 — Loss of seam-edge marking on closed surfaces (cylinder seam emitted as ordinary edge)

§12.13 writer-pathology (sub-class: round-trip data loss)

P1 — silent data loss

round-trip writer

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-13-writer-pathology/Wr037.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0027xxx-class
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP cylinder split at seam after re-export" (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Sender

Writers that linearise a kernel's "seam edge" annotation into two ordinary edges with a duplicate vertex

Description

The input had a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` face whose UV-seam edge was marked (the wire crosses the U=0/U=2π identification), so the receiver knows the surface is closed at that edge. The re-emitted file contains two ordinary edges with duplicate vertices at the seam, no `seam` flag; the receiver sees an open shell where the original was closed. Bug-reporter language: "cylinder seam lost on re-export", "shell became non-watertight after round-trip", "duplicate vertices at seam".

Reproducer recipe

a cylindrical face whose `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` is composed of four ordinary `EDGE_CURVE` entries (top, bottom, side-A, side-B), where side-A and side-B share endpoints in 3D but at distinct VERTEX_POINT entities at U=0 and U=2π.

Expected kernel behavior

detect duplicate-coincident vertices at the U-seam of a closed surface; merge and re-mark the edge as a seam.

Notes

Twi-class read-side; Wr037 attributes to writer.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'VERTEX_POINT') >= 4
  • count(b'VERTEX_POINT') >= 4
