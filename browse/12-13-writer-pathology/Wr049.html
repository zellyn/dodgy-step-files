Wr049 — Subtractive pipe at right-angle joints emits hidden sphere of material in joint

§12.13 writer-pathology (sub-class: feature-construction artefact)

P2 — spec deviation (no data loss)

writer

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-13-writer-pathology/Wr049.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #26161
  • bug-reporter language: "subtractive pipe joints get a solid ball in joints that are 90 degrees or more", "solid balls do not show up in FreeCAD but appear after re-importing", "stl shows ball, step shows ball, view does not".

Sender

FreeCAD PartDesign subtractive-pipe feature with rounded-corner transition between segments.

Description

A subtractive-pipe (PartDesign-style sweep used for material removal) along a path with 90° or greater bends with rounded corner transitions emits the corner-fill sphere as part of the additive-result solid in the STEP file, even though the source kernel correctly hides it during display. On re-import the geometry shows a solid sphere occupying the would-be-hollow joint region. The on-disk symptom is a stray closed sub-shell whose centre coincides with the bend pivot point and whose radius equals the pipe radius; a sub-shell that should have been Boolean-subtracted before emission.

Reproducer recipe

STEP file with a `SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` containing two cylindrical sub-shells joined at a right angle, plus an extra closed `SPHERICAL_SURFACE`-bounded sub-shell at the joint pivot — the sphere is the residual that should have been hidden.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: writer Boolean-subtracts construction-residual primitives before emission; receivers may warn and accept on isolated sub-shells with no NAUO/styled-item parent that were obviously construction artefacts. Loading stray construction residuals as visible geometry is a coverage gap.

Notes

Synonyms: "subtractive pipe leaves sphere at joint", "right-angle pipe joint has invisible ball", "STEP export shows extra material in pipe corners", "construction residual sphere in joint".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'SPHERICAL_SURFACE')
  • count_entity_def(b'SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL') >= 1 or count_entity_def(b'CLOSED_SHELL') >= 2

Tier-3 assertions

  • load == "ok"
