Wr035 — Coordinate scale-factor applied twice (model 1000× larger or smaller than expected)

§12.13 writer-pathology (sub-class: unit / coordinate)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0030xxx-class
  • bug-reporter language: "model 1000× too small / too large" (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Sender

Writers with bugs in unit-conversion: applying the m-to-mm factor in both the coordinate transform and the unit declaration

Description

Numeric coordinates are scaled by 1000 (input was in metres, internal store is in millimetres, output applied the m-to-mm factor *and* declared the unit as `MILLI METRE` while the coordinates already reflect the mm scale, so the receiver applies the factor a second time). The result: the receiver sees a model 1000× larger or smaller than intended. Bug-reporter language: "model 1000× too small after import", "STEP file scaled by 1000", "tiny model", "huge model", "millimetre-vs-metre mistake".

Reproducer recipe

a CARTESIAN_POINT at `(1000.0, 1000.0, 1000.0)` with `LENGTH_UNIT` declared as `MILLI METRE`, comment noting input was a 1.0-metre cube.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: normalize by trusting the DATA-section unit declaration. If a receiver application has a "model size sanity check" it warns when the bounding box exceeds plausible bounds, but the kernel itself does not second-guess.

Notes

§12.5 covers unit defects from receiver perspective.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • matches(rb'CARTESIAN_POINT\([^;]*1000(?:\.0)?,1000') or contains(b'1000.0,1000.0,1000.0')
  • matches(rb'1000') and contains(b'.MILLI.')
