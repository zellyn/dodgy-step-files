Gs044 — `INTERSECTION_CURVE` between two surfaces with multiple disjoint intersection branches, only one represented

§12.2c surface-degeneracy (sub-class: intersection curve)

P1 — silent data loss

silent-loss spec-violation geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2c-surfaces/Gs044.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-42 §INTERSECTION_CURVE

Description

An INTERSECTION_CURVE entity holds the curve of intersection between two surfaces. The spec models the intersection as a single CURVE_ON_SURFACE pair, but in general two surfaces (e.g. a plane and a torus, or two non-coaxial cylinders) intersect along *multiple disjoint branches*. A single INTERSECTION_CURVE entity cannot faithfully represent this; the producer chose one branch arbitrarily, silently dropping the others.

Reproducer recipe

TOROIDAL_SURFACE major=10, minor=3; PLANE z=0. The intersection is two concentric circles at radius 7 and 13. The fixture's INTERSECTION_CURVE represents only the outer branch (r=13).

Expected kernel behavior

detect that the producer's basis curve does not cover the full surface intersection and warn; or reject INTERSECTION_CURVE if the kernel expects a single branch and detect-time analysis disagrees.

**See also**: Gp- pcurve entries.
