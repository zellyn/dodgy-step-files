Gs014 — Zero-area / sliver / degenerate `ADVANCED_FACE` (sliver, spot, strip, pin; tiny aspect-ratio rectangle)

§12.2c surface-degeneracy

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

Sources

  • F024, F025, F026, F027, R017, W004, W020, L004, L011, M002, H013, H014, H049, H077, H094, H104, H105

Description

Family of degenerate `ADVANCED_FACE` defects: spot (face collapses to a point, e.g. from Pro/E export rounding), strip (one parametric direction is near-zero), pin / sliver (extreme aspect ratio; for example a rectangle on a `PLANE` 100 units wide but only 1e-7 units tall, width many orders of magnitude smaller than length and within distance tolerance), and zero-area faces with collinear vertices. The underlying surface may be well-defined; the face's trim collapses the area.

Reproducer recipe

4-edge `ADVANCED_FACE` whose two long sides are 100mm and two short sides are 1e-7 mm; or `PLANE` whose ref direction equals normal direction (zero-area on construction); or face with all vertices within tolerance ball.

Expected kernel behavior

drop the face when below tolerance and merge its incident edges (or accept it and reject downstream operations that would deref it), never crash on a zero-area surface definition.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa006, Tfa007, Tfa008, Tfa012, Tfa015. Synonyms: "zero-area face sliver", "spot face collapses to point", "strip face one direction near zero", "pin face tiny aspect-ratio rectangle", "ADVANCED_FACE area below tolerance".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(5) ifc=schema_n/a
