Gs043 — `OFFSET_CURVE_3D` with `ref_distance` equal to the basis curve's radius of curvature (collapse)

§12.2c surface-degeneracy (sub-class: offset curve)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2c-surfaces/Gs043.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-42 §OFFSET_CURVE_3D

Description

An OFFSET_CURVE_3D with ref_distance equal to the local radius of curvature creates a cusp at every point of that curvature. The mathematical offset is undefined at such points (the offset point coincides with the curve's center of curvature; 0/0 arises in the offset's first derivative). For a circular basis of radius R, an offset of -R collapses the entire offset to the center.

Reproducer recipe

CIRCLE radius 5.0; OFFSET_CURVE_3D over it with ref_distance = -5.0 → entire curve collapses to center point.

Expected kernel behavior

detect collapse / cusp at construction time and either reject the offset as degenerate or treat the result as a point set rather than a curve.

Notes

**See also**: Gs048. Synonyms: "OFFSET_CURVE_3D ref_distance equal to radius of curvature creates collapse", "circle offset by negative radius collapses to centre", "offset curve cusp at every point", "0/0 in offset curve first derivative", "offset distance equals local radius produces degeneracy".
