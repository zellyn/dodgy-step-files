Gs196 — `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` trimmed to within tolerance of a full period forces a half-surface closure probe before splitting

Description

A single `ADVANCED_FACE` on a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` (radius 5.0) is trimmed to U:[0, 2π−1e-9] — a genuine 1e-9 radian sliver short of the full 2π period — bounded by two structurally DISTINCT (non-shared) near-coincident end edges (the u≈0 and u≈2π sides are two separate `EDGE_CURVE` entities with two separate `VERTEX_POINT`s ~5e-9 mm apart, not the same edge referenced twice), so the seam-detection scan's identity-based check never flags a seam. The gap (1e-9 rad) is below the surface adaptor's own U-direction resolution tolerance for this radius (empirically measured ≈2e-8 rad), so the face's U-trim range is within the closure-test's tolerance of the surface's full native period.

Reproducer recipe

`CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` (radius 5.0) with a face bounded by a bottom rim (near-full circle, u:0 -> 2π−1e-9), a top rim (same, offset in Z), and two distinct straight "end" edges at u≈0 and u≈2π−1e-9 connecting them — no edge referenced twice, no `IsSeam` flag anywhere.

Expected kernel behavior

Since no seam edge is present, the closed-face splitter must fall through to the geometric-closure test; because the surface itself is unconditionally U-closed for a cylinder and the face's actual U-trim range is within resolution tolerance of the surface's full period, the implementation must build a half-surface probe patch, confirm it is NOT closed (as a genuine half necessarily is not), and split — as opposed to either skipping the near-full face entirely (leaving it unsplit) or mis-treating the thin residual sliver as itself splittable.

Notes

Live-verified directly against the closed-face split healer: raw read is 1 face (a cylinder surface, U-closed and V-open, UV bounds `(0, 2π−1e-9, 0, 3)`); after running the healer, the result is 2 faces (rectangular-trimmed patches, U-halves at the midpoint) — confirming the near-full-period thin-sliver path triggers a genuine split via the half-surface closure test, distinct from Gs193's/Gs194's explicit-seam path and Gs195's basis-curve-closed path. Fills the `tkshh-face-closed-surface-unsplit-at-seam` "face trimmed to within tolerance of a full period (thin near-closed face)" subvariant. Synonyms: "near-full-period cylindrical face no explicit seam", "RectangularTrimmedSurface half-surface closure probe", "thin sliver trim below UResolution tolerance", "geometric closure detection without seam edge", "almost-closed face forces split not skip".
