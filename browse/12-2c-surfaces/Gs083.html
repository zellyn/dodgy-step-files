ShapeUpgrade_ConvertSurfaceToBezierBasis quasi-uniform vs non-uniform mismatch

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

Accept and flag: the B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS declares surface_form=.QUASI_UNIFORM_KNOTS. but its actual U knot vector (0.0, 0.3, 1.0) is not uniformly spaced, and its U multiplicities (3,2,2) sum to 7 against the 5 required for 2 U-direction control points at degree 2 -- both the form tag and the raw knot data disagree with the pole count. A kernel must derive surface behavior from the real, validated knot values, never from a form tag, and should reject a knot vector whose multiplicity sum does not match its own pole count.
