Description

A single `ADVANCED_FACE` on a `TOROIDAL_SURFACE` (major radius 6.0, minor radius 2.0) is a quarter-wedge in the MAJOR (U) angle direction (u:0→π/2) that is FULLY CLOSED in the MINOR (V, tube) direction. Its boundary loop is 4 edges: two full-circle tube cross-sections (at u=0 and u=π/2, each genuinely closed since V is periodic — these are the wedge's two open U-boundary edges, analogous to Tsh209's bottom/top full cylinder-rim circles) and, closing the loop at v=0≡2π, TWO SEPARATE, UNSHARED but geometrically coincident outer-equator arc edges (radius 8 = major+minor, u:0→π/2) — the V-periodic seam pair, built from two distinct `CIRCLE`+`TRIMMED_CURVE` entity chains (opposite-sense local `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` frames) rather than one shared `EDGE_CURVE`. This is the V-periodic (tube-direction) analogue of Tsh209's U-periodic (cylinder-axis-direction) seam pair.

Reproducer recipe

`TOROIDAL_SURFACE` (major 6.0, minor 2.0); two full `CIRCLE` edges (radius 2.0) at the u=0 and u=π/2 tube cross-sections (`UIso(0)`/`UIso(π/2)` of the torus, each a genuine closed loop, start==end vertex); two `TRIMMED_CURVE`-over-`CIRCLE` (radius 8.0, u:0→π/2) edges closing the v=0≡2π seam, each a SEPARATE entity chain (opposite local placement sense) sharing only their two endpoint vertices, not the edge itself.

Expected kernel behavior

A sewing pass over this single face's boundary must recognize that the two outer-equator arc edges are the SAME physical seam location (2D pcurve footprints on the periodic surface overlap across the V-closed direction, per `IsMergedClosed`'s footprint-overlap test) and merge them into one shared edge, rather than leaving both as independent free/floating boundary edges (which would misreport the wedge as having 4 free edges instead of the geometrically-correct 2).
