Expected kernel behavior

A `DIRECTION`'s ratios are not required to be normalised and their magnitude carries no meaning, so ratios of (1e-11, 0, 0) denote +X exactly as (1, 0, 0) do: normalise by the Euclidean norm and use the resulting unit vector as the axis. Reject only when the norm is zero — every ratio zero, which violates the schema's own rule and leaves the axis undefined — and guard the normalisation against underflow by scaling the components before squaring, so that denormal ratios cannot yield a zero or non-finite norm.
