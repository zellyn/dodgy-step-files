Gs025 — `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` C0 cusp / kink at interior knot of full multiplicity (C1 expected)

§12.2c surface-degeneracy

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2c-surfaces/Gs025.stp

Sources

  • R006, F047, F075, M006, L043, H054

Description

A `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` (or surface) whose interior knot multiplicity equals the curve / surface order produces only C0 continuity at the join; a visible cusp / kink on an otherwise smooth curve. Typical shape: a degree-3 B-spline with 7 control points that peak at y=2 and immediately drop to y=-2 with an interior knot of multiplicity 3. Downstream CAD/CAM/CAE tools assume at least C1; after format conversion, neighbouring faces show G0/G1/G2 discontinuities at shared edges that were smooth in the source.

Reproducer recipe

BSpline curve with internal knot of multiplicity equal to order producing a G0-only spot; or two adjacent faces emitted as `RATIONAL_B_SPLINE_SURFACE` patches whose tangent planes at the shared edge differ by more than the resolution tolerance.

Expected kernel behavior

split the curve / surface at the continuity drop, report the discontinuity as a quality warning, or refuse to claim a smoother continuity than the data supports.

Notes

**See also**: Gn012. Synonyms: "B-spline curve C0 cusp at interior knot full multiplicity", "BSpline curve has visible kink", "knot multiplicity equals order produces C0 join", "G0/G1/G2 discontinuity at shared edge", "rational B-spline patches show tangent break".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS(')
  • contains(b'(4,4,4)')
  • contains(b'(0.0,0.5,1.0)')
