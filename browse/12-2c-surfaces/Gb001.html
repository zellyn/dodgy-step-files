Description

A user requests a B-spline approximation of a non-rational curve (e.g., a polynomial of high degree) within tolerance ε. The approximation algorithm does not converge; the residual remains above ε after the maximum number of refinement iterations. Bug-reporter language: "approximation didn't converge", "B-spline fit failed", "tolerance too tight for fit".

Reproducer recipe
