Gs045 — `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` whose revolution axis crosses the basis curve at an interior point

§12.2c surface-degeneracy (sub-class: surface of revolution)

P1 — silent data loss

spec-violation geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2c-surfaces/Gs045.stp

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-42 §SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION

Description

A SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION sweeps a basis curve about an axis. If the axis crosses the basis curve at an interior point, one side of the curve is swept "inward" while the other is swept "outward", producing a self-intersecting surface that folds through itself near the axis. Distinct from existing entries about co-axial / parallel sweeps; this is the *crossing* pathological case.

Reproducer recipe

basis LINE from x=-2 to x=+2 along y=0,z=0; revolution axis = Y-axis at origin. The line crosses the axis at (0,0,0) interior to the basis parameter range.

Expected kernel behavior

detect axis-crosses-basis at construction; reject or split the basis at the crossing into two non-crossing sub-curves and revolve each separately.

Notes

**See also**: Gn015, Gn016. Synonyms: "SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION axis crosses basis curve at interior point", "basis curve crosses revolution axis", "self-intersecting surface from axis-crossing revolution", "revolution axis through interior of basis line", "surface folds through itself near axis".
