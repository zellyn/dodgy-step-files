Description

A single `ADVANCED_FACE` on a `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` sweeps a full `CIRCLE` profile (radius 1.5, offset 5.0 from the Z revolution axis — a torus-like elbow generatrix) through a 270° wedge (U trimmed from π/2 to 2π, NOT the full period). The face's outer bound is two structurally DISTINCT profile-circle edges (the u=π/2 copy and the u=2π≡0 copy of the swept curve) — genuinely different `EDGE_CURVE` entities, not the same edge referenced twice, so the seam-detection scan (identity-based, matching only a shared underlying edge instance) never flags either as a seam. V is completely untrimmed (the profile curve is a full circle, so its own parameter range already covers 0..2π with no boundary edge needed there).

Reproducer recipe

`SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` sweeping a `CIRCLE` (radius 1.5, center offset 5.0 from the Z axis, lying in the plane containing the revolution axis) through `AXIS1_PLACEMENT` about Z; `ADVANCED_FACE` bounded by two `FACE_BOUND`/`FACE_OUTER_BOUND` wires, each a single closed circle edge (the u=π/2 and u=2π profile copies), with no edge shared between them and no boundary curve at all in V.

Expected kernel behavior

Since no seam edge is present, the closed-face splitter must fall through to the geometric-closure test; because the face's own U trim (270°) is NOT within tolerance of the surface's full U period, the U-closure branch must correctly decline to split; because V is genuinely closed (basis curve reports closed) and untrimmed, the V-closure branch must split — U bounds must remain unchanged while V is divided.

Notes

Live-verified directly against the closed-face split healer: raw read is 1 face (a rectangular-trimmed patch wrapping the revolution surface, closed in both U and V, UV bounds `(π/2, 2π, 0, 2π)`); after running the healer, the result is 2 faces, BOTH still spanning the full `(π/2, 2π)` U range but split at V=π — confirming the split occurred exclusively along V, with U genuinely untouched (the intended V-closed-but-U-not-splitting fallback branch, distinct from Gs193's/Gs194's explicit-seam path). Note the packet spec that motivated this fixture described the missing subvariant as "V-closed surface (apex/pole direction), sphere/cone" — direct inspection of the elementary-surface V-closure queries for sphere and cone (both hardcoded to report V never closed, cited in Sources above) shows this is NOT achievable for elementary sphere/cone surfaces in OCCT: the general surface-closure query's per-type switch also hardcodes "never closed" for sphere/cone/cylinder/plane/extrusion surface types (cited in Sources). This fixture substitutes the mechanistically-correct, empirically-verified V-fallback carrier (a surface of revolution with a closed swept profile) for the sphere/cone framing, which is not realizable. Fills the `tkshh-face-closed-surface-unsplit-at-seam` "V-closed surface split via the V fallback" subvariant. Synonyms: "surface of revolution closed profile curve V-fallback split", "no explicit seam geometric V-closure detection", "partial wedge on closed revolution profile", "closure query returns closed for basis curve", "V-only split leaves U untouched".
