Gs007 — Pcurve U coordinate outside canonical [0, 2π) seam range on `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` (whole wire off by period)

§12.2c surface-degeneracy

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

Sources

  • F010, F072, F082, R082

Sender

ProSTEP UG ug_exhaust-A.stp #284920

Description

Pcurves of an entire wire on a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` (or other periodic surface) are shifted by an integer multiple of the surface period; for example a pcurve `LINE` whose start U coordinate is 12.566 (= 4π) on a radius-5 cylinder, more than 2π outside the canonical [0, 2π) seam range. The wire's UV image is correctly shaped but offset by 2π (or 4π) in U from where it should be. The whole contour is in the wrong period band; a re-alignment pass that does not produce a single coherent band leaves the wire un-meshable.

Reproducer recipe

Cylinder face; wire with edges whose pcurves trace U in [4π, 6π] instead of [0, 2π], with otherwise correct shape.

Expected kernel behavior

detect the period-shift and re-base the wire's pcurves into a canonical / contiguous band. The post-condition is that all wire endpoints land in one period; otherwise the fix has not converged and a downstream mesher will reject the band.

Notes

Synonyms: "pcurve U coordinate outside [0,2pi) seam range", "whole wire shifted by 4pi on cylinder", "wire pcurves in wrong period band", "pcurve U far outside canonical range", "cylinder pcurves all offset by full periods".
