Gs006 — Surface singularities (degenerate poles) not in declared form

§12.2c surface-degeneracy

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2c-surfaces/Gs006.stp

Sources

  • F070, W018, K010

Sender

PRO/E PRO9234, PRO18206, PRO7226

Description

Surface has degenerate row in UV grid (cone apex, sphere pole, collapsed BSpline row) but the file does not flag the degeneracy. Surface-surface intersection algorithms and pcurve projection misbehave near these. Tech Soft 3D fixed singularity-handling at intersections in HOOPS Exchange 2025.3.0 (W018).

Reproducer recipe

BSpline surface with one interior control row at a single point producing a parametric singularity; or sphere face whose outer loop touches both poles.

Expected kernel behavior

Accept; intersection/projection routines must heal-and-accept the singularity (insert degenerate edge, handle U/V wrap).

Notes

Synonyms: "surface singularity not declared", "degenerate pole on sphere/cone unflagged", "BSpline surface has collapsed row at pole", "intersection routine misbehaves at singular pole", "sphere outer loop touches both poles without degenerate edge".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
