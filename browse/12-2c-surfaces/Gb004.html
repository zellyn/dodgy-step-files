Gb004 — Pcurve and 3D curve disagree by a measurable distance (CheckCurveOnSurface)

Description

For an edge represented by both a 3D curve and a pcurve, the kernel computes the maximum point-to-curve distance between the 3D curve and the surface evaluation of the pcurve. This distance is non-trivial (not just numerical noise) and exceeds the edge's declared tolerance. Bug-reporter language: "pcurve and 3D curve disagree", "edge representation gap", "pcurve drift".

Reproducer recipe

An `EDGE_CURVE` with a `LINE` 3D curve and a `PCURVE` consisting of a `B_SPLINE_CURVE_2D` whose surface-evaluation traces a curve that diverges from the line by 0.01 mm at the midpoint.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: detect via sample-based check; bump tolerance to encompass the gap, or reproject to align.

Notes

Distinct from Gp-style entries (which are about pcurve construction); this is *measured deviation*. Synonyms: "pcurve and 3D curve disagree by measurable distance", "edge representation gap", "pcurve drift", "CheckCurveOnSurface reports max distance exceeds tolerance", "pcurve diverges from 3D curve mid-edge".
