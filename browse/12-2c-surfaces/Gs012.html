Gs012 — Non-simple `EDGE_LOOP` (face boundary self-crosses in UV: bow-tie quadrilateral)

§12.2c surface-degeneracy

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • W008, B022

Description

A face's outer or inner `EDGE_LOOP` has segments that cross each other in surface UV parameter space, even when 3D positions look fine — a bow-tie / self-crossing quadrilateral. Typical shape on a planar face: an `EDGE_LOOP` visiting four vertices where two diagonal `EDGE_CURVE`s ((0,0)→(10,10) and (10,0)→(0,10)) are interleaved with axial edges, producing a bow-tie in the face's UV plane. CADfix has a dedicated repair flow; failure in UV closure means the contour does not partition the plane (Jordan-curve violation).

Reproducer recipe

`FACE_OUTER_BOUND` with four `ORIENTED_EDGE`s whose 2D pcurves form a bow-tie in the face's UV plane.

Expected kernel behavior

reject — boundary cannot be unambiguously interpreted; healers must split the face or report. Do not assume orientation by ad-hoc winding.

Notes

**See also**: Gp026. Synonyms: "non-simple EDGE_LOOP self-crosses in UV", "bow-tie quadrilateral face boundary", "face wire crosses itself in parameter space", "Jordan-curve violation in face outer bound", "edge_loop forms bow-tie in UV plane".
