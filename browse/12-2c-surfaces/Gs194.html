Description

A single `ADVANCED_FACE` on a `TOROIDAL_SURFACE` wraps the full period in BOTH parametric directions: one explicit seam `EDGE_CURVE` closes the U (main-angle) direction (referenced forward and reversed from the `FACE_OUTER_BOUND`'s `EDGE_LOOP`), and a second explicit seam `EDGE_CURVE` closes the V (tube-angle) direction the same way; the two seams meet at a single shared vertex, so the entire boundary loop is exactly these two edges (four `ORIENTED_EDGE` references, two edges). This is Gs193's cone/single-seam pattern generalized to a surface that is closed in BOTH directions simultaneously — a torus, unlike a cone or cylinder, has no direction left over that can serve as an ordinary (non-periodic) bound.

Reproducer recipe

`TOROIDAL_SURFACE` (major radius 6.0, minor radius 2.0) with a single `ADVANCED_FACE`; `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` walks an `EDGE_LOOP` of `(seam_v forward, seam_u forward, seam_v reversed, seam_u reversed)` where `seam_v` is the v=0 equator circle (radius 8.0, spanning the full U period) and `seam_u` is the u=0 meridian circle (radius 2.0, spanning the full V period); both seam edges share the single vertex at their intersection `(8,0,0)`.

Expected kernel behavior

A correct closed-face splitter must detect the explicit seam, split along the anisotropic direction it identifies (U, from the seam pcurve's bounding-box gap), and then RECURSE on each resulting sub-face — since each sub-face is still closed in the untouched direction (V), leaving a still-closed result if only one split pass runs.

Notes

Live-verified directly against the closed-face split healer (bypassing the default STEP-reader pipeline, which does not auto-invoke this healer, matching Gs193/Gs049's established convention): raw read is 1 face (a torus surface, closed in both U and V, UV bounds `(0,2π,0,2π)`); after running the healer, the result is 4 faces, each a rectangular-trimmed patch covering one quadrant of the full U×V domain (U-halves × V-halves) — confirming the FIRST split (on the explicit seam) triggers a SECOND, recursive split on the resulting sub-faces. Fills the `tkshh-face-closed-surface-unsplit-at-seam` subvariant "torus/sphere face closed in both directions requiring recursive split (U then V)" / "surface closed in both U and V, requiring recursive splitting of the first split's results" — missing from Gs193 (cone, U-closed only, no recursion needed since V is bounded, not periodic). Synonyms: "torus face closed in both parametric directions", "doubly-periodic surface needs recursive closed-face split", "torus seam-pair single-vertex boundary loop", "recursive re-split after first split on a doubly-closed face", "full torus single face two seam edges".
