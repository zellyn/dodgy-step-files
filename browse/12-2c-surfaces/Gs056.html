Gs056 — `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` of an ellipse around its own centre produces a degenerate surface

§12.2c surface / curve degeneracies (sub-class: revolution of conic)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0027722
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP error for ellipse revol shape", "revolution of ellipse fails on import", "degenerate surface from ellipse-of-revolution". (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Sender

Modellers that emit `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` whose generatrix is an `ELLIPSE` and whose axis passes through the ellipse centre.

Description

A `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` references an `ELLIPSE` as its swept curve and a vertical axis that passes through the ellipse's centre. The resulting analytic surface is degenerate at both poles where the generatrix meets the axis (zero-radius circles). On import, OCCT cannot recover a usable parametric surface; the face appears empty, geometry is dropped silently, or a heal pass replaces the analytic with a high-cost B-spline approximation. Writers should refuse to emit this construction and instead emit two half-spheres / a torus depending on whether the axis is in-plane.

Reproducer recipe

`#1=ELLIPSE('',#2,1.0,0.5);` with `#2` an axis placement at the origin; `#10=SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION('',#11,#1);` with `#11` an axis through the ellipse centre. Importer should warn or normalize.

Expected kernel behavior

warn-and-proceed — detect axis-through-centre construction, emit `W_DEGENERATE_REVOLUTION_AXIS`, and either heal to canonical analytic (sphere / torus) or fall back to B-spline; never silently drop the face.

Notes

**See also**: Gs049, A083. Synonyms: "STEP error for ellipse revol shape", "ellipse revolved around its own centre", "SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION poles", "degenerate ellipse-of-revolution surface".
