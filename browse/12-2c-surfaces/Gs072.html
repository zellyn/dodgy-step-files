Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

Accept and report: the RECTANGULAR_TRIMMED_SURFACE's window is [1e-10, 1e-9] in both u and v -- a genuinely sub-nanometer parametric span on otherwise ordinary, non-degenerate surface geometry. A kernel should not silently clamp this to zero or to a default range; it should build the (vanishingly small but well-defined) face and report its true, extremely small extent, since the file's numeric window is exact and intentional.
