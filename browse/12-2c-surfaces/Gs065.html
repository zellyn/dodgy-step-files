Expected kernel behavior

Accept: the B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS sits alone in a GEOMETRIC_CURVE_SET with no ADVANCED_FACE, so there is no face-construction step to fail. Its U-direction control rows share the same X-coordinate, giving a near-vanishing metric determinant along part of the domain -- unusual but well-defined parameterization, not corruption. A kernel should load and represent the surface as declared; a near-degenerate metric only matters once a face or point-inversion query against this surface exists, and then it should trigger a fallback projection strategy rather than a hard failure.
