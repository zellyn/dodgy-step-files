Gs050 — Toroidal surface stored incorrectly: major / minor swapped

§12.2c surfaces

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

  • OCCT MANTIS#0032922

Description

A `TOROIDAL_SURFACE` is emitted with `major_radius` and `minor_radius` swapped (a producer convention from a legacy tool that stored them in opposite order). Reader interprets the values literally, producing a torus with reversed proportions; what should be a thin donut becomes an apple/lemon (minor > major, no central hole).

Reproducer recipe

`TOROIDAL_SURFACE('',#axis, 0.5, 5.0)` where the intended torus has `major_radius=5.0` and `minor_radius=0.5`. Producer emitted the values reversed.

Expected kernel behavior

detect inverted-radius case (minor ≥ major is the lemon/apple geometry); flag as suspicious for healers; reject if strict.

Notes

**See also**: Gs002. Synonyms: "toroidal surface major/minor radii swapped", "torus stored with reversed radius proportions", "thin donut becomes apple/lemon from swapped radii", "TOROIDAL_SURFACE radius order inverted", "producer wrote major and minor radius in wrong order".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'0.5,5.0')
