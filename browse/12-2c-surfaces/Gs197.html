Description

A single `ADVANCED_FACE` on a `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` whose swept curve is a degree-2 `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` with an interior knot at v=0.5 of multiplicity 2 (= degree, a legal, genuine C0 position-only kink — non-collinear control points around the knot, distinct from an illegal degree+1 "gap" multiplicity), revolved through u:0..π. The SURFACE's own `Continuity()` inherits C0 from the basis curve (revolution in U is always smooth; the discontinuity is purely a V-direction artifact of the swept profile). The continuity-split healer special-cases revolution and linear-extrusion surfaces: rather than splitting the SURFACE directly, it extracts the basis curve and delegates the continuity analysis (and resulting split values) to a dedicated 3D-curve continuity splitter on that 1-D curve, then maps the returned split parameter(s) back onto the surface's V axis.

Reproducer recipe

`SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` sweeping a degree-2 `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` (5 poles, knots `(0.0,0.5,1.0)`, multiplicities `(3,2,3)`, non-collinear poles around the v=0.5 knot) about the Z axis through u:0..π; face bounded by two profile-curve copies (u=0, u=π) and two circular arcs (v=0, v=1) closing the rectangle.

Expected kernel behavior

A receiver enforcing a C1 (or better) continuity floor on faces must detect that `Surface->Continuity()` is below the criterion, locate the offending parameter via the BASIS CURVE's own continuity analysis (not by independently re-analyzing the swept surface's isoparametric lines), and split the surface at that V value while leaving U (the revolution direction, unaffected by the curve defect) untouched.

Notes

Live-verified directly against a programmatically-constructed revolution surface and the continuity-split healer (matching the STEP file's exact control points/knots/multiplicities): curve continuity is C0, surface continuity is C0; running the healer with a C1 criterion returns `USplitValues=[0, π]` (UNCHANGED — no U split) and `VSplitValues=[0, 0.5, 1]` (split inserted exactly at the basis curve's C0 knot). Raw STEP read (no healer invoked) succeeds as 1 face with C0 continuity and UV bounds `(0, π, 0, 1)`, confirming the file is well-formed (unlike Gs198's illegal-multiplicity case below). Fills the `tkshh-surface-curve-continuity-below-required` "surface of revolution ... whose C0 BASIS CURVE forces the split" subvariant, distinct from Gs049 (a B-spline SURFACE's own C0 knot, no swept-curve indirection) and Gp033 (a standalone curve, not a surface's basis curve). Synonyms: "surface of revolution basis curve C0 knot", "swept profile discontinuity forces surface split", "continuity split healer delegates to basis curve for revolution/extrusion", "revolution surface continuity inherited from generatrix", "C0 kink in generatrix not surface itself".
