Gs193 — Fusion 360 → PTC Creo circular chamfer: single 360° `CONICAL_SURFACE` face with internal seam edge

Sources

  • PTC community post 27769 (https://community.ptc.com/t5/3D-Part-Assembly-Design/Circular-chamfer-import-faulty/td-p/27769) — MartinHanak's PTC community-expert diagnosis of the Fusion 360 → Creo circular chamfer import failure. B4 wave-8 DEF-EEE (LGPL-clean — pattern only, no upstream bytes copied). Autodesk Fusion 360's STEP exporter emits circular chamfers as a single 360° `ADVANCED_FACE` on a `CONICAL_SURFACE` with the seam represented as an internal `EDGE_CURVE` on the periodic boundary (referenced twice from the outer loop, once forward and once reversed). PTC Creo Parametric requires periodic surfaces to be split at 180° into two half-faces and cannot process the single-face encoding.

Sender

Autodesk Fusion 360 STEP export (any recent version) exporting a circular chamfer feature (a chamfer along the edge between a hole and a flat face).

Description

STEP file with a `SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` containing one `OPEN_SHELL` with a single `ADVANCED_FACE` whose `face_geometry` is a `CONICAL_SURFACE('gs193_chamfer',#axis,6.5,π/4)` (reference radius 6.5, half-angle 45°). The face covers the full 360° angular extent — its `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` walks an `EDGE_LOOP` with four `ORIENTED_EDGE` entries: (1) bottom circle (`EDGE_CURVE('gs193_bottom_ring',#v_seam_lo,#v_seam_lo,#lo_circ,.T.)`), forward; (2) seam edge (`EDGE_CURVE('gs193_seam_edge',#v_seam_lo,#v_seam_hi,#seam_sc,.T.)`), forward — the internal edge on the periodic boundary; (3) top circle (`EDGE_CURVE('gs193_top_ring',#v_seam_hi,#v_seam_hi,#hi_circ,.T.)`), reversed; (4) the same seam edge, reversed — the internal edge referenced a second time so the loop closes topologically on the periodic surface. This is topologically valid (the face wraps around the periodic seam via the internal edge referenced twice). Fusion 360 emits this encoding as its default for circular chamfers; PTC Creo Parametric drops the chamfer with `CONICAL_SURFACE not processed`; SolidWorks, FreeCAD, Rhino, and ZW3D accept the file correctly. Producer-consumer convention mismatch specific to periodic surfaces.

Reproducer recipe

`SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` / `OPEN_SHELL` with one `ADVANCED_FACE` on `CONICAL_SURFACE` (half-angle 45°, reference radius 6.5, 360° angular extent); `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` walks `EDGE_LOOP` with (bottom ring, seam forward, top ring reversed, seam reversed) — the seam `EDGE_CURVE` referenced twice as an internal edge on the periodic boundary.

Expected kernel behavior

CAD readers must handle periodic conical surfaces with internal seam edges by (a) accepting the single-face encoding and preserving the seam via the standard periodic-surface machinery, OR (b) automatically splitting the face at 180° into two half-faces during import with a diagnostic (`W_PERIODIC_SURFACE_SPLIT_ON_IMPORT`); silent-drop of the entire chamfer face without diagnostic (Creo's current behavior) is unacceptable.
