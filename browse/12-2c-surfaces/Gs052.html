Gs052 — Surface of revolution with offset basis curve breaks on export

§12.2c surfaces

P1 — silent data loss

round-trip geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2c-surfaces/Gs052.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0023771 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A surface of revolution whose basis curve is itself an `OFFSET_CURVE_3D` round-trips through STEP/IGES; the offset relationship is lost; the receiver sees a freshly-evaluated B-spline approximation of the offset curve rather than the original "offset by 0.1 of curve X" representation. Subsequent edits (changing the original X) no longer propagate.

Reproducer recipe

`SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` whose basis is `OFFSET_CURVE_3D` referencing a `B_SPLINE_CURVE`. Round-trip; offset is baked into a new B-spline.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: offset relationships are preserved through export when the receiver schema supports them; AP242 supports offset-based curves. Must not silently drop offset on export.

Notes

**See also**: Gs046, A083. Synonyms: "surface of revolution with offset basis curve breaks on export", "OFFSET_CURVE_3D relationship lost on STEP round-trip", "offset baked into B-spline approximation on export", "AP242 offset curve dropped in serialization", "edits to original X no longer propagate after round-trip".
