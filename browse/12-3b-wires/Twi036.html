Twi036 — Lacking edge: UV gap not closable by vertex tolerance

§12.3b wire-loop

Sender

CATIA01 #1727

Description

Two adjacent edges meet in 3D within tolerance but their pcurve endpoints in UV are separated by a parametric gap that no vertex tolerance can bridge; the wire is connected in 3D but missing a "lacking edge" in UV. The face's parametric outline cannot be closed without adding a synthetic edge to span the UV gap.

Reproducer recipe

Two `EDGE_CURVE`s sharing a vertex in 3D, but their `PCURVE` endpoints on the face surface differ in UV by Δu,Δv > 1e-3 with surface scale 1.

Expected kernel behavior

synthesise a short bridging edge (degenerate in 3D, finite in UV) to close the parametric outline, or reject the input as a topology-vs-parametrisation mismatch.

Notes

Synonyms: "wire has gap in UV but connected in 3D", "lacking edge in parameter space", "face boundary won't close in UV", "topology connected but parametrisation has hole", "parametric outline has gap kernel can't bridge".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

Tier-3 assertions

  • n_edges_total >= 2
  • face[0].surface_type == "cylinder"
  • n_vertices_total >= 4
  • face[0].quadric.radius == 1.0
  • edge[1].curve_type == "circle"
  • edge[1].analytic.radius == 1.0
  • edge[3].curve_type == "circle"
  • edge[3].analytic.radius == 1.0
