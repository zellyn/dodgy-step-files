Description

Seam edge in a periodic or wrapped wire starts at a position not matching the nominal wire start point. The healing operation detects parametric misalignment and adjusts the edge's parametric range without altering 3D geometry, ensuring consistency with wire traversal order.

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

heal: reparametrize seam edge to align with wire start

Notes

Specific to seam-edge handling in trimmed or toroidal surface contexts; involves parameter domain shifting
