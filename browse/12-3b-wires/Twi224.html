Description

Every LINE puts a direction entity in the slot that requires a vector, so the reader aborts before any 3D gap repair is reached

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

Before closing a gap by moving a curve's end, weigh the gap against the edges it would alter: the 2.5-unit separation between the edge ending at (1,0,0) and the edge starting at (3.5,0,0) exceeds the whole 1.0-unit length of the first edge. Refuse to extend an edge by more than its own length — insert a fresh bridging edge between the two free vertices instead, or report the wire as unrepairably open quoting the gap and both edge lengths. Getting that far means refusing to build a curve whose vector attribute holds a direction entity, as all three lines here do, and saying which entity failed rather than dereferencing a null curve.
