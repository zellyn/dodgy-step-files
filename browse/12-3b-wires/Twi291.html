Twi291 — Two adjacent wire edges' shared corner is encoded as two distinct `VERTEX_POINT`s coincident within tolerance (not bit-identical)

§12.3b wire-loop (sub-class: vertex merge, step-reader edge-loop translation)

P2 — spec deviation (no data loss)

Sources

  • OCCT `StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdgeLoop::Init` (`StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdgeLoop.cxx:369-415`) — adjacent oriented edges that don't share a vertex entity but whose translated points coincide within tolerance are rebound ("Adjacent edges do not have common vertex
  • set confused"), or left as an unresolved connectivity gap if not repairable

Description

Two distinct STEP vertex entities used within the same wire — the endpoints two adjacent edges are each supposed to share at their common corner — turn out to be geometrically coincident within tolerance even though STEP declared them as separate entities. This targets the STEP READER's own edge-loop translation logic (`StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdgeLoop::Init`), distinct from the general-purpose `ShapeFix_WireVertex`/`ShapeFix_EdgeConnect` healing utility (Twi284's mechanism) and from Twi017's single-edge closed-curve case (one `EDGE_CURVE`, bit-identical coordinates). Here a regular hexagon's corner between edge 3 and edge 4 is split across two `VERTEX_POINT`s offset by `1e-8` in each coordinate — well within the file's declared `1e-7` uncertainty, but genuinely distinct entities.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` on a `PLANE` (regular hexagon, radius 5); `EDGE_LOOP` of six edges where the corner between edge 2 and edge 3 uses `#v3b` (end of edge 2) at one coordinate and `#v4a` (start of edge 3) at a coordinate offset by `1e-8`, both within a global `UNCERTAINTY_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT` of `1e-7`.

Expected kernel behavior

detect the within-tolerance coincidence between adjacent edges' translated vertex points and rebind one vertex to the other so the wire stays topologically connected; if both vertices are already independently bound elsewhere so the rebind can't be done, flag the mismatch as an unresolved connectivity gap rather than silently repairing it.

Notes

**See also**: Twi017 (single-edge closed-curve, bit-identical coordinates — a different call site/mechanism); Twi284 (the general `ShapeFix_WireVertex` healing-utility mechanism, TKShHealing domain, built with bit-identical coordinates) — this fixture is deliberately distinct from both: different domain (`exchange/step-reader`, not `TKShHealing`), different geometry (hexagon, not square), and near-tolerance (not bit-identical) coordinates, matching Gp171's precedent for this exact "1e-8 apart, within declared tolerance" pattern cited in `occt-coverage/exchange/problems.json`. Verified live: the hexagon loads as a single clean face (`n_faces_total==1`, `n_edges_total==6`, `brepcheck.valid==True`) with all six edge lengths coming back at (or within floating-point noise of) `5.0` — confirming the near-coincident corner was successfully reconciled rather than leaving a visible gap or split. Synonyms: "adjacent edges near-coincident vertex within tolerance", "loop vertex merge below uncertainty threshold", "StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdgeLoop rebind", "hexagon corner split across two vertex entities", "set confused adjacent vertex rebind".
