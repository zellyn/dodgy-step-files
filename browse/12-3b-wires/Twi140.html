Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with a diagnostic naming entities #110 and #131: both LINEs put a DIRECTION where Part 21 requires a `#N` reference to a VECTOR, so neither of the wire's two straight edges can be built. Separately, the middle edge's B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS (#122) repeats its first pole and its last pole back-to-back (poles #102,#102,#120,#120), which collapses the derivative to zero at both ends of that degree-3 curve — a genuinely degenerate tail tangent that a kernel must also flag once the file parses at all, rather than silently accepting an undefined direction.
