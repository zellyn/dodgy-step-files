Twi091 — Inner wire on closed surface lacks natural bound: face read with no boundary

Description

A face on a closed surface (cylinder, sphere, torus) has a single inner wire (`FACE_INNER_BOUND`) representing a hole, but no outer bound — under STEP semantics for closed surfaces, the natural surface boundary plays the role of outer bound. STEP reader does not synthesise the natural bound and reads the face as having only an inner wire — making it ill-formed (no outer boundary at all) or interpreted as a tiny disk rather than the cylinder-minus-hole.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` on a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` with one `FACE_INNER_BOUND` (a circular wire on the cylinder representing a window cut-out) and no `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` element in the bounds list.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: when a face on a closed surface has no outer bound, normalize / coerce the entire surface (modulo seam handling) to the implicit outer bound; the inner wire then describes a hole through the natural face.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa038, Tsh001. Synonyms: "face on cylinder has hole but no outer bound", "natural surface bound not synthesized", "face read with no boundary at all", "inner wire on closed surface treated as disk", "cylinder face missing implicit outer boundary".
