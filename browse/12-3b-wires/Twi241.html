Description

Edges form a notch pattern at a junction vertex where multiple curves meet at shallow angles or with tangency that creates local concavity. The healing operation splits the offending curve at the notch point to eliminate the discontinuity and restore smooth wire traversal.

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

heal: split edge at notch point / reject: if notch detection fails

Related to surface/edge consistency; involves tangent-vector analysis and curve splitting
