Twi076 — EDGE_LOOP self-intersects (figure-eight): edges crisscross inside the wire (e.g., diagonal-like edges between rectangle corners)

§12.3b wires / loops / edges (sub-class: figure-eight)

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-3b-wires/Twi076.stp

Sources

  • OCCT `ShapeAnalysis_Wire::CheckLoop` (`ShapeAnalysis_Wire.hxx:512`)

Description

A vertex referenced by more than two oriented-edges in a single wire creates a figure-eight or pinched topology. The wire is technically traversable but cannot bound a face without splitting at the offending vertex.

Reproducer recipe

As Twi049 (self-intersecting figure-eight) but framed as a vertex over-visit problem.

Expected kernel behavior

identify "loop vertices" (those with degree > 2 in the wire's edge graph); split the wire at those vertices into multiple simple wires, or reject as malformed.

Notes

Related to Twi049 but vertex-centric rather than edge-intersection-centric. **OCC behavior**: the validity-checker does not flag a wire vertex with edge-graph degree > 2; tier-3 valid-flag should be ignored for this entry. The reader most likely silently fixes or ignores the figure-eight wire on import. Synonyms: "vertex visited more than twice in wire", "loop vertex with degree greater than two", "wire pinches at high-degree vertex", "pinched wire centered on vertex".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(13) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • n_edges_total >= 6
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[1].surface_type == "plane"
