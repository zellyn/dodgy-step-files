Twi087 — Wire is non-manifold: a vertex is incident to >2 edges of the wire

§12.3b (sub-class: non-manifold wire)

Description

A "wire" entity (linear chain of edges) has a vertex shared by three or more edges, branching the wire. The expected invariant — a manifold wire is a sequence of edges where each interior vertex is shared by exactly two edges — is violated. Bug-reporter language: "branching wire", "wire forks", "non-manifold wire vertex".

Reproducer recipe

A wire built from three edges all sharing a common endpoint vertex `#V`, forming a Y-shape rather than a path.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject the wire as non-manifold, split into manifold pieces, or accept and tag as branching.

Notes

Synonyms: "Y-junction wire", "wire branches", "wire has T-vertex".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
