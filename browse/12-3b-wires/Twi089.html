Twi089 — Two collinear edges meeting at a degree-2 vertex should be fused into one edge

§12.3b (sub-class: redundant vertex)

P1 — silent data loss

Sender

deduced — OCCT uncovered-class sweep

Description

Two edges with collinear (or co-curve) geometry share a vertex of degree 2, and that vertex is not topologically necessary (no other face uses it). The expected post-condition: fuse the two edges into one, removing the spurious vertex. Bug-reporter language: "extra vertex on edge", "edge unnecessarily split", "kernel didn't merge collinear edges".

Reproducer recipe

A wire of two `LINE`-edges (0,0,0)→(1,0,0) and (1,0,0)→(2,0,0) joined at `#V` (1,0,0), where `#V` is not used by any other wire/face.

Expected kernel behavior

Fuse the two edges; or leave as-is if downstream needs the split.

Notes

Synonyms: "spurious vertex", "non-essential vertex split".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
