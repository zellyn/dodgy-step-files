Twi290 — Non-closed curve whose two distinct vertices project to an identical parameter (null arc length) is discarded in favor of a straight-line fallback

Sources

  • OCCT `StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdge::MakeFromCurve3D` (`StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdge.cxx:459-481`) — non-closed-curve branch: builds a `Geom_Line` between the vertex points when `V1 != V2` ("this edge has null arc length"), otherwise nullifies the result ("NULL EDGE, SKIPPED", Twi018's branch)

Description

An edge's 3D curve cannot be validly trimmed between its two vertex parameters — a near-zero-length or otherwise malformed arc — even though the curve itself is not closed and the two declared vertices are genuinely distinct points. OCCT discards the STEP-specified curve for this edge and substitutes a straight line segment built directly between the two vertex points. This fixture's "right" edge of an ordinary square wire uses a `LINE` anchored at the edge's own midpoint `(10,5,0)` running perpendicular (+Z) to the square's plane; both corner vertices `(10,0,0)` and `(10,10,0)` are distinct 3D points, but each one's orthogonal projection onto this perpendicular line lands at the identical curve parameter `w=0`, so the trim collapses to null arc length on a genuinely open (non-periodic) curve. Twi018 covers the sibling `V1==V2` branch, where the edge is dropped entirely instead of substituted.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` on a `PLANE` (square); `EDGE_LOOP` of four edges — three ordinary `LINE`-based edges plus one `EDGE_CURVE('',#v1,#v2,#line,.T.)` where `#line` runs perpendicular to the square's plane through the edge's midpoint, and `#v1`/`#v2` are the square's own (distinct) corner vertices, both of which project orthogonally onto the same point on `#line`.

Expected kernel behavior

detect that the declared curve cannot be validly trimmed between the two vertex parameters (construction failure, e.g. OCCT's `BRepBuilderAPI_LineThroughIdenticPoints`); since `V1 != V2`, substitute a straight `Geom_Line` directly between the two vertex points rather than dropping the edge; or reject the edge as malformed.

Notes

**See also**: Twi018 (the sibling identical-vertices branch — edge dropped, not substituted). Verified live via two independent checks: (1) direct OCP construction — `BRepBuilderAPI_MakeEdge(line, v1, v2, w1=0.0, w2=0.0)` raises `BRepBuilderAPI_LineThroughIdenticPoints` for this exact vertex/curve configuration; (2) full STEP round-trip through this corpus's `occt_heal_on`/`occt_heal_off` oracle — the resulting edge's analytic curve comes back as a fresh `line` with direction `(0,1,0)` (a straight segment from `(10,0,0)` to `(10,10,0)`), NOT the declared perpendicular `(0,0,1)`-direction `LINE` — confirming the fallback substitution fired exactly as the catalog claim describes, and the shape stays a clean single face (`n_faces_total==1`, `brepcheck.valid==True`), unlike the periodic-curve "same parameter -> full period" outcome (Twi017/Twi019) that a `CIRCLE`-based attempt at this mechanism produced instead. Synonyms: "malformed arc trim falls back to straight line", "null arc length on non-closed curve", "distinct vertices same curve parameter", "line substituted for undeclared arc", "BRepBuilderAPI_LineThroughIdenticPoints fallback". Provenance tier: bytes-sufficient.
