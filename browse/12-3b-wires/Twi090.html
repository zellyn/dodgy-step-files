Twi090 — Cached EDGE_LOOP closed-flag not refreshed after one ORIENTED_EDGE replaced by two collinear halves at a midpoint vertex

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0030927 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A wire's `closed` flag was computed at construction. Subsequent healing replaces one of the wire's edges (e.g., to fix a SameRange flag). The new edge connects the same vertices as the old one, so the wire is still closed — but the flag is not recomputed and propagates `closed=false` to downstream consumers (face validity check, STEP exporter).

Reproducer recipe

A four-edge rectangular wire where one straight edge has been replaced by a polyline of two collinear edges joined at a vertex in the middle of the original edge. Wire is still closed; cached `closed_flag` says open.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: any edge insertion / replacement on a wire normalizes / invalidates cached topological flags (closed, simple, ordered); recompute on demand or eagerly.

Notes

**See also**: Twi053, Twi064. Synonyms: "wire closed flag not refreshed after edge replacement", "stale closed flag after healing", "wire fix updates topology but not flags", "closed wire reported as open downstream", "split edge keeps wire closed but flag says no".

Expected validation

  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • n_vertices_total >= 8
