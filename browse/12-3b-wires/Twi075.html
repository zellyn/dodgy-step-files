Twi075 — Connect-shape diagnostic: how does a foreign ORIENTED_EDGE attach to an EDGE_LOOP wire (e.g., open 3-edge wire plus a free edge near its tail with a sub-mm gap)?

Sources

  • OCCT `ShapeAnalysis_Wire::CheckShapeConnect` (`ShapeAnalysis_Wire.hxx:496`)

Description

Given an existing wire and a foreign edge or wire, the analyzer reports four possible connection outcomes: foreign-follows-wire-direct, foreign-follows-wire-reversed, foreign-precedes-wire-direct, foreign-precedes-wire-reversed. Plus the smallest gap distance and whether tail vs head attachment is closer.

Reproducer recipe

An open three-edge wire and a separate single edge whose endpoints could plausibly attach in any of four ways with different residual gaps.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: normalize / enumerate the four orientations and report distances; let the caller pick.

Notes

Combinatorial geometric search; used by interactive wire-grow tools. Synonyms: "how does foreign edge attach to existing wire", "four orientations check for edge attachment", "wire-grow connection diagnostic", "edge attaches to wire head or tail forward or reversed".
