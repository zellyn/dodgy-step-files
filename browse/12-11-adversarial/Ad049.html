Ad049 — Real literal lacks decimal point or has Fortran `D` exponent

  • 16-iso-spec.md N015, N016, N017, N018

Description

Per spec, REAL must have `.`; exponent is uppercase `E`; integers may not have leading `.`. Generators emit `1`, `.5`, `1e+18`, `1.0D5`, `+1.0`, `1_000.0`, `1,5` (locale comma), `0x10` (hex). Stepcode silently coerces INTEGER→REAL via `istringstream`, hiding unit-corruption bugs.

Reproducer recipe

`#1=IFCDIRECTION((1,0,0));`, `#1=IFCDIRECTION((1.0D5,0.,0.));`, `#1=IFCREAL(1_000.0);`.

Expected kernel behavior

Strict spec lex rejects with diagnostic; tolerant mode warns and accepts (coerces). Must not silently accept malformed reals.

Notes

**See also**: Ls001.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'1_000.0') or contains(b'1.5e+02') or contains(b'1.0D5') or matches(rb'\(\s*1\s*,\s*0\s*,')
  • matches(rb'1\.5e\+02|1\.0D5|1_000\.0|\(1,0,0\)')
