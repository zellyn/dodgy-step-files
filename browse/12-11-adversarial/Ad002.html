Ad002 — Stack overflow via deeply nested aggregate parentheses

Description

Recursive descent over `LIST/SET/BAG` aggregates without depth bound; thousands of nested `(((..)))`.

Reproducer recipe

`#1=FOO(((((…10⁴ levels…0…)))));`

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with diagnostic: enforce depth ceiling (e.g. 64); emit `E_AGGREGATE_TOO_DEEP`. Must not crash via stack overflow.

Notes

M; trivial DoS even before semantic phase. **See also**: Pf012.
