Ad102 — Crash transferring HLR-created shapes via STEP writer

§12.11 adversarial

P2 — spec deviation (no data loss)

adversarial

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0027070 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A shape produced by Hidden Line Removal (HLR) consists of 2D edges in 3D space (no faces, no surfaces). The STEP writer's topology-to-STEP translator expects every edge to belong to a face; segfaults on the bare-edge HLR result.

Reproducer recipe

Compound of `EDGE_CURVE`s with no incident face; pass to STEP writer.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: writer handles bare-edge / bare-vertex shapes; emit them as `WIRE_SHELL` representations with no face references. Must not crash on bare shapes.

Notes

**See also**: Tsh037.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'EDGE_CURVE') and contains(b'hlr')
  • contains(b'EDGE_CURVE') and contains(b'VERTEX_POINT')
