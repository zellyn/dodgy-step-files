Description

Naive `cat a.stp b.stp` yields two `ISO-10303-21;` openers, two `END-ISO-10303-21;` closers, overlapping `#NNN`. Stepcode `STEPfile::AppendExchangeFile` ID-renumbering misses references inside multi-dimensional aggregates inside complex records.

Reproducer recipe

`cat file1.stp file2.stp > merged.stp`.

Expected kernel behavior

reject second `ISO-10303-21;`; require ID-rewrite that visits every `#` token recursively through nested aggregates and complex-record components.

**See also**: Lh002, Lh022. **OCC behavior**: silently accepts (no diagnostic, empty result); outside catalog's allowed set ({reject}). Kernel-bug witnessed: receivers must reject this fixture per the catalog's stated invariant.
