Description

WHERE rules invoke EXPRESS functions transitively; attacker references entities such that rule evaluation loops through entity graph repeatedly. Even O(n²) evaluation with cycles is weaponisable.

Reproducer recipe

graph of 10⁴ `EDGE_CURVE` entities each referencing two `VERTEX_POINT`s in star topology that triggers WHERE-rule re-checks.

Expected kernel behavior

per-file rule-evaluation budget; abort with `E_RULE_BUDGET`.
