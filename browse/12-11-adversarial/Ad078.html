Ad078 — `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` child as PRODUCT_DEFINITION_SHAPE (mis-typed select)

§12.11 (sub-class: type-confusion)

P2 — spec deviation (no data loss)

adversarial

Sources

  • 08-occt-gitlog.md G016 (Mantis 0025167)
  • 03-occt-tests.md R049

Description

NAUO can refer to either `PRODUCT_DEFINITION` or `PRODUCT_DEFINITION_SHAPE`; schema-strict reader rejected the latter, while permissive reader cast across vtables.

Reproducer recipe

NAUO referencing PDS instead of PD.

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept PDS child; resolve PDS→PD silently with warn. Do not reject.

Notes

**See also**: Ad030.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • count_entity_def(b'NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE') >= 1 and contains(b'PRODUCT_DEFINITION_SHAPE')
  • contains(b'NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE')

Tier-3 assertions

  • load == "ok"
