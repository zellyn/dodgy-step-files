Ad120 — SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION on 3-point-arc profile crashes consumer on import

§12.11 adversarial (sub-class: revolution-near-axis crash)

crash adversarial

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-11-adversarial/Ad120.stp

Sources

  • Pattern-mined from FreeCAD/FreeCAD#16681 (LGPL-clean — pattern only, no bytes copied). User-reported reproducer: sketcher arc-by-3-points + 360° revolution produces a STEP that hangs FreeCAD on import.

Description

A SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION generated by revolving a 3-point-arc profile (encoded as a degree-2 B-spline approximation) around a near-perpendicular axis produces a near-degenerate revolved surface. One endpoint of the profile sits sub-epsilon close to the revolution axis (0.001 vs zero), creating a near-singular pin at the revolved-surface pole. Several receivers crash, hang, or produce invalid shape on import.

Reproducer recipe

B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS degree 2 with control points (0.001, 0, 0), (1, 0, 0.5), (2, 0, 0). Revolution axis: Y axis through (0, 0, 0). Wrap in ADVANCED_FACE + OPEN_SHELL + SBSM.

Expected kernel behavior

detect near-axis-degeneracy on the revolved profile; flag as pin singularity or snap-to-axis before continuing.

Notes

Synonyms: "revolved arc crashes STEP reader", "3-point-arc revolution hangs import", "SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION near-axis profile invalid", "FreeCAD revolution hang on round-trip", "arc by 3 points revolved STEP crash".
