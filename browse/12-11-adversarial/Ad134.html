Ad134 — `DIRECTION` with an empty `direction_ratios` list used as the axis of the transferred shape's `TOROIDAL_SURFACE`

§12.11 adversarial (sub-class: empty mandatory aggregate on a geometry entity reached by the transferred shape — null-deref crash during transfer)

P0 — receiver crash / hang / OOM

  • reproducibility-verified 2026-07-04).

Sender

STEP producers or hand-edited/machine-truncated files that emit a `DIRECTION` entity whose `direction_ratios` REAL-list is empty `()` while it is still referenced as the axis (or ref_direction) of an `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` that places a surface belonging to a transferred `ADVANCED_FACE`; also fuzzers and aggregate-truncation corruption on any producer path.

Description

STEP file with a single `ADVANCED_FACE` (#69) on a `TOROIDAL_SURFACE` (#5, major_radius 50.0, minor_radius 10.0). The surface is placed by an `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` (#4) whose axis direction is `#2=DIRECTION('',())` — a `DIRECTION` with an EMPTY `direction_ratios` list. Because the placement's surface IS the transferred face's `face_geometry`, OCCT reaches `#2` during `TransferRoots` and reads its direction-ratios aggregate; the aggregate has zero elements, so `StepGeom_Direction::NbDirectionRatios` / the coordinate access dereferences a null/empty backing array and the kernel dies with signal(11). The empty mandatory aggregate on an entity reached by the transferred shape is the crash trigger; a `DIRECTION('',())` that were NOT on the transfer path would be silently skipped.

Reproducer recipe

any OCCT-parseable STEP whose transferred shape reaches a `DIRECTION` entity (via `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D.axis` or `.ref_direction`, or a `VECTOR.orientation`) whose `direction_ratios` list is empty `()`; the entity must be on the transfer path of a root shape (e.g. the placement of the `ADVANCED_FACE`'s `face_geometry`), then run `TransferRoots`.

Expected kernel behavior

a `DIRECTION` with an empty (or wrong-arity) `direction_ratios` aggregate must be rejected or repaired (treated as a missing/default axis and diagnosed) — never dereferenced. The transfer must check `NbDirectionRatios() > 0` before accessing coordinates and produce one of {reject, heal-with-diagnostic}, never signal(11).
