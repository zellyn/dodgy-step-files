Ad046 — STEP writer crashes on `ADVANCED_FACE` `EDGE_CURVE` with no `SURFACE_CURVE` / pcurve plus empty `APPLIED_GROUP_ASSIGNMENT`

Description

Writer-side null-deref when a shape has missing handles or empty group attributes. Common trigger: an `EDGE_CURVE` used on an `ADVANCED_FACE` whose curve is a bare 3D `LINE` (no `SURFACE_CURVE` wrapper, no pcurve), combined with an `APPLIED_GROUP_ASSIGNMENT` whose `items` list is empty; both attributes the writer dereferences without a null check. Producer crashes producing zero-byte output.

Reproducer recipe

shape with missing 2D-parametric curve fed to STEP writer; or SolveSpace lathe-then-extrusion-difference part.

Expected kernel behavior

detect missing handle, fall back to recompute, or skip with warning; never crash.

A behavioral test against the writer is the appropriate venue. **OCC behavior**: silently accepts (no diagnostic, empty result); outside catalog's allowed set ({warn-and-proceed}).
