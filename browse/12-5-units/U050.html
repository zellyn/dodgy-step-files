Sources

  • VERDICT_AUDIT.md overturn #10, 2026-07-01/02): a corpus-wide sweep found ZERO shell-bearing fixtures anywhere in the corpus that declare a non-millimetre length unit, so `XSAlgo_AlgoContainer::PrepareForTransfer` (installs the target length unit before each transfer) and `STEPControl_ActorRead::Transfer`'s unit-scaling of both translated geometry and repair tolerances were structurally unexercised. The five previously-cited fixtures (U001/U002/U003/Wr044/Wr045) are all faceless `GEOMETRIC_CURVE_SET` scaffolds with `occt=empty/empty` — none reach a live `TopoDS_Shape`.

Description

A minimal 2-face `OPEN_SHELL` (same sewing-pair skeleton as N152/N153: two coplanar unit squares sharing one `PLANE`, free boundaries facing each other) whose `GEOMETRIC_REPRESENTATION_CONTEXT` declares its length unit as `CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT('INCH', LENGTH_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT(LENGTH_MEASURE(25.4), <mm-basis SI_UNIT>))` — a genuine, schema-legal inch unit on the SAME context that wires the shape (unlike A038, whose `.INCH.` unit sits on an unused `CONSTRUCTIVE_GEOMETRY_REPRESENTATION` side-context while the shape itself stays in mm). Coordinates are authored in inch-scale: Face A is a 1×1 unit square (y∈[0,1]); Face B is a 1×1 unit square at y∈[1.001,2.001] on the same `PLANE`, leaving a raw 0.001 gap between the two faces' facing free-boundary edges.

Reproducer recipe

`(CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT('INCH',#lmwu)LENGTH_UNIT()NAMED_UNIT(*))` where `#lmwu=LENGTH_MEASURE_WITH_UNIT(LENGTH_MEASURE(25.4),#mm)`, wired into the `GLOBAL_UNIT_ASSIGNED_CONTEXT` of the `GEOMETRIC_REPRESENTATION_CONTEXT` that backs a real `MANIFOLD_SURFACE_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION`/`OPEN_SHELL`/`ADVANCED_FACE` chain (not a `GEOMETRIC_CURVE_SET`).

Expected kernel behavior

`STEPControl_Reader` must scale every transferred coordinate by the declared 25.4 factor (1-inch-scale input becomes a 25.4mm-scale shape) AND scale the reading precision by the same factor before healing/tolerance checks run, per `XSAlgo_AlgoContainer::PrepareForTransfer`.

Notes

Provenance_tier: bytes-sufficient. Live-verified 2026-07-12 (OCP 7.8.1 `STEPControl_Reader`, local oracle harness — not mirrored/guessed): `Bnd_Box` on the transferred shape spans x=[0, 25.4mm], y=[0, 50.8254mm] — the reader genuinely applies the 25.4× factor to every coordinate (raw 1.0 → 25.4mm, raw 2.001 → 50.8254mm), confirming the unit-scaling mechanism runs on this fixture. The interesting repair interaction: the raw 0.001 gap, if a receiver forgot to rescale-by-unit before comparing against `Precision::Confusion` (~1e-7 in target-unit "mm"), would read as a trivially sub-tolerance, auto-mergeable gap; correctly scaled it is a real 0.0254mm crack between the two free boundaries — exactly the class of tolerance-scaling defect `PrepareForTransfer`/`IGESToBRep_Reader::TransferRoots` (precision multiplied by the file's unit factor before `ProcessShape`) exist to get right. `validate2` (2026-07-12, live, not mirrored) reports `occt_heal_on`/`occt_heal_off` both `shape(1)` with `shape_counts` vertex=16 edge=8 wire=2 face=2 shell=2 solid=0 compound=1 — the identical topology signature to N152/N153's 2-face sewing pair, now genuinely unit-scaled. `tier3_geometric.geometric_report` (live): shape_null=False, n_faces_total=2, n_edges_total=8, n_vertices_total=16. Structural linter reports `UNITS_INCONSISTENT` — this is the same false-positive class already present (and accepted) on U006 and A038's existing `CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT` usage: the mm-basis `NAMED_UNIT` feeding the conversion factor plus the `INCH` `NAMED_UNIT` itself both count as "reachable length units" under the linter's `>1-distinct-unit` heuristic even though only one (`INCH`) is actually assigned in `GLOBAL_UNIT_ASSIGNED_CONTEXT`; no `**Structural assertion**` line is registered here, matching U006/A038. Synonyms: "inch-declared STEP shell", "CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT on live geometry", "unit-scaled tolerance gap", "XSAlgo unit mismatch on shell-bearing fixture", "STEP inch unit real shape scaling".
