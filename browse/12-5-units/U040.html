U040 — Pressure unit `Pa` built from a derived-unit chain (`kg / (m * s^2)`)

§12.5 (sub-class: derived-unit composition)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-41 §DERIVED_UNIT + §DERIVED_UNIT_ELEMENT
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP pressure unit garbled", "Pa unit not recognised"

Sender

deduced — exporters that emit pressure as a fully-decomposed SI derived unit rather than a CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT('Pa',..) shorthand

Description

Pascal (Pa) is dimensionally `kg·m^(-1)·s^(-2)`. A producer that emits pressure as a `DERIVED_UNIT` composed of three `DERIVED_UNIT_ELEMENT` entries (mass at exponent +1, length at exponent -1, time at exponent -2) is spec-conforming but stresses receivers that pattern-match on a single named pressure unit. Defects: an element exponent is wrong (mass at exponent +2 instead of +1); the element ordering differs across files (some receivers' lookup is order-sensitive); the underlying base units mix SI prefixes inconsistently (mass in `kg`, length in `mm` — pressure becomes `kg·mm^(-1)·s^(-2)`, off by 1000× from `kg·m^(-1)·s^(-2)`). Bug-reporter language: "STEP pressure unit not recognised", "Pa from derived units", "validation property pressure 1000x wrong".

Reproducer recipe

`#1=DERIVED_UNIT_ELEMENT(#mass_unit,1.0)`, `#2=DERIVED_UNIT_ELEMENT(#length_unit,-1.0)`, `#3=DERIVED_UNIT_ELEMENT(#time_unit,-2.0)`, `#4=DERIVED_UNIT((#1,#2,#3))`. Variant fixture: length unit is millimetre (`MILLI METRE`), mass unit is kilogram, time unit is second — the composed unit is 1000× off from Pa.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: detect / normalize the dimensional signature of pressure (mass^1 length^-1 time^-2) and bind to a Pa context regardless of element order; coerce SI prefix mismatches deterministically; warn when the composition does not reduce to a recognised SI unit.

Notes

First entry exercising multi-element DERIVED_UNIT composition. **See also**: U021, U026. Synonyms: "STEP pressure Pa garbled", "Pa unit not recognised from derived chain", "DERIVED_UNIT pressure 1000x wrong", "validation pressure derived unit", "Pa from kg.m^-1.s^-2 not bound".
