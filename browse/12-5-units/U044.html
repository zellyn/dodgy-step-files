U044 — `gp_Trsf::SetDisplacement` between two `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` records produces wrong transform

§12.5 units & coordinate systems (sub-class: incorrect placement composition)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0027980
  • bug-reporter language: "gp_Trsf computes incorrect transformation between two gp_Ax3", "placement-to-placement transform is wrong", "STEP MAPPED_ITEM placement gives wrong location". (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Sender

STEP files with `MAPPED_ITEM` placements where the `mapping_origin` and the `mapping_target` differ in handedness (one is right-handed, the other left-handed).

Description

A `MAPPED_ITEM` requires composing `mapping_target / mapping_origin` to produce the placement transform. When one of the placements is left-handed (the cross product `x × y` does not point along `z`), `gp_Trsf::SetDisplacement` produces a transform that flips the orientation but leaves the geometry in the wrong half-space. The imported instance ends up mirrored or rotated 180° relative to the producer's intent.

Reproducer recipe

STEP file with `MAPPED_ITEM` whose `mapping_origin` is `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D(..)` with right-handed axes and `mapping_target` has axes such that `x × y ≠ z`. Imported instance is in the wrong location.

Expected kernel behavior

heal-and-accept; verify both axes orthogonality AND right-handedness when constructing transforms; emit `W_LEFT_HANDED_PLACEMENT` and either flip-and-record or reject. Never produce a silently wrong transform.

Notes

**See also**: U015, U016. Synonyms: "gp_Trsf incorrect transformation between two Ax3", "left-handed placement gives wrong transform", "STEP MAPPED_ITEM placement composition wrong", "AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D handedness ignored".
