U042 — Specific-heat-capacity unit `J/(kg·K)` built from a derived-unit chain (`m^2 / (s^2 * K)`)

§12.5 (sub-class: derived-unit composition)

P1 — silent data loss

units

Sources

  • 16-iso-spec ISO 10303-41 §DERIVED_UNIT + §THERMODYNAMIC_TEMPERATURE_UNIT
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP specific heat unit garbled"

Sender

deduced — thermal-simulation export pipelines that emit material thermal properties via DERIVED_UNIT chains

Description

Specific heat capacity J/(kg·K) is dimensionally `m^2·s^(-2)·K^(-1)` (the kg cancels in the underlying SI derivation J = kg·m^2·s^(-2), so J/(kg·K) reduces to m^2·s^(-2)·K^(-1)). A producer that emits the unit as a four-element DERIVED_UNIT (length^+2, time^-2, temperature^-1, optionally mass^0) confuses receivers that pattern-match on the kg-cancelled form vs the explicit J/kg/K form. Bug-reporter language: "STEP specific heat capacity not recognised", "thermal property unit garbled".

Reproducer recipe

DERIVED_UNIT with three DERIVED_UNIT_ELEMENT entries: metre^+2, second^-2, kelvin^-1; the underlying THERMODYNAMIC_TEMPERATURE_UNIT context for kelvin must be present.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: detect / normalize the dimensional signature of specific heat capacity and bind to a J/(kg·K) context; coerce / tolerate both the kg-cancelled and kg-explicit forms; warn on dimensional mismatch.

Notes

First entry exercising THERMODYNAMIC_TEMPERATURE_UNIT in a derived-unit chain. **See also**: U040, U041. Synonyms: "STEP specific heat unit garbled", "J/(kg.K) derived chain unrecognised", "thermal property unit garbled", "specific heat capacity unit dropped", "STEP material thermal unit not bound".
