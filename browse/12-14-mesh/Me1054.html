Description

an 8-triangle polygon soup with 4 independent duplicate pairs: group A={tri0,tri1} both (a0,a1,a2), group B={tri2,tri3} both (b0,b1,b2), group C={tri4,tri5} both (c0,c1,c2), group D={tri6,tri7} both (d0,d1,d2). Forces the outer while(!all_duplicate_polygons.empty()) loop to iterate 4 times (one per group). More groups than Me040's 3 to provide stronger iteration coverage.

Reproducer recipe

4 disjoint triangle shapes at x=0,2,4,6, each duplicated; no singletons.

Expected kernel behavior

outer loop iterates 4 times; 8 triangles reduced to 4.
