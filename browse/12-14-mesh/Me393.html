Description

Two triangles meeting at exactly one vertex v0=(0,0,0) with no shared edge. t0 is in the first quadrant (v1=(2,0,0), v2=(0,2,0)) and t1 is in the third quadrant (v3=(-2,0,0), v4=(0,-2,0)), so their interiors are completely disjoint. Branch 4 detects the shared vertex (hv[i]==gv[j]) and routes to segment-triangle tests; the result is no intersection.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0) shared; v1=(2,0,0), v2=(0,2,0); v3=(-2,0,0), v4=(0,-2,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v3,v4); assert_vertex_pair_no_shared_triangle(v1,v3); assert_triangles_do_not_intersect(0,1).
