Description

Vertex v0 is a triple-fan non-manifold pinch vertex. Fan A (t0,t1 sharing edge (v0,v2)), Fan B (t2 isolated at v0), Fan C (t3 isolated at v0). After the primary pinch merges Fan A with Fan B, v0 still has Fan C remaining non-manifold — Branch 7 fires to dispatch recursive pinch(e_1, true) at v0.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0) triple-fan; Fan A: t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3); Fan B: t2=(v0,v4,v5); Fan C: t3=(v0,v6,v7); assert vertex_fan_disconnected v0; assert t2,t3 not reachable from t0.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 7 fires; `if (e_1 != NULL) pinch(e_1, true)` called recursively to resolve remaining v1 non-manifold.
