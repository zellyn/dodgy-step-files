Me622 — removeRegion neighbor_t3_distance_check: seed t0 e3-neighbor t3 within L=2.5 of center (Branch 3)

Description

Four triangles where seed t0's e3 edge (v0,v1) is shared with neighbor t3 and t3's opposite vertex n3=(1,1,0) lies within radius L=2.5 of center C=(0,0,0). The e1 and e2 sides of t0 are boundary edges, isolating Branch 3. Branch 3 fires during BFS expansion for the e3 (third) adjacency.

Reproducer recipe

c=(0,0,0) center; t0=(c,v0,v1) seed; t3=(v0,n3,v1) e3-neighbor; t4,t5 context. Assert vertex_pair_distance_lt([3,2], 2.5); euler V=6, E=9, F=4, chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 3 fires; !VISITED(t3) and dist(n3,C)<=L; t3 added to removal list; e1 and e2 sides are boundary.
