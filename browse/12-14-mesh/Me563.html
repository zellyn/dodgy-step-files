Description

A 2×2 strip of 4 triangles (t0–t3). Interior shared edges (v1,v2), (v1,v4), (v3,v4) are n=2 — split using Loop-scheme weights with valence k. Perimeter edges (v0,v1), (v0,v2), (v2,v4), (v4,v5), (v3,v5), (v1,v3) are n=1 — split using simple midpoint. Both sub-branches of isOnBoundary() fire during subdivision. Euler: V=6, E=9, F=4, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,2,0), v1=(1,2,0), v2=(0,0,0), v3=(2,2,0), v4=(1,0,0), v5=(2,0,0); t0=(0,1,2), t1=(1,4,2), t2=(1,3,4), t3=(3,5,4); interior edges n=2; boundary edges n=1; euler V=6,E=9,F=4,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 4 fires twice per edge traversal — once with isOnBoundary()=true (midpoint used) for the 6 perimeter edges, once with false (Loop weights computed) for the 3 interior edges.
