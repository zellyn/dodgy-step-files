Description

Quadrilateral hole (v0..v3) plus one isolated Steiner vertex vs=(1.5,1.5,0) that was interior to the unlinked region. After unlinking, vs is unreferenced by any remaining triangle — it is the `vl` vertex list entry for TriangulateHole. Four frame triangles bound the hole; vs is confirmed as isolated_vertex.

Reproducer recipe

v0..v3 as hole quad; vs=(1.5,1.5,0) isolated; 4 frame triangles; assert isolated_vertex=vs; hole_boundary=[v0,v1,v2,v3]; boundary edges n=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 7 extracts `vl = ms->head()->next()->data`; vl is the list containing vs; passed to TriangulateHole as Steiner points.
