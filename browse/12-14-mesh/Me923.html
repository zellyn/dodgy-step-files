Description

Two disconnected components — a closed 4-triangle tetrahedron (component A, large) and a 2-triangle open strip at (15,15,0) (component B, small). With desired_num=1 and a live output_iterator (not emptyset_iterator), Branch 4 populates the iterator with component B's 2 face descriptors before removing them, enabling the caller to inspect what was discarded.

Expected kernel behavior

output_iterator != emptyset_iterator → Branch 4 fires; component B face descriptors written to iterator; remove_connected_components then removes them.
