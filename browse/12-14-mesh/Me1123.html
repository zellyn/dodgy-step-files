Description

triangles 0 (floor, z=0 plane) and 1 (wall, x=1 plane) share edge (v1,v2) at exactly 90° dihedral — a strong feature crease well above the strong_dihedral_angle threshold (≈150°). Triangle 2 is an intruder with apex v4 at z=-0.4, crossing the floor face (tri 0). Branch 9 fires because the repair region contains the sharp crease edge (v1,v2): constrain_sharp_edges=true locks (v1,v2) during smoothing to prevent the 90° corner from flattening. Outer edges of floor and wall are boundary (n=1).

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(1,1,0); floor t0=(v0,v1,v2); wall apex v3=(1,0.5,1); wall t1=(v1,v2,v3); crease (v1,v2) n=2; intruder t2=(v4,v5,v6) with v4=(0.4,0.4,-0.4), v5=(0.2,0.1,0.5), v6=(0.6,0.1,0.5).

Expected kernel behavior

detect SI between floor and intruder; identify crease edge (v1,v2) at 90° as a constraint; invoke constrained smoothing that preserves the right-angle corner.
