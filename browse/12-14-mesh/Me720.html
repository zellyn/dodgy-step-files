Description

Two coplanar triangles in the XY plane with no shared edges whose interiors overlap: large t0=(v0,v1,v2) with area=8.0, and smaller t1=(v3,v4,v5) with area=2.0 centred inside t0. After coordinate quantization, edges of t1 return true for overlaps() — Branch 1 fires as nos is incremented.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(4,0,0), v2=(2,4,0); v3=(1,1,0), v4=(3,1,0), v5=(2,3,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v3,v4,v5); all 6 edges n=1; triangles_self_intersect(t0,t1); triangle_area_lt(t1,3.0).

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 1 fires; nos is incremented for each edge of t1 that overlaps t0's interior.
