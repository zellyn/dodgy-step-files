Description

Two adjacent CW triangles sharing edge (v0,v1). After pass 1 flips both, pass 2 processes the seed (Branch 8) and finds the t1-slot neighbor also has bit 6 set (Branch 9); it unmarks t1 and enqueues it for further cleanup.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(0.5,1,0), v3=(0.5,-1,0); t0=(v0,v2,v1) CW, t1=(v0,v1,v3) CW; shared edge (v0,v1).

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 9 fires when t1 neighbor's bit 6 is set; t1 unmark and enqueue for cleanup.
