Description

Five triangles forming a connected fan from center C=(0,0,0) with all rim vertices within L=3.0. BFS enqueues them in FIFO order (t0→t4), but the tail-traversal loop processes them in reverse (t4→t0), triggering Branch 4 for each member after the first. The reverse-FIFO order ensures consistent unlink sequencing.

Reproducer recipe

5-triangle fan around c=(0,0,0); all rim vertices dist <= sqrt(5)~2.24 from C; L=3.0; all in removal region. Assert euler V=7, E=11, F=5, chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 4 fires for each triangle in the multi-member removal region; tail-traversal processes them in deterministic reverse-FIFO order.
