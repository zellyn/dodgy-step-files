Description

triangle 0 lies in the XY plane and triangle 1 lies in the XZ plane; the planes intersect along the X axis and both triangles span x=0..1, so their interiors cross. Common in CSG output and scan reconstructions.

Reproducer recipe

triangle 0 = `(0,0,0), (1,1,0), (1,-1,0)`; triangle 1 = `(0,0,0), (1,0,1), (1,0,-1)`.

Expected kernel behavior
