Description

A 5-triangle fan around central vertex v0, forming a pentagonal open disk. All 5 spoke edges (v0,vi) are interior (n=2, shared by consecutive fan triangles). The ring boundary edges (v1-v2, …, v5-v1) are n=1. Since all triangles are IS_VISITED and all spoke edges have t2 non-NULL, the MARK_VISIT branch fires for both new sub-triangles after every interior-edge split. Euler: V=6, E=10, F=5, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0.5,0.5,0) hub; v1–v5 form a pentagon; t0=(0,1,2), t1=(0,2,3), t2=(0,3,4), t3=(0,4,5), t4=(0,5,1); all (v0,vi) assert_edge_shared n=2; ring edges n=1; euler V=6,E=10,F=5,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 5 fires for each interior spoke-edge split; ls->e->t2 is non-NULL for all 5 spokes; MARK_VISIT(ls->t1) and MARK_VISIT(ls->t2) are both called → 10 new sub-triangles all marked visited.
