Me561 — loopSubdivision selection_constrained_vs_global: selected patch t0+t1+t2 vs unselected t3+t4; bridge edges n=2 (Branch 2)

Description

Five triangles split into a selected patch (t0,t1,t2 — IS_VISITED) and an unselected patch (t3,t4). Bridge edges (v1,v4) and (v3,v4) are interior (n=2) crossing the selection boundary; within each patch, shared edges are also n=2. Only the selected triangles' edge-visits fire the subdivision mark; unselected triangles' edges are skipped by the is_selection branch. Euler: V=6, E=10, F=5, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

v0–v5 placed in two-group layout; t0=(0,1,2), t1=(1,3,2), t2=(1,3,4) selected; t3=(3,4,5), t4=(1,4,5) unselected; assert_edge_shared(v3,v4,2), assert_edge_shared(v1,v4,2) as bridge edges; euler V=6,E=10,F=5,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 2 fires for t3 and t4 (is_selection=true, IS_VISITED=false → continue); only t0,t1,t2 edges are marked for subdivision; partial-subdivision result is produced.
