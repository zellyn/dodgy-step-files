Description

Vertex v2=(2,0,0) is DoubleFlat with two symmetric ridge neighbors v1 and v3 on either side of the axis. Both e1=(v1,v2) and e2=(v2,v3) are non-NULL; Branch 5 removes e1, Branch 6 removes e2, leaving only the axis edges (v0,v2) and (v2,v4) as collapse candidates.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,1,0), v2=(2,0,0), v3=(1,-1,0), v4=(4,0,0), v5=(3,1,0), v6=(3,-1,0). Six triangles in double-sided fan.

Expected kernel behavior

both e1 and e2 removed from candidate set.
