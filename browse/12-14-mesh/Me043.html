Me043 — duplicate_polygons treated_skip: shared polygon in two groups, skip on second pass

Description

4 identical triangles form one group. When the inner removal loop processes a polygon that was already swapped-to-end (marked treated) by a prior iteration, it hits the if(treated[polygon_to_remove_id]) continue guard and skips it without double-processing.

Reproducer recipe

4 copies of (0,1,2) plus clean triangle; treated[] bit-vector prevents any polygon being swapped twice.

Expected kernel behavior

treated[] guard fires for any polygon already in removal zone; continue skips it cleanly.
