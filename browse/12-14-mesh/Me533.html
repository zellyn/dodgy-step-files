Me533 — holeFilling::joinBoundaryLoops BOUNDARY_LOOP_LENGTH_ACCUMULATION: two-loop triangular annulus; perimeters tl1+tl2 accumulated before bridging (Branch 4)

Description

Triangular annulus (6 triangles) with two distinct closed boundary loops. Inner triangle hole (i0,i1,i2) — 3 boundary edges, short perimeter. Outer triangle (o0,o1,o2) — 3 boundary edges, longer perimeter. Six interior bridge edges (n=2). Branch 4 fires during perimeter measurement: the algorithm walks each boundary loop summing edge lengths (tl1 for inner, tl2 for outer) before bridging. Two hole_boundary assertions confirm the two-loop topology.

Reproducer recipe

i0=(0,0,0), i1=(1,0,0), i2=(0.5,0.87,0); o0=(-0.5,-0.87,0), o1=(1.5,-0.87,0), o2=(0.5,1.73,0); 6 annulus triangles; inner edges n=1; outer edges n=1; bridge edges n=2; assert_hole_boundary([i0,i1,i2]); assert_hole_boundary([o0,o2,o1]).

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 4 fires for each edge in both loops; tl1 accumulates inner perimeter (~2.7 units), tl2 accumulates outer perimeter (~5.2 units) before triangulation begins.
