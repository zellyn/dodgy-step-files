Description

Two coplanar triangles sharing edge (v0,v1), both with normals pointing +Z (same orientation: CGAL::POSITIVE). The smaller triangle's apex v3=(1.5,1,0) lies inside the larger triangle's interior. Branch 3 fires after detecting shared-edge coplanarity and same normal orientation — classifying this as an overlapping self-intersection. The shared edge (v0,v1) is incident on both triangles (n=2).
