Description

Three triangles (t0 flat in XY, t1 folded steeply downward, t2 extending t1). t0 normal=(0,0,+1); t1=(v2,v1,v3) normal dot-product with t0 ≈ −0.35 (normals anti-parallel → inconsistent winding). The shared edge (v1,v2) is the crease with a dihedral angle ~110° (far above the 60° IS_SHARPEDGE threshold). t1 and t2 share interior edge (v1,v3) n=2. Euler: V=5, E=7, F=3, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(2,0,0), v2=(1,2,0) flat; v3=(1,−0.5,−3) below plane; v4=(3,−0.5,−1.5) extension; t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v2,v1,v3), t2=(v1,v4,v3); assert_edge_shared(v1,v2,2); adjacent_triangles_inconsistent_winding(t0,t1); euler V=5,E=7,F=3,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 3 fires on edge (v1,v2); IS_SHARPEDGE returns true; detected_sharp is set to 1; subdivision proceeds but sharp-edge warning is logged.
