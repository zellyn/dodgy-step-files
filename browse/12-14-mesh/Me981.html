Me981 — merge_reversible_connected_components orientation_flip_feasibility: Patch-B wound CW vs Patch-A CCW; flip Patch-B enables stitch (Branch 2)

Description

Two disconnected flat quad patches (each two triangles). Patch A at z=0 is wound CCW (normal=+Z). Patch B at z=0.001 covers the same XY rectangle but wound CW (normal=-Z; inverted). Their boundary loops are geometrically coincident after flip. The orientation_flip_feasibility branch fires: flipping Patch B's winding makes its border halfedges align with Patch A's, enabling stitch_borders to merge them. Interior diagonal of each patch shared by 2 triangles. Euler: V=8, E=10, F=4, chi=2.

Reproducer recipe

a0-a3 at z=0 CCW; b0-b3 at z=0.001 CW (t2,t3 reversed winding); assert_triangle_not_reachable_from(2,0); assert_edge_shared(a0,a2,2); assert_edge_shared(b0,b2,2); assert_triangle_normal_z_negative(2); euler V=8,E=10,F=4,chi=2.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 2 fires; can_stitch_after_flip returns true because reversing Patch B's winding aligns its boundary halfedges with Patch A's boundary; reverse_face_orientations + stitch_borders merge the two components.
