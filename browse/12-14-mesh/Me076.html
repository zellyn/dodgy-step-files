Description

vertex 0 is a singular vertex. The upper fan is a **closed** 4-triangle ring (all edges at v0 shared by exactly 2 ring triangles; no boundary at v0 within the ring). The lower fan is a single isolated triangle. CW traversal of the closed ring returns to the starting polygon — Branch 8 (*wraparound_check*: `if (next==v_id) break`) fires to terminate the first CC. The isolated lower triangle then forms the second CC.

Reproducer recipe

closed ring t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3), t2=(v0,v3,v4), t3=(v0,v4,v1) with outer rim at radius 2; isolated t4=(v0,v5,v6) with v5,v6 at y=−4.

Expected kernel behavior

split v0 into 2 copies; ring retains one; isolated lower fan gets the duplicate.
