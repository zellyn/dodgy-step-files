Me534 — holeFilling::joinBoundaryLoops BALANCED_TRIANGULATION_CRITERION: two-loop square annulus; c1 vs c2 cost alternates between loops (Branch 5)

  • `MESH_HEAL_COVERAGE.md`.

Description

Square annulus (8 triangles) with two distinct closed boundary loops. Inner unit square (i0..i3) — 4 boundary edges. Outer 3x square (o0..o3) — 4 boundary edges. Eight interior bridge edges (n=2). Symmetric geometry: both loops have equal perimeter → cost comparison (c1 vs c2) alternates sides, exercising Branch 5 repeatedly. gv=i0 on inner loop, gw=o0 on outer loop.

Reproducer recipe

i0=(0,0,0), i1=(1,0,0), i2=(1,1,0), i3=(0,1,0); o0=(-1,-1,0), o1=(2,-1,0), o2=(2,2,0), o3=(-1,2,0); 8 annulus triangles (2 per side); inner+outer edges n=1; bridge edges n=2; assert_hole_boundary([i0,i1,i2,i3]); assert_hole_boundary([o0,o3,o2,o1]).

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 5 fires repeatedly in the triangulation loop; symmetric perimeters cause c1 and c2 to alternate; bridge triangles inserted from both loops until both are exhausted.
