Description

Two disconnected components: component A is a 3-triangle fan around apex v0 (t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3), t2=(v0,v3,v4)); component B is an isolated triangle (v5,v6,v7, face 3). BFS exercises the t2-slot adjacency when the middle fan triangle finds its t2 neighbor unvisited. Interior fan edges at (v0,v2) and (v0,v3) are each shared by 2 triangles.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0) apex, v1=(1,0,0), v2=(0.5,1,0), v3=(-0.5,1,0), v4=(-1,0,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3), t2=(v0,v3,v4); v5=(10,0,0), v6=(11,0,0), v7=(10.5,1,0); t3=(v5,v6,v7).

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 3 fires for t2-slot neighbor; component A (3 triangles) becomes biggest; component B (1 triangle) is removed.
