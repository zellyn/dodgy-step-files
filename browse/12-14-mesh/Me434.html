Description

Four triangles forming a closed surface (V=4, E=6, F=4, chi=2) with one non-orientable seam. Seam edge (v0,v1) is shared by t0=(v0,v1,v2) and t1=(v0,v1,v3) — BOTH list the edge as v0→v1 (same direction). In a correctly-oriented manifold, one should go v0→v1 and the other v1→v0. The sign product tmp1*tmp2 < 0 because the edge orientation is contradictory relative to the face normals: t0 has +z normal and t3=(v2,v0,v3) has −z normal (dot t0,t3 = −1). The propagator creates a new cut edge via newEdge(e->v2, e->v1) and increments wrn. All 6 edges are interior (n=2); no boundary.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(1,1,0), v3=(0,1,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v1,v3), t2=(v1,v2,v3), t3=(v2,v0,v3); assert_edge_shared(v0,v1,2); assert_adjacent_triangles_inconsistent_winding(0,3); all 6 edges n=2; euler V=4,E=6,F=4,chi=2.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 5 fires; newEdge(e->v2, e->v1) creates a cut along the seam; wrn is incremented (to be checked by Branch 7).
