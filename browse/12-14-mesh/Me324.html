Description

Three collinear vertices v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(2,0,0) forming a single degenerate zero-area triangle. Any triangulation candidate for this hole produces a zero-area triangle; Is_not_degenerate_triangle rejects all such candidates. Branch 5 fires when the Is_valid_compose functor is constructed with the base degenerate-triangle predicate.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(2,0,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2); all edges n=1; triangle_area_lt t0 < 1e-12; euler v=3 e=3 f=1 chi=1.
