Description

v0 has a 5-triangle fan; the split sector spans 2 triangles. The intermediate spoke (v0,v3) between e2=(v0,v2) and e3=(v0,v4) is redirected to v_new in one replaceVertex call. The pre-split spoke is interior (n=2).

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0) with v1..v5; t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3), t2=(v0,v3,v4), t3=(v0,v4,v5), t4=(v0,v5,v1).

Expected kernel behavior

call f->replaceVertex(this, v2) once for the intermediate edge (v0,v3).
