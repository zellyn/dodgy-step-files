Me404 — detect_identical_mergeable_vertices group_break: coincident b1==b3 at (1,0,0) bounded by distinct neighbors; inner while exits via else branch (Branch 5)

Description

Four triangles (two left-sector, two right-sector) with a hexagonal boundary cycle. One coincident pair b1 and b3 both at (1,0,0), each in a different triangle with no shared triangle. In the sorted boundary halfedge array, b1 and b3 are adjacent, surrounded by distinct-coordinate neighbors (b0 at (0,0,0) and b4 at (2,2,0)). After the group {b1,b3} is collected, the inner while checks the next halfedge target (different coordinate) → else branch fires, i is incremented, and while exits (Branch 5). Interior edges (b0,b5),(b2,b4),(b2,b5) n=2; six outer edges n=1. Euler: V=6, E=9, F=4, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

b0=(0,0,0), b1=(1,0,0), b2=(2,0,0), b3=(1,0,0) [=b1], b4=(2,2,0), b5=(0,2,0); t0=(b0,b1,b5), t1=(b2,b4,b5), t2=(b2,b3,b4), t3=(b0,b2,b5); interior edges n=2; outer edges n=1; vertex_pair_distance_lt(b1,b3,1e-9); vertex_pair_no_shared_triangle(b1,b3); euler V=6,E=9,F=4,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 5 fires once; inner while exits when next target differs; group remains at size 2 (b1,b3).
