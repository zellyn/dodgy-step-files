Description

A quad (two triangles sharing diagonal) with vertices v0–v3, plus two isolated vertices v4=(8,8,0) and v5=(9,9,0) appended at the end of the vertex list. After detection swaps v4 and v5 into the tail, Branch 6 erases the last 2 entries from the points container, leaving a clean 4-vertex soup.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(1,1,0), v3=(0,1,0), v4=(8,8,0), v5=(9,9,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3); isolated_vertex(4); isolated_vertex(5); diagonal edge [0,2] n=2.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 6 fires; points.erase removes 2 tail entries; remap follows for the 4 surviving vertices.
