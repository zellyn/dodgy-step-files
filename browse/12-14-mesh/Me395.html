Description

Two triangles with completely disjoint vertex sets that cross geometrically. t0 lies in the XY plane: (v0=(0,0,0), v1=(3,0,0), v2=(0,3,0)). t1 is near-vertical with vertices (v3=(1,1,-2), v4=(1,1,2), v5=(5,5,0)) — the edge (v3,v4) pierces t0's interior at (1,1,0). No shared vertices or edges. Branch 6 executes the full `do_intersect(th, tg)` geometric test and returns true.
