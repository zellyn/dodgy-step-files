Me522 — iterativeEdgeSwaps convergence_iteration: multi-pass swap loop (totits++ < 10) across 6-triangle grid (Branch 3)

Description

Seven-vertex, six-triangle 2×2 grid with apex. Interior edges (v1,v3) and (v2,v4) are sub-optimal diagonals (non-Delaunay); swapping them generates new candidate edges in adjacent quads, requiring a second pass. The outer while loop fires at least twice (totits > 1) before the queue drains, exercising the convergence_iteration branch. Four interior edges (n=2); six boundary edges (n=1). Euler: V=7, E=12, F=6, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(2,0,0), v3=(0,1,0), v4=(1,1,0), v5=(2,1,0), v6=(1,2,0); t0=(v0,v1,v3), t1=(v1,v4,v3), t2=(v1,v2,v4), t3=(v2,v5,v4), t4=(v3,v4,v6), t5=(v4,v5,v6); interior edges (v1,v3),(v2,v4),(v3,v4),(v4,v5) n=2; euler V=7,E=12,F=6,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 3 fires at least twice; `totits` increments each pass; swap of (v1,v3) re-queues adjacent edges; second pass needed before convergence.
