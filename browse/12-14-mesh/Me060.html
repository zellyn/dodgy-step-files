Description

a 6-vertex polygon has its first vertex (v0) repeated at position 3, forming a figure-8 pinch. When the unique_points.insert() call fails at position 3, pinch_detection fires. The polygon splits into two triangles (v0,v1,v2) and (v0,v3,v4). Both sub-triangles meet at the pinch vertex v0, whose fan is disconnected — the two triangles share no edge, only the hub vertex.

Reproducer recipe

5 vertices; hexagon index sequence (v0,v1,v2,v0,v3,v4); render as two triangles sharing only v0.

Expected kernel behavior

detect the failed insert at position 3; split into sub-polygon 1 (v0,v1,v2) and sub-polygon 2 (v0,v3,v4); push both to clean polygon list.
