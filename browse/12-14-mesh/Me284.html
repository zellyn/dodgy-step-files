Me284 — checkGeometry_coincident_edges: two spatially identical edges with different vertex indices (Branch 5)

§12.14 mesh defects (sub-class: checkGeometry / coincident-edges)

Sources

  • MeshFix `checkAndRepair::checkGeometry` Branch 5 (*COINCIDENT_EDGES*: adjacent sorted entries share same vertex-coordinate pair)
  • `MESH_HEAL_COVERAGE.md`.

Description

Triangle t0=(v0,v1,v2) and triangle t1=(v3,v4,v5) share the same geometric edge position: v0=(0,0,0)≡v3 and v1=(1,0,0)≡v4 by coordinate, but are distinct vertex indices. After lex-sorting all edges by coordinate, entries for (v0,v1) and (v3,v4) are adjacent with distance 0. checkGeometry marks edge v1 (index 4) as defective — two distinct half-edge objects map to the same geometric line segment.

Reproducer recipe

t0=(v0,v1,v2) upper triangle; t1=(v3,v4,v5) lower triangle with v3≡v0, v4≡v1 by coordinate but different indices; assert edge_pair_coincident and vertex pair distances.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 5 fires; defect logged as 'detected coincident edges'; mesh marks edge v1 as defective geometry.
