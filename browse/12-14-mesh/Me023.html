Description

triangles 0 and 1 cross at the center (XY and XZ planes). Triangles 2–5 form a surrounding belt sharing edges with the center pair. The center pair alone has non-disk topology; only after expanding by 2 topological steps (step > 0) does the selection become disk-fillable. This exercises the expand_face_selection loop that iteratively grows the face set.

Reproducer recipe

center crossing pair (no shared vertices except apex); 4 belt triangles each sharing one edge with a center triangle; no isolated vertices.

Expected kernel behavior

detect that the initial SI region is not disk-fillable; expand by step parameter; include belt triangles before hole-filling.
