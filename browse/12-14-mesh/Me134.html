Description

Vertex v2=(2,0,0) is collinear with v0 and v4 on the x-axis (DoubleFlat). Its fan has four triangles, two on each side. The ridge edge e1=(v1,v2) is identified at classification; Branch 5 removes e1 from the candidate set so it won't be chosen as the collapse edge.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,1,0), v2=(2,0,0), v3=(1,-1,0), v4=(4,0,0). t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3), t2=(v2,v1,v4), t3=(v2,v4,v3).

Expected kernel behavior

e1=(v1,v2) removed from candidate list; collapse continues with remaining edges.
