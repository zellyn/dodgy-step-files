Description

triangle 1 is a needle-sliver with base ~1.0 unit and altitude 0.001 units, giving aspect ratio (longest-edge × semi-perimeter / (4 × area)) ≈ 500. The face normal is numerically unstable; barycentric computations and normal interpolation will amplify floating-point error. CGAL `is_needle_triangle_face` flags it; `remove_almost_degenerate_faces` collapses it.

Reproducer recipe

clean control triangle `(0,0,0),(1,0,0),(0.5,1,0)`; needle triangle `(0,-1,0),(1,-1,0),(0.5,-0.999,0)` — base 1.0, altitude 0.001.

Expected kernel behavior

detect triangles with aspect ratio above threshold and either collapse the short edge or split the long one.
