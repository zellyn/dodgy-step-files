Description

An 8-triangle flat annular ring with outer boundary (v0-v3) and inner hole boundary (v4-v7). The degenerate edge (v4,v5) with v4≈v5 is on the inner hole boundary (n=1). The annular region has Euler chi=0 (two boundary loops). When the algorithm processes the marked region, the union-find detects index != nb_cc: the inner hole forms a closed cycle of border edges (nested hole), so Branch 10 skips the removal.

Reproducer recipe

outer square v0-v3 at radius 2, inner square v4=(−0.5,−0.5,0), v5=(−0.5+1e-10,−0.5,0), v6=(0.5,−0.5,0), v7=(0,0.5,0). 8 triangles forming ring. chi=0.

Expected kernel behavior

inner hole boundary forms a cycle (index != nb_cc); skip removal.
