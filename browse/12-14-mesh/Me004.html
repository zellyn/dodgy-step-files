Me004 — boundary hole: 3×3 patch with one triangle missing

§12.14 mesh defects (sub-class: topology/boundary)

topology

View raw fixture (.mesh.json) mesh-examples/12-14-mesh/Me004.mesh.json

Sources

  • `MESH_DEFECT_TAXONOMY.md` #11 (`boundary_hole`)
  • CGAL `triangulate_hole`
  • MeshFix `fillSmallBoundaries`.

Description

a 3×3 vertex grid that should be covered by 8 triangles (two per cell) has one triangle deliberately omitted, leaving a triangular hole. The boundary cycle of edges incident on only one face is the unfilled hole.

Reproducer recipe

9 vertices on the (i, j) integer lattice with i, j ∈ {0, 1, 2}; triangle list omits the upper triangle of cell (1, 1).

Expected kernel behavior

detect boundary cycles on what should be a closed patch and either fill or report.
