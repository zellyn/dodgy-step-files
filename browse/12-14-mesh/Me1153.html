Me1153 — is_degenerate_triangle_face collinear: zero-area triangle with all three vertices collinear (Branch 1)

Description

A single triangle (v0,v1,v2) with all three vertices on the x-axis: v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(2,0,0). The cross product (v1-v0)×(v2-v0) = (1,0,0)×(2,0,0) = (0,0,0); area is exactly zero. PMP.is_degenerate_triangle_face fires Branch 1 immediately. The middle vertex v1 lies exactly on the segment v0→v2.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(2,0,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2); assert_triangle_area_lt(t0,1e-9); assert_vertex_on_edge(v1,v0,v2).

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 1 fires; cross product is zero; function returns true (triangle is degenerate).
