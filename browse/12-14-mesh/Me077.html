Me077 — duplicate_singular_vertices: open-strip fan (CW+CCW traversal) plus isolated lower fan

  • `MESH_HEAL_COVERAGE.md`.

Description

vertex 0 is a singular vertex. The upper fan is an open strip of 3 triangles (boundary edges at both ends of the strip, interior shared edges in the middle). CW traversal from the starting triangle reaches one strip boundary but must then switch to CCW traversal (Branch 9: `next_ccw_vertex_around_target`) to visit the triangle on the other side. The lower fan is a separate isolated triangle.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0); strip: t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3), t2=(v0,v3,v4) with outer vertices at y=1; isolated: t3=(v0,v5,v6) at y=−1.

Expected kernel behavior

split v0 into 2 copies; strip group gets one; lower fan gets the duplicate.
