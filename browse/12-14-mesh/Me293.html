Me293 — connected_components visited_face_dedup_inner: diamond mesh where face f3 is discoverable from two BFS paths

Sources

  • CGAL `PMP.connected_components` Branch 4 @ line 234 (*Visited_vertex_dedup*)

Description

A diamond of 4 triangles (f0=top-left, f1=top-right, f2=bottom-left, f3=bottom-right) sharing central vertex v4. BFS from f0 discovers f1 and f2. Both f1 and f2 discover f3 — it is pushed to the BFS queue twice. Branch 4's `!handled[fq_id]` guard fires on the second encounter and skips re-labelling. An isolated triangle forms a second CC.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(1,2,0), v1=(0,0,0), v2=(2,0,0), v3=(1,-2,0), v4=(1,0,0). f0=(v0,v1,v4), f1=(v0,v4,v2), f2=(v1,v3,v4), f3=(v4,v3,v2). Isolated triangle at x=10.

Expected kernel behavior

f3 pushed twice to BFS queue; Branch 4 fires on second encounter and skips re-labelling.
