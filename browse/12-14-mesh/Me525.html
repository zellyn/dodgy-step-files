Me525 — iterativeEdgeSwaps convergence_fail: 4-triangle symmetric fan oscillates swap queue; totits reaches 10 (Branch 6)

Description

A unit square with a center vertex v4=(1,1,0) connected to all four corners, forming four symmetric triangles. All four spoke edges (v0,v4),(v1,v4),(v2,v4),(v3,v4) are interior (n=2). The configuration is exactly Delaunay-neutral: swapping any spoke replaces it with a cross-diagonal that immediately re-creates a non-Delaunay condition in the adjacent quad. The swap queue oscillates: it never empties, so `totits` reaches 10 and Branch 6 fires with a warning. All four outer boundary edges are n=1. Euler: V=5, E=8, F=4, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(2,0,0), v2=(2,2,0), v3=(0,2,0), v4=(1,1,0); t0=(v0,v1,v4), t1=(v1,v2,v4), t2=(v2,v3,v4), t3=(v3,v0,v4); all spoke edges n=2; outer edges n=1; euler V=5,E=8,F=4,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 6 fires; `totits` increments to 10 before the swap queue drains; `TMesh::warning` issued; function returns totits (10) rather than 0.
