Description

Three fully isolated single-triangle components (A at origin, B at x=50, C at x=100), sharing no edges or vertices. The outer for-loop in PMP.connected_components must iterate over all faces to seed a new BFS from each unhandled component. All three disconnected triangles force Branch 1 to fire three times.

Reproducer recipe

t0=(v0,v1,v2) at origin; t1=(v3,v4,v5) at x=50; t2=(v6,v7,v8) at x=100. No shared edges.

Expected kernel behavior

outer for-loop seeds 3 BFS traversals; assigns CC labels 0, 1, 2 to t0, t1, t2 respectively.
