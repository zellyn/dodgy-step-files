Description

vertex 0 is a pinch-neck bowtie hub shared by two disconnected triangle fans. Fan A (triangles 0,1) lies in the Z=0 plane. Fan B (triangles 2,3) lies in the Y=0 plane; triangle 3 dips below Z=0 and self-intersects triangle 0 from fan A. The hub's vertex fan is disconnected — the non-manifold precondition that preserve_genus must evaluate before SI removal can proceed.

Reproducer recipe

8 vertices; hub at origin; fan A upper-left (hub,fa0,fa1) and lower-left (hub,fa1,fa2) in Z=0; fan B right (hub,fb0,fb1) in Y=0 and crossing (hub,fb2,fb3) cutting into Z<0; assert_vertex_fan_disconnected(0); assert_triangles_self_intersect(0,3).

Expected kernel behavior

detect disconnected vertex fan at hub; evaluate preserve_genus; when false, duplicate hub before hole-filling; when true, route to complex-topology handler.
