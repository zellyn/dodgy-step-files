Me690 — connected_component loop_control: 5-triangle zigzag strip forces BFS while-loop to cycle 5 times

§12.14 mesh defects (sub-class: disconnected_components / connected-component-BFS)

topology

Sources

  • CGAL `PMP.connected_component` Branch 1 @ line 140 (*Loop_control*)
  • `MESH_HEAL_COVERAGE.md`.

Description

A zigzag strip of 5 triangles (t0-t4) where each adjacent pair shares one interior edge. BFS seeded at t0 must iterate 5 times through the `while (!face_queue.empty())` loop before the queue drains. An isolated triangle (t5) forms a second CC. Interior edges: (b1,p0) shared by t0+t1, (b1,p1) by t1+t2, (b2,p1) by t2+t3, (b2,p2) by t3+t4.

Reproducer recipe

b0=(0,0,0), b1=(1,0,0), b2=(2,0,0), b3=(3,0,0); p0=(0.5,1,0), p1=(1.5,1,0), p2=(2.5,1,0). t0=(b0,b1,p0); t1=(b1,p1,p0); t2=(b1,b2,p1); t3=(b2,p2,p1); t4=(b2,b3,p2). iso=(q0,q1,q2) at x=20.

Expected kernel behavior

BFS while-loop runs 5 iterations for the strip component; outer BFS seeds iso as a second CC.
