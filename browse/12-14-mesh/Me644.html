Description

A mesh with a quadrilateral hole (4-edge boundary). TriangulateHole fills it with four triangles and a Steiner point sc=(1.5,1.5,0) at the patch centroid. With refine_patches=true, Branch 5 fires and calls refineSelectedHolePatches to apply Laplacian-style smoothing and edge splits on the new patch. All edges from sc are interior (n=2).

Reproducer recipe

4 frame triangles surrounding quadrilateral hole (h0-h3); fill triangles tp0-tp3 from Steiner point sc; all edges from sc n=2; frame outer edges n=1; euler V=9, E=16, F=8, chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 5 fires; TriangulateHole succeeds and refine_patches=true; refineSelectedHolePatches called on the 4-triangle patch.
