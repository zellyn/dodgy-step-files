Description

Five triangles forming a connected hole-fill patch (4-triangle fan around hub v0 plus one extra outer triangle). t0=(v0,v1,v2) is the BFS seed (pre-selected/IS_VISITED). BFS propagates via 5 interior edges (hub spokes plus one extra) to reach all 5 region triangles. Interior edges (n=2): (v0,v1),(v0,v2),(v0,v3),(v0,v4),(v1,v4). Outer boundary edges (n=1): (v1,v2),(v2,v3),(v3,v4),(v1,v5),(v5,v4). Euler: V=6, E=10, F=5, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(1,1,0) hub; v1=(0,1,0), v2=(1,2,0), v3=(2,1,0), v4=(1,0,0), v5=(0,0,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3), t2=(v0,v3,v4), t3=(v0,v4,v1), t4=(v1,v5,v4); assert interior edges n=2; boundary edges n=1; euler V=6,E=10,F=5,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 1 fires on t0 (IS_VISITED); t0 unmarked and used as BFS seed; BFS expands via interior edges to collect all 5 region triangles into the refinement patch.
