Description

A 3-triangle fan around a center vertex v0. BFS traversal finds unvisited e2-neighbors by traversing the interior edges (v0,v2) and (v0,v3), each shared by 2 triangles. Five boundary edges form the open fan perimeter.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(0.5,1,0), v3=(-0.5,1,0), v4=(-1,0,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v0,v2,v3), t2=(v0,v3,v4).

Expected kernel behavior

BFS from t0 pushes t1 via shared interior edge (v0,v2); BFS from t1 pushes t2 via (v0,v3).
