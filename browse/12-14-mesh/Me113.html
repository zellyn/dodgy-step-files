Description

t0 occupies x ∈ [3,5] and t1 occupies x ∈ [0,1]. All three vertices of t1 have x-coordinate strictly less than t0's x-minimum (mx=3). MeshFix exits immediately with false before any plane-orientation or segment tests. Models where imported meshes have spatially disjoint parts must not have SI falsely reported.

Reproducer recipe

t0: (3,0,0),(5,0,0),(4,2,0). t1: (0,0,0),(1,0,0),(0.5,2,0). x-gap of 2 units.
