Me431 — forceNormalConsistence t2-inconsistent: adjacent triangle flipped across shared edge e2; antiparallel normal → t2->invert() (Branch 2)

Description

Three triangles. t0=(v0,v1,v2) is the reference (+z normal). t1=(v1,v0,v3) shares edge (v0,v1) in the correct opposite sense (v1→v0) → consistent +z normal. t2=(v1,v2,v4) shares edge (v1,v2) in the SAME sense as t0 (both v1→v2), giving t2 a −z normal — ADJACENT_NORMAL_T2_INCONSISTENT. The inconsistency is on the SECOND edge checked (e2), triggering Branch 2. V=5, two interior edges n=2, five boundary edges n=1.

Reproducer recipe

v0=(0,0,0), v1=(1,0,0), v2=(0,1,0), v3=(1,-1,0), v4=(0.5,2,0); t0=(v0,v1,v2), t1=(v1,v0,v3), t2=(v1,v2,v4); assert_edge_shared(v0,v1,2); assert_edge_shared(v1,v2,2); assert_adjacent_triangles_inconsistent_winding(0,2); boundary edges n=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 2 fires; t2->invert() reverses t2's winding to align its normal with t0's +z orientation.
