Me403 — detect_identical_mergeable_vertices consecutive_identical_collection: q0==q1==q2 at (1,0,0); inner while extends group to size 3 (Branch 4)

Description

Five triangles (three cell triangles + two bridge triangles) forming a single open disk with boundary cycle visiting p0,q0,p1,q1,p2,q2,p3. Three coincident boundary vertices q0,q1,q2 (indices 4,5,6) all at (1,0,0); no two share a triangle. After sort, halfedges targeting q0,q1,q2 are consecutive; Branch 3 opens group {q0,q1} and Branch 4's inner while fires once to push q2 (group size → 3). Interior edges (p0,p1),(p1,p2),(p0,p2),(p2,p3) n=2; seven outer edges n=1. Euler: V=7, E=11, F=5, chi=1.

Reproducer recipe

p0=(0,1,0),p1=(2,1,0),p2=(4,1,0),p3=(6,1,0); q0=q1=q2=(1,0,0) distinct indices; t0=(p0,q0,p1), t1=(p1,q1,p2), t2=(p2,q2,p3), t3=(p0,p1,p2), t4=(p0,p2,p3); no two q's share a triangle; euler V=7,E=11,F=5,chi=1.

Expected kernel behavior

Branch 4 fires once (extending the group from size 2 to 3); inner while exits when the next halfedge target differs from q2's coordinate.
