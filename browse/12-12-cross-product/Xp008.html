Xp008 — Empty EDGE_LOOP × empty FACE_OUTER_BOUND × otherwise-valid solid

§12.12 cross-product (wires × faces × shells)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • synthesized cross-product.

Description

A `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` containing 7 faces of a cube. One face's `EDGE_LOOP` has an empty edge aggregate; another face's `face_geometry` slot is `$`. The remaining 5 faces are valid. A robust kernel must skip both malformed faces and decide whether the result (an open shell) can be repaired into a solid.

Reproducer recipe

6 cube faces but #5 has `EDGE_LOOP('',())` and #6 has `face_geometry = $`.

Expected kernel behavior

each malformed sub-shape rejected/skipped independently; the kernel decides whether to surface the resulting open shell or attempt repair.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(26) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

solid — authored as a closed solid; openness is the defect — heal it shut.

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'FILE_SCHEMA')
  • contains(b'EDGE_CURVE')
  • contains(b'ADVANCED_FACE')

Tier-3 assertions

  • load == "ok"
