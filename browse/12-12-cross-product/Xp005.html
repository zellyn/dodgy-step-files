Description

A `RATIONAL_B_SPLINE_CURVE` has a knot vector with two consecutive equal knots not justified by multiplicity; one of its weights is zero (rendering the rational evaluation indeterminate at that pole); and the control polygon has a cusp where two consecutive control points are separated by ~1e-7; exactly at the OCCT default `Precision:: Confusion()` boundary. Three independent NURBS-quality invariants are violated.

Reproducer recipe

Cubic `(BOUNDED_CURVE() B_SPLINE_CURVE(..) RATIONAL_B_SPLINE_CURVE(..) ..)` with five control points, knot vector `(0,0,0,0,0.5,0.5,1,1,1,1)` (the inner pair is duplicated without justification), weights `(1.0,0.0,1.0,1.0,1.0)`, and CPs `(0,0,0)`, `(1e-7,0,0)`, `(1,0,0)`, `(2,1,0)`, `(3,0,0)`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or reject with E_NURBS_INVARIANT diagnostic: each NURBS invariant is inspected independently; the cusp-at-tolerance-boundary case is decided by the kernel's working precision policy.

Expected validation
