Xp002 — Periodic-surface seam gap × pcurve missing × unit-context mismatch

Description

A face on a closed `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` is bounded by a wire whose 3D endpoints meet but whose 2D pcurves are missing entirely (`SURFACE_CURVE.associated_geometry` carries no `PCURVE` entry). The containing `GEOMETRIC_REPRESENTATION_CONTEXT` declares `SI_UNIT(.MILLI., .METRE.)` while coordinate magnitudes are 25.4× too large (i.e., authored in inches but tagged as mm). Three independent invariants are violated and three healing strategies (synthesize pcurve, close UV seam, scale consistently with declared unit) interact.

Reproducer recipe

`CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` of radius 1.0 on Z-axis, bounded by an `EDGE_LOOP` of two `EDGE_CURVE`s whose 3D endpoints share vertices but whose `SURFACE_CURVE` has empty `associated_geometry`. `GEOMETRIC_REPRESENTATION_CONTEXT` units = mm; coordinates suggest inch authorship (radius emitted as 25.4).

Expected kernel behavior

Each invariant addressed independently. A kernel may synthesize pcurves, close the UV seam, and elect a unit-policy (honor declaration, infer from magnitudes, or warn).
