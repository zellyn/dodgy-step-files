Xp004 — PMI annotation × tessellation-vs-BRep mix in a single AP242 file

Description

An AP242 file declares one `tessellated_shape_representation` whose items list mixes a `TESSELLATED_FACE` and an `ADVANCED_FACE` (Ed.1 forbids the latter). A PMI `dimensional_size` is attached via a `geometric_item_specific_usage` to the BRep face, while the saved-view `draughting_model.items` list is missing the required `mapped_item`. The PMI association traverses both geometry kinds and depends on a saved-view linkage that is broken.

Reproducer recipe

One `ADVANCED_FACE` on a planar surface and one `TRIANGULATED_FACE` referencing a `COORDINATES_LIST`, both placed in a single `tessellated_shape_representation`. A PMI `dimensional_size` (a diameter) cites a `shape_aspect` referencing the BRep face. A `draughting_model` exists with `items` list lacking the required `mapped_item`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or warn and accept: each defect addressed independently; the representation-items invariant is checked, the PMI-association is resolved (or flagged with a warning), the saved-view linkage is repaired or warned about.
