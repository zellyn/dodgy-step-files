Xp029 — Onshape STEP/SLDPRT export drops cosmetic thread metadata

§12.12 cross-product (PMI/feature × writer metadata)

interop

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-12-cross-product/Xp029.stp

Sources

  • forum.onshape.com discussion 3489 "Export to STEP or SLDPRT, thread/hole characteristics wrong"
  • discussion 6463 "STL export - tapped hole threads not showing up in 3d print slicer"
  • bug-reporter language: "tapped holes export as smooth cylinders", "thread information lost when I send the STEP to my supplier".

Sender

Onshape STEP/SLDPRT writer

Description

A hole modelled in Onshape with a cosmetic thread (e.g. M3×0.5) exports as a plain CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE at the tap-drill diameter, with no helical curve, no thread_specification entity, and at most a free-form PROPERTY_DEFINITION carrying a string label. Receivers see a smooth hole; downstream BOM and fabrication processes lose the thread spec. AP242 supports thread metadata (thread_specification ARM) but Onshape's writer does not emit it.

Reproducer recipe

CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE radius 1.25 mm (M3 tap-drill); SHAPE_ASPECT named 'M3x0p5_thread'; PROPERTY_DEFINITION carrying 'M3x0.5 - 6H - 8mm depth' as a free-form string; no helical curve, no thread_specification.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: receivers should preserve the SHAPE_ASPECT and PROPERTY_DEFINITION as opaque metadata even when the parametric thread information is absent; writers should emit thread_specification when AP242 is the schema. Warn and accept with W_THREAD_METADATA_DROPPED when the metadata cannot be carried; rendering threaded holes as smooth cylinders without diagnostic is a coverage gap.

Notes

Synonyms: "tapped hole exports as smooth cylinder", "thread spec lost in STEP", "cosmetic threads not in STEP file", "M3 thread becomes 2.5mm hole", "thread information dropped at export".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE')
  • contains(b'SHAPE_ASPECT')
