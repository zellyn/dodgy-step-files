Xp039 — Inventor "simplified part" loses appearance via empty SURFACE_SIDE_STYLE

Sender

Autodesk Inventor 2025 with "Simplify" feature applied before STEP export

Description

The Simplify feature in Inventor strips per-face appearance overrides but retains the STYLED_ITEM → PRESENTATION_STYLE_ASSIGNMENT → SURFACE_STYLE_USAGE chain with an empty SURFACE_SIDE_STYLE. The presentation graph remains structurally valid (no NULL pointers) but the colour leaf has been pruned. Receivers walk the chain, find no actual colour to apply, and fall back to the default (Inventor's gray, OCC's yellow, etc.).

Reproducer recipe

SURFACE_SIDE_STYLE with an empty styles list `()`; SURFACE_STYLE_USAGE referencing it; PRESENTATION_STYLE_ASSIGNMENT; STYLED_ITEM bound to an ADVANCED_FACE.

Expected kernel behavior

detect empty SURFACE_SIDE_STYLE and treat it as "appearance explicitly cleared"; surface a diagnostic; never crash on the empty styles list; do not invent a colour.

Notes

**See also**: Xp036. Synonyms: "Inventor Simplify drops appearance", "empty SURFACE_SIDE_STYLE on STEP export", "appearance pruned but binding retained", "simplified part gray instead of red", "STEP appearance lost on simplification".
