Xp018 — PMI without saved-view × unit-system mismatch × forward references

Description

A `geometric_tolerance` references a `shape_aspect` that is forward-referenced (`#999` → not yet defined at the point of use, but defined later in the file). The PMI is meant to be visible in a saved view, but the `draughting_model.items` list is missing the `mapped_item`. The whole assembly's `GEOMETRIC_REPRESENTATION_CONTEXT` declares mm but PMI text values are emitted assuming inches.

Reproducer recipe

usual PMI scaffolding plus three independent defects: forward-reference shape_aspect, missing mapped_item, mm context with inch-magnitude PMI value.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or warn and accept: PMI ordering invariant, saved-view link invariant, and unit-consistency invariant each addressed independently; normalize where possible, warn otherwise.
