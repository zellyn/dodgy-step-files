Xp013 — Cone apex pcurve × surface-folded × non-manifold vertex

Description

A `CONICAL_SURFACE` has a face whose wire passes through the apex (singular pcurve row). The same vertex is shared by 4 edges belonging to disjoint shell components (non-manifold vertex). A second face nearby is on a `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` whose Jacobian flips sign within the parametric range.

Reproducer recipe

a cone with apex at origin; one face's wire goes through the apex; a B-spline surface face nearby with control net that folds; one vertex incident to 4 edges across multiple shell components.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or reject with diagnostic: each pathology is addressed independently; apex-singularity policy (heal/repair), folded-surface detection (warn or reject), non-manifold-vertex policy.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(17) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

  • load == "ok"

  • face[0].bspline.u_degree == 2
  • face[0].bspline.v_degree == 1
  • face[1].surface_type == "cone"
