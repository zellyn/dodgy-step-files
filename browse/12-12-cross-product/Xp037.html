Xp037 — Creo Parametric assembly STEP with overlapping bodies fails manifold solid

Sources

  • PTC community 359011 "STEP file imported into CREO has errors- can't fix"
  • PTC community 785330 "STEP exports from CREO Parametric fail to import in other software products"
  • bug-reporter language: "interfering surfaces prevent making a manifold solid", "Solidify closed volumes fails because the bodies overlap".

Sender

Creo Parametric (interferences allowed in source assembly)

Description

A STEP-exported assembly contains two MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP bodies that interfere by 1 mm in one axis. Creo's import option "Solidify closed volumes" attempts to merge them and fails (the union is non-manifold). Receivers either (a) preserve both interfering bodies and emit a "interference detected" warning, (b) reject the file, or (c) silently keep one body and drop the other.

Reproducer recipe

two MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREPs in the same SHAPE_REPRESENTATION; bounding boxes overlap by 1 mm along one axis; both backed by ADVANCED_FACE entities. PRODUCT.name encodes the intended overlap region.

Expected kernel behavior

load both bodies independently; surface an interference-detected diagnostic; dropping bodies without diagnostic is unacceptable; the kernel cannot abort on attempted union.

Notes

Synonyms: "Creo overlapping bodies fail manifold", "interfering solids prevent solidify", "STEP assembly with body interference", "non-manifold union from overlap", "two solids overlap by 1mm in STEP".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions
