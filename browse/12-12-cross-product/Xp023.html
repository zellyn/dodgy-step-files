Xp023 — Deeply nested aggregate × overlong string literal in one fixture (Pf × Ad cross-product)

§12.12 cross-product (parser robustness × performance)

Description

One fixture combines two parser-stressing patterns. (1) A `LIST_OF_LIST_OF_INTEGER` attribute is built as a deeply nested parenthesised aggregate, around 60 open parens deep; a recursive descent parser hits stack-overflow before reaching the inner literals. (2) A `PRODUCT.name` carries a string literal exceeding the Edition-3 32 769-octet cap by ten times; a parser using a fixed-size string buffer truncates or overruns. Combined: the parser may segfault on the deep-nesting path before it reaches the overlong literal, hiding the second defect; or it may allocate an oversized string, then segfault on the deep aggregate, hiding which root cause was hit first. Bug-reporter language: "STEP file kills my parser two ways", "deep nesting plus huge string", "stack overflow then OOM".

Reproducer recipe

a `LIST_OF_LIST_OF_INTEGER` attribute spelled as `((((((((((((((( .. 1 .. ))))))))))))))))` (60+ levels deep); a `PRODUCT.name` whose string literal is 327 690 characters of `'A'` (10× the ISO cap).

Expected kernel behavior

cap recursion depth and string-literal length on input; reject either pattern with a precise diagnostic naming which budget was exceeded; the order of diagnostics across the two defects is documented.

Notes

**See also**: Pf012.
