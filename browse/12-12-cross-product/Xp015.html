Xp015 — Self-intersecting wire on cylindrical (periodic) face × seam-edge missing

Description

A face on a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` has a wire that self-intersects in UV near the seam line (u=0 / u=2π). The seam edge is missing — what should be a single edge along the seam appears as two separate edges, neither shared. The pcurve self-intersection happens exactly at u=2π (a parametric ambiguity). All three are interactions between the periodic surface's UV space and the wire's combinatorics.

Reproducer recipe

cylinder, face wire of 4 edges crossing the seam; the crossover is a self-intersection in UV; no seam edge listed.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: seam-edge synthesis policy (synthesize / repair), UV-shift policy (normalize), self-intersection resolution (heal) — all three engage.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
