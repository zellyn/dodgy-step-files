Description

A `TOROIDAL_SURFACE` carries `major_radius = -10.0` (vendor orientation marker). A face on it has an `EDGE_CURVE` whose pcurve runs from (u=0, v=0) to (u=0.001, v=0.001) but whose 3D curve endpoints are nominally identical (length < 1e-9). Three independent geometry-quality concerns intersect on the same face.

Reproducer recipe

`TOROIDAL_SURFACE` with negative major radius; one face with a sub-tolerance edge whose pcurve and 3D curve evaluations disagree by ~1e-3 in UV.

Expected kernel behavior

vendor-radius policy applied; pcurve/3D reconciliation strategy invoked; tiny-edge collapse decision.
