Sender

Siemens NX (which tolerates super-multiplicity knots in B-spline curves)

Description

An EDGE references a B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS whose knot vector contains an interior multiplicity equal to (degree+1)+1 = 5 (for a degree-3 curve). ISO 10303-42 caps interior multiplicity at degree+1 to ensure C^0 continuity; super-multiplicity makes the curve discontinuous at the knot. NX's writer accepts this; Parasolid's importer flags it as a discontinuity and aborts. The Onshape user sees a generic "translation error" with no specific entity callout.

Reproducer recipe

B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS degree 3 with 8 control points; knot multiplicities (4, 5, 4) at parameters (0, 0.5, 1) — interior knot multiplicity 5 violates the (degree+1) cap.

Expected kernel behavior

validate knot-multiplicity invariants at parse time; either reject with a precise "interior knot multiplicity exceeds degree+1" diagnostic or split the curve at the discontinuity. Translators must surface specific entity / knot index, not a generic "translation error".

Notes

Synonyms: "NX super-multiplicity knot", "Onshape translation error generic", "B-spline interior multiplicity > degree+1", "Parasolid rejects discontinuous spline", "knot vector violates ISO cap".

Expected validation
