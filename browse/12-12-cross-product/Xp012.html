Xp012 — Reversed face normal × non-watertight shell × duplicate edge

Description

A `CLOSED_SHELL` of 6 faces representing a cube. One face has an inverted `same_sense`. A second face's outer wire contains a duplicate `ORIENTED_EDGE` (the same edge listed twice with different orientations). A third face's outer boundary doesn't quite reach the shared edge of its neighbour; leaving a 1e-3 gap. Three classic shell-quality problems coexist.

Reproducer recipe

6-face cube; face 3's `same_sense=.F.`; face 4's EDGE_LOOP has the same edge twice; face 5 ends 1e-3 short of the shared edge.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or reject with E_TOPOLOGY_INVARIANT diagnostic: orientation, watertightness, and edge-uniqueness invariants each independently addressed.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
