Xp032 — CATIA V5 degenerate B-spline surface → Inventor "empty PartBody"

  • bug-reporter language: "the result is an empty PartBody plus geometric_set debris", "solid not created on import".

Sender

CATIA V5 R25 / R26 STEP writer

Description

A B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS has its first row of control points all coincident (4 CPs at the same location), creating a degenerate row. CATIA's writer accepts and emits the surface; downstream receivers either heal the row (treating it as a degenerate edge) or refuse the face. When the face is refused, the shell is incomplete, the solid never closes, and the result is an empty PartBody with the failed faces parked in geometric_sets.

Reproducer recipe

B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS degree (3, 3) with first row of 4 control points all at (0,0,0); second row scattered; uniform knot vector. Wrap into a single ADVANCED_FACE.

Expected kernel behavior

detect degenerate rows/columns at parse time; either contract them to a degenerate-edge representation or reject the surface with a clear diagnostic. Zero-area or self-overlapping geometry must not be produced without diagnostic.

Notes

**See also**: Gn015. Synonyms: "CATIA degenerate spline", "empty PartBody after STEP import", "B-spline first row all coincident", "geometric_set debris", "solid not created CATIA Inventor".
