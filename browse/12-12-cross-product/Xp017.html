Description

A `GEOMETRIC_SET` has an empty items list. A separate `CARTESIAN_POINT` carries coordinates `(1.0e308, 0.0, 0.0)`. A third `CARTESIAN_POINT` has a NaN component (parsed from textual `NaN` or produced by underflow). The kernel must decide each: empty, gigantic, not-a-number.

Reproducer recipe

`#10=GEOMETRIC_SET('',());` `#11=CARTESIAN_POINT('',(1.0E308,0.,0.));` `#12=CARTESIAN_POINT('',(NaN,0.,0.));` (textual `NaN` literal, which itself is non-conforming for ISO-10303-21 REAL).

Expected kernel behavior

each anomaly addressed independently — empty-aggregate policy, extreme-magnitude clamp/reject, NaN-detection.
