Xp022 — Time-bomb tolerance × negative-radius torus × cyclic seam edge

Description

A `TOROIDAL_SURFACE` has `major_radius = -10.0` (vendor flip) and `minor_radius = 1.0`. A face on it has a seam edge whose 3D-curve identical-vertex check is exactly at the kernel's working precision (the two endpoint coordinates differ by exactly 1e-7). The seam edge appears in the loop twice (once forward, once reverse), forming a cycle. Three time-bomb / vendor / topology defects.

Reproducer recipe

torus with negative major radius; seam edge with 3D length 1e-7; loop = `(seam_fwd, other, seam_rev, other2)` such that the seam edge appears doubled.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or warn and accept: vendor-radius policy normalizes the radius, tolerance-boundary policy decides at the boundary, repeated-seam invariant coerces / repairs the seam; each independently.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(4) ifc=schema_n/a
