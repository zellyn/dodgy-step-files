Xp038 — Onshape 2-56 UNC tap drill misclassified as M1.6 metric clearance hole

§12.12 cross-product (PMI × receiver feature recognition)

round-trip interop

Sources

  • forum.onshape.com discussion 3489 "Export to STEP or SLDPRT, thread/hole characteristics wrong"
  • discussion 22945 "step file export/import (bug?)"
  • bug-reporter language: "my 2-56 thread shows up as M1.6 in SolidWorks", "hole metric/imperial confusion after STEP round-trip".

Sender

Onshape STEP/SLDPRT writer

Description

A 2-56 UNC tapped hole exports as a CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE of radius 0.925 mm (= tap drill 1.85 mm). SolidWorks's hole-wizard recognition fits the cylinder to its nearest standard size; M1.6 clearance hole is 1.8 mm, very close to 1.85 mm and within tolerance. The hole is mis-tagged metric, the BOM lists "M1.6", and the imperial intent is lost. The defect class is loss of unit/standard context for hole features under STEP round-trip.

Reproducer recipe

CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE radius 0.925 mm in a SI_UNIT(.MILLI., .METRE.) context; ADVANCED_FACE; CLOSED_SHELL; MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP. PRODUCT.name records the original imperial intent.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: feature recognition should preserve any incoming SHAPE_ASPECT or PROPERTY_DEFINITION naming the standard/imperial intent; in absence of metadata, the kernel must reject relabeling imperial features as metric without a diagnostic. Warn and accept with W_FEATURE_STANDARD_MISMATCH when the inferred standard contradicts the source; accepting a re-classified standard without diagnostic is a coverage gap.

Notes

**See also**: Xp029. Synonyms: "2-56 thread becomes M1.6", "imperial thread misread as metric", "hole feature metric/imperial confusion", "Onshape thread wrong size", "tap drill matched to wrong standard".

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'0.925')
