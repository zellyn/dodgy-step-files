Xp021 — Disconnected EDGE_LOOP × pcurve missing × wire bypassing seam

§12.12 cross-product (wires × pcurves × surfaces)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • synthesized cross-product.

Description

A face on a closed `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` is bounded by three edges. Edge[1].end ≠ Edge[2].start (gap of 0.5 in 3D, far above any reasonable tolerance). The pcurves are absent. The wire's 3D path also wraps around the cylinder (covers > one period in U). All three classes interact on the same wire.

Reproducer recipe

cylinder; face wire with three open edges, gaps, no pcurves, total U-extent > 2π.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or reject with E_CONNECTEDNESS / E_MISSING_PCURVE diagnostic: connectedness invariant, pcurve-existence invariant, and period-spanning invariant each addressed; repair where possible, otherwise reject.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'FILE_SCHEMA')
  • contains(b'EDGE_CURVE')
  • contains(b'ADVANCED_FACE')
