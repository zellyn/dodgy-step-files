Xp033 — NX-emitted cylinder split into two ADVANCED_FACE halves at the seam

Sender

Siemens NX (default writer behaviour for closed cylinders)

Description

A 360-degree cylindrical face is emitted as two 180-degree ADVANCED_FACE entries sharing a seam edge, each backed by its own CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE entity (same axis, same radius, complementary parameter ranges). Receivers expecting one periodic face emit "non-merged cylinder" warnings; downstream feature recognition fails to identify the holes / shafts as parametric features. Users mitigate with Creo's "Optimize Face" or SolidWorks's "Combine faces".

Reproducer recipe

two CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE entities with identical AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D and radius 5.0; two ADVANCED_FACE entries (one per half) with empty bound lists; CLOSED_SHELL referencing both halves.

Expected kernel behavior

detect equivalent CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE entries (same axis, same radius) and merge their hosted faces into a single periodic face; emit "merged split cylinder" diagnostic; leaving the split unresolved breaks feature recognition downstream.

Notes

Synonyms: "split cylinder face on seam", "NX writes cylinder as two halves", "feature recognition fails on split surface", "non-merged periodic face", "Optimize Face needed after import".
