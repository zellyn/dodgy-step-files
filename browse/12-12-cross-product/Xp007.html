Description

An entity `#100` is a complex `(NAMED_UNIT(*) LENGTH_UNIT() SI_UNIT(..))` whose first parameter is a deeply-nested aggregate (50 levels of `((((..))))`). The same entity references `#100` indirectly via a `REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP` chain that cycles back. A naive recursive parser hits both stack overflow (nesting) and infinite loop (cycle).

Reproducer recipe

`#100=REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP('','',#100,#100);` (self-reference). Plus a long-list aggregate `((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((1)))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))))).` somewhere in scope.

Expected kernel behavior

cycle-detection invariant and bounded-nesting invariant both upheld; reject or load with diagnostics — kernel's choice.
