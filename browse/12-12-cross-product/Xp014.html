Xp014 — Open shell as MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP.outer × tolerance-violation × unit mismatch

§12.12 cross-product (shells × tolerance × units)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • synthesized cross-product.

Description

A `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` whose `outer` slot references an `OPEN_SHELL` (schema requires `CLOSED_SHELL`). The shell's vertex tolerances exceed the edge tolerances (hierarchy inverted). The containing context declares mm but coordinates are 1000× too small (i.e., authored in metres). Three orthogonal invariants violated.

Reproducer recipe

`MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP('',#OPEN_SHELL_REF);` with `VERTEX_POINT.tolerance > EDGE_CURVE.tolerance`; coordinates in the range 0.001 with mm declared.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or warn and accept: schema-class invariant, tolerance-hierarchy invariant, and unit-vs-magnitude policy each addressed independently; repair where possible, warn otherwise.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(25) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

solid — authored as a closed solid; openness is the defect — heal it shut.

  • contains(b'EDGE_CURVE')
  • contains(b'ADVANCED_FACE')
