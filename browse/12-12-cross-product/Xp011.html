Xp011 — REAL missing decimal × integer in DIRECTION × empty FILE_NAME author

Description

`DIRECTION('',(1,0,0))` written without decimal points (integer literals in REAL slots); `FILE_NAME` has all author/organisation fields empty (`('')`). Both lexical defects co-occur in a file otherwise valid.

Reproducer recipe

header with `FILE_NAME('Xp011.stp','..',(''),(''), '','','')`; data section with `DIRECTION('',(1,0,0))`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or warn and accept: lex policy on integer-in-REAL coerces / normalizes the literal; metadata policy on missing author warns. Both independently decided.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
