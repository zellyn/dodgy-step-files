Xp003 — Sliver face × non-manifold edge (3 faces share one edge)

§12.12 cross-product (faces × shells)

Description

An `OPEN_SHELL` contains three planar faces that all share the same `EDGE_CURVE`. One of the three faces is a sliver; its area is near-zero because two of its bounding edges differ only in the third decimal. The non-manifoldness and the sliver are co-located: the same edge that is shared by three faces is also one of the two near-coincident edges of the sliver.

Reproducer recipe

Three `ADVANCED_FACE`s on a planar surface, each bounded by an `EDGE_LOOP` referencing a common `EDGE_CURVE` `#E`. The third face's outer wire contains `#E` plus a second edge whose endpoints differ from `#E`'s by 1e-4.

Expected kernel behavior

Two independent decisions; whether to collapse the sliver (and if so, into which neighbour), and whether to treat the 3-face junction as legitimate non-manifold topology or a malformed shell. The corpus does not prescribe order.

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(21) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.
