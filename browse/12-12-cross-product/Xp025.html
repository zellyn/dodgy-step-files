Xp025 — Onshape→SolidWorks: assembly children snap to origin (placements lost)

Sender

Onshape STEP exporter, round-tripped through SolidWorks 2021

Description

A STEP-exported assembly carries ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION entities where every NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE references the same identity AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D. The receiver's instance-positioning relies on per-NAUO transforms; with all transforms identity, every child renders at world origin. Onshape's writer apparently embedded child placements into the child's own SHAPE_REPRESENTATION items list rather than into the relating transformation, which works for Onshape's reader but not SolidWorks/Inventor.

Reproducer recipe

top-level PRODUCT_DEFINITION with two child PRODUCTs whose intended positions are (50, 0, 0) and (0, 75, 0) mm; both NAUOs reference an identity ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION whose origin AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D is the world origin. PRODUCT.name carries the intended position as a string for diagnostic purposes.

Expected kernel behavior

the kernel must either (a) honour the per-NAUO transform exactly (children at world origin), surfacing a diagnostic that placements look suspicious; or (b) reject the file as ambiguous. Inventing placements from other entity references is not part of the spec.

Notes

**See also**: P003. Synonyms: "STEP subassemblies snap to origin", "SolidWorks imports Onshape STEP all on top of each other", "child placements lost after round-trip", "identity IDT for every child", "assembly children stacked at origin".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION')
