Xp020 — Tessellated face × BRep face sharing PMI × unit-system inch/mm collision

§12.12 cross-product (PMI × tessellation × units)

P1 — silent data loss

interop

Sources

  • synthesized cross-product.

Description

A `TESSELLATED_FACE` and an `ADVANCED_FACE` both represent halves of one through-hole. PMI `dimensional_size` (a diameter) cites a `shape_aspect` bound to both faces via two `geometric_item_specific_usage` records, but no style-binding exists for the tessellated face. The `LENGTH_UNIT` says inches, but a separate `PLANE_ANGLE_UNIT` is missing and the PMI text value carries the diameter in mm.

Reproducer recipe

BRep half-cylinder face + tessellated half-cylinder triangulation; PMI binding spans both; missing `STYLED_ITEM` for the tess; mixed-unit context.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept, or warn and accept: style-binding policy normalizes the binding, PMI cross-kind resolution coerces / repairs the link, mixed-unit policy normalizes the units.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'FILE_SCHEMA')
  • contains(b'EDGE_CURVE')
  • contains(b'ADVANCED_FACE')
