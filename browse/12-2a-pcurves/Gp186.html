Description

Two independent, unstitched coplanar `PLANE` rectangles (Face A upper, Face B lower) share a geometrically coincident but topologically distinct boundary segment. Face A's bottom edge (`A=(0,0,0)` to `B=(1,0,0)`) carries a 3D `LINE` of magnitude 1 (parameter domain `[0,1]`) and a matching-speed pcurve. Face B's top edge (`B'=(1,0,0)` to `A'=(0,0,0)`, distinct `VERTEX_POINT` entities from A's, at the same coordinates) is the SAME physical segment traversed in the OPPOSITE 3D direction, parametrized 4x slower (vector magnitude 0.25, domain `[0,4]`), with its own matching-speed pcurve. Neither face references the other's edge or vertex entities — a genuinely unstitched two-body input, not Gp050/Gp052's single-edge the edge-healing stage probes.

Reproducer recipe

Two coplanar `PLANE` rectangles sharing one physical boundary segment via two independent `EDGE_CURVE`s: one traversed forward at unit speed (domain `[0,1]`), the other traversed reversed at quarter speed (domain `[0,4]`), each with its own matching pcurve; wrapped in one `OPEN_SHELL` containing both `ADVANCED_FACE`s (never explicitly sewn in the Part-21 data itself).

Expected kernel behavior

a sewing pass (the sewing stage) recognizes the two edges as geometrically coincident within tolerance, reverses and rescales the non-reference (Face B) contributor's pcurve onto the reference edge's shared domain, and merges them into one topologically-shared edge.

Notes

**See also**: Gp050, Gp052 (single-edge the edge-healing stage.same-parameter repair probes — not an actual two-edge merge; this fixture is the correction). **Empirical finding (live-verified, decisive)**: this fixture is NOT stitched by the default the reader (which does not invoke sewing) — the raw translated shape is a compound of the 2 independent `ADVANCED_FACE`s (`n_faces_total=2`, `n_edges_total=8`, no shared edges), exactly as authored. To verify the class's actual claim (that sewing's own merge-time pcurve reconciliation is what this input demands), a standalone the sewing stage(1e-2)` pass was run over the two translated faces (not part of the standard tier-3 harness — a supplementary live check specific to this class). Result, read via `TopExp.MapShapesAndAncestors_s`: the shape drops from 8 distinct boundary edges to 7, with exactly ONE edge shared by both faces — the two independently-parametrized, reversed, 4x-domain-mismatched edges were genuinely merged. On the merged edge: 3D range `(0.0, 1.0)` (Face A's reference domain won), `SameRange=True`, `SameParameter=True`; Face A's pcurve is an unchanged `Geom2d_Line` over `[0,1]`; Face B's pcurve is now a `Geom2d_TrimmedCurve` over `[0,1]` — its original `[0,4]`, reversed-direction curve was reversed and rescaled onto the shared domain, exactly the `Reverse()`+`ReversedParameter`+`SameRange`-rescale codepath this class targets. Both faces retain valid, non-null pcurves post-merge (the `c2d2.IsNull()` skip-on-failure path was NOT taken).
