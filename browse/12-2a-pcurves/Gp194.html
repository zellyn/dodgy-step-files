Description

The CATIA like-seam idiom (a single `EDGE_CURVE` whose `SURFACE_CURVE` carries TWO `PCURVE`s on one and the same host surface, used twice with opposite orientations in one `EDGE_LOOP`) hosted on a `SURFACE_OF_LINEAR_EXTRUSION` swept 1.0 along +Z from a CLOSED but NON-PERIODIC `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` — 9 control points around a unit circle with the first pole equal to the last, degree 2, clamped knots. An extrusion inherits its periodicity from its basis curve, so this host wraps around in U while declaring no period, and it is not a B-spline surface. That combination is precisely what the subvariant names and what none of the existing fixtures can present: Gp013 and Gp181 are B-spline hosts (so no approximation is needed, and the reader has already periodized them by the time a shape exists), and Gp182's `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` is inherently periodic, so it can never require the approximation either.

Reproducer recipe

`SURFACE_OF_LINEAR_EXTRUSION('',#closed_nonperiodic_bspline_curve,#vector)` as the surface of an `ADVANCED_FACE`; one `EDGE_CURVE` whose `SURFACE_CURVE` lists TWO `PCURVE`s, both with `basis_surface` = that extrusion, one a 2D line at u=0 and one at u=1; that `EDGE_CURVE` referenced twice, with opposite `ORIENTED_EDGE` senses, in the face's single `EDGE_LOOP`, closed off by the two wrap-around `CIRCLE` edges at v=0 and v=1.

Expected kernel behavior

recognise that the host is closed in U but carries no declared period and cannot be periodized in place because it is not a B-spline; approximate it to a B-spline surface first, declare that surface periodic in U, migrate every pcurve to it, and merge across the like-seam edge — rather than treating the two pcurve banks as an ordinary non-seam duplication.

Notes

Live-verified (2026-07-31, this worktree, OCP/OCCT 7.8.1) against this fixture's own bytes: the face reads back on a `Geom_SurfaceOfLinearExtrusion` with `IsUPeriodic() == False`, `IsUClosed() == True`, U bounds [0,1], and the reader does NOT periodize it — the direct contrast with Gp013 (`IsUPeriodic == True` straight out of the reader) and Gp181 (`IsVPeriodic == True`), both live-rechecked here, and with Gp182's cylinder (`IsUPeriodic == True` by construction). **Control**: the byte-identical construction with only the basis curve swapped for a `CIRCLE` reads back on a `Geom_SurfaceOfLinearExtrusion` with `IsUPeriodic() == True`, isolating the closed-but-non-periodic basis curve as the sole cause of the missing period. **HONEST LIMITATION — what this fixture does NOT show**: it supplies the input precondition (and is the only fixture in the class that genuinely does), but it does not independently demonstrate the unify-level repair firing, and no STEP file appears able to. That branch additionally needs `faces.Length() > 1` in one same-domain group plus an `EdgeWith2pcurves` — an edge carrying two pcurves on the reference face while NOT being really-closed in that face (`ShapeUpgrade_UnifySameDomain.cxx:3206-3214`) — and the two ways a STEP file can produce two pcurves are mutually exclusive with that. Route (a), the true-seam route, requires the same edge to appear TWICE in one `EDGE_LOOP` (`StepToTopoDS_GeometricTool.cxx:99-104`), which is what makes the reader call the two-pcurve `BRep_Builder::UpdateEdge` overload (`StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdgeLoop.cxx:611`, `:621`) — but an edge appearing twice in a face IS really-closed there, so `:3206` classifies it as a seam, `:3218` sets `SeamFound`, and `:3221` disables the branch. Route (b), the like-seam route, requires the edge to appear exactly ONCE (`StepToTopoDS_GeometricTool.cxx:136-146`) and stores only the SINGLE-pcurve overload per face (`StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdgeLoop.cxx:615`, `:625`); with both faces on the same surface and identity location the second call replaces the first, so no `BRep_CurveOnClosedSurface` is ever built and `BRep_Tool::IsClosed(edge, face)` stays false. Live-confirmed: a two-face variant of exactly this idiom (two half-faces on one shared host, the like-seam edge used once in each) was built and read in this worktree and came back with ZERO edges carrying two pcurves on their face, and `ShapeUpgrade_UnifySameDomain` with ConcatBSplines on and off produced identical output. **See also**: Gp013 (U-direction B-spline host), Gp181 (V-direction B-spline host), Gp182 (the earlier non-B-spline attempt, whose cylinder is inherently periodic — this fixture is its corrected successor and does not replace it). Synonyms: "extrusion of closed non-periodic curve", "surface wraps but declares no period", "like-seam on non-bspline host", "two pcurve banks non-periodic extrusion", "CATIA seam surface needs approximation".
