Gp040 — Pcurves emitted by default duplicate / contradict the surface 3D curve

§12.2a pcurve defects (sub-class: writer-emitted pcurves disagree with 3D)

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2a-pcurves/Gp040.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0025654
  • bug-reporter language: "disable writing pcurves to STEP and IGES by default", "pcurves and 3D curves disagree on round-trip", "writer-emitted pcurves cause downstream failures". (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Sender

OCCT's STEP writer when configured to emit pcurves (`write.surfacecurve.mode = 1`).

Description

When pcurves are emitted alongside 3D curves on `EDGE_CURVE`, every receiver re-projects them to verify consistency. The writer's pcurve, computed by a separate pass after the 3D curve is fixed, often disagrees with the 3D curve at the edge tolerance; a re-import yields edges that drift off the surface, faces with `BRepCheck_InvalidEdgeOnSurface` errors, or sewing failures. The community workaround is to disable pcurve emission entirely; the kernel-side bug is that the two representations are not co-fitted.

Reproducer recipe

STEP file with `SURFACE_CURVE` carrying both a 3D curve and a `PCURVE` whose 2D parametric curve evaluates ~ε off the 3D curve when projected onto the surface. Receiver reports edge-on-surface tolerance violation.

Expected kernel behavior

heal-and-accept; pcurves must be co-fitted with the 3D curve so that surface-evaluated pcurve and 3D curve agree within the edge tolerance; if they cannot be fitted, omit the pcurve and let the receiver re-project.

Notes

**See also**: Gp028, Tfa023. Synonyms: "STEP writer pcurves disagree with 3D curves", "disable writing pcurves to STEP and IGES by default", "edge tolerance violated by writer pcurve", "pcurve drift after STEP round-trip".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'PCURVE')
  • contains(b'SURFACE_CURVE')
