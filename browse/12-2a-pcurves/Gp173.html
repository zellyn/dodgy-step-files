Gp173 — General (non-analytic) `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` 3D edge on a `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` forcing the sampled pcurve-projection fallback

Sender

STEP translators that emit an `EDGE_CURVE` with a general free-form 3D B-spline lying on a curved analytic surface (sphere/torus) but supply only a crude straight-line placeholder pcurve (or none), leaving the receiver to build the true 2D image by projection.

Description

An `EDGE_CURVE` (`#24`) has a 3D geometry that is a general, non-iso, cubic `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` (`#11`, control points near but not on a radius-5 `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` `#5`) tracing a diagonal path from `(5,0,0)` toward the north pole. There is no closed-form analytic projection of a general B-spline onto a sphere, so `ShapeConstruct_ProjectCurveOnSurface` cannot use its analytic path and falls back to the **sampled** branch: it evaluates the 3D curve at a fixed grid of sample points (~23), projects each onto the sphere, and approximates a B-spline pcurve through the projected 2D points. The approximated pcurve's endpoints do not land exactly on the vertex UV parameters, so `correctExtremity` must nudge the projected 2D endpoints back onto the vertices. Two additional stressors are baked in: the supplied pcurve (`#22`) is a crude straight 2D `LINE` that does not match the true projection, and the terminal `VERTEX_POINT` (`#13`, `(0.02,0.48,4.98)`) is offset ~0.02 mm from the 3D curve endpoint, forcing a real (non-zero) endpoint residual. OCCT loads the shape (`shape(1)`) after running the sampled projection and adding the sphere's natural bounds (seam + pole degenerate edges), so the single declared edge becomes a 5-edge, 10-vertex healed face.

Reproducer recipe

`SPHERICAL_SURFACE` radius 5; an `ADVANCED_FACE` whose outer bound is one `EDGE_CURVE` whose 3D curve is a cubic `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` running diagonally across the sphere (no analytic pcurve exists); attach a `SURFACE_CURVE` with a single straight-`LINE` placeholder `PCURVE` on the sphere, and offset one endpoint vertex ~0.02 mm off the 3D curve end. OCCT: `shape(1)`, sampled projection + `correctExtremity` run at load; gmsh: reject.

Expected kernel behavior

detect that no analytic projection exists, sample the 3D curve, approximate the pcurve, and snap the projected extremities to the vertex parameters within tolerance; or reject the edge if the sampled approximation exceeds the edge tolerance. The residual endpoint gap must be closed by re-projecting the vertices, not left as a `SameParameter` violation.
