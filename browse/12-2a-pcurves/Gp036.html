Gp036 — Pcurve shifting on non-periodic surface produces wrong result

§12.2a pcurves

P1 — silent data loss

geometry

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-2a-pcurves/Gp036.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0028595 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A 2D-curve-shift routine intended for periodic surfaces is invoked on a planar (non-periodic) surface. The routine shifts the pcurve by the surface's "period"; but on a non-periodic surface the period is undefined, returning zero or NaN. The pcurve becomes invalid.

Reproducer recipe

Planar face with a wire whose pcurves were emitted with U values outside the face's parametric range. Healer applies a shift; on planar surface the shift is degenerate.

Expected kernel behavior

Reject as precondition error: pcurve-shifting only runs on surfaces flagged periodic; non-periodic surface invocation is a precondition error.

Notes

**See also**: Twi084, Gp008. Synonyms: "pcurve shift on non-periodic surface", "period shift applied to planar surface returns NaN", "pcurve shift routine misfires on plane", "shift-by-period on non-periodic surface degenerates", "shifting pcurves on flat surface gives invalid result".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'EDGE_CURVE')
  • count_entity_def(b'PCURVE') >= 1
