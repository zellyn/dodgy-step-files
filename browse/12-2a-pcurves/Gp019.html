Gp019 — Edge on a composite-surface face is missing per-patch pcurve

§12.2a pcurve

crash geometry

Sources

Description

A face is built on a composite surface (a grid of patches as in `RECTANGULAR_COMPOSITE_SURFACE`), but an edge that bounds a sub-face of the composite does not have a pcurve on the relevant sub-patch. The composite shell builder cannot decompose the face into per-patch sub-faces without the per-patch pcurve.

Reproducer recipe

A face on a composite surface containing an `EDGE_CURVE` whose `SURFACE_CURVE.associated_geometry` is empty for the relevant sub-patch.

Expected kernel behavior

synthesise the per-patch pcurves by projecting the 3D curve onto each sub-surface, or reject the face when projection fails. Composite surface decomposition without per-patch pcurves is undefined.
