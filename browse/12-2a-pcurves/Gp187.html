Description

A `PLANE`-hosted edge's 3D curve is a plain `LINE` from `(0,0,0)` to `(1,0,0)`, uniformly parametrized over `[0,1]`. Its `PCURVE` is a degree-3 `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` with 7 non-collinear control points tracing a gentle S-wiggle from `(0,0)` to `(1,0)` in UV — but the interior knot vector is `(0.0001, 0.0002, 0.0003)`: three knots crammed into the first three ten-thousandths of the parameter range, leaving a single `~0.9997`-wide span for the rest of the curve. The smallest:largest knot-interval ratio is roughly 1:10000 — the "severely non-uniform (ill-conditioned) knot spacing" signature the class description says defeats a polynomial-domain `SameParameter` approximation and forces curvilinear (arc-length) reparametrization.

Reproducer recipe

3D `LINE` `(0,0,0)`→`(1,0,0)`, domain `[0,1]`; `PCURVE` degree-3 B-spline, 7 control points, knot multiplicities `(4,1,1,1,4)`, knots `(0.0, 0.0001, 0.0002, 0.0003, 1.0)`.

Expected kernel behavior

`BRepLib::SameParameter` detects the ill-conditioned knot ratio and reparametrizes by arc length rather than attempting a direct polynomial-domain approximation that would be numerically unstable at that scale.
