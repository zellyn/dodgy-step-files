Gp027 — Closed-face splitter leaves new pcurves out of sync with 3D curves on `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE`

§12.2a pcurve

P1 — silent data loss

Description

A pass that splits closed periodic faces / solids (full `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE`, `TOROIDAL_SURFACE`) along their seams produces new edges whose pcurves do not satisfy the same-parameter invariant — pcurve and 3D curve disagree at the same parameter value. Typical pattern: an `EDGE_CURVE` between vertices (10,0,0) and (-10,0,0) on a cylinder traverses the seam from u=0 to u=π in 3D, but the pcurve `LINE` is parameterised over [0, 1] instead of [0, π] — same UV path, different parameterisation, so the seam/branch handling is wrong. A topology checker reports invalid edges. The splitter returns a topologically inconsistent intermediate state.

Reproducer recipe

Take a full cylinder solid; split along its seam; run a topology checker — invalid edges flagged. Recompute same-parameter on the result — becomes valid.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: a face-splitter must restore the same-parameter invariant on the new edges before returning, or reject the operation. Returning an invalid intermediate burdens every caller.

Notes

**See also**: Gp022, Twi032. Synonyms: "closed face splitter leaves invalid edges", "split-along-seam produces same-parameter violations", "topology checker flags edges after splitting cylinder", "face splitter returns invalid intermediate geometry", "splitting cylinder along seam breaks edge invariant".
