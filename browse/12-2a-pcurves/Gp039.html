Gp039 — Pcurve projection unstable on closed B-spline curve

§12.2a pcurves

Description

A 3D B-spline curve, closed (first control point == last), is projected onto a surface to compute a pcurve. The projection algorithm walks the curve with discontinuity at the closing seam; the resulting pcurve has a wild excursion near the seam, parameter range no longer matches the input curve's range.

Reproducer recipe

Closed B-spline curve (degree 3, 8 control points with first == last); project onto plane. Pcurve has spike at seam parameter.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: closed-curve projection treats the seam as a continuity constraint; pcurve range matches input range; no spike. Must not silently produce spike.

Notes

**See also**: Gp037, Twi088. Synonyms: "pcurve projection unstable on closed B-spline", "spike in pcurve at closure seam", "closed-curve projection has wild excursion at seam", "projection of closed B-spline produces wrong parameter range", "pcurve bumps near closing seam of periodic curve".
