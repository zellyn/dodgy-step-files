P024 — Flattened sub-assembly hierarchy after merge

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

round-trip assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/P024.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #11441

Sender

pcb-3d.com (KiCad-adjacent component library) re-exported through FreeCAD

Description

Two STEP assembly files merged in a tree and re-exported emit `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` chains that don't preserve the original product hierarchy — receiver shows flattened/scrambled tree.

Reproducer recipe

STEP assembly with `PRODUCT_DEFINITION_USAGE` graph that drops one or more layers of sub-assembly grouping.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: preserve the original sub-assembly hierarchy across round-trip.

Notes

**See also**: A005. Synonyms: "subassembly hierarchy flattened during merge", "merge step squashes assembly tree", "import merges children into parent product".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • count_entity_def(b'MAPPED_ITEM') == 2
