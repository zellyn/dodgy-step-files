P007 — High-curvature B-spline surface flattens between OCCT versions

§12.2 geometric / NURBS

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/P007.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #14600

Sender

HyGEARS / Involute Simulation Softwares (gear-design tool)

Description

Files with `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` of degree (3,3) representing strongly curved involute / hypoid surfaces with closely spaced knots load fine in OCCT 7.5 but display as flat planes in 7.6.x. The geometry is parsed but tessellation degenerates.

Reproducer recipe

Generate `B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS` with bicubic degree, strongly curved, knot vector with closely-spaced knots representing gear flank.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: surface honored as curved on display tessellation across versions. Must not silently flatten.

Notes

Synonyms: "sharp B-spline surface flattens between OCCT versions", "high-curvature NURBS surface loses detail", "B-spline curvature flattened on import", "surface detail lost across version upgrade".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS') or contains(b'B_SPLINE')

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == True
