A016 — UDA on multi-level reference designator (MLRD) ignored

§12.6 (sub-class: MLRD attribute attachment)

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A016.stp

Sources

  • CAx-IF UDA §6.2/§8 (entry from 02-caxif).

Description

UDAs at the assembly-instance level can be attached to NAUOs or to MLRDs (multi-level paths). They must be counted at the product representing their context node. Some receivers ignore MLRD-attached UDAs entirely.

Reproducer recipe

AS1 with one UDA on the NAUO from AS1 to L-Bracket-Assembly, and one UDA on the MLRD from AS1 to a specific Nut instance.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept by walking both NAUO and MLRD attachments when counting per-context UDAs; must not silently ignore MLRD-attached UDAs.

Notes

**See also**: A029. Synonyms: "user-defined attribute on multi-level path ignored", "MLRD attribute dropped on import", "UDA on nested-instance reference designator lost", "deep-path attribute not preserved".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'AS1_TO_LBR')
  • contains(b'LBR_TO_NUT')
