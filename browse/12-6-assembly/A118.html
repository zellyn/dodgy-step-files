Description

A `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` declares Parent as relating (assembly) and Child as related (component). A `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` wraps a `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP` whose `rep_1`/`rep_2` are wired OPPOSITE to OCCT's own writer convention for that NAUO direction (`rep_1`=Parent's representation, `rep_2`=Child's — `STEPConstruct_Assembly::MakeRelationship` always emits `rep_1`=child, `rep_2`=parent). Both Parent and Child carry real, distinguishable geometry via their own independent `PRODUCT_DEFINITION`→...→`SHAPE_DEFINITION_REPRESENTATION` chains (Parent: 1×1 marker face at the origin; Child: 5×5 marker face at x∈[100,105]) — unlike M062, whose NAUO/SRR pair sits inside a `GEOMETRIC_CURVE_SET` with EMPTY `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` items, so the reversal there is bytes-present but shape-unobservable (empty either way). `TransferEntity(NAUO)` walks `graph.Sharings(NAUO)`→PDS→`graph.Sharings(PDS)`→CDSR and calls `CheckSRRReversesNAUO`, which detects the swap and returns True; the reader emits `AddWarning(SRR, "SRR reverses relation defined by NAUO; NAUO definition is taken")` and silently resolves using NAUO's direction rather than rejecting the conflicting file.

Reproducer recipe

`NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE('1','','uses',#parent_pd,#child_pd,$)`; `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION(#srr,#pds)` where `#pds=PRODUCT_DEFINITION_SHAPE('Placement','Placement of an item',#nauo)` and `#srr=SHAPE_REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP('rel','',#parent_rep,#child_rep)` (opposite of the writer's own `#child_rep,#parent_rep` convention).

Expected kernel behavior

reject the conflict with a diagnostic (the SRR and NAUO disagree on parent/child direction — one of the two is authored wrong); or trust NAUO by convention and make the choice explicit in a loud warning. Must not silently resolve without surfacing the conflict to the caller.

Notes

**See also**: M062 (byte-only precedent — this fixture is the live-shape-observable upgrade). Root-reachability finding: a bare, unreferenced CDSR does not independently transfer via `TransferRoots()` in this OCCT build (see U051's Notes) — the CDSR here is instead discovered via the standard NAUO-root walk (`TransferEntity(NAUO)`'s internal `graph.Sharings` traversal), which requires both Parent and Child to carry their own real, independently-reachable representations. Live-verified (2026-07-12, OCP 7.8.1, direct `STEPControl_Reader` probing — not mirrored/guessed): `shape_null=False`; the transferred compound carries geometry from BOTH the Parent's own SDR chain and the Child's own SDR chain PLUS an additional CDSR-driven contribution (`n_faces_total=4`, `n_edges_total=16`, `n_vertices_total=32` — twice the 2-face/8-edge/16-vertex baseline of either SDR alone, confirming the CDSR's own `TransferEntity` call independently produced a live shape rather than being skipped or erroring). Provenance tier: bytes-sufficient. Synonyms: "assembly direction conflict", "SRR vs NAUO direction mismatch", "parent/child conflict in STEP", "assembly relation reversed", "CDSR silently resolved reversal", "NAUO definition is taken".
