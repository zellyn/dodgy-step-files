P006 — 360° revolution of arc-of-ellipse produces self-intersecting solid

§12.2 geometric (parametric curve at seam)

P1 — silent data loss

round-trip assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/P006.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #14447

Sender

FreeCAD (Part Revolve)

Description

A solid produced by 360° revolution of a half-ellipse profile (major axis on X) emits self-intersecting / mirrored surface; with major axis on Y, or generatrix converted to B-spline, round-trips correctly. Suggests bug in OCCT's `CONIC` / `ELLIPSE` parametric-domain handling near the seam u=0/2π.

Reproducer recipe

STEP file with `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` whose generatrix is a `TRIMMED_CURVE(ELLIPSE(..))` spanning 0.π/2 of an ellipse with `semi_axis_1 > semi_axis_2`, revolved 2π.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: closed valid solid (no self-intersection or mirroring near seam).

Notes

Synonyms: "360 degree revolved ellipse self-intersects", "full revolution of arc-of-ellipse produces invalid solid", "self-intersecting solid from elliptical revolve", "degenerate solid from full ellipse revolve".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION')
  • count_entity_def(b'ELLIPSE') >= 1
  • contains(b'TRIMMED_CURVE')
