A120 — `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP_WITH_TRANSFORMATION` where only ONE of Origin/Target belongs to its expected representation

Description

A007 demonstrates the FULLY-repairable case: both Origin and Target axis placements are found cross-referenced (Origin sits in Target's representation's item list, Target sits in Origin's), so `ComputeTransformation` transparently swaps them and computes a repaired transform. This fixture is the narrower, distinct half-resolved case: Origin (`Ax1`) genuinely belongs to its OWN representation's item list (`isOKOrigin=True`), but Target (`Ax2`) belongs to NEITHER representation's item list — a completely foreign placement (`isOKTarget=False`, and neither `isSwapOrigin` nor `isSwapTarget` is True since Ax1/Ax2 never cross-appear). The membership check cannot determine a consistent swap, so OCCT falls through to the unrepairable `"Axis placement used by SRRWT does not belong to corresponding representation"` branch and silently computes a transform from the inconsistent org/trg pair anyway, rather than rejecting the file.

Reproducer recipe

`ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION('','',#Ax1,#Ax2)` inside a `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP_WITH_TRANSFORMATION('srrwt','',#rep1,#rep2,#idt)` where `#Ax1` is listed in `#rep1.items` but `#Ax2` is listed in neither `#rep1.items` nor `#rep2.items`, wrapped via `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` + `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` (same NAUO/CDSR wiring as A118).

Expected kernel behavior

reject the SRRWT as malformed (an axis placement that belongs to neither declared representation cannot be trusted to compute a correct transform), or clearly flag the resulting transform as unreliable rather than silently using it.

Notes

Structure note: Origin/Target are wired through DEDICATED placement-only representations (`parent_placement_rep`/`child_placement_rep`), mirroring A007's own `asm_rep`/`cmp_rep` split, rather than the parts' real geometry-bearing representations — mixing a bare `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` item into a geometry-bearing `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION`'s item list was independently tested and found to break the Parent-PD root's own normal shape transfer; A007's established split avoids that. The parts' REAL geometry (Parent's 1×1 face, Child's 5×5 face) each transfer via their own normal, independent product chain, exactly like A007. Live-verified (2026-07-12, OCP 7.8.1, direct probing — not mirrored/guessed): `shape_null=False`, `n_faces_total=1` (Parent's own face, bbox `[0,1]×[0,1]` at the origin, confirmed un-transformed via its own SDR chain). Since the placement-only representations carry no geometry (`Recognize()` does not accept a bare `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` item), `TransferEntity(CDSR)`'s own contribution resolves to a null/empty shape and is silently absent from the compound — the SRRWT/NAUO/CDSR defect bytes are present, well-formed, and structurally correct (the same epistemic status as A007's own already-shipped SRRWT fixture, which has the identical placement-only-representation limitation); the specific membership-check outcome is confirmed via source-level mechanism analysis rather than an observable shape-count difference. Provenance tier: bytes-sufficient. **See also**: A007 (fully-swapped, repairable sibling), A118 (CDSR/NAUO wiring precedent). Synonyms: "SRRWT axis placement does not belong", "half-swapped SRRWT", "unrepairable axis-placement mismatch", "ComputeTransformation membership check fails one side".
