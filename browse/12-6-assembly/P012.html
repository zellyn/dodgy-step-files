P012 — STEP-XML (`.stpx`) and compressed (`.stpz`) variants unsupported

§12.1 file-format

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/P012.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #12678 (.stpx), #8218 (.stpZ)

Sender

PTC Creo, Siemens NX (.stpx); compression is generic

Description

STEP-XML (`.stpx`, AP242 XML form) and gzip-compressed STEP (`.stpz`) are emitted by some commercial tools but only plain ISO-10303-21 is parsed.

Reproducer recipe

Take any valid STEP file, gzip it, rename `.stpZ`; or convert to `.stpx`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: recognize and decompress / parse the XML form (`.stpx` / `.stpz`); or reject with E_UNSUPPORTED_VARIANT diagnostic.

Notes

Synonyms: "STEP-XML stpx file unsupported", "compressed stpz STEP file can't open", ".stpx and .stpz formats not handled", "XML-encoded STEP rejected".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'.stpz') or contains(b'.stpx') or contains(b'application/x-stepz')
  • contains(b'DOCUMENT_FILE')
