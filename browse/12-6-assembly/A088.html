A088 — Empty assembly causes writer to throw

assembly

Sources

Description

A writer is invoked on a document whose root is an empty assembly (`PRODUCT_DEFINITION` with no shape). Writer iterates assembly members, finds none, and throws "no top-level shape" exception, halting export.

Reproducer recipe

XCAF document with one empty assembly label, no shape attached. Write to STEP.

Expected kernel behavior

Accept empty assemblies and emit a minimal `PRODUCT_DEFINITION` plus empty shape representation; do not throw or crash.

Notes

Provenance tier: writer-side; writer throws on empty assembly.
