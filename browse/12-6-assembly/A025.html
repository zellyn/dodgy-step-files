A025 — Names with special / non-ASCII / encoded characters lost or corrupted

§12.6 (sub-class: characters in names)

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A025.stp

Sources

  • G040 (control directives in names), G041 (cp1251/GB2312/DOS850), G042 (Japanese), G043 (embedded apostrophes), G044 (single space), G045 (backslashes), R052/R053 (OCCT bug25440/bug33815), J004 (FreeCAD GH#7987), W031 (CAD Exchanger).

Sender

SolidWorks (Chinese/Japanese locales); Inventor (locale-dependent codepages); Pro/E and Inventor with control-directive names.

Description

STEP `name` fields contain ISO 10303-21 control directives, raw 8-bit Cyrillic/CJK/CP850 bytes, doubled apostrophes (`''` for "O'Brien"), backslashes (Windows paths), or single-space placeholders; receivers either fail to decode, replace with `?`, drop the name, or trim whitespace too aggressively.

Reproducer recipe

PRODUCT name `'\X2\03B1\X0\'` (Greek alpha); or `PRODUCT('O''Brien',..)`; or `PRODUCT(' ',..)`; or `PRODUCT('C:\path\file',..)`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: decode all 10303-21 directives; provide `read.step.codepage` parameter; preserve literal backslashes through encode/decode; standard doubled-quote escape parsing; trim whitespace before assigning names but preserve the original. Must not silently corrupt names.

Notes

**See also**: A010, U016. Synonyms: "non-ASCII component name garbled on import", "Unicode characters in part name corrupted", "Part 21 backslash-X encoding not decoded", "special characters in NAUO instance name lost", "Chinese / Japanese / German labels broken on round-trip".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'PRODUCT')
