A067 — Baking placements into geometry breaks shared-subshape sharing

§12.6 assembly

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A067.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0028471 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A compound containing several instances of the same shape via different `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D`s is processed by a pass that bakes locations into geometry. Each instance is duplicated with its own geometry — shared topology between instances is lost. Memory blows up and downstream feature recognition treats every instance as independent.

Reproducer recipe

Compound of 10 `MAPPED_ITEM`s of one prismatic shape at 10 different translations. After RemoveLocations, geometry is duplicated 10 times.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: when baking locations, retain shape sharing for instances at identical post-location geometry; or prefer not to bake at all if downstream tooling is location-aware.

Notes

**See also**: A035, Tsh051. Synonyms: "baking placements into geometry breaks instance sharing", "transformed instances duplicate geometry", "shared subshapes lost when location is baked in".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(81) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • n_faces_total == 18
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[6].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[12].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[0].edge_count == 4
  • face[0].area > 99
  • face[0].area < 101
