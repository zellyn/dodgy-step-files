A102 — Cap part exports as STEP that downstream slicers flag as damaged (open shell wrapped as solid)

§12.6 assembly hierarchy

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • FreeCAD #29647
  • bug-reporter language: "Sometimes exported STEP/STL damaged or contains errors", "Cap object marked as damaged in slicers like Orca or Bambu Studio", "step is broken but stl is fine".

Sender

FreeCAD PartDesign Cap-feature export in 1.2.0dev.

Description

A Cap part — a thin-walled cup/cap shape — exports a `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` whose outer shell is missing one face (the cap-bottom interior). Downstream slicers (Orca, Bambu Studio) detect non-manifold geometry and refuse the print job. The companion STL export tessellates fine because the StL writer ignores topology. Distinct from P015 because the missing-face symptom is reproducibly tied to the Cap-feature pipeline (not a SubShapeBinder offset).

Reproducer recipe

thin-walled cup `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` (8 faces expected: 4 outer-side, 4 inner-side, 1 outer-bottom, 1 inner-bottom, 1 top-rim) with one face omitted, leaving a 7-face shell wrapped as a solid.

Expected kernel behavior

writer validates closure before wrapping in `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`; or downgrades to `SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` with a warning.

Notes

**See also**: P015. Synonyms: "cap export marked damaged by slicer", "thin-walled body has open shell", "PartDesign cap STEP fails slicer ingest", "step broken but stl fine".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(7) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP')
  • contains(b'CLOSED_SHELL')
