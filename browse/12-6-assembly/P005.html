P005 — Inverted spherical face on certain sweep orientations

§12.3 topology

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/P005.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #14710

Sender

FreeCAD itself (Part workbench Sweep)

Description

A face on a sphere produced by sweeping with a particular profile orientation has its `FACE_OUTER_BOUND.orientation` reversed in the output STEP; visually inside-out. Mirror-sweep variant is correct.

Reproducer recipe

Author a `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` containing an `ADVANCED_FACE` with `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` underlying, whose `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` includes seam edges with explicit `.F.` orientation flags on a `surface_curve`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: face orientation preserved; both sweep variants yield the same closed solid.

Notes

Synonyms: "spherical face inverted on certain sweep orientations", "sphere normal pointing inward", "revolved sphere face flipped", "inside-out spherical surface".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(6) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'SPHERICAL_SURFACE')
  • contains(b'FACE_OUTER_BOUND')

Tier-3 assertions

  • n_faces_total == 1
  • face[0].surface_type == "sphere"
  • face[0].quadric.radius == 5.0
  • edge[0].curve_type == "circle"
  • edge[0].analytic.radius == 5.0
