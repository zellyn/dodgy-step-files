A029 — User-Defined Attribute (UDA) target-binding inconsistency

§12.6 (sub-class: UDA attachment / metadata)

silent-loss round-trip assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A029.stp

Sources

  • M014 (CAx-IF UDA v1.8).

Description

Same UDA template implemented differently across CAD systems — bound to `product`, to `product_definition`, to `next_assembly_usage_occurrence`, or to a shape aspect. Round-trip loses the binding because the receiver expects a different target.

Reproducer recipe

NX writes UDA on `product_definition`; CATIA reader expects it on `next_assembly_usage_occurrence`; data is ignored on reimport.

Expected kernel behavior

preserve UDA verbatim using the rec-practice template; do not silently re-bind.

Notes

**See also**: A016. **OCC behavior**: silently drops UDAs whose target binding does not match its expectations rather than preserving them verbatim; kernel mishandling — the catalog above forbids silent re-binding (or silent loss). Synonyms: "UDA bound to wrong target after import", "user-defined attribute target inconsistent", "attribute assigned to part but appears on instance", "UDA target-binding mismatch".
