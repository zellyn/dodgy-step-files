A035 — Product with both sub-assemblies and direct shape

§12.6 (sub-class: hybrid product structure)

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A035.stp

Sources

  • Q040 (OCCT STEPControl_ActorRead.cxx:735-736).

Description

A `PRODUCT` simultaneously has both sub-assemblies (NAUO children) and a direct geometric `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` of its own. Strict assembly-vs-leaf classifiers misroute one or the other.

Reproducer recipe

PRODUCT_DEFINITION referenced by both `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` (as parent) and a `SHAPE_DEFINITION_REPRESENTATION` carrying its own faces/solids.

Expected kernel behavior

Accept hybrid; treat as both parent and leaf; emit both sub-assembly tree and direct geometry. Do not reject hybrid pattern.

Notes

**See also**: A036. Synonyms: "product node has both subassemblies and direct geometry", "mixed product structure confuses reader", "leaf shape attached to non-leaf product", "PRODUCT_DEFINITION carries shape AND children".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a
