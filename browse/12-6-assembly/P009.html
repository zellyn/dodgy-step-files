P009 — Helical sweep tessellates with garbled facets at seam

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • FreeCAD #18151

Sender

Autodesk Inventor

Description

A spring (helical sweep solid) renders as garbled facets in OCCT due to pathological chord-deviation tessellation at the helix seam, but renders correctly in Inventor.

Reproducer recipe

STEP `SWEPT_SURFACE` whose directrix is a parametric helix with constant pitch and several turns.

Expected kernel behavior

Smooth tessellation matching producer.

Notes

**See also**: Pf014. Synonyms: "helical sweep tessellation garbled at seam", "thread mesh broken at start/end", "helix tessellator produces bad facets at seam", "screw thread visualizer artifacts at seam". Provenance tier: bytes-only (Q5 full-corpus 2026-07-01).
