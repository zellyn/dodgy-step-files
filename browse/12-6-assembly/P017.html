P017 — Free wires in a top-level COMPOUND silently dropped

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

silent-loss assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/P017.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #17807

Sender

any tool emitting free wires alongside solids (KiCad, electronics packaging)

Description

STEP file with a top-level `COMPOUND` mixing solids and free `EDGE_CURVE`/`WIRE` items at the same level: with importer's "compound merge" preference OFF, loose edges are dropped silently.

Reproducer recipe

STEP `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` containing both a solid and free-standing `EDGE_CURVE` / `WIRE` items at the same level.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: the default setting must preserve free wires in a top-level COMPOUND.

Notes

Synonyms: "free wires in top-level COMPOUND silently dropped", "loose curves in compound stripped on write", "wireframe-only entities lost on export", "construction wires not exported".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'EDGE_CURVE') >= 2
  • count_entity_def(b'VERTEX_POINT') >= 2
