A081 — Material attribute overridden by generic colour on round-trip

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

round-trip assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A081.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0022982
  • OCCT MANTIS#0022962 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A document has both a colour and a richer material record (with reflectance / shininess) attached to one face. STEP writer emits both but the colour record overrides the material record on re-import; the reader's "first matching style wins" logic loses the material details.

Reproducer recipe

Face with `Quantity_Color` + `Graphic3d_MaterialAspect` attached. Round-trip through STEP.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: material record subsumes colour; if both are present, prefer the higher-fidelity material record on import. Must not silently override material with colour.

Notes

**See also**: A068, A079. Synonyms: "material attribute overridden by generic colour on round-trip", "material color clobbered by surface color", "round-trip swaps material for color".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'SURFACE_STYLE_REFLECTANCE_AMBIENT')
  • contains(b'COLOUR_RGB')
