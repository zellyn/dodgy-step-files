A023 — Mapped_item / representation_map identity transform requirement (Saved Views)

§12.6 (sub-class: mapped_item / representation_map)

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A023.stp

Sources

  • CAx-IF PMI v4.1 §9.4.2.1 (entry from 02-caxif), Q031 (MAPPED_ITEM unrecognized location), Q084 (mapped_item with scaling).

Description

To include part geometry in a Saved View's `draughting_model`, a `mapped_item` plus a `representation_map` must be used. The `axis2_placement_3d` referenced as `mapping_source`/`mapping_origin` MUST be a unit identity transform. Files that put a non-identity transform inadvertently move the geometry only in that view. Variants: MAPPED_ITEM with unrecognized location (location dropped); writer skips MAPPED_ITEM with non-rigid (scaling) transform.

Reproducer recipe

Saved view's mapped_item has `mapping_source = AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D(P0=(10,0,0), Z=(0,0,1), X=(1,0,0))` instead of unit identity.

Expected kernel behavior

validate identity transforms on saved-view mapped items; reject scaling on assembly mapped items, or heal by collapsing the non-identity transform on saved-view mapped items.

Notes

Synonyms: "Saved View mapped_item must be identity but isn't", "non-identity transform on representation_map for Saved View", "Saved View mapping_origin not unit transformation".
