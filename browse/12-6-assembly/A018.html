A018 — STYLED_ITEM at wrong scope: assembly-level color override lost or misbound

§12.6 (sub-class: color/layer attribution per-instance)

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A018.stp

Sources

  • V046 (SolidWorks assembly-level color), V047 (per-face color → white), V048 (Inventor strips appearances), G097 (OCCT root-label color), J008 (Onshape Apr-2024 pipeline), Y004 (KiCad nested STYLED_ITEM lost on flatten), W024 (HOOPS material merging).

Description

When color is overridden at assembly node level (not part level), STEP writers emit per-part STYLED_ITEM rather than per-instance, so receivers see all instances colored alike. Or per-face STYLED_ITEM chains land at the wrong scope (top-level shape-rep instead of per-`SOLID_MODEL`). Multiple `MECHANICAL_DESIGN_GEOMETRIC_PRESENTATION_REPRESENTATION` chains may contradict on the same STYLED_ITEM. When nested compound is flattened/copied, inner STYLED_ITEM is dropped entirely.

Reproducer recipe

assembly with a top-level color override; export. Verify whether STYLED_ITEM attaches to MAPPED_ITEM-level instances or only to SHAPE_REPRESENTATION. Or build a one-level vs two-level wrapped product and flatten via `XCAFDoc_ShapeTool::CopyShape`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: STYLED_ITEM must attach to MAPPED_ITEM-level instances; prefer most-specific styled-item; fall back to next ancestor; never inherit from sibling sub-assembly; propagate STYLED_ITEM through every level of NAUO during flatten.

Notes

**See also**: A019, A020, M039, M040, M042. Synonyms: "assembly-level color override lost on import", "STYLED_ITEM at wrong scope ignored", "component color binding misplaced", "color override applied at wrong assembly level".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(2) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[1].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[1].surface_type == "plane"
