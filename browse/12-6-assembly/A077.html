A077 — Sub-assembly extraction loses XDE attributes

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A077.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0028104
  • OCCT MANTIS#0028055
  • OCCT MANTIS#0029888 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A "save sub-assembly" operation extracts a child branch of a document into a new document. Names, colours, layers, and PMI attached to the extracted branch are not copied; extracted document has bare geometry only.

Reproducer recipe

Two-level assembly with names + colours on every level; extract the lower sub-assembly into a new XCAF document.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: extraction copies the entire attribute subtree rooted at the extracted label; downstream document is fully styled. Must not silently lose XDE attributes.

Notes

**See also**: A064, A073. Synonyms: "sub-assembly extraction loses XDE attributes", "extracted subassembly missing labels and colors", "splitting out a subassembly drops metadata".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

  • contains(b'NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE')

  • contains(b'COLOUR_RGB')
