A065 — Empty `AppliedGroupAssignment` raises exception in writer

§12.6 assembly

crash round-trip assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A065.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0025168 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A document containing an empty group (no sub-shapes assigned) round-trips through the STEP writer and fails because the writer's `AppliedGroupAssignment` emitter requires at least one member. Empty groups are legitimate (placeholder for later population) and should not crash the writer.

Reproducer recipe

STEP file with `APPLIED_GROUP_ASSIGNMENT(#1, ())` (empty members list) at top level.

Expected kernel behavior

empty groups are emitted as legal records with empty members list, or skipped entirely; never crash.

Notes

**See also**: Ad004. Synonyms: "empty AppliedGroupAssignment makes writer throw", "writer crash when group is empty", "AppliedGroupAssignment with no items causes exception".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'APPLIED_GROUP_ASSIGNMENT')
  • contains(b'GROUP(')
  • matches(rb'APPLIED_GROUP_ASSIGNMENT\(#\d+,\s*\(\s*\)\)')
