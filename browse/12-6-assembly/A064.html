A064 — Subshape names not transferred on STEP import

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0021802
  • OCCT MANTIS#0023895 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

`STYLED_ITEM` and `PRESENTATION_LAYER_ASSIGNMENT` carry human-readable names attached to specific edges, faces, or sub-solids inside an assembly. STEP reader records names only at the top `PRODUCT_DEFINITION` level, dropping per-subshape names.

Reproducer recipe

Assembly with one solid; six face-level styled-items carrying names "top", "bottom", "left", "right", "front", "back". Read via XCAF document.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: every named entity (face, edge, vertex, shell, solid) must propagate its name into the receiving model's attribute store keyed by the corresponding sub-shape. Must not silently drop names.

Notes

**See also**: A030. Synonyms: "subshape names not transferred from STEP", "face/edge labels missing after import", "STEPCAFControl_Reader doesn't transfer named subshapes".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(54) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'STYLED_ITEM') == 6
  • contains(b'PRESENTATION_LAYER_ASSIGNMENT')
  • contains(b'COLOUR_RGB')

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == False
  • n_faces_total == 6
  • n_edges_total == 24
  • n_vertices_total == 48
