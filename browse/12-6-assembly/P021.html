P021 — Edge-curve geometry mismatches face geometry within tolerance

§12.3 topology / edge-surface association

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/P021.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #17422, #17201

Sender

Fusion 360

Description

Two `EDGE_CURVE` instances share the same trimmed `CIRCLE` geometry but bind to different `FACE_OUTER_BOUND`s with different radii (radius mismatch within tolerance). Quick Measure samples the underlying curve and reports the wrong radius.

Reproducer recipe

STEP file with two edges sharing a single underlying `CIRCLE` curve geometry, attached to faces with materially different radii.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: resolve via face geometry rather than edge curve; or warn and accept on mismatch.

Notes

Synonyms: "edge curve doesn't lie on adjacent face within tolerance", "edge geometry mismatches face geometry", "edge-curve / surface gap exceeds tolerance", "sliver between edge and face".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(8) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'CIRCLE') >= 1
  • count_entity_def(b'EDGE_CURVE') >= 2
