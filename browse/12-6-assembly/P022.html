P022 — Helical seam degeneracy from arc-by-3-points + 360° revolve

§12.2 geometric / seam

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • FreeCAD #16681

Sender

FreeCAD

Description

A specific construction — sketch with arc-by-3-points (NOT center-on-axis) closed with horizontal/vertical lines, revolved 360° — produces a STEP whose `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` has a seam degeneracy where finite (non-degenerate) seam edges meet at the rotation axis instead of collapsing to a degenerate point.

Reproducer recipe

STEP `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` with generatrix `TRIMMED_CURVE(CIRCLE(…))` whose start/end-parameter encoding produces a self-touching meridian at the rotation axis with finite seam edge.

Expected kernel behavior

Auto-degenerate the seam edge, or reject with clear error.

Notes

Synonyms: "helical seam degeneracy from arc-by-3-points plus 360 revolve", "thread seam broken because arc was reconstructed", "revolved 3-point arc collapses at seam".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(1) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION') >= 1
  • contains(b'TRIMMED_CURVE')
  • contains(b'CIRCLE')

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == False
  • n_vertices_total == 1
