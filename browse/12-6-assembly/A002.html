A002 — Hidden / suppressed bodies leak into export, or are dropped silently

§12.6 (sub-class: visibility-bound serialization)

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A002.stp

Sources

  • W023 (HOOPS Exchange / Creo), V024 (Fusion 360), J022 (Fusion 360), K026 (Rhino INVISIBILITY).

Sender

PTC Creo (hidden bodies serialized anyway); Autodesk Fusion 360 (writer drops anything hidden); CATIA / NX (uses INVISIBILITY entity).

Description

Symmetric pair: (a) writer serializes bodies the source CAD intended to hide (with no presentation_style flag), so consumers see "ghost geometry"; (b) writer omits hidden bodies and the consumer sees an incomplete assembly with `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` pointing at a `PRODUCT_DEFINITION` whose `SHAPE_DEFINITION_REPRESENTATION` is empty.

Reproducer recipe

Hide one body; export STEP. Inspect for `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` whose target product has either no `SHAPE_DEFINITION_REPRESENTATION` (J022) or geometry but no `STYLED_ITEM` invisibility flag (W023). Or use AP214 `INVISIBILITY((#face))` (K026).

Expected kernel behavior

Warn and accept on write when hidden bodies are dropped; on read, treat `INVISIBILITY` as a presentation flag, not a participation gate; surface dangling NAUOs. Receivers must not silently accept without diagnostic.

Notes

**See also**: Pmi058. Synonyms: "hidden bodies still appear in exported STEP", "suppressed parts leak into output", "ghost geometry visible after import", "writer drops hidden bodies but leaves dangling assembly references", "INVISIBILITY flag ignored on round-trip".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • count_entity_def(b'NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE') == 2
  • contains(b'HIDDEN')
