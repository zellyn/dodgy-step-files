A079 — Visibility / colour ignored for sub-shapes by `XCAFPrs_AISObject`

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

  • OCCT MANTIS#0028641 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

An XCAF document carries a sub-shape that is invisible or semi-transparent. The viewer object derived from the document ignores the sub-shape-level visibility / transparency, displaying everything opaque.

Reproducer recipe

Document with one solid; one face has `transparency = 0.5` and one face has `invisibility` set. Render via XCAF presentation.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: viewer respects per-face visibility and alpha; AP242 alpha channel propagates from style record to renderer. Must not silently ignore per-face properties.

Notes

**See also**: A071. Synonyms: "XCAFPrs_AISObject ignores subshape visibility/colour", "subshape colors not displayed", "viewer ignores XCAF subshape attributes".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'INVISIBILITY')
  • count_entity_def(b'STYLED_ITEM') >= 1
  • contains(b'transparent_face') or contains(b'invisible_face')
