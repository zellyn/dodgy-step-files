A066 — `UpdateAssemblies` produces wrong compounds for located roots

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A066.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0029282
  • OCCT MANTIS#0031517 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

An assembly's `UpdateAssemblies` pass rebuilds top-level compounds from current sub-assembly state. When the root assembly itself carries a non-identity location, the rebuild applies the location once at the leaf level and again at the root, producing parts displaced twice from origin.

Reproducer recipe

Assembly tree where root has a translation `(100,0,0)` and one child has a translation `(50,0,0)`. After `UpdateAssemblies`, the leaf appears at `(200,0,0)` (sum applied at both levels) instead of `(150,0,0)`.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: location-chain composition follows the hierarchy strictly once; UpdateAssemblies recomputes from leaves up, honouring identity at each level it climbs.

Notes

Synonyms: "UpdateAssemblies makes wrong compounds for located roots", "located root produces incorrect compound after UpdateAssemblies", "compound wrong after UpdateAssemblies on positioned part".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE')
  • contains(b'ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION')
  • contains(b'root_loc') or contains(b'child_loc')
