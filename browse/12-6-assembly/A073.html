A073 — `Expand Compounds` loses sub-shape locations

§12.6 assembly

assembly

Description

An "expand compounds" operation flattens nested compounds into a single-level XCAF document. Sub-shapes that carry local `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` locations have those locations dropped during expansion; flattened parts pile up at the world origin.

Reproducer recipe

Compound containing two `MAPPED_ITEM` instances of one solid at distinct locations. Run expand-compounds; both instances now have identity location.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: expansion bakes parent locations into child shape representations or preserves them as XCAF child labels with `XCAFDoc_Location` attribute. Must not silently lose sub-shape locations.

Notes

**See also**: A066, A067. Synonyms: "Expand Compounds loses sub-shape locations", "expanding compound drops nested transforms", "subshape positioning lost after Expand Compounds".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(2) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[1].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[1].surface_type == "plane"
