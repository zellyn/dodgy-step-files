P011 — AP242 PMI / GD&T / kinematics annotations silently discarded

§12.7 PMI / GD&T

P0 — receiver crash / hang / OOM

crash assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/P011.stp

Sources

  • FreeCAD #19795

Sender

any AP242-capable CAD (Siemens NX, CATIA, Creo)

Description

AP242 files carry semantic PMI (dimensional / geometric tolerances, datum features), graphic PMI, saved views, clipping planes, materials, kinematic joints, design-history attributes. OCCT silently discards these; the geometric BREP imports but assembly is dimensionally context-less.

Reproducer recipe

AP242-ed2 file emitting GD&T tolerances on faces, plus kinematic joints, plus saved camera views.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept (preservation), or at minimum warn-and-accept with diagnostics listing dropped entity types. Must not silently discard PMI.

Notes

Synonyms: "PMI annotations silently discarded on import", "GD&T tolerances dropped", "AP242 kinematics annotations lost", "tolerances and notes vanish from STEP".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=signal(11) ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'GEOMETRIC_TOLERANCE_WITH_DATUM_REFERENCE')
  • contains(b'KINEMATIC_JOINT') or contains(b'MECHANISM')
  • declared_schema.startswith(b'AP242')
