A019 — Per-face `STYLED_ITEM` colours collapse to a single hue when multiple `ADVANCED_FACE`s share one supporting `PLANE`

§12.6 (sub-class: color attribution / face binding)

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A019.stp

Sources

  • V047 (CATIA → SolidWorks all white), W024 (HOOPS face-level styling), M032 (CAx-IF styling RP), R063 (OCCT bug33317 reference shape color), G036 (tessellated face style binding), G098 (OCCT non-manifold loses attributes).

Description

Multiple distinct face colours in the producer become identical white in the consumer. Per-face colours are bound via `STYLED_ITEM` / `PRESENTATION_STYLE_ASSIGNMENT` / `SURFACE_STYLE_FILL_AREA` / `FILL_AREA_STYLE_COLOUR` chains. Common signature: three `ADVANCED_FACE` instances all share the same supporting `PLANE` (e.g. `#64`) but each has its own distinct `STYLED_ITEM` / `COLOUR_RGB` — XCAF risks merging the faces (since they overlap geometrically) and collapsing the per-face colour binding. After re-tessellation or face-merge healing the binding is lost. Non-manifold STEP reader path skips XCAF attribute application.

Reproducer recipe

Face with full styled_item chain on COLOUR_RGB(0.8,0.1,0.1); subject to sliver-face removal and re-export.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: honor face-bound colour_assignment without normalizing; re-bind to surviving merged face when possible; bind style to tessellated shape; apply colors/styles on non-manifold path. Must not silently collapse colours.

Notes

**See also**: A018, M020, M042, M045, N003. Synonyms: "all faces become one color after import", "face colors lost on round-trip", "uniform white instead of per-face hues", "per-face STYLED_ITEM collapsed to single color", "shared plane causes color merge across faces".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(3) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[2].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[2].surface_type == "plane"
