A095 — STEP export of a part with a fillet/round generates malformed shape on receiver

§12.6 assembly hierarchy (sub-class: writer-side fillet round-trip break)

P1 — silent data loss

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0026367
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP export fail for a round part in FreeCAD", "fillet edges go missing on round-trip", "round part exports as broken shape". (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Sender

FreeCAD / OCCT writer when exporting a part whose fillet edges have very small radii (sub-millimetre) compared to the part bounding box.

Description

A part with a fillet of radius `r << boundingBox/1000` is exported. The writer emits the fillet as a small `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` plus tiny `EDGE_CURVE`s, but the edge-vertex coordinates round to the same `CARTESIAN_POINT` at the writer's emission precision. On re-import the small fillet collapses to a self-loop edge, the face becomes degenerate, and the round part imports as a sharp-edged version. Visible especially on small mechanical parts (~10mm) with sub-mm fillets.

Reproducer recipe

`MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` of a 10mm cube with a 0.05mm fillet on one edge. Writer emits coordinates rounded to 1e-6; the fillet's distinct vertices collapse to one. Receiver sees a degenerate face.

Expected kernel behavior

heal-and-accept; writer chooses precision based on minimum-feature-size, not just bounding box; emit `W_PRECISION_LIMITED` if any pair of distinct vertices would round to the same coordinate at the chosen precision and bump precision automatically.

Notes

**See also**: N028, Twi099. Synonyms: "STEP export fail for round part in FreeCAD", "fillet edges go missing on round-trip", "writer precision drops sub-mm fillet", "round part exported as sharp edges".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE')
  • contains(b'CARTESIAN_POINT')
