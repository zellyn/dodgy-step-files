A069 — Material with zero density blocks STEP write

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A069.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0025910 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A material entity carrying density of zero (e.g., a notional / placeholder material) causes the writer to throw a "validation error" when emitting the material record because it expects positive density. Writer halts the entire export.

Reproducer recipe

Document with one part assigned a material whose density is `0.0` and other physical attributes are similarly zero.

Expected kernel behavior

Accept zero density as legal (= unknown / not applicable); writer emits the material with zero density value or omits the density record entirely. Do not reject.

Notes

Provenance tier: writer-side; writer rejects zero-density material; symptom is on the writing kernel, not the file.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Provenance tier

writer-side — defect not fully embodied in fixture bytes.

Byte assertions

  • contains(b'MASS_DENSITY_MEASURE(0.0)')
  • contains(b'PROPERTY_DEFINITION')
