A071 — Visibility flag of free shapes lost on STEP write

§12.6 assembly

P1 — silent data loss

assembly

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0023951
  • OCCT MANTIS#0023950 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A document contains a "free" shape (top-level, not in any assembly) marked invisible in XCAF. The STEP writer emits the shape but never the visibility attribute; on re-import the shape is visible again.

Reproducer recipe

Free top-level cube with `XCAFDoc_VisMaterial` visibility flag set to false; write to STEP.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: visibility attribute round-trips via `STYLED_ITEM` + `INVISIBILITY` (AP242 entity); reader populates XCAF visibility on import. Must not silently lose visibility.

Notes

Provenance tier: writer-side — visibility flag lost on STEP write; symptom is on the writing kernel.

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Provenance tier

writer-side — defect not fully embodied in fixture bytes.
