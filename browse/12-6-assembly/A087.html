A087 — STEP exporter loses shapes after import-export cycle

§12.6 assembly

round-trip assembly

Description

A document is imported from STEP, exported back to STEP, and re-imported. The final document has fewer shapes than the original — some sub-shapes were not reachable through the export's traversal even though they were reachable on import.

Reproducer recipe

Document with one solid plus one isolated face; export. Re-imported document has only the solid.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: exporter walks every top-level shape in the document, not only the "main" representation. Must not silently lose shapes.

Notes

**See also**: A075, A078. Synonyms: "STEP exporter loses shapes after import-export cycle", "round-trip drops bodies", "import then export produces empty file".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(2) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[1].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[1].surface_type == "plane"
