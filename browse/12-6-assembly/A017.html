A017 — Component labels/colors/names lost when subshape carries a transform on round-trip

§12.6 (sub-class: instance-flattening / location handling)

round-trip assembly

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-6-assembly/A017.stp

Sources

  • I033 (build123d #1014: Pos*Rot drops labels), I036 (build123d #954: nested compound labels via STEPControl_Writer), I006 (OCCT #541: STEP re-export loses XCAF colors), F056/F084/B028 (orphan TopLoc_Location on subshapes), B001 (spurious face-level locations from STEP round-trip).

Description

A component carries a placement transform attached at the face / edge / vertex level rather than at the assembly node, and the writer's component-attribution path expects identity transforms on leaf subshapes. Names, colors, and other attached metadata bound to the inner subshape are lost in the STEP round-trip, or end up bound to the wrong product instance. Symptom: a translated/rotated component reimports as untitled, uncoloured geometry; or nested compounds (Root → Arm → Cube) drop their inner-level labels.

Reproducer recipe

face with a non-identity transform reinserted into a shell; export to STEP; reimport and observe a near-identity transform on the leaf face plus missing color/name attributes. Or build a two-level Compound (Root → Arm → Cube) and observe inner-level labels missing from the resulting STEP file.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: preserve component metadata across round-trip regardless of where the transform is attached. Either coerce the subshape transform into the underlying surface/curve before write, or fold the metadata down to the level where the writer can attach it. Identity-collapsing on read keeps round-trips stable. Must not silently lose metadata.

Notes

**See also**: A024. Synonyms: "subshape colors disappear when component has location", "labels lost on transformed sub-shape", "XCAF attributes stripped when location is non-identity", "color on subshape lost after round-trip with placement".
