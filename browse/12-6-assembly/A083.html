A083 — STEP exporter generates bad geometry for revolution / extrusion since 7.4.0

Description

A solid built from `SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION` of a non-circular profile is exported. The exporter emits a B-spline approximation of the original analytic surface but uses a parameterisation that introduces a C0 isoline at every control-point row, breaking the round-trip's surface smoothness.

Reproducer recipe

Solid with one face on a revolution of a parabolic profile around the Z axis. Round-trip through STEP. Re-imported face has visible faceting along control-point rows.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: preserve analytic representation through export when receiver supports it; only fall back to B-spline approximation on demand.

Notes

Provenance tier: misfiled — NURBS surface defect filed in §12.6 assembly; defect is §12.2c-class. Kept here for ID stability.
