Tfa068 — STEP export duplicates surfaces, leading to non-conformal geometry between adjacent faces

§12.3c faces / sewing / free bounds (sub-class: writer-side duplicate surfaces)

P1 — silent data loss

topology

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0032132
  • bug-reporter language: "STEP export duplicates surfaces, leading to non-conformal geometry", "two adjacent faces emit two distinct surfaces for what should be one", "writer breaks face-face conformality". (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Sender

OCCT's STEP writer when exporting a sewn shell whose adjacent faces share an underlying analytic surface (e.g., two halves of a cylinder split at the seam).

Description

Two faces that share an underlying surface (e.g., two adjacent planar faces co-planar in 3D) are written out with two separate `PLANE` / `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` instances rather than one shared instance. On re-import, the receiver computes face-face adjacency from surface-identity and concludes the two faces are not adjacent; the shell has free edges where it should be closed, downstream Booleans fail.

Reproducer recipe

Two adjacent rectangular faces, both on the plane `z=0`. Writer emits `#10=PLANE(..)` and `#20=PLANE(..)` with identical numerical content rather than sharing one instance. Receiver sees two non-conformal faces.

Expected kernel behavior

heal-and-accept; writer canonicalises analytic surfaces and shares one instance for surface-equal faces; emit one `PLANE`/`CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE`/etc. per equivalence class. Receivers must additionally re-canonicalise on import.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa023, A079. Synonyms: "STEP export duplicates surfaces", "non-conformal faces after STEP round-trip", "writer emits two surfaces for one shared surface", "face-face adjacency lost on round-trip".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

  • count_entity_def(b'PLANE') >= 2 or count_entity_def(b'CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE') >= 2
  • contains(b'ADVANCED_FACE')

Tier-3 assertions

  • shape_null == True
