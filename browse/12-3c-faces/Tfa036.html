Tfa036 — Face on composite surface with boundary crossing patch seams

§12.3c faces / sewing / free bounds (sub-class: composite-surface)

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-3c-faces/Tfa036.stp

Sources

  • OCCT `ShapeFix_ComposeShell::Perform` (`ShapeFix_ComposeShell.hxx:106`)

Description

An ADVANCED_FACE references a RECTANGULAR_COMPOSITE_SURFACE assembled from multiple sub-patches, *or* two ADVANCED_FACEs share the same FACE_OUTER_BOUND wire while referencing two different host PLANEs (e.g., one PLANE at origin, another offset to (1,0,0)) that abut at a patch seam. The face's boundary wire crosses one or more patch seams; pcurves are not split per patch. To evaluate the face's geometry, the kernel must reassemble per-patch fragments while keeping pcurves continuous across patch boundaries.

Reproducer recipe

Two coplanar PLANE patches abutting at U=1, either as a RECTANGULAR_COMPOSITE_SURFACE or as two ADVANCED_FACEs sharing one FACE_OUTER_BOUND; the wire is a 2x1 rectangle whose top/bottom edges cross the U=1 seam.

Expected kernel behavior

split the face's edges at patch boundaries (or treat the composite as one logical surface and lift pcurves accordingly); produce a shell of patch-local sub-faces with continuous wire connectivity, or reject if the composite cannot be unified.

Notes

Existing Tfa entries cover related composite-surface defects. **See also**: Tfa053, Twi055, Gp019. Synonyms: "face spans multiple surface patches", "wire crosses composite surface seam", "rectangular composite surface boundary discontinuity", "pcurves not split per patch on composite surface".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(28) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • n_edges_total >= 8
  • face[1].surface_type == "plane"
  • n_vertices_total >= 16

Cross-references
