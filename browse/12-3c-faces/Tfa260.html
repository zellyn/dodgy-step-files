Description

The mirror of Tfa259. Same `CONICAL_SURFACE` (radius 3 at V=0, semi-angle 30 degrees, apex at V=-6), but the sole boundary is the full-revolution belt `CIRCLE` at V=-10 (`belt_above_apex`) — BELOW the apex in the surface's own parameter, i.e. on the mirrored nappe of the double cone, where the parametric radius `3 + (-10)*sin(30) = -2` is negative and the U direction is consequently mirrored. The belt is emitted as a 3D `CIRCLE` of radius 2 at z=-8.6602540 whose `AXIS2_PLACEMENT_3D` ref_direction is (-1,0,0), so its 3D parametrization agrees with the surface's mirrored U at every point and not merely at the seam. Reaching this case is forced by the schema: `CONICAL_SURFACE` requires radius >= 0 and 0 < semi_angle < pi/2, so the apex V coordinate -radius/sin(semi_angle) is always <= 0 and a belt on the ordinary nappe always puts the apex BELOW; the mirrored nappe is the only conforming way to put it ABOVE. As in Tfa259, the apex row is left unbounded — no apex edge and no seam in the file.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` on a `CONICAL_SURFACE`; `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` referencing an `EDGE_LOOP` with exactly ONE `ORIENTED_EDGE` whose `EDGE_CURVE` is a full-revolution `CIRCLE` with identical start and end `VERTEX_POINT`, positioned at a V strictly less than -radius/sin(semi_angle) (i.e. beyond the apex, on the mirrored nappe), with the circle's ref_direction negated so its parametrization matches the surface's mirrored U; no other edge in the file.

Expected kernel behavior

heal; as in Tfa259 recognise the belting loop and locate the apex in V, but — with the apex ABOVE the belt — synthesize the apex-bridging degenerate edge as a 2D line at the loop's MAXIMUM U running in the -U direction, applying the reversal guard for the opposite U-direction sense; the seam then follows and the face becomes a properly bounded cone on the mirrored nappe.

Notes

Live-verified (2026-07-31, this worktree, OCP/OCCT 7.8.1) against this fixture's own bytes: ONE declared `EDGE_CURVE` reads back as a face with THREE distinct edges / FOUR oriented edges — the belt (`Geom2d_Line` at v=-10.0), a seam used twice (spanning v=[-10, -6]), and one edge with `BRep_Tool::Degenerated == True` whose pcurve is a `Geom2d_Line` with **loc=(6.283185, -6.0), dir=(-1, 0)**, range [0, 6.283185]. Set against Tfa259's **loc=(0.0, -6.0), dir=(+1, 0)** on the identical surface, that is a clean two-way split: same apex V (both -radius/sin(semi_angle) = -6), opposite U anchor (maxLoopU vs minLoopU) and opposite traversal direction — exactly `ShapeFix_Face.cxx:2748-2749` versus `:2740-2741`. The belt edge's own orientation in the healed wire also flips between the two fixtures (FORWARD here, REVERSED in Tfa259), reflecting the different `aSoleWire.Reverse()` guard in each arm. **See also**: Tfa259 (the apex-BELOW mirror — the pair is only meaningful together), Tfa258 (the sphere arm), Twi021 (the wire-level cone-apex insert). Synonyms: "apex above wire V range", "cone belt on mirrored nappe", "second nappe conical face", "negative parametric radius cone", "apex degenerate edge decreasing U".
