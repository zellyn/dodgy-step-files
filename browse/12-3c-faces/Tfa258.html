Tfa258 — Spherical face bounded only by the equator: the pole-bridging degenerate edge and the seam are both absent

Description

A `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` of radius 5 centred at the origin carries one `ADVANCED_FACE` whose `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` is a single-edge `EDGE_LOOP`: the equator `CIRCLE`, authored as a closed `EDGE_CURVE` (`equator_only_bound`) with the same `VERTEX_POINT` at both ends. That is one wire, open in 2D by exactly one full U period, and it is the entire boundary in the file — neither the seam nor the pole-bridging degenerate edge that a complete parametric boundary needs is present. The producer has described a hemisphere by its rim alone and left the receiver to invent the rest.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` on a `SPHERICAL_SURFACE`; `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` referencing an `EDGE_LOOP` with exactly ONE `ORIENTED_EDGE`, whose `EDGE_CURVE` is the equatorial `CIRCLE` with identical start and end `VERTEX_POINT`; no other edge of any kind in the file.

Expected kernel behavior

heal; recognise that the sole wire is open in U by a full period on a U-closed surface, synthesize the degenerate edge that collapses the pole row (a 2D line at v = +pi/2 spanning the full U period), and only then lay in the seam that closes the parametric rectangle — yielding a valid hemisphere rather than a face with an unbounded parametric side.

Notes

Live-verified (2026-07-31, this worktree, OCP/OCCT 7.8.1) against this fixture's own bytes: ONE declared `EDGE_CURVE` reads back as a face with THREE distinct edges / FOUR oriented edges — the equator (`Geom2d_Line` at v=0, dir (+1,0)), a seam used twice (`IsSeam == True`, at u=0 and u=6.283185), and one edge with `BRep_Tool::Degenerated == True` whose pcurve is a `Geom2d_Line` with loc=(6.283185, 1.570796), dir=(-1, 0), range [0, 6.283185]. That is bit-for-bit the construction at `ShapeFix_Face.cxx:1652-1654` for ismodeu=+1 (`p.SetCoord(2*pi, +pi/2)`, `d.SetCoord(-1, 0)`, `aRange = 2*pi`): v = +pi/2 is the north pole and nothing else in the healing stack produces that coordinate. **Negative control**: the byte-identical construction with the host surface changed from `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` to `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` of the same radius — also U-closed, also one wire open in U by a full period, so everything upstream of the surface-type branch is unchanged — reads back with ONE edge, ZERO degenerate edges and NO seam, because a cylinder falls through to `ShapeFix_Face.cxx:1680`. That isolates the sphere arm specifically. **See also**: Tfa259/Tfa260 (the conical apex arm of the same repair, a different mechanism reached through `FixPeriodicDegenerated`), Twi296 (degenerate-torus arm), Twi297 (B-spline pinch arm), Twi021 (the wire-level cone-apex insert).
