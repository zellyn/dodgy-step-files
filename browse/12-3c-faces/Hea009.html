Hea009 — Convert curves and surfaces of a shape (e.g., FACE_OUTER_BOUND on a degree-2 B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS) to Bezier representation

§12.3 (sub-class: shape-healing) — representation conversion

  • OCCT `ShapeUpgrade_ShapeConvertToBezier::Perform` (`ShapeUpgrade_ShapeConvertToBezier.hxx:108`)

Description

A shape whose surfaces and curves are mixed analytic (LINE, PLANE, CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE) and B-spline. The convert-to-Bezier pipeline must handle each representation kind, splitting at non-Bezier breaks (C0 knots, kinks) where needed. Typical fixture: an ADVANCED_FACE with a single FACE_OUTER_BOUND whose curve is a B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS with 5 control points and degree 2 — a B-spline that is not yet a single Bezier segment and must be converted. Some receivers (e.g., Bezier-only meshers) require this normalization.

Reproducer recipe

An ADVANCED_FACE on a B-spline surface with C0 knot, or an EDGE_CURVE whose 3D curve is a degree-2 B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS (not yet Bezier) — both must convert to Bezier.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: emit a shape whose every curve and surface is a Bezier (single segment) or a sequence of Beziers; preserve geometric fidelity within working precision.
