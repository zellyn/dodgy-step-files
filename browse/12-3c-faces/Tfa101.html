Description

Full-torus face with a single placeholder boundary loop loads intact as encoded (no second loop added or expected); FixAddNaturalBound is never invoked on an ordinary read

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

A full toroidal face that already declares a boundary loop must load with that loop exactly as encoded — do not manufacture a second loop, and do not reject the face for declaring "too few". Natural-bound construction is reserved for a face on a closed surface that declares no bounds at all, or hole wires only; neither condition holds here.

Notes

Corrected 2026-07-17 (truth-in-labeling audit): retitled/redescribed — original claimed FixAddNaturalBound under-constructs boundary loops on this full torus; that method is never invoked on an ordinary read, and the fixture's own single placeholder loop loads unaltered. Part of the ~127-entry face-repair/small-face-check misnomer family — see `BACKLOG.md` Q14. Synonyms: "full torus with single placeholder loop loads intact", "natural boundary insertion never invoked on torus".
