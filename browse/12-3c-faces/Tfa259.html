Description

A `CONICAL_SURFACE` with radius 3 at V=0 and semi-angle 30 degrees carries one `ADVANCED_FACE` whose `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` is a single-edge `EDGE_LOOP`: the full-revolution belt `CIRCLE` at V=+2 (`belt_below_apex`, 3D radius 4 at z=1.7320508), authored as a closed `EDGE_CURVE` with the same `VERTEX_POINT` at both ends. The apex row is left completely unbounded — no apex-bridging degenerate edge and no seam appear anywhere in the file. Because the surface's apex sits at V = -radius/sin(semi_angle) = -6 and the belt sits at V = +2, the apex is BELOW the belt.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` on a `CONICAL_SURFACE`; `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` referencing an `EDGE_LOOP` with exactly ONE `ORIENTED_EDGE` whose `EDGE_CURVE` is a full-revolution `CIRCLE` with identical start and end `VERTEX_POINT`, positioned at a V strictly greater than -radius/sin(semi_angle); no other edge in the file.

Expected kernel behavior

heal; recognise the sole boundary as a loop belting the cone through a full revolution, compute where the apex falls in the surface's own V parameter, and — with the apex below the belt — synthesize the apex-bridging degenerate edge as a 2D line at the loop's MINIMUM U running in the +U direction, reversing the belt wire when needed so the two wires are contraversal; the seam then follows and the face becomes a properly bounded cone.

Notes

Live-verified (2026-07-31, this worktree, OCP/OCCT 7.8.1) against this fixture's own bytes: ONE declared `EDGE_CURVE` reads back as a face with THREE distinct edges / FOUR oriented edges — the belt (`Geom2d_Line` at v=2.0), a seam used twice (`IsSeam == True`, at u=0 and u=6.283185, spanning v=[-6, 2]), and one edge with `BRep_Tool::Degenerated == True` whose pcurve is a `Geom2d_Line` with **loc=(0.0, -6.0), dir=(+1, 0)**, range [0, 6.283185]. Both the coordinate and the direction are the apex-below construction at `ShapeFix_Face.cxx:2740-2741` (`gp_Pnt2d(aMinLoopU, anApexV)`, `gp_Dir2d(1,0)`) with the range from `:2762` (`fabs(aMaxLoopU - aMinLoopU)`). **Fingerprint check**: V = -6.0 is exactly `-radius/sin(semi_angle)`, a quantity computed at `ShapeFix_Face.cxx:2716-2717` and nowhere else in the healing stack; a sweep over four (radius, semi-angle, belt-V) triples confirms the synthesized edge tracks it — (3, 30 deg) gave -6.000000, (7, 45 deg) gave -9.899495, (2, 15 deg) gave -7.727407, (5, 60 deg) gave -5.773503, each matching the prediction to all printed digits. **Negative control**: the same single-belt-circle construction on a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` reads back with ONE edge, zero degenerate edges and no seam (the surface-type gate at `:2681-2683` rejects it), and the same construction on a `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` (Tfa258) produces a structurally different edge at v=+pi/2 from an entirely different repair — so cone, sphere and cylinder each give a distinct, predicted outcome. **See also**: Tfa260 (the apex-ABOVE mirror), Tfa258 (the sphere arm), Twi021 (the wire-level cone-apex insert, a different mechanism at a different level).
