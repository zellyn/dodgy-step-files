Tfa012 — Face area zero / negative after fixshape

§12.3c face-sliver-sewing

topology

Description

After healing, `BRepGProp::SurfaceProperties` returns zero or negative area for a face. Either the face is fully degenerate, or healing reversed its orientation and signed-area integration flipped sign.

Reproducer recipe

Construct a face whose outer wire winds clockwise on the natural surface orientation; pass through ShapeFix; compute signed area.

Expected kernel behavior

heal — restore consistent winding; reject if both surface and wire are themselves degenerate (zero unsigned area).

Notes

**See also**: Gs014. Synonyms: "face has zero area after healing", "face area went negative after fix", "fixshape flipped face orientation", "signed area is wrong sign", "face is degenerate after repair".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(9) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • n_edges_total >= 4
  • n_vertices_total >= 8
  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e6
