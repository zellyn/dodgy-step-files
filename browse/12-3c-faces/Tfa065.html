Tfa065 — Healed face uses inflated tolerance instead of original (small gap between adjacent EDGE_LOOP vertices, e.g., 1e-3 mm between V3 and next-edge start)

§12.3c faces/sewing

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0026620
  • OCCT MANTIS#0027122 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A face whose constituent edges have native tolerance ~1e-7 goes through a healing pass that inflates the face-level tolerance to ~1e-1 to absorb a single localised gap. The bloated tolerance then contaminates all downstream operations on the face.

Reproducer recipe

Rectangular face with three good edges (tolerance 1e-7) and one edge with a 1e-3 gap to its neighbour. After healing the face tolerance is set to 1e-1 (>> the worst gap) rather than 1.5e-3.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: normalize face / shell tolerance to the maximum measured deviation across constituent edges, not orders of magnitude above; do not inflate tolerance preemptively.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa017, N040. Synonyms: "healed face overuses inflated tolerance", "tolerance inflated even where original gap was tiny", "healer applies global tolerance bump per-vertex", "small EDGE_LOOP gap causes whole-face tolerance bloat".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(18) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

Tier-3 assertions

  • n_edges_total >= 4
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • n_vertices_total >= 8
