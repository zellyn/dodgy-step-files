Tfa255 — Sphere hole wire touches the pole via a degenerated edge: merges into the natural whole-surface boundary

§12.3c faces (sub-class: `tkshh-face-natural-bound-missing` GAP, pole-touching-hole subvariant)

Sources

  • closes occt-coverage tkshhealing GAP `tkshh-face-natural-bound-missing` missing subvariant (occt-coverage/tkshhealing audit). OCCT `ShapeFix_Face::FixAddNaturalBound` (`ShapeFix_Face.cxx:937-983`, sphere: hole wire containing a degenerated pole edge is merged into the natural boundary
  • gate `isNeedAddNaturalBound` at `:1010-1049` requires a doubly-periodic surface, no outer bound, and (for this subvariant) a hole touching the pole). Distinct from Tfa002 (empty bounds on a full sphere, no hole at all) and Tfa038 (a hole wire that does NOT touch the pole, kept as a genuine subtracted hole).

Description

An `ADVANCED_FACE` on a `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` (radius 5, centred at origin) has NO `FACE_OUTER_BOUND`. Its single `FACE_BOUND` is a small lune-shaped wire that starts at the north pole (0,0,5), runs down a meridian to a point at 70° latitude, across a short chord to a second point at the same latitude (40° of longitude away), back up another meridian to the pole, and closes via an explicit DEGENERATE edge at the pole itself: `EDGE_CURVE` between two independent `VERTEX_POINT` entities both located at (0,0,5), with a zero-length `LINE` as its curve (the corpus's standard degenerate-edge encoding, per Tfa125). The wire genuinely touches the pole through this degenerate edge, not merely through incidental vertex reuse.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` on a `SPHERICAL_SURFACE` with no `FACE_OUTER_BOUND`, a single inner `FACE_BOUND` lune wire that reaches the pole and closes via a zero-length degenerate `EDGE_CURVE` at the pole vertex — on a live `OPEN_SHELL`/`SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` path.

Expected kernel behavior

recognize the pole-touching hole on the doubly-periodic surface and merge it into the synthesized natural (whole-surface) boundary rather than treating it as a separate subtracted hole; the resulting face should cover the full sphere area minus nothing (the lune contributes no area, only a boundary notch).

Notes

**See also**: Tfa002 (no hole at all — empty bounds), Tfa038 (hole wire that does not touch the pole — genuine subtracted hole), Tfa125 (degenerate-edge encoding pattern, different mechanism — U-degenerate south pole). Live-verified (this worktree's OCP/OCCT 7.8.1, default import path, both heal-on/heal-off): shape(1)/shape(1); `n_edges_total==4` (matches the 4 authored edges, 2 of which OCCT marks `Degenerated` after translation, vs. only 1 authored explicitly as zero-length). `face[0].surface_type=="sphere"`, `face[0].area==314.1592653589792==4*pi*5^2` — the EXACT full-sphere area: the lune-shaped hole wire contributes zero area loss, confirming `FixAddNaturalBound` genuinely merged it into the natural whole-surface boundary (a real subtracted hole, as in Tfa038, would reduce the area by the lune's extent instead). Synonyms: "sphere hole touches pole degenerate edge", "natural bound merge with pole hole", "FixAddNaturalBound pole merge", "lune hole absorbed into natural boundary", "degenerate pole edge hole merge".
