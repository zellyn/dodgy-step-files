Tfa253 — Outer/inner wire share a >50% collinear overlap segment

Description

A 10×10 `PLANE` face's `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` is the unit square (0,0)-(10,0)-(10,10)-(0,10). Its inner `FACE_BOUND` (hole) is a 7×3 rectangle (2,0)-(9,0)-(9,3)-(2,3) whose bottom edge lies exactly on the line y=0 — the same line as the outer wire's bottom edge — for x in [2,9]: a 7-unit overlap against the outer edge's 10-unit length (70%) and the inner edge's own full 7-unit length (100%), both well over the 50% threshold that selects the large-overlap 3-edge reconstruction path over the small-overlap 2-edge split.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` whose `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` and inner `FACE_BOUND` each contain a straight edge collinear with, and >50% overlapping, the other — on a live `OPEN_SHELL`/`SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` path.

Expected kernel behavior

detect the collinear segment overlap between the two wires and reconstruct the boundary (cut the overlapping edges back and rebuild with a shared segment edge), producing a well-formed face; or reject with a clear diagnostic.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa039 (transverse crossing, not overlap), Tfa131/Tfa160 (same overlap *input* pattern, unreachable — `GEOMETRIC_CURVE_SET`-hosted). Live-verified (this worktree's OCP/OCCT 7.8.1, default import path, both heal-on/heal-off): shape(1)/shape(1); authored topology is 8 edges (4 outer + 4 inner) but the live shape carries `n_edges_total==10` (+2 over authored) — direct evidence the overlap-handling repair fired and restructured the collinear-overlap boundary rather than leaving it untouched. `brepcheck.valid==True` after heal (well-formed result); `face[0].area==79.0==100-(7*3)`, confirming the hole geometry survives the repair intact. Synonyms: "collinear wire overlap large", "hole edge overlaps outer boundary", "3-edge reconstruction sewing", "intersecting wires segment overlap 50 percent", "coincident collinear segment repair".
