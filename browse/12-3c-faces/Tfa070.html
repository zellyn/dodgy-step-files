Tfa070 — `CURVE_BOUNDED_SURFACE.implicit_outer = .U.` (unset BOOLEAN) leaves boundary semantics ambiguous (BRL-CAD step-g)

Sources

  • BRL-CAD `src/conv/step/step-g/CurveBoundedSurface.cpp` `Load` reads `implicit_outer` BOOLEAN with default `BUnset`
  • `LoadONBrep` is a stub printing `"not implemented for <entity>"`
  • bug-reporter language: "CURVE_BOUNDED_SURFACE implicit_outer unset", "STEP face with unknown implicit_outer flag dropped", "step-g cannot decide outer-boundary handling".

Sender

Hand-edited fixtures and a small population of fuzzed AP203 files where the producer left `implicit_outer` unspecified; also some early Inventor STEP exports that emitted `.U.` literal rather than `.T.`/`.F.`.

Description

The `CURVE_BOUNDED_SURFACE.implicit_outer` BOOLEAN slot has two valid states under the schema (`.T.` — natural surface boundary is implicitly the outer loop; `.F.` — every boundary in the `boundaries` list is explicit). step-g's `getBooleanAttribute` accepts a third state, `BUnset`, which leaves the receiver unable to choose between the two valid interpretations. Because `LoadONBrep` is a stub anyway the immediate symptom is silent face-drop; but even when LoadONBrep is implemented, the absence of a default for `.U.` means the importer has no defined behaviour. The schema-strict reading is to reject; a forgiving reader could heal by computing whether the listed boundaries cover the surface domain and inferring `implicit_outer` automatically.

Reproducer recipe

`#80=CURVE_BOUNDED_SURFACE('cbs_face', #14, (#70), .U.);`. step-g loads `implicit_outer = BUnset` and the LoadONBrep stub drops the face.

Expected kernel behavior

reject — emit `E_AMBIGUOUS_IMPLICIT_OUTER` and refuse the entity; alternatively heal by inspecting whether the listed boundaries cover the surface's natural domain and inferring the flag, with `W_INFERRED_IMPLICIT_OUTER` diagnostic.
