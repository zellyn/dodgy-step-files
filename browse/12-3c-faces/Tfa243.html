Notes

Corrected 2026-07-17 (truth-in-labeling audit, Q14 second half): retitled/redescribed — original claimed orientation repair needs a diagonal (u AND v) periodic shift to correctly classify containment between diagonally-opposite wire boxes; live-oracle shows the real, more consequential behavior: the reader gives up on establishing containment and silently promotes the un-nestable inner wire into its own independent face (1 declared face → 2 actual torus faces, 3 faces total in the shape). Compare Tfa186 (same silent split-into-independent-faces pattern, there from a missing `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` rather than a non-nested bound pair). Also fixes a literal `em-dash` text-corruption artifact left in the original title/body by a prior markdown-conversion bug. This entry is part of a 5-fixture torus family (Tfa241–Tfa245, all sharing an `em-dash`-garbled title from a prior markdown-conversion mishap, now fixed) built around periodic-surface wire/bound placement; each was independently live-oracle-verified — the family does NOT behave uniformly (see siblings' Notes for the differing outcomes). Part of the ~127-entry face-repair/small-face-check misnomer family identified in the truth-in-labeling audit — see `BACKLOG.md` Q14 for the full list and remediation approach. Synonyms: "non-nested torus bound pair silently split", "diagonal wire placement inflates face count".
