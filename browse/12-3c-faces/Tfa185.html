Notes

Corrected 2026-07-17 (truth-in-labeling audit, Q14 second half): retitled/redescribed — original claimed `FixAddNaturalBound` bridges the discontinuity but selects the wrong boundary topology; that method is never invoked, and the real, verified behavior is more severe than a topology mistake: the face is dropped in its entirety, silently, while the shape as a whole still reports a successful (non-null, 1-root) load — a genuine "empty face silently accepted" defect distinct from the claimed mechanism. **See also**: Tfa114, Tfa115. (Same silent-whole-bound-drop family — there triggered by collapsed/zero-length geometry rather than a knot-multiplicity crease.) Part of the ~127-entry face-repair/small-face-check misnomer family identified in the truth-in-labeling audit — see `BACKLOG.md` Q14 for the full list and remediation approach. Synonyms: "C0 knot-multiplicity crease drops face entirely", "empty shell hides dropped B-spline face".
