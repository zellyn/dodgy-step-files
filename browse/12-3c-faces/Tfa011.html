Tfa011 — `ADVANCED_FACE` with multiple `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` entries (face needs split)

§12.3c face-sliver-sewing

Sources

  • 13-quaoar B004 (face with duplicated outer contour)

Description

An `ADVANCED_FACE` is emitted with two or more `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` entries when STEP requires exactly one. Common signature: `ADVANCED_FACE.bounds` contains two distinct `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` instances for two disjoint regions (e.g. two 10×10 squares each with its own `EDGE_LOOP`). Often the result of a botched rehost/MakeFace where every 3D edge — including those already representing the contour — was reprojected onto a new surface without dedup, producing two pcurves tracing the same or two separate contours.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` with two `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` records (or, equivalently, one outer wire and one `FACE_BOUND` that geometrically duplicates it).

Expected kernel behavior

heal; split into two faces (one per outer wire) or merge if outer wires coincide; reject if the loops cross.

Notes

Synonyms: "face needs splitting into separate faces", "two outer boundaries on one face", "face has multiple outer loops", "ADVANCED_FACE with two FACE_OUTER_BOUND entries", "face encloses two disjoint regions".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(18) ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

sheet — the open shell is the intended final geometry; a solid-only kernel should cleanly refuse.

Tier-3 assertions

  • load == "ok"
  • n_edges_total >= 8
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • face[1].surface_type == "plane"
