Tfa064 — Missing-seam reconstruction fails on ADVANCED_FACE on CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE whose wire contains a partial seam line plus horizontal CIRCLE arcs

§12.3c faces/sewing

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-3c-faces/Tfa064.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0025553 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A face on a closed surface has a wire that includes part of the seam edge but not all. The seam-fix routine adds the missing portion to the wire; but inserts it at the wrong position, producing a wire whose edges are no longer in topological order. Wire becomes invalid.

Reproducer recipe

Cylindrical face whose wire has the seam at `U=0` for the lower half (`V=[0, h/2]`) but not the upper half (`V=[h/2, h]`). Heal inserts the missing upper half at the wire's tail rather than splicing it adjacent to its existing partner.

Expected kernel behavior

seam-fix must splice the missing seam segment in topological order, adjacent to the existing partial seam; preserve wire-edge ordering.

Notes

**See also**: Twi084. **OCC behavior**: the validity-checker does not flag the seam-fix wire-ordering defect; tier-3 valid-flag should be ignored for this entry. The reader most likely silently fixes or ignores the misordered seam splice on import. Synonyms: "missing-seam reconstruction fails with circle arcs", "cylinder face with partial seam plus circles", "seam rebuild can't handle mixed line and circle wire", "partial seam line confuses missing-seam helper".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(7) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].surface_type == "cylinder"
  • n_edges_total >= 4
  • n_vertices_total >= 8
  • brepcheck.valid == True
