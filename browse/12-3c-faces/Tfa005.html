Tfa005 — Periodic face given by single belt wire (degenerate pole case)

§12.3c face-sliver-sewing

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-3c-faces/Tfa005.stp

Sources

  • 05-occt-shapefix F032 (ShapeFix_Face::FixPeriodicDegenerated)

Description

A periodic surface (sphere, cone, toroidal pole) is bounded by a single closed wire that wraps once around the periodic direction. The face has a degenerate pole and the wire crosses (or skirts) the apex/pole.

Reproducer recipe

Cone face whose only outer wire passes through the apex `vertex_point`, no degenerate edge inserted.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: detect the single-belt periodic configuration; repair by inserting the implicit degenerate edge at the pole; coerce the wire orientation so it traces the UV rectangle correctly.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa004, Twi021. Synonyms: "wire wraps once around cone apex", "single closed wire across the pole", "boundary skirts singular point of surface", "missing degenerate edge at periodic pole", "wire crosses sphere pole without seam".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(12) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].surface_type == "sphere"
  • n_edges_total >= 8
  • face[1].surface_type == "cone"
  • n_vertices_total >= 16
