Tfa061 — Sewing of thin faces takes excessive time during seam-edge creation

§12.3c faces/sewing

P1 — silent data loss

topology

View raw fixture (.stp) step-examples/12-3c-faces/Tfa061.stp

Sources

  • OCCT MANTIS#0030831 (OCCT MANTIS tracker 502 as of 2026-05-02)

Description

A shell contains many extremely thin faces (strip width below tolerance) on closed surfaces. The seam-edge-creation step in shape healing iterates per-face and per-edge with quadratic complexity, taking minutes on shells with thousands of thin faces. The defect is not algorithmic correctness but the file-pattern: input that exposes a known performance pathology.

Reproducer recipe

Shell of ~400 ribbon-thin faces, each 0.001 mm wide by 100 mm long, sharing edges along their long sides. Process through sewing.

Expected kernel behavior

Heal and accept: coerce sewing / seam-creation to sub-quadratic in face count; thin-face handling must not degenerate to all-pairs edge comparison. Must not hang on thin-face inputs.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa040, Tsh046, Pf027. Fixture is a scaled representative (12 ribbon faces) of the catalog's ~400; topology pattern (shared long edges between thin ribbons) is identical. Synonyms: "sewing many thin faces is extremely slow", "seam-edge creation runs forever", "thin face sewing performance regression", "sewing time blows up on strip faces".

Expected validation

occt=empty/empty gmsh=empty ifc=schema_n/a

Closure intent

solid (defect kind: unstitched_seam) — authored as a closed solid; openness is the defect — heal it shut.
