Description

Face on a SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION crashes the reader (SIGSEGV) with just the outer 4-edge boundary present — not attributable to the 'degenerate apex' meridian wires the title describes; periodic-degenerate seam repair is never reached

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with a diagnostic naming the ADVANCED_FACE on this SURFACE_OF_REVOLUTION: isolation shows the crash persists even after stripping all four apex/meridian inner-loop wires down to the outer 4-edge boundary alone, so a correct kernel must detect that this face cannot be safely translated on this surface and refuse to build it with a diagnostic naming the face and surface, rather than crashing the process — the specific structural trigger beyond the outer boundary is not established, so the check must be conservative rather than target a specific meridian construct.
