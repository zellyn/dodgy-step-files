Hea010 — Shape-divide pipeline propagates B_SPLINE_SURFACE C0 knot-line splits (e.g., U-knot multiplicity = degree) to topology

§12.3 (sub-class: shape-healing) — split propagation

topology

Sources

  • OCCT `ShapeUpgrade_ShapeDivide::Perform` (`ShapeUpgrade_ShapeDivide.hxx:71`)

Description

When a surface is split (at a seam, knot line, or area threshold), every face built on it must be re-bounded; every edge that crosses the cut must be sub-divided; every wire containing those edges must be re-stitched. The shape-divide pipeline propagates splits transitively through the topology. Typical fixture: an ADVANCED_FACE on a B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS where one U-knot (e.g., 0.5) has multiplicity equal to the degree (degree 3, multiplicity 3 → C0 continuity), and the face's outer rectangular EDGE_LOOP crosses through that knot value where the surface has a C0 crease.

Reproducer recipe

An ADVANCED_FACE on a B_SPLINE_SURFACE_WITH_KNOTS with a C0 cut line (knot multiplicity = degree); after dividing the surface, the face's wires and EDGE_CURVEs must all be re-built consistently.

Expected kernel behavior

emit a topologically equivalent shape whose underlying geometry is split; preserve incidence relations.

Cross-references to Tfa051 (face split), Twi080 (wire propagation), Gp033 (curve split). Synonyms: "surface split at C0 knot propagates to faces", "C0 knot multiplicity equal to degree splits topology", "shape-divide propagates through wires and edges", "knot-line split rebuilds faces and edges".
