Tfa135 — Face with three small inner wires (not a claimed cascading-removal effect) crashes the reader (SIGSEGV) as soon as the extra bound wire is present; loads cleanly with only the outer wire — small-area-wire repair is never reached

Description

Face with three small inner wires (not a claimed cascading-removal effect) crashes the reader (SIGSEGV) as soon as the extra bound wire is present; loads cleanly with only the outer wire — small-area-wire repair is never reached

Reproducer recipe

Expected kernel behavior

Reject with a diagnostic naming the extra bound wire: a planar face whose outer wire alone loads cleanly but which crashes as soon as any one of its declared inner (small-area) wires is added must have that construction rejected during shape translation before the crash, not allowed to abort the process — building the face from the outer wire only, with a diagnostic identifying the dropped inner wire, is preferable to a hard crash.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa093. Corrected 2026-07-17 (truth-in-labeling audit): retitled/redescribed — original claimed small-area-wire repair removes the first small wire, which changes the area context and cascades removal of the second; that method is never reached because the file crashes (SIGSEGV) during shape translation before any wire-level healing pass runs. Isolation testing (stripping the extra bound wire) confirms the outer-bound-alone face loads cleanly; the crash is reproduced by restoring the additional wire. Part of the ~127-entry face-repair/small-face-check misnomer family — see `BACKLOG.md` Q14. Synonyms: "multi-bound face crashes reader, outer-alone loads clean", "first inner wire alone crashes reader, no cascade needed".
