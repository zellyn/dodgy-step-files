Notes

Part of the ~127-entry `ShapeFix_Face`/`ShapeAnalysis_CheckSmallFace` misnomer family identified in the truth-in-labeling audit — see `BACKLOG.md` Q14 for the full list and remediation approach. Synonyms: "curved outer boundary with rectangular hole loads without polygon-approximation area check".
