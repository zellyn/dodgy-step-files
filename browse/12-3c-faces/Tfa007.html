Tfa007 — Strip face: face one-dimensional within tolerance

§12.3c face-sliver-sewing

  • 05-occt-shapefix F026 (ShapeFix_FixSmallFace::FixStripFace)
  • 09-healing-menus H049/H077/H094/H105 (StripSupportFace, SingleStripFace)

Description

A face whose UV bounds collapse one parametric direction to within tolerance. The face is geometrically a thin strip; perpendicular distance between its two long boundary edges < tolerance.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` on `PLANE` with two long parallel edges of length 100 mm separated by `1e-7` mm and two short connecting edges.

Expected kernel behavior

heal — collapse the strip face to a `tolerant_edge` between its two long sides; remove face; merge incident shells.

Notes

**See also**: Gs014. Synonyms: "face is a thin strip", "two long edges sit on top of each other", "face width less than tolerance", "face is geometrically a line", "strip face needs absorbing into neighbor".

Expected validation

occt=shape(1)/shape(1) gmsh=shape(5) ifc=schema_n/a

Tier-3 assertions

  • face[0].sliver_aspect_max_min > 1e3
  • face[0].surface_type == "plane"
  • n_edges_total >= 2
