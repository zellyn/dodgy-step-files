Tfa254 — Outer/inner wire touch at a clean endpoint-endpoint contact

Distinct from Tfa039 (transverse interior crossing) and Twi250 (crossing near one edge's endpoint, `FindVertAndSplitEdge`, not a clean both-endpoints touch).

Description

A 10×10 `PLANE` face's `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` is the unit square (0,0)-(10,0)-(10,10)-(0,10). Its inner `FACE_BOUND` (hole) is a small right triangle (0,0)-(3,0)-(0,3): its own corner at (0,0) sits at the exact same coordinate as the outer wire's own (0,0) corner, but is encoded as an INDEPENDENT `VERTEX_POINT` entity — not the same STEP id reused. Both occurrences of (0,0) are genuine edge ENDPOINTS on their respective wires (never a mid-edge point), so the contact is a clean endpoint-endpoint touch, not a crossing.

Reproducer recipe

`ADVANCED_FACE` whose `FACE_OUTER_BOUND` and inner `FACE_BOUND` each have an edge endpoint at the identical 3D coordinate, encoded as two distinct `VERTEX_POINT` entities — on a live `OPEN_SHELL`/`SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` path.

Expected kernel behavior

detect the coincident-but-distinct endpoint vertices and unify them into one shared topological vertex (`UnionVertexes`); classify the resulting wire relationship consistently (hole-touching-boundary is a degenerate but well-defined case) or reject with a clear diagnostic.

Notes

**See also**: Tfa039 (transverse crossing, not endpoint contact), Twi250 (near-endpoint crossing, different repair branch). Live-verified (this worktree's OCP/OCCT 7.8.1, default import path, both heal-on/heal-off): shape(1)/shape(1); authored topology has 7 independent `VERTEX_POINT` entities (4 outer + 3 inner) but the live shape carries only 6 unique vertex instances — direct evidence the two independently-authored (0,0) vertices were merged into one shared vertex, the vertex-union path this entry targets. The resulting face fails the overall validity check: the raw wire-crossing test itself reports clean (the touch point is excused as a shared vertex, not flagged as a crossing), but a separate wire-nesting/containment check fails because it cannot cleanly decide the hole is "inside" the outer wire once its corner is unified with the boundary; reported honestly as a genuine, live side effect of the touching-at-a-shared-corner configuration rather than mirrored from an unrelated fixture. Synonyms: "endpoint-endpoint wire contact", "coincident distinct vertices unified", "hole touches outer boundary at corner", "clean vertex-union not crossing", "wire touch not intersection".
